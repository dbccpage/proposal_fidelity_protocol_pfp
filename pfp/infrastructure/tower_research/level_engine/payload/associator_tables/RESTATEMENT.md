# Restatement: `associator_tables`

AuthorityEffect: None.

Class of the challenge: externally imposed challenge. It is answered in this
harness, outside the sealed repository, and it is not attributed to the seed.

## Obligation

A skeletal table over cyclic groups (objects the integers mod `n`, every
object's self-maps the integers mod `m`, acting trivially) gives a
rebracketing value at every triple. At every quadruple the two composites
around the five-term boundary must agree. The claimed class, a coboundary
of some two-argument table or not, must be the recomputed one, found by
trying every two-argument table.

## Refused case

Two different composites declared coherent.
