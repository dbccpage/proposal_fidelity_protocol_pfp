# Restatement: `cell_orientation`

AuthorityEffect: None.

Class of the challenge: externally imposed challenge. It is answered in this
harness, outside the sealed repository, and it is not attributed to the seed.

## Obligation

The sides of a hosted complex agree with the sides of its cells, and the cells
carry a consistent choice of sign: two cells listing a common side traverse it
in opposite directions.

## Refused case

A complex with a side of a cell missing is refused.

## Notes

The consistent choice is a witness, found by the minimal search on the witness
form. Its non-uniqueness is the native form of a choice that is declared, not
forced.
