# Claim status procedure

Name what formal declarations prove, with assumptions and axiom policy; separate
written arguments, finite tests and supplied premises. Keep the actual result,
a quantitative bound and its proven parameter range distinct. Unknown outside
one certificate is not false, and a stronger-margin endpoint need not exhaust a
weaker qualitative consequence of the same argument.
For Meron's reported bound, 1/2-c*theta is 1/4 at theta_*, 1/8 at
3*theta_*/2, and zero at 2*theta_*. The middle point remains conditionally
positive; a zero lower bound at the endpoint does not make the true value zero.
State endpoints and unresolved bridges in the next brief. Exact arithmetic
checks this implication only; it does not verify the analytic bound or formalize
the model. Preserve earlier finite-versus-formal scope discipline.

Read a check's deciding predicate and cited evidence before using its PASS.
A written-argument or audit-table-presence check does not verify the argument.
Imported criteria remain assumptions until their applicable hypotheses are
established; metadata, citations and arithmetic-only Lean builds cannot close
that analytic obligation. Keep historical compile scope and later repairs.
