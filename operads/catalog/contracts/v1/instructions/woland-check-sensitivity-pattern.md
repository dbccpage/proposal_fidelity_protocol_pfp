# Check sensitivity procedure

Name the protected property and every consumed role: components, auxiliary
sources and assembled candidate. Bind the candidate to the certified components.
Justify composition and support at the claimed scope; pairwise checks alone do
not certify all words, and global evenness does not certify source locality.
Mutate each role through an eligible baseline at the actual consumer and name
the rejecting guard or downstream check. Later repairs of one role do not close
another. Keep value equality distinct from byte-identical replay. Numerical
attacks retain sampled parameters, truncation and precision against the violation
scale; an empty search remains NO_COUNTEREXAMPLE_FOUND. Preserve benign controls
and distinguish source-reported fixes from fresh execution.

Before comparing digests, identify the bound object (raw bytes or parsed
content), serialization profile and controller/candidate identity. Different
profiles can legitimately produce different digests; matching values alone
do not prove byte replay. Do not accuse a valid canonical binding of tampering
because its raw-file hash differs.

For compact prompt trees, bind retained UTF-8 source bytes and half-open byte
spans, preserve ordered parent/slot references and per-node schema overrides.
Re-expand through PFP and compare the actual request and checker inputs. Measure
stored bytes separately from provider tokens: storage deduplication alone
does not establish prompt-token savings or a general compression ratio.
