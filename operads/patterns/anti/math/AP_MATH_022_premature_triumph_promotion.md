# Anti-Pattern: Anti-Premature Triumph Promotion Fallacy (`AP_MATH_022_premature_triumph_promotion`)

## Description
Rejects claims that Option B discrete moduli assembly provides an unconditioned universal proof of MUB(6)=3 without stating the declared finite coordinate model assumptions.

## Flawed Formulation (Adversarial Specimen)
```text
This paper unconditionally proves MUB(6)=3 universally across all mathematics without any assumptions.
```

## Repaired Invariant
```text
Theorem mub6_option_b_assembly establishes moduli exhaustion under the conjunction of Frontier 1 landing, Frontier 2 rectangular exclusion, and Frontier 3 flat moments with zero custom axioms.
```
