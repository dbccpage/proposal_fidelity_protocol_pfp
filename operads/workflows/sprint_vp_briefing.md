# Workflow: Sprint VP Briefing

**Version:** 2.0
**Roles:** `product-manager`, `blackrod`, `techne-sprint-vp-briefing`, `procurators`

---

## Briefing Protocol

1. **Briefing Assembly:** `product-manager` and `blackrod` summarize sprint delivery, evidence status, and unmitigated engineering risks.
2. **VP of Engineering Compilation:** Compile `vp_engineering_report.md` and `blackrod_action_report.md`.
3. **Logos Techne Council Briefing:** Prepare executive report for the next meeting of the Logos Techne Council (`L3_logos_techne`).
4. **Convening Authority:** Call `L3_logos_techne` on demand via `procurators`.
