# Workflow: Sprint Retrospective

**Version:** 2.0
**Roles:** `sprint-manager`, SADC Council Architects (`Logos`, `Aletheia`, `Dianoia`, `Ergon`, `Glossa`, `Kybernesis`, `Panepistimion`), `blackrod`

---

## Retrospective Protocol

1. **SADC Architect Presentations:** Each present SADC council architect provides a 1-minute delivery and architectural assessment.
2. **Review Delivered Evidence:** Cross-check evidence paths under `omega_engine/research/product_planning_office/plans/sprints/{NNN}/evidence/` against completed stories.
3. **Capture Lessons Learned:** Document operational friction, process defects, and skill improvements.
4. **Emit `retrospective.md` & `completion.md`:** Finalize sprint closing documentation.
5. **Handoff:** Invoke `/techne-sprint-vp-briefing` to brief the VP of Engineering for Logos Techne Council reporting.
