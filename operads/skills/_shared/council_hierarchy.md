## Philosophy

### The 1→2→4→N Pattern

- **1**: Single human owner (oooutwire - Λ_Ω)
- **2**: Two executive modes (Structure via Logos, Humanity via Nous)
- **4**: Four core functions (Inquiry/L1, Synthesis/Nous, Standards/Logos, Enforcement/Blackrod)
- **N**: Unlimited domain specialization (L3 councils and L4 teams)

### Recursive Self-Improvement

Each layer improves the layers below through:

- Peer review (ego deflation)
- Calibration feedback (accuracy improvement)
- Jester-General moments (truth through play)
- Blackrod verification (claims → artifacts)

### Anti-Sycophancy Design

- ERC specifically monitors for sycophantic drift
- Jester-General facets in every council
- Peer review pairs prevent echo chambers
- Calibration tracking reveals overconfidence
