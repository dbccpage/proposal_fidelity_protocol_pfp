# The Rules of the General Theory of Layered Authority (GTLA) -- LLM Reference Rubric

**AuthorityEffect:** None.

> **Mandatory Governance Directive:** Every AI agent, subagent, and LLM executing within the Sentinel Engine repository MUST observe these 7 GTLA Invariants across all code, prose, agendas, minutes, and proof ledgers. Aligned with published monograph `007_gtla.md` (v2.0.1).

---


## 1. Non-Creation & Authority Ceiling (`AuthorityEffect: None`)

* **Law:** Authority is a proof-relevant record requiring explicit evidence, obligations, witness-persistence data, and a separate `VerifiedPromotion` certificate. The central theorem proves that composable transitions whose authority effect is `None` strictly preserve grade `None`.
* **Fence:** Neither LLM fluency, confidence scores, prose descriptions, heuristic metrics, nor safe-API non-construction can mint operational authority. All AI-generated outputs carry `AuthorityEffect: None`. Human promotion releases to PROD.

---

## 2. Admitted Root & Primitive Difference Separation

* **Law:** The admitted root of the engine and tower is $\text{Difference} \sqcup \text{Structure}$. Primitive difference $\delta_0$ is primitive; structure is carrier.
* **Fence:** Never preload continuous manifolds, arbitrary weights, or unverified graphs into genesis ($\text{MSS-0}$). Morphisms are admitted transitions with explicit generated and discharged obligation vectors in a free commutative monoid.

---

## 3. Zero View-from-Nowhere (Observer Indexing)

* **Law:** Every metric, evaluation, role, or observation must be explicitly indexed by a declared, finite local observer or role packet ($\text{Role}_i \in \text{Tower}$).
* **Fence:** An un-indexed, global, or omnipresent "View-from-Nowhere" observer apex is strictly forbidden. All observers are quarantined to their declared layer scope.

---

## 4. Zero-Smuggling & Four-Axis Separation

* **Law:** Systems must maintain strict separation across four independent axes with zero implicit coercions:
  1. *Diagnostic Axis:* Exact repair ($\Phi_1 = 0$), closed residual ($\Phi_1 > 0, \Gamma_2 = 0$), open obstruction ($\Gamma_2 > 0$).
  2. *Routing Axis:* Terminal routing values (`CheckPassed`, `RequiresLift`, `Repair`, `Refusal`, `NoRule`, `BudgetUnknown`, `Refused`), owned by `core_types::routing`.
  3. *Extension Axis:* Presentation depth from proved lifts.
  4. *Authority Axis:* Grade + `AuthorityEffect` + external `VerifiedPromotion` certificate, with transition guards owned by `core_types::fda`.
* **Fence:** Higher-layer authority or unearned proof status cannot be smuggled into lower-layer proposals. Diagnostic green or residual mass is **not** authority.

---

## 5. Witness Dominance & Budget Honesty

* **Law:** Replayable open-obstruction witnesses ($\Gamma_2 > 0$) dominate resource terminals. If an open obstruction is witnessed, the terminal disposition MUST be `OpenObstruction` / `ActiveClosureFailure` (`Repair` or `Refusal`).
* **Fence:** `BudgetUnknown` is a resource debt record (precommitted exhaustion or unproven exhaustiveness), NEVER an obstruction class. Emitting `BudgetUnknown` or a pass when $\Gamma_2 > 0$ is witnessed violates budget honesty and fails the audit gate.

---

## 6. Disjoint Terminal Outcome Taxonomy

* **Law:** Every evaluation path must terminate in exactly one pairwise disjoint token per registry axis:
  * *Diagnostic Class Axis:* `ExactRepair`, `ClosedResidual`, `OpenObstruction`.
  * *Routing Terminal Axis:* `CheckPassed`, `RequiresLift`, `Repair`, `Refusal`, `NoRule`, `BudgetUnknown`, `Refused` (owned by `core_types::routing`, with guards owned by `core_types::fda`).
* **Fence:** Overlapping or ambiguous terminal states are prohibited.

---

## 7. Bridge Admissibility & Factorization

* **Law:** Evaluation profiles must factor through cofinal finite shadow towers and degree-bounded query families ($q = \bar{q} \circ \text{Prof}$).
* **Fence:** Unbounded, non-separable profile spaces or non-jointly separating query families are strictly fenced as `BOUNDED_EVALUATION_SCOPE`.

---
