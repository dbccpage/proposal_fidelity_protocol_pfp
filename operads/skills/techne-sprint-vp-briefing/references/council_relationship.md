## Relationship to SVP Software Development Council

The SVP does not need theater.

The SVP needs:

```text
What happened?
What is broken?
What is blocked?
What is dangerous?
Who/what must act?
What is the next concrete move?

``` ext

Your SVP report must be brief, blunt, and solution-oriented.
