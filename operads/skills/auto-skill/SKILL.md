```text
[Genesis Replay & Dual L/F Regime Scan]
                  │
                  ▼
[Orbit-Quotiented Pair-Separation Policy]
                  │
                  ▼

                  │
                  ▼
[Token-Blind Isomorphism Quotienting]
                  │
                  ▼
[Episode Formulation & Resolution Gate]
                  │
                  ▼
[Genesis DAG Replay & Bounds Check]
```

1. **Dual L/F Regime Carrier Scan**:
   - Carry both $L$ (literal) and $F$ (formal) readings in parallel.
   - Assert that no internal fact is fabricated to prematurely collapse the dual reading.
2. **Pair-Separation Closure Policy**:
   - For collapsed pairs, form exactly one challenge per automorphism orbit.
   - Enforce orbit-policy equivariance: prevent least-pair or presentation-dependent selection.
3. **Cost Measure Minimization ($\mu_1 / \mu_2$)**:
   - Enumerate minimal candidate responses under declared cost measures $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$.
   - Retain all non-isomorphic tied classes; do not discard structural ties arbitrarily.
4. **Token-Blind Isomorphism Quotienting**:
   - Forget presentation-level tokens; quotient candidate deltas by structural isomorphism.
   - Enforce strict token-blindness across Lean 4 proof kernels and Python carriers.
5. **Episode Encoding & Disposition Routing**:
   - Encode candidate delta into structured episode JSON (`omega.skill_compiler.episode.input.v1`).
   - Route disposition: `NOISE` (discard), `CODE_GATE` / `SHARED_CONTRACT` (patch contract), `EXISTING_OP` (compressive edit), `NEW_SKILL` (ooutwire proposal only).
6. **Genesis DAG Replay & Bound Enforcement**:
   - Replay execution trace from genesis node.
   - Validate that state update strictly contracts residual $\Delta > 0$ and restores symmetries.
   - Halt at exact declared bounds or at fixpoint termination.
