# Premise transfer procedure

Match each bound's premises across proof, public display, structured claim and
consuming input contract. Repairing one projection does not repair them all.
Keep different bounds' domains separate. Check an admitted boundary and a
distinct excluded input at the actual consumer, with its expected reason.
An applicable structural argument may discharge a premise at its stated scope;
finite pair checks alone cannot certify all composed words. Read later repairs
before calling an old defect current. Separate historical observations, missing
premises and unavailable checks in the next brief.
