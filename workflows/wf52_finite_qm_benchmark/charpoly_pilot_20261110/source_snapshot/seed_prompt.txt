Consider two distinguishable spin-1/2 systems with computational basis |00>, |01>, |10>, |11>, where Z|0>=|0> and Z|1>=-|1>. Set hbar=k_B=1 and let X,Y,Z denote the Pauli matrices. The Hamiltonian is
H = J (X tensor X + Y tensor Y) + Delta (Z tensor I - I tensor Z) + K Z tensor Z,
with real J, Delta, K. The initial state is |01>.

Give a self-contained derivation of all the following, with conventions and exceptional cases explicit:
1. The full spectrum and spectral projectors of H, including degeneracies and the limit J=Delta=0.
2. The exact state at time t and the transition probability P_01_to_10(t). Determine its supremum over t>=0 and the earliest time attaining it, including the constant-probability case.
3. The reduced density matrix of the first spin, its von Neumann entropy in bits, the pure-state concurrence, and the maximal CHSH expectation over local dichotomic projective spin measurements. Determine exactly when a maximally entangled state is reachable and the earliest time reaching one.
4. For inverse temperature beta>=0, the Gibbs density matrix exp(-beta H)/Z_beta, its partition function, all four eigenvalues of its partial transpose with respect to the second spin, and a necessary and sufficient condition for thermal entanglement. Explain the beta=0 and J=Delta=0 limits.
5. Provide independent consistency checks (normalization, initial conditions, the decoupled limit, and one nontrivial numerical example). Distinguish algebraic identities from numerical checks. Do not assume a formula remains defined when its denominator vanishes.

Report exact formulas and reasoning, not only numerical answers. Use the same Hamiltonian convention throughout.
