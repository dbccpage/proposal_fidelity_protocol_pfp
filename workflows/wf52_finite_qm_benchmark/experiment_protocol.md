# Frozen-seed experiment protocol

Status: live experiments are underway. Frozen receipts, costs and post-freeze grades are indexed in results_summary.json. This protocol records the evaluation boundary; it does not assert a workflow benefit.

The source seed is `seed_prompt.txt`. Every workflow receives these identical UTF-8 bytes as a separate immutable input. Wrappers may add instructions or candidate artifacts but may not replace assumptions, omit requested parts, change the Hamiltonian convention, or substitute an integer/cochain task. A preserved seed hash is necessary but not sufficient: the executed prompt must actually include that seed, and its full byte hash is retained.

The answer key and its worked derivation are held outside the repository and committed by hash in `commitment.json`. Its independent dense-matrix numerical validation is recorded in `validation_receipt.json`. Those numerical checks support specified formulas; they are not a formal proof of every symbolic statement. No workflow or research-agent context receives the answer key, its path, or its derivation. The workflow broker must have no repository-wide retrieval, shell, filesystem or private-evaluator tools.

There are two distinct checks. Online workflow checks may assess declared schemas, candidate-supplied algebraic identities, explicit assumptions and non-secret consistency predicates. They must not consult the private answer key. The trusted evaluator scores the frozen final response afterward, with no feedback channel into the workflow. Any experiment that admits answer-key feedback is a separately labeled oracle-assisted experiment and cannot be compared as a blind run.

The fixed rubric is 100 points: spectrum/projectors/degeneracies 20; dynamics/probabilities/times 20; pure-state entanglement/Bell results 25; Gibbs/partial-transpose/thermal results 25; independent checks/conventions 10. A score requires a trusted mathematical review of the derivation, parameter branches and equivalent formula forms. Numeric substitution alone cannot mark a universal analytic claim correct. The evaluator records each deduction and supporting counterexample or invalid step; a model's self-score is never accuracy.

Start with a direct same-model baseline and a small preregistered set of mechanisms. Randomize run order and repeat independent provider samples. Retain failures, refusals, truncations, timeouts and incomplete accounts in the denominator. Report raw per-run scores and costs before averages. Within-item paired differences compare the same seed and matched model/settings. A confidence interval across repeated stochastic calls concerns this one item, not the distribution of graduate QM problems.

Use separate comparisons for equal money, equal token allowance and equal wall-time allowance; they are different constraints and cannot generally be equalized simultaneously. Report total provider cost, input/output/reasoning/cache token fields, wall time, sum of provider durations, and rubric score. Where a provider withholds a field, record unavailable rather than zero. Baseline cost/accuracy ratios are undefined when their denominator is zero; show raw deltas and the nondominated cost/accuracy/time frontier instead.

PCIV mutations must protect the exact seed, hash parent/child prompts and keep the mutation rule fixed for a run. Reusing scores to choose future prompts is exploratory tuning on this item. It does not establish held-out learning or transfer. EIS detectors require independent calibration fixtures and false-rejection checks; no detector is adequate merely because it agrees with the solver.

Every execution shares one USD 0.10 reservation ledger across all stages, branches and retries. Before a paid campaign, freeze model/provider and tariff observations, compiled workflows, randomization, replicate counts, stopping rules and an aggregate campaign budget. The user supplied a per-run cap, not authorization for an arbitrarily large total spend. Each launched campaign is finite and separately frozen; inspect its manifest and settled ledger for actual spending.
