## 4-Directory Lesson Architecture

In accordance with `governance-pedagogy`, all curriculum tracks maintain four aligned subdirectories:
- `concepts/`: Theoretical exposition, definitions, invariants, and checks for understanding.
- `notebooks/`: Interactive Jupyter workbenches with preflight checks, prediction gates, and adversarial falsifiers.
- `scripts/`: Video storyboard scripts with second-by-second timing, on-screen cues, and voiceover pacing.
- `videos/`: Recorded lectures, terminal animations, and multimedia assets.
