## System in one paragraph

OEWAS is a shadow-mode package with skill-document and catalog models, a limited
planar typed-composition scaffold, workflow graph validation, legacy projections,
an evidence-oriented anti-skill admission boundary, and SQLite/API adapters. The
separate `.agents/operad` tree supplies a reference registry, residual diagnostics,
dispatch resolution, and a skill-compiler pipeline. Together they provide useful
syntax, custody, and refusal mechanisms. They do not currently constitute a
production workflow executor, a symmetric-operad implementation, a lossless
CommonMark or event-sourced system, a contractivity proof, or a semantic migration
proof.

## Reader contract

Read the implementation state separately from the aspiration. Operadic language
describes typed composition syntax unless a named witness establishes more.
Receipts bind only the fields they actually digest. Human decisions remain human
authority; generators and agents do not acquire it by emitting a schema-conforming
artifact. Research references motivate tests and designs but do not validate this
repository.
