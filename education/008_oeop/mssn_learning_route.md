## Four distinct operations

| Operation | Input | Output | Finite check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typed composition | Operation and compatible ordered ports | Composed workflow | Types, arity and substitution laws |
| Detection | Candidate bytes and a scoped detector | Finding or no finding | Defect examples and clean near misses |
| Prompt mutation | Parent bytes and a declared operator | Candidate child with ancestry | Content hashes, parent references and budgets |
| Evaluation | Fixed cases and a candidate | Scoped result | Independent case outcomes and refusal paths |

The current Rust pharmacy separates findings from repairs. Calling a detector an
anti-skill does not give it an inverse or antimorphism law. A typed reversal has
its own carrier and equations, explored in the separate reversal demos.

The Python immune admission entry points are retired compatibility boundaries.
They should return a typed retirement refusal. They do not activate the richer
negative-selection or prompt-population lifecycle described in planning specs.
The educational route tests that distinction and presents local teaching
detectors under their own scope.
