# Prompt Civilization & Lamarckian Evolution Curriculum Track

**AuthorityEffect:** None.

## Overview

The `4_prompt_civilization_and_lamarckian_evolution` curriculum track provides pedagogical laboratories, interactive notebooks, and mathematical walkthroughs covering **Prompt Civilization & Lamarckian Evolution**:
From governed prompt material and lineage DAG separation to hop-bounded Lamarckian retro-inheritance, Pareto selection, inverse PageRank, ecological reserves, lineage taint, durable event logging, and multi-island ecology.

## 4-Directory Lesson Architecture

In accordance with `governance-pedagogy`, all curriculum tracks maintain four aligned subdirectories:
- `concepts/`: Theoretical exposition, definitions, invariants, and checks for understanding.
- `notebooks/`: Interactive Jupyter workbenches with preflight checks, prediction gates, and adversarial falsifiers.
- `scripts/`: Video storyboard scripts with second-by-second timing, on-screen cues, and voiceover pacing.
- `videos/`: Recorded lectures, terminal animations, and multimedia assets.
