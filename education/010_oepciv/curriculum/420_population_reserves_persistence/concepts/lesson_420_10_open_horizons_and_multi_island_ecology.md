## 1. Pedagogical Objective
Examine v0.2 open horizons including multi-island evolutionary ecology, homologous block crossover recombination, island migration policy, and intelligence metabolism.

## 2. Key Concepts
1. **Multi-Island Ecology:** Population sub-civilizations evolve on semi-isolated islands, exchanging migrants at controlled intervals to prevent local optima stagnation.
2. **Crossover Recombination:** Swapping homologous plastic scaffold blocks between non-dominated genomes while preserving canalized system boundaries.
3. **Intelligence Metabolism:** Regulating resource consumption, provider call budgets, and token expenditure across population islands.

## 3. Check for Understanding
- How does island migration prevent evolutionary stagnation across prompt populations?
- Why must crossover recombination respect canalized boundary spans?
