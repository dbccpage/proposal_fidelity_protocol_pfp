## 1. Pedagogical Foundation & 2026 Attention Model

### 1.1 The 2026 Attention Decay Law
In 2026, uninterrupted digital attention decays exponentially with a measured half-life:

$$\text{Attention}(t) = A_0 \cdot 2^{-t / t_{1/2}}, \quad t_{1/2} \approx 4.0\text{ minutes}$$

Passive video lecturing or wall-of-text reading triggers steep cognitive attrition past $t = 6\text{ minutes}$. Every lesson in this curriculum is strictly engineered into **3–5 Minute Pulse Units**:

```text
[0:00 - 0:45] Hook & Sensory Paradox  ──> (Why can't two straight lines enclose a room?)
[0:45 - 1:45] Direct Visual Inspection ──> (Inspect the drawing: the angle remains open)
```

## 2. Quantitative Learning & Teaching Efficacy Metrics

We measure teaching and learning effectiveness objectively through four ring-protocol metrics:

### 2.1 Diagnostic Probe Pass Rate ($P_{\text{diag}}$)
Before advancing, a student answers a targeted diagnostic question targeting common misconceptions:
$$P_{\text{diag}} = \frac{N_{\text{passed on first try}}}{N_{\text{total attempts}}} \ge 0.90$$

### 2.2 Representation Switch Transfer Index ($I_{\text{transfer}}$)
True comprehension requires converting knowledge across four representations (Prose $\leftrightarrow$ Diagram $\leftrightarrow$ Equation $\leftrightarrow$ Code):
$$I_{\text{transfer}} = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{i=1}^M \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{correct translation } T_i\}} \ge 0.85$$

### 2.3 Teaching Efficacy Score ($E_{\text{teach}}$)
The ratio of demonstrated concept retention ($\Delta K$) to presentation friction ($F_{\text{friction}}$):
$$E_{\text{teach}} = \frac{\Delta K}{\log_2(1 + \text{TokenCount} / 250)} \cdot (1 - \text{DefectRate})$$
Where:
- $\Delta K = P_{\text{post-test}} - P_{\text{pre-test}}$.
- $\text{DefectRate} = \text{percentage of students falling into identified cognitive traps}$.

### 2.4 Claim Hygiene Metric ($S_{\text{hygiene}}$)
Zero tolerance for speculative promotion:
$$S_{\text{hygiene}} = 1.0 \iff \text{Forbidden Words Count} = 0 \land \text{Speculative Claims Tagged}$$
