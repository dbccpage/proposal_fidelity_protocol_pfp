# Advanced integration route

AuthorityEffect: None.

These lessons compute declared finite geometric and dynamic quantities while
keeping their broader interpretation separate from the arithmetic.

1. [Four-simplex volume](system_integration/01_four_simplex_volume.ipynb)
   computes its determinant independently by two methods.
2. [Pairing and degree-four certificate](system_integration/02_pairing_and_degree_four_certificate.ipynb)
   evaluates a declared intersection form and checks a finite quotient witness.
3. [Survivor birth and death](system_integration/03_survivor_birth_and_death.ipynb)
   checks element identities behind unchanged obstruction counts.
4. [Transfer trace and shadow](system_integration/04_transfer_trace_and_shadow.ipynb)
   computes return counts and an exact finite determinant identity.

[integration roadmap](../README.md) for evidence scope and
