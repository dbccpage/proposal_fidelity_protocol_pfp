# Researcher integration route

AuthorityEffect: None.

These lessons connect finite checks to the source, permission, and claim
boundaries needed to interpret research artifacts.

1. [Coverage and licensed depth](system_integration/01_coverage_and_licensed_depth.ipynb)
   distinguishes registry rows, proposal status, and accepted lift evidence.
2. [Immune boundary and negative selection](system_integration/02_immune_boundary_and_negative_selection.ipynb)
   checks typed retirement refusals and bounded detector controls.
3. [Governed prompt evolution](system_integration/03_governed_prompt_evolution.ipynb)
   filters a finite teaching population before declared cost selection.
4. [Source-bound research audit](system_integration/04_source_bound_research_audit.ipynb)
   checks the per-file inventory and detects changed source bytes.

Read the [curriculum review](../curriculum_125_review.md) and
[integration roadmap](../README.md) for evidence scope and
