## One bipartite incidence matrix

This sitting checks subdeterminants of one directed complete bipartite graph $K_{2,3}$. Every square submatrix of that one integer matrix has determinant in $\{-1, 0, 1\}$. The Hoffman-Kruskal statement for every directed graph is background. This cell does not decide it.
