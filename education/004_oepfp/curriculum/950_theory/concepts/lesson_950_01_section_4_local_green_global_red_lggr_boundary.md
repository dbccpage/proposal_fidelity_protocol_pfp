## Section 4: Local Green / Global Red (LGGR) Boundary Dynamics

### [Declared] The Asymmetric Verdict Law
The central epistemological principle of the Symbol Game and CODA is the **Asymmetric Verdict Law**:
$$\mathbf{RED \text{ is sound}} \quad \text{vs.} \quad \mathbf{GREEN \text{ is unsound}}$$

- **RED is Sound**: A detected contradiction $\Phi_1(K) > 0$ or non-zero Čech defect constitutes a witnessed, mathematically verified invariant violation. A single cycle with non-zero holonomy proves global inconsistency.
- **GREEN is Unsound**: A collection of local evaluations reporting $\Phi_1(U_i) = 0$ (all patches locally green) does **not** prove that the global system is consistent. Macroscopic holonomy can accumulate around boundary cycles without appearing inside any single contractible patch.

### [Declared] Evidence Ceilings
Because green is locally unsound, no agent may issue an unconditional `GlobalGreen` certificate. Admissible statuses are strictly capped:
$$\text{Unchecked} \longrightarrow \text{LocalGreenEvaluated} \longrightarrow \text{GreenNumericallySupported} \longrightarrow \text{BoundaryChecked}$$
The status `GlobalGreen` is a forbidden self-certification keyword (enforced by the Epistemic Immune System).
