outcomes. Raw-provider provenance, cost custody and every stored row's acceptance
have not been independently replayed in this delivery.

There is a more important interpretation limit. The inspected
`operad_mining/miner.py:1120-1200` renderer supplies evaluated RHS values and tells
the participant to set `cochain_d1_d0_exact_zero = true`. The M3 text explicitly
states the intended reduced result. The associated gate compares scalars and
anchor strings with an expected dictionary. This is evidence about answering a
highly scaffolded arithmetic/extraction task; it is contaminated for any claim of
answer-free mathematical discovery. The historical source and receipts are
preserved, rather than rewritten into cleaner-looking evidence.

For the 142 generated specifications, `remaining_142_specs.py:486-540` constructs
shadow and countermodel numbers from the node index, stage rank and route degree.
No semantic projection from those numbers to each node's mathematical target is
established by the generator. Passing this arithmetic cannot discharge the
original DAG theorem or an unpaid import, even if its metadata says PROVED_LEAN.

## A valid next M3 experiment

Freeze a public task, admissible witness type, independent checker, source
revision and total budget. Both arms retain every definition needed to solve the
same problem. Remove only explanatory decoy hints in the ablated arm; do not
remove the quotient definition or mandatory checks. Keep oracle solutions and
evaluated results outside both prompts. Hash and review both requests before
dispatch. Use fresh paired submissions, counterbalanced order, pinned decoding,
and the same checker. Record every first attempt and every repaired attempt;
report provider/checker/infrastructure failures and unknown costs separately.

Validate the checker with a valid certificate and decisive mutations before a
live trial. Retain the paired discordant counts and uncertainty. A successful
repair arm is not a same-budget first-attempt comparison. A fixture or regraded
reply is not another independent model run. No fixed campaign size is required;
use the smallest experiment that can answer the stated improvement question.

## Claim hygiene

1. **Supports:** the summary's counts are internally consistent with its stage
   subtotals, and the inspected renderer/generator expose the stated limits.
2. **Does not support:** clean derivation performance, causal general M3 efficacy,
   an optimal prompt, subsecond model reasoning, or completion of QM foundations.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim:** historical reported extraction-task results
   motivate an answer-free controlled experiment; they do not certify its outcome.
4. **Next test:** same-task, same-checker, no-answer-leakage paired M3 evaluation
   with original raw bytes and reconciled per-attempt accounting.
