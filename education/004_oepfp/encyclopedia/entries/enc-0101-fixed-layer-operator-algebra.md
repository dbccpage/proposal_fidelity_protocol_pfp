# ENC-0101 — Fixed-Layer Operator Algebra

## Statement

Fix a layer $n$ with carrier
$C_n^0 \xrightarrow{d_{0,n}} C_n^1 \xrightarrow{d_{1,n}} C_n^2$ ($d_{1,n}d_{0,n}=0$),
residual quotient $Q_n^1=C_n^1/\operatorname{im}(d_{0,n})$ and observer-selected mass
$\Phi_n([h])=\inf_{\alpha\in C_n^0}\|h+d_{0,n}\alpha\|_{J_n}$. The **admissible operator
monoid** $\mathcal{O}_n$ is the monoid generated under composition by the four admissible
effect-classes $\{E,I,C,R\}$ of §IV.3 (Def IV.9), with the refused class $M$ as its
non-composable complement.

- $E$ (Exact Repair): $x \mapsto x+d_{0,n}\alpha$; preserves $[x]\in Q_n^1$, $\Phi_n$,
  $\Gamma_n$, $R_{cl,n}$.
- $I$ (Quotient Isometry): a $\Phi_n$-isometric automorphism of $Q_n^1$ ($[x]\mapsto[I_C(x)]$,
  $\Phi_n$-preserving, cf. §IV.3 effect table); restricts to
  $\mathrm{Aut}(H_n^1,\Phi_n)$ on the closed sector.
- $C$ (Context Embedding): applies a declared context $\rho(c)$ to test congruence;
  no direct $Q_n^1$-action (quotient-level only).
- $R$ (Residual Projection): selects the $L^1$-optimal representative,
  $\|R(x)\|=\Phi_n([x])$, idempotent.
