# PFP rewriting: representation, repair and evidence

AuthorityEffect: None. This is a reading guide, not an executable gate or an authorization to apply a proposed change.

Start with [finite-QM residual repair](finite_qm_residual_repair.md) after the [C4 certificate supplement](../math/10_finite_qm_certificate_scope.md). You will distinguish a lossless representation change, a new witness for the same task, a coboundary subtraction, and a change to the problem itself. The [workflow walkthrough](../docs/23_finite_qm_workflow_walkthrough.md) supplies the actual preparation, binding and checking interface.

## What kind of rewrite is being proposed?

| Kind | Required question | What the result can establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Representation | Can the declared decoder recover the original carrier and orientation? | A round trip on the specified domain; no automatic improvement in reasoning. |
| Witness replacement | Does a complete replacement satisfy the original task and a fresh turn binding? | The predicates actually checked on that submitted certificate. |
| Cochain repair | Is the change exactly `d0*a` under the fixed maps? | The same quotient class; the optimal quotient obstruction is preserved. |
| Problem change | Were the carrier, edge observations, norms, maps or assumptions changed? | A different problem, requiring separate provenance and evidence. |
| Prompt heuristic | Does a controlled comparison improve a predeclared outcome? | Bounded empirical evidence for the tested tasks, model and budget. |

**Check:** Does changing `7` to `4` in the C4 input show that the original optimum was zero? No. It changes the observed edge data. Subtracting `d0*a`, said "d zero star a", leaves a representative of the original quotient class and cannot erase its positive optimum.

## One changed edge

An **observation** is a number supplied with the problem. On this square the direct edge is 7. A **problem change** replaces that observation. A **cochain repair** subtracts vertex differences from the edge list and keeps the same question.

Change the direct edge from 7 to 4 before stating a general rule. The new edge list is (2, −3, 5, 4). Its face sum is 2 + (−3) + 5 − 4 = 0. The original list (2, −3, 5, 7) has face sum 2 + (−3) + 5 − 7 = −3, and the original optimum is 3. The new zero belongs to the new list.

**Check.** A learner writes, "I changed 7 to 4 and the optimum became 0, so the original problem was exact." That sentence fails. The original direct edge is still 7, and the original optimum is still 3.

### Spoken segment

About 35 seconds. One idea: replacing 7 with 4 changes the question. No screen is required.

"The direct observation is seven. Replace seven with four and the face sum becomes zero. Zero is the size of the new question. It does not grade the old question. The old face sum is negative three, and the old optimum is three."

## Reference hierarchy and its limits

The [master rewriting reference](pfp_master_rewriting.md) describes a five-layer taxonomy and an eleven-dialect pilot. Its historical benchmark tables concern the stated small pilot; they do not establish a general model-improvement theorem. A recount of the retained `pilot_0000000` test specs on 2026-10-20 confirms its P* table (5 accepted, 6 wrong answers caught by the host checker, one run per dialect on one model) and adds the plain arm it omits (1 of 11 accepted, 10 malformed); see that reference's section 5. Read each reported outcome with its task, source bytes, model, budget and checker. The phrase "central authority" in that reference denotes document organization; executable predicates and human authorization retain separate owners.

## Teach and assess one boundary at a time

Use the supplement's spoken script and pencil exercises before recording. Ask a learner to identify the fixed input, propose a witness, check conservation, then explain the result's scope. A correct scalar with an infeasible dual is incomplete. A reversible encoding with an incorrect certificate is still incorrect. A clearer prompt remains a heuristic until evaluated under a declared comparison.

The current supplement is text and a proposed storyboard. No new audio, video, provider experiment or native build accompanies it. A recording should have a corrected transcript, synchronized captions, explicit edge directions and a text alternative to every color-coded status.
