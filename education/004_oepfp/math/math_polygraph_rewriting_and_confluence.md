## 1. Higher-Dimensional Rewriting & 2-Polygraphs

In categorical algebra and formal term rewriting (Burroni 1993, Guiraud 2006), a **2-polygraph** provides a cellular presentation of a 2-category or term rewriting system without spurious parenthesization or syntactic bureaucracy.

### 1.1 Formal Definition of a 2-Polygraph
A 2-polygraph is a diagram of sets and source/target maps:

$$\Sigma_2 \overset{s_1}{\underset{t_1}{\rightrightarrows}} \Sigma_1^* \overset{s_0}{\underset{t_0}{\rightrightarrows}} \Sigma_0$$

where:
1. **0-Cells ($\Sigma_0$):** A finite set of sorts or types (e.g. $\{\text{term}, \text{context}\}$).
2. **1-Cells ($\Sigma_1$):** A finite set of generating operations or terms $f: x \to y$ ($x, y \in \Sigma_0$). $\Sigma_1^*$ denotes the free 1-category generated by $\Sigma_1$, whose morphisms are formal terms or words.
3. **2-Cells ($\Sigma_2$):** A finite set of generating rewrite rules $r: u \Rightarrow v$, where $u, v \in \operatorname{Mor}(\Sigma_1^*)$ are parallel 1-cells satisfying $s_0(u) = s_0(v)$ and $t_0(u) = t_0(v)$.

Each 2-cell $r \in \Sigma_2$ is an oriented rewrite rule transforming term $u$ into term $v$:
$$s_1(r) = u \quad (\text{source term}), \qquad t_1(r) = v \quad (\text{target term})$$

## 2. Rewrite Chains & Step Continuity

A **rewrite chain** represents a multi-step reduction sequence in the 2-category generated by $\Sigma$.

### 2.1 The Rewrite Step Tuple
An individual rewrite step is a 4-tuple:
$$R = (\mathrm{rule\_id}, \mathrm{stage\_tag}, u, v)$$
where $\mathrm{rule\_id} \in \Sigma_2$ is the applied rule identifier, $\mathrm{stage\_tag}$ is the execution stage tag, and $u \Rightarrow v$ is the instantiated reduction.

### 2.2 The Step Continuity Invariant
Let $\sigma = (R_1, R_2, \ldots, R_k)$ be a finite sequence of rewrite steps with $R_i = (\mathrm{rule}_i, \tau_i, u_i, v_i)$.

**Definition (Step Continuity):**  
The rewrite chain $\sigma$ is **step-continuous** if and only if:
$$\boxed{v_i = u_{i+1} \quad \text{for all } 1 \le i < k}$$
That is, the target term of step $i$ must be syntactically identical to the source term of step $i+1$.

**Host Invariant:**  
If $v_i \ne u_{i+1}$ for any index $i$, the chain contains an unverified semantic gap. The host immediately halts evaluation and emits `Refusal(PolygraphConfluenceError)` with code `REFUSED_DISCONTINUOUS_CHAIN`.

## 3. Divergent Peaks & Peak Join Confluence

A central property in equational logic and term rewriting is **confluence** (the Church-Rosser property).

### 3.1 Divergent Peak Configuration
A **divergent peak** occurs when a common ancestor term $s$ (the peak apex) can be rewritten along two distinct paths:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Left branch:} \quad & s \Rightarrow^* u \\
\text{Right branch:} \quad & s \Rightarrow^* v
\end{aligned}$$

```
                           s (Apex)
                         ╱   ╲
           (Left Path) ╱       ╲ (Right Path)
                     ▼           ▼
                     u           v
                     ╲           ╱
        (Left Join)   ╲         ╱   (Right Join)
                       ▼       ▼
                           w (Common Join)
```

### 3.2 The Peak Claim Record
In the Symbol Game, an agent claiming confluence for a divergent peak submits a structured `PeakClaim`:
$$\operatorname{PeakClaim} = \left( s, u, v, \sigma_L, \sigma_R, w \right)$$
where:
- $s$: Peak apex term.
- $u$: Target of left branch ($s \Rightarrow^* u$).
- $v$: Target of right branch ($s \Rightarrow^* v$).
- $\sigma_L = (L_1, \ldots, L_p)$: Sequence of rewrite steps joining $u \Rightarrow^* w$.
- $\sigma_R = (R_1, \ldots, R_q)$: Sequence of rewrite steps joining $v \Rightarrow^* w$.
- $w$: Claimed common join term.

### 3.3 Verification Conditions for Confluence
The host certifier verifies the peak claim against three strict predicates:
1. **Left Path Validity & Continuity:**
   $$\sigma_L \text{ is continuous}, \quad s(L_1) = u, \quad t(L_p) = w$$
2. **Right Path Validity & Continuity:**
   $$\sigma_R \text{ is continuous}, \quad s(R_1) = v, \quad t(R_q) = w$$
3. **Join Term Identity:**
   $$t(L_p) = t(R_q) = w$$
4. **Step validity (added 2026-10-20):** every step $(\mathrm{rule\_id}, \tau, u_i, v_i)$ in $\sigma_L$, $\sigma_R$ and in the two branches $s \Rightarrow^* u$, $s \Rightarrow^* v$ must be an instance of its rule: $u_i = C[\ell\theta]$ and $v_i = C[r\theta]$ for the rule $\ell \Rightarrow r$ named by $\mathrm{rule\_id}$, some context $C$ and substitution $\theta$.

> **Why condition 4 is needed (2026-10-20).** Conditions 1 to 3 check only that steps chain and end at $w$. Without condition 4 the two-step "chain" $(r_1, \tau, u, w)$, $(r_2, \tau, v, w)$ passes for any terms $u, v, w$, so a fabricated join is accepted. The record as written also omits the branch steps from $s$ to $u$ and $v$, so the peak itself is not checked. The current implementation, `check_peak_claim_confluence` in [`rewriting_policies.py`](../../../../omega_engine_pfp/domain/mssn_research/grammar/rewriting_policies.py), checks path endpoints and continuity only (read 2026-10-20), so condition 4 is an open implementation obligation.

If $t(L_p) \ne w$ or $t(R_q) \ne w$, the join fails, and the claim is rejected with `REFUSED_MISMATCHED_JOIN_TERM`.

### 3.4 Newman's Lemma (Well-Founded Confluence)
**Theorem (Newman 1942):**  
Let $\to$ be a binary rewrite relation on a set of terms $T$. If $\to$ is **terminating** (well-founded, having no infinite reduction sequences) and **locally confluent** (for all $s, u, v$, if $s \to u$ and $s \to v$, then there exists $w$ such that $u \to^* w$ and $v \to^* w$), then $\to$ is **globally confluent**.

*Operational Meaning in the Symbol Game:*  
To verify global confluence of a finite terminating ruleset, it is necessary and sufficient to verify peak joinability for all critical pairs (divergent peaks formed by overlapping redexes). This combines Newman's lemma with the Knuth-Bendix critical pair lemma: a term rewriting system is locally confluent exactly when all its critical pairs are joinable.

## 4. The Polygraph Packet Contract & Stage Floor Policy

### 4.1 Packet Schema (`PolygraphPacket`)
```yaml
schema: symbol-game-polygraph/v1
authority_effect: None
presentation_id: string
ruleset_digest: ContentDigest (SHA-256)
step_list: tuple[RewriteStep, ...]
peak_claim: PeakClaim
```

### 4.2 Stage Floor Enforcement (Level 11 Policy)
Polygraph operations operate on 2-cells and critical pairs, requiring higher categorical infrastructure than low-level 1-skeleton graph descent.
- **Stage Floor:** Polygraph validation is strictly restricted to **Stage Level $\ge 11$**.
- **Refusal on Low Stages:** Any invocation of `validate_polygraph` against a `TowerGrammar` with $\mathrm{stage\_level} < 11$ is immediately rejected with:
  $$\operatorname{Refusal}(\text{StageLevelViolation: Polygraph requires stage } \ge 11, \text{ received } k)$$

### 4.3 Deterministic RFC 8785 Canonical JSON Receipts
Upon successful verification of all continuity and peak join conditions, the host emits an RFC 8785 canonical JSON receipt binding:
- `packet_digest`: SHA-256 digest of the canonical presentation.
- `ruleset_digest`: Digest of the active rewrite ruleset.
- `apex_digest`: Digest of the root apex term $s$.
- `join_term_digest`: Digest of the certified join term $w$.
- `left_steps_count`: Cardinality of the left join chain.
- `right_steps_count`: Cardinality of the right join chain.
- `confluence_status`: `CERTIFIED_CONFLUENT`.

## 5. Host Refusal Taxonomy for Polygraph Packets

| Refusal Code | Trigger Condition | Host Disposition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `REFUSED_DISCONTINUOUS_CHAIN` | Intermediate terms in a rewrite chain fail equality: $v_i \ne u_{i+1}$. | Unverified gap in proof trajectory. |
| `REFUSED_MISMATCHED_JOIN_TERM` | Terminal endpoints of left and right join paths differ: $t(L_p) \ne t(R_q)$. | Divergent peak fails to join at common term. |
| `REFUSED_STAGE_FLOOR` | Attempted polygraph packet submission below Stage Level 11. | Structural capability floor violation. |
| `REFUSED_RULESET_DIGEST_MISMATCH` | Packet references a ruleset digest not pinned in host active grammar. | Unbound rewrite rule context. |

> **Source check (2026-10-20, pin `c419cc6beb`).** These four codes are a specification. The implementation, `TowerGrammar.validate_polygraph` in [`entities.py`](../../../../omega_engine_pfp/domain/mssn_research/grammar/entities.py) with the checks in [`rewriting_policies.py`](../../../../omega_engine_pfp/domain/mssn_research/grammar/rewriting_policies.py), returns `RefusalCode.UNKNOWN_OPCODE` for the stage floor and `RefusalCode.HOST_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` for continuity and join failures (one message carries the text `REFUSED_DIVERGENT_PEAK_JOIN`). No ruleset-digest check was found. No RFC 8785 receipt for polygraph packets was found either (Section 4.3), so that receipt is specified, not implemented.
