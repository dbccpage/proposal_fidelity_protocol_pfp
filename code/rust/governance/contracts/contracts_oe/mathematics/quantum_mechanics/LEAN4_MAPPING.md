## 2. Grounding of the Four Foundational Research Papers
The four foundational quantum mechanics research papers (Papers 000, 020, 080, and 120) form the theoretical backbone of the finite quantum program. Each paper is constructively grounded in dedicated named Lean 4 modules and formal theorem sets:

### Paper 000: Primitive Difference for Quantum Carriers (`000_primitive_difference_for_quantum_carriers`)
- **Core Epistemic Premise**: Quantum carriers originate from discrete witnessed primitive difference relations before any metric, inner product, or Hilbert space structure is assigned.
- **Primary Lean 4 Formalizations**: `PrimitiveDifference.lean`, `PrimitiveTerminalPacket.lean`, `PrimitiveAdmissibleRepair.lean`, `PrimitiveObstructionPipeline.lean`, `PrimitiveDifferenceBridge.lean`.
- **Proved Theorems & Data Types**:
  - `structure PrimitiveDifference (alpha : Type u) (beta : Type v)`: records carrier difference map `delta : alpha -> alpha -> beta` and reflexive/symmetric/transitive `Zero` relation.
  - `def ResidualQuotient (P : PrimitiveDifference alpha beta)`: exact quotient type `Quotient (zeroSetoid P)` via `Quotient.sound`.
  - `theorem classOf_eq_of_zero`: shows in this model that zero-difference equivalence classes are identical.
  - Zero synthetic axioms; 0 sorry; standard kernel equality only.
- **Mapped Contracts**: `relative_state_refinement.contract.yaml`, `residual_density_calculus.contract.yaml`, `exact_ket_bra_sesquilinearity.contract.yaml`, `repair_capacity.contract.yaml`.

### Paper 020: Born Normalization & Conditional Hilbert Lift (`020_born_normalization_and_conditional_hilbert_lift`)
- **Core Epistemic Premise**: Born normalization is not an ad-hoc dynamical postulate; it is an algebraic redundancy of ray projective equivalence and POVM identity resolution.
- **Primary Lean 4 Formalizations**: `BornNormalization.lean`, `BornRedundancy.lean`, `BornRedundancyAxioms.lean`, `H001ProbabilitySimplex.lean`, `H059RationalSector.lean`, `H053EverettForkingTree.lean`.
- **Proved Theorems & Data Types**:
  - `theorem born_normalization_redundancy (rho : DiscreteDensity2) (povm : DiscretePOVM2)`: proves `born_outcome_0 rho povm + born_outcome_1 rho povm = rho.denom` identically once unit trace `a + d = denom` and resolution of identity `e0 + e1 = 1` hold.
  - `theorem density_trace_eq_denom`: proves unit trace is an exact integer identity.
  - `theorem normalize_idempotent`, `theorem normalize_smul`: proves normalization factors cancel on projective rays.
- **Mapped Contracts**: `born_probabilities.contract.yaml`, `arbitrary_precision_branch_counting.contract.yaml`, `everett_branch_weights.contract.yaml`, `constructive_same_field_hermitian_eigenspaces.contract.yaml`.

### Paper 080: CPTP Trace-Norm Geometry & Quantum Dual Witnesses (`080_cptp_trace_norm_geometry_and_quantum_dual_witnesses`)
- **Core Epistemic Premise**: Finite quantum state discrimination under the trace distance achieves an exact zero duality gap on rationals via centered operator norms and dual witness certificates.
- **Primary Lean 4 Formalizations**: `TraceZeroDuality.lean`, `TraceZeroDualityAxioms.lean`, `DualWitness.lean`, `FiniteN3ResidualDual.lean`, `H063CptpChannelsStinespring.lean`, `H015DilationCount.lean`.
- **Proved Theorems & Data Types**:
  - `theorem trace_zero_duality_gap_zero`: proves the primal trace-norm optimization equals the centered operator norm infimum over identity shifts without duality gap.
  - `theorem optimal_pairing_eq_spread`: proves max Tr(M X) = (lambda_max - lambda_min) / 2 on trace-zero unit ball.
  - `theorem optimal_shift_achieves_spread`: proves centering shift c* = (lambda_max + lambda_min) / 2 minimizes operator norm.
  - `theorem spread_nonneg`: spectral spread is unconditionally non-negative.
- **Mapped Contracts**: `exact_trace_norm_and_helstrom.contract.yaml`, `finite_trace_distance_quotient.contract.yaml`, `weighted_choi_kraus_channels.contract.yaml`, `weighted_stinespring_metric_dilation.contract.yaml`, `quantum_gap_functional.contract.yaml`.

### Paper 120: Mermin-Peres Contextuality Cochain Obstruction (`120_mermin_peres_contextuality_cochain_obstruction`)
- **Core Epistemic Premise**: Quantum contextuality is a topological non-triviality: a non-vanishing 1-cochain boundary obstruction (d1 omega != 0) in Cech cohomology on measurement hypergraphs.
- **Primary Lean 4 Formalizations**: `ContextualityObstruction.lean`, `H020CabelloSignPolytope.lean`, `H064ContextualityThresholds.lean`, `PointedObstructionClassifier.lean`.
