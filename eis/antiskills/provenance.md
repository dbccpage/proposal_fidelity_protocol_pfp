## Retained idea

The archive recorded failed runs, mined recurring motifs, turned them into
reusable constraints, and made those constraints visible in later prompts. OE
anti-skill v0 retains that learning direction:

```text
correction episode -> candidate invariant -> held-out evaluation
                   -> explicit human admission -> prompt finding

```

## Rejected authority mechanisms

The archive also automatically promoted rules after raw frequency thresholds,
used phrase-oriented suppression, and assigned quantum-sounding or scalar
fitness interpretations to hashed text representations. Those mechanisms do
not establish semantic validity and are excluded here. In particular:

- duplicate transcripts do not count as independent evidence;
- recurrence counts cannot write the admitted registry;
- model or caller verdicts cannot create evaluation results;
- prompt wording is not classified as true or false by phrases, density,
  entropy, purity, or a scalar score; and
- repair remains a proposal until independently replayed and accepted by an
  operator.

The historical archive remains outside the runtime dependency graph.
