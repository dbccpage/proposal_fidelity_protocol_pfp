# PO035: five derivation pilots and reviewed final prompts

AuthorityEffect: None.

The five substantive seeds attempt corrected causal refinement, microscopic defect
current, a categorical relational spine, a metric/cochain bridge, and obstruction
versus operational halting. Each ran five provider trials with four host stages per
trial. All 25 calls replay offline. **No model certificate was accepted.** Three
packets reached the finite mathematical predicates and failed; the other 22 failed
JSON, schema or prompt identity checks. Analytical derivations remain unverified.

## What the stages taught

Each loop traversed proposal, lift grant, witness check, negative control and learning
refinement. The 25 stage capsules per pilot are **five independent provider trials
and twenty deterministic host stages**, not 25 independent LLM trials. Each pilot
also exposes 25 host control tests; these overlap the central 125-case fixture matrix.

Pilot 199 used the original recipe. Its malformed packets prevented live learning
and grants. After its fifth call, independently typed items were harvested offline
from valid JSON at recorded pointers: 21 candidate artifacts and eight lift requests.
Its requested `EXACT_RATIONAL`, `REFINEMENT_HYPOTHESES` and `TYPED_REFUSAL` grants
enter only the final prompt. They did not affect any of its five live requests.
The fifth invalid JSON reply was retained without parser repair or extraction.

Pilots 200-203 used a revised placement instruction and independently typed side
channels. Safe allowlisted requests were granted for subsequent prompts even when
the full proposal remained refused. Every grant remains an explicit operational
license or added hypothesis; it does not change the original finite fixture.
Unrelated requests were retained too: a commuting-projector license in pilot 201
does not establish a categorical construction. No useful requested lift was denied.

The system collected 100 typed, model-authored candidates across all five kinds.
They are evidence of proposed learning, not 100 validated or novel improvements.
Some advice restates the seed; some is repeated; some is false. Independent Codex
reviews selected 12 items, retained 88 only in evidence, and supplied 21 separately
attributed procedural lessons. Pilot 201 had no safe whole model-authored item.
Its final prompt uses reviewer-derived lessons from the observed failures.

The strongest actionable failures were confused JSON placement and complex/scalar
types, treating an instantaneous state as constant, conflating identity and nonflat
transport, trusting an invented witness over arithmetic, and treating a boolean
halting flag as a policy. Reusing unfiltered advice amplified these errors. All
historical contexts remain intact; reviewed final contexts omit those proposals.

## Potential corrected derivation routes

These are **reviewer-authored conditional routes**, not successful model outputs or
new general proofs. Checked fixture outputs belong in this report, not participant
guidance. The original paper claim ledger remains `REQUIRES_CORRECTION`.

1. **199, causal refinement.** Assume bounded prefactors, a uniform positive lower
   bound on decay rates and physical speed `a*v_hop(a) -> c`. At a fixed strict
   outside-cone margin in the l1 norm, the negative exponential suppresses the upper
   envelope as `a -> 0`. On the nominal null boundary, its behavior depends on
   `(a*v_hop(a)-c)*t/a`; convergence of the speed alone does not fix the answer.
   Nothing in an upper bound establishes nonzero inside-cone propagation. Positive
   spacetime dilations preserve the homogeneous cone order while rescaling the
   interval, so scale and Euclidean isotropy need independent premises. The finite
   controls give radii 2 and 2, intervals -1 and -4, and separate matched-speed null
   envelope 1/4. Remaining work includes a genuine scaling-limit theorem and any
   proposed isotropic metric reconstruction.
2. **200, microscopic current.** For time-independent orthogonal `P`, Hermitian
   graph-supported `H`, `Q=I-P`, and `r=Q psi`, derive
   `i rdot = H r + [Q,H] psi`. For edge `u->v`, use
   `j_uv=2 Im(conj(r_v) H_vu r_u)` and
   `S_v=2 Im(conj(r_v) ([Q,H]psi)_v)` to obtain `nudot=Bj+S`.
   If `[P,H]=0`, the source vanishes; Hermiticity cancels internal edge currents.
   The unchanged noncommuting fixture instead gives `r=(0,i,1)`,
   `rdot=(0,-2i,1)`, `nudot=(0,-4,2)`, `j=(0,2)` and `S=(0,-2,0)`.
   Total density derivative is -2. A continuum result still needs uniform estimates,
   convergence of states and generators, and a justified current scaling.
3. **201, relational spine.** Begin with fibers and a free path category. Transport
   descends to a category imposing `02=12 composed with 01` only if the maps preserve
   that relation. Define `(D0 sigma)_ij=sigma_j-Tij sigma_i` and
   `(D1 eta)_012=T12 eta01+eta12-eta02`. Then
   `D1 D0 sigma=(T02-T12 T01)sigma0`, a typed map into the destination fiber.
   Its vanishing is basis-invariant; its numerical coordinate value need not be.
   Identity transport separately gives `delta=d0 sigma`, repaired by the 0-cochain
   `alpha=-sigma`. The fixture has composition 6 versus direct transport 5 and
   covariant differences `(0,-2,-1)`; identity differences `(1,2,3)` are repairable.
   Full eight-stage categories, functors, compression/retraction compatibility and
   higher-cell constructions remain open. Nonflatness alone does not imply an
   obstruction to a separately defined repair problem.
4. **202, metric/cochain bridge.** The complex identity makes
   `dbar1: C1/im(d0) -> C2`, `[delta] -> d1 delta`, well-defined, with kernel `H1`.
   It does not establish exactness or a geometric Bianchi law. Separately, an oriented
   nondegenerate intrinsic piecewise Euclidean 2D star with Levi-Civita transport,
   flat within faces and canonically matched across shared edges, has SO(2) holonomy
   angle equal to its deficit modulo `2*pi`. Metric compatibility alone is
   insufficient. An unwrapped
   equality needs an angle branch and orientation convention. The fixture central
   cosines are 1/2 and 7/8; the deficits are 0 and `2*pi-6*acos(7/8)`.
   The separate additive triangle curvature stays 1. A scalar additive gauge shift
   is not a metric-preserving frame change. General bridge and continuum claims
   remain unverified.
5. **203, obstruction and halting.** Define
   `Phi(delta)=min_alpha ||delta-d0 alpha||_1`. For feasible
   `d0^T y=0`, `||y||_infinity<=1`, weak duality follows from
   `y^T delta=y^T(delta-d0 alpha)<=||delta-d0 alpha||_1`.
   Finite-dimensional feasible LPs here attain their bounded primal/dual optima;
   a positive equal objective is an exact obstruction certificate. On the fixture,
   `alpha=0`, `y=(1,1,1)` attain `3/100000000`. The continuing system `n->n+1`
   is independent of this static result. Halting follows only after declaring a
   transition/terminal policy that checks the positive certificate and routes to a
   terminal state. Measurement and physical interpretation remain separate work.
