## 1. Challenge

> Use the existing Symbol Game and models no larger than 70 billion parameters to build, in core Lean,
> a proof-relevant cell calculus on 19L57G1's native passage with non-degenerate units,
> associativity, interchange and quotient compatibility, or to prove that no non-degenerate one
> exists in the declared form, for under $5.

## 2. Why This Task

Two open items in 19L57G1 ask for the same object. The native-structure tree proves that swap-generated cells are sound, whisker and compose, but its `Cell` is a proposition, so the laws hold only because any two proofs are equal. The G013 trace-calculus tree declares interchange as a constructor and leaves unit, associativity and quotient compatibility open. A proof-relevant version must make cells data, keep the native guard (a swap is valid only at the state its prefix actually reaches), and still satisfy the laws on a quotient that does not collapse everything.

Generation is hard: dependent types, quotients and guard-sensitive constructors under core Lean with no Mathlib and no `Classical.choice`. Verification is cheap: `lake build` and the axiom audit. The task stresses Lean decomposition in the Symbol Game, OEWAS (Omega Engine Workflows, Agents, Skills) statement discipline, and the OEEIS (Omega Engine Epistemic Immune System) danger gate against laws that hold only by proof irrelevance. A pass is a formal result inside the calculus of inductive constructions, not a claim about any physical or categorical reading.

## 3. Formal Problem and Bounds

**Pins.** Research trees at 19L57G1 `b55fc96a`. The sealed passage state `PState` and its step are consumed as the native-structure tree consumes them. They were read at `b55fc96a`; the packet build checks whether they exist at `3100c4e` and cites them to `b55fc96a` if they do not, since sealed paths have grown after the tag.

- **NS-5** (`19L57G1:research/native_structure/CLAIMS.md`): OPEN. A proof-relevant cell calculus with composition, unit, associativity and interchange laws that are not trivial. `Cell` is a proposition, so those laws hold only in the degenerate sense.
- **Proved there.** NS-1: a swap exchanges two adjacent events only where both orders reach the same state at the actual prefix state; cells relate lists that replay to the same state and are permutations (`swap_sound`, `cell_sound`, `cell_perm`). NS-2: whiskering and horizontal composition (`cell_append_right`, `cell_append_left`, `cell_hcomp`). NS-3: the license, budget and occurrence frame commutations generate swaps (`swap_license`, `swap_budget`, `swap_occ`). NS-4 REFUTED: parallel native paths need not be related by a cell (`parallel_not_cell`).
- **G013** (`19L57G1:docs/section3_claim_corrections.md`, row "2, G013"; `19L57G1:research/g013_trace_calculus/`): traces have composition and identity laws; the higher-cell relation `EqCell2` declares commutation and interchange constructors; unit, associativity, quotient compatibility and interpretation by guarded native transitions remain open.

**The problem.** Over the native passage events and step:

1. define a `Type`-valued family of cells between event lists, generated by guarded swaps (reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, whiskering on both sides);
2. define vertical and horizontal composition and identities;
3. declare a congruence on cells: the unit, associativity and inverse laws, plus interchange for swaps on disjoint positions, with independence as a premise rather than a free constructor;
4. prove, on the quotient, the unit laws, associativity, interchange, and that composition is well defined (quotient compatibility);
5. prove soundness: every cell denotes lists that replay to the same state (reusing NS-1), and erasing a cell to `Prop` gives the existing `Cell`;
6. prove non-degeneracy: two parallel cells that are distinct in the quotient, with the inequality decided constructively.

**Frontier outcomes.** Item 6 is the crux. If every congruence satisfying items 3 and 4 identifies all parallel cells, the honest result is a Lean theorem saying so for the declared form. That refutation is valuable and scores as solved.

**Axiom policy.** Core Lean 4, axioms within `{propext, Quot.sound}`, no `Classical.choice`, no forbidden tokens even in comments. `Type`-valued definitions produce no axiom reports, the same gap as the parked isomorphism modules, so every law must also be stated as a `Prop`-valued theorem the audit can see.

## 10. Interpretation

- **Full pass** closes NS-5 and the algebraic half of G013 for the declared calculus. Interpretation by guarded native transitions (the other half of G013) is item 5 and stays scoped to it.
- **Refutation of non-degeneracy** says the declared form collapses. A different congruence is a different claim.
- **Laws without item 6** are the degenerate result NS-5 already has, and score as unsolved on the main statement.
- **E finds distinct parallel cells** but cannot prove the laws: a search result, not a calculus.
