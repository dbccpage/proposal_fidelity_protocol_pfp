# Paper 034: The PFP Workflow DAG — process and mathematics of WF1–WF49

**Status:** author-review draft, 2026-11-05. Not a deposit.

[`paper.md`](paper.md) summarises, for each workflow family, the procedure
and the mathematics of its acceptance rule. WF1–WF5 are the implemented
runners (`pfp/workflows/`). WF6–WF23 are designs derived from the retained
pilot estate of the sibling research repository; no new model call was made.
Each family also has a team design: goal, model tiers by role, teamwork
pattern and a bounded boost step.
