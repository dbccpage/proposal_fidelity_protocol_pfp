# Demo 3: witness search with an epistemic immune host

**Status:** proposed controlled experiment, 2026-09-23. **AuthorityEffect:** None. This file specifies tests and expected failure modes; it reports no Demo 3 model result. The executable finite controls are in [Demo 2](../demo_2/2_demo.md). The old Demo 1 and Demo 2 terminals are observations about their historical runners, not physical certificates. See the [trace audit](../anti_morphism_observations.md).

## Question and scope

Can a model produce a **checkable witness** more often, faster, or with fewer invalid attempts when its problem is presented through symbols, a typed YAML packet, a phase cue, or a host-generated anti-morphism hint? The model is a candidate generator. A separate deterministic host fixes the problem and decides each candidate. The primary outcome is an independently checked witness under a **declared finite representation**, rather than a model's class name or proof prose.

The larger FSP question is to characterize Hamiltonians with zero BQNF-FSIGN quotient defect across justified Hamiltonian-to-cochain adapters. Demo 2 gives the conditional exact packet criterion `c1 in im(d0)` and a complete answer for one narrower fixed-basis diagonal sign-gauge family. Demo 3 tests the proposal mechanism needed to find such witnesses. A result here cannot enumerate the representation-independent set of Hamiltonians, prove a free-fermion or Hubbard theorem, or validate an adapter that was not supplied. In particular, a site swap, a Wick label, and a particle-hole phrase are not an explicit involution on a specified signed path carrier.

The longer-range goal is a **Gin-style index of Hamiltonian presentations** and a parallel search over rewrites and witnesses, starting from Genesis-level distinctions. The index's key is a *presentation plus its proof obligations*, not just a Hamiltonian name. Search may expose new representations, gauges, and cancellation structures; it does not turn finite evidence into a universal sign-problem decision procedure or promise a result in seconds.

## Five layers from Genesis to a searchable physical claim

1. **Genesis primitives.** Begin with a primitive difference, a witness and reflexive relation, and typed incidence/cochain structure. Candidate moves are elimination of obstruction by projection, retraction, gauge or carrier change, or a lift that builds additional structure. This layer is intended to be domain agnostic. It has no automatic physical time, spatial lattice, Hilbert space, particle species, or exchange sign. The 19L57G1 sign-tower audit reports `SIGN-ABSENT / NEITHER` for certified machine observables: carrier and history canonicalization erase parity. The proposed *signed rewrite language* therefore requires a new typed orientation or character carrier with explicit admission, rather than relabeling existing sealed states as fermions.
2. **Physical adapter.** To speak about bosons or fermions, import a concrete physical interpretation: states, a Hamiltonian, an ensemble, an exchange action, and a rule for amplitudes or weights. One possible finite import represents particle permutations trivially for a bosonic reading or by parity for a fermionic reading. The ordered-exchange package explicitly adds sorted occupancy and `(-1)^r` insertion/removal conventions; its coefficient correspondence does not make those conventions endogenous. The adapter must state and justify which interpretation is used. The appearance of a minus sign downstream is a *research target*, not a derivation from the primitive difference alone. Keep an import ledger so a physical assumption is never presented as a Genesis theorem.
3. **Presentation and cochain.** Choose a basis, boundary and filling conditions, representation (worldlines, determinants, auxiliary fields, or another specified carrier), allowed gauges/regroupings, and exact signed rows or `C0 --d0--> C1 --d1--> C2` with `c1`. Different presentations of the same named Hamiltonian can have different sign behavior. A proposed rewrite must carry a proof that the relevant partition sum and observables are preserved; a syntax-level rename is insufficient.
4. **Finite exact oracle.** For a declared packet, the host checks `d1*d0=0` and `c1 in im(d0)`, or an exact route-specific sign-gauge, determinant, or partial-`Xi` certificate. A positive result attaches to that presentation and adapter. A negative result excludes only the fully specified finite transformation family searched. If an adapter or checker is missing, the result is `UNKNOWN_NOT_CERTIFIED`.
5. **Search frontier and index.** Candidate models propose admissible rewrites and witnesses in parallel. A host scheduler allocates branch budgets, replays every proposal, deduplicates only under a checked equivalence, and stores immutable receipts. The index records both successful and surviving branches with their scope, rather than reducing them to a binary class label. This is the proposed Core workflow built from a minimal Alpha language; the bridge itself remains to be established.

The physical fork between a trivial and parity exchange action is an explicit **import**. Showing that a particular Genesis witness structure forces that fork, or derives a spin-statistics relation, would require separate definitions and proof. Demo 3 can test whether its language helps find a valid finite witness after the import; it cannot certify that ontological origin. With arbitrary unconstrained unitary basis changes, every finite Hermitian Hamiltonian is diagonalizable. The research question therefore requires an explicit transformation grammar, cost bound, sampling method, and preserved observables.

## An operational model

Let `F` be a host-pinned fixture, `s_t` its host state, `u` a prompt treatment, and `m_t` the raw model response. The distinction is:

```text
m_t ~ q_theta(. | render(F, s_t, u), sampling_settings)
(s_(t+1), disposition_t, receipt_t) = Host_F(s_t, parse(m_t))
```

`q_theta` is an unknown proposal distribution. Symbols, YAML, phase cues, and hints change the rendered input and may change `q_theta`; they have no authority to modify `Host_F`. The host state minimally binds the fixture digest, allowed move grammar, current phase, exact coverage set, candidate witness, exact checker outputs, remaining fuel, and prior state/decision digests. `Phi_1` is populated only when a valid cochain packet and corresponding checker are present; otherwise it is `null`. `Gamma_2` denotes a **specified** closure or cycle check, not an all-purpose physical-sign verdict. Output purity and token entropy are diagnostic measures, not mathematical certificates.

The host phase machine is `INTAKE -> COVERAGE -> WITNESS -> VERIFY -> TERMINAL`, with typed `REFUSAL`, `LIFT_REQUEST`, `UNKNOWN`, and fuel-exhausted exits. An ASSERT can update coverage only against pinned IDs and exact values. A transition to VERIFY requires complete coverage and a witness of the route's type. A JSON array is one transaction: parse the entire bounded batch, replay all items against a copy of the state, and commit only if every item passes. A late bad item must leave coverage and terminal unchanged. This tests the batch behavior observed in Demo 2 R2 without treating batch compression as certificate quality.

### Three finite certificate routes

1. **Fixed-basis sign switching.** For a finite real symmetric `H` and only `D_alpha=diag((-1)^alpha_i)`, the host checks each nonzero off-diagonal constraint `alpha_i XOR alpha_j = 1[H_ij>0]`. It can produce or verify vertex bits, or an odd-parity cycle certifying failure **within that gauge family**. This is a complete finite classifier for that family. `RestrictedSignGaugeRepair` is not BQNF `ExactRepair` unless an adapter establishes the bridge.
2. **Exact rational BQNF packet.** Given host-supplied `d0,d1,c1` with `d1*d0=0`, `check_zero_defect` tests `d0*alpha=c1`. A failed membership test returns a left-null functional `lambda` with `lambda^T d0=0` and `lambda^T c1=1`. This completely decides zero/nonzero quotient membership of the packet. It neither constructs the packet from `H` nor establishes that the packet captures a physical sign representation.
3. **Finite meron pairing.** Given host-supplied signed path rows, a candidate provides `S0` and an explicit path-ID map `Xi`. The host checks domain and closure, `Xi^2=id`, no fixed points in `S0`, equal weights, opposite signs, and nonnegative survivors. This certifies one finite signed-sum identity. Pairing every nonzero path would sum to zero, so a useful construction may leave a positive survivor sector. `Xi=identity` cannot reverse a nonzero sign. No route inference from a Hamiltonian is available without a path adapter.

Determinant/Pfaffian, particle-hole, free-fermion, and Hubbard ideas are **future adapter targets**. For each, pin the ensemble, lattice, filling, auxiliary-field choice, configuration domain, exact weights and observables; then implement an independent checker. Until then the proper verdict is `UNKNOWN_NOT_CERTIFIED`. An `OpenSignObstruction` on a narrow route cannot establish that every representation fails.

## What the pilots actually teach

- Demo 1's 3-cycle R2b and R3 both reached the old host terminal after an explicit CUT-semantics sentence. R3 also changed the packet envelope. This suggests a semantic-instruction effect on that one graph and model; it does **not** isolate a universal YAML effect or demonstrate that a model reasoned algebraically rather than used a pattern. Replicate with frozen prompts and randomized carrier relabeling.
- Demo 1 R4 asserted one of five diamond edges and then submitted CUTs on undeclared edges. R4b asserted five edges, then repeated the same stationary CUT after initial descent. These are different failures. The R4 code placed supposed hints in a private field that did not appear in the next raw prompt, so those traces cannot measure hint efficacy.

- Demo 2 R1's old phase signal changed the next model move to VERIFY, but it fired before complete matrix coverage. Its old host accepted identity as a sign-reversing `Xi`, which cannot invert sign parity. The corrected R1 makes a fixed-basis positivity/gauge claim only after complete 4x4 coverage.
- Demo 2 R2's single reply contained six hopping ASSERTs and a VERIFY. The old host accepted a `bipartite_symmetry` type label without a checked worldline map or weights. The new offline audit retains `BATCH_FORMAT_OBSERVED` and `WITNESS_UNCHECKED`; it cannot establish `ExactCancellation`.
- The newer Demo 2 R3/R4 historical terminals require separate audit. A regex match on proof text cannot verify `Xi`. R4 hardcodes an obstruction for a repulsive two-site dimer but leaves filling, chemical potential, ensemble, and representation unspecified. Treat both as candidate failure fixtures until an exact adapter/checker replays them.

The offline trace auditor marks a repeated stationary CUT as observed only when two consecutive flat CUTs have identical canonical proposals and an identical logged `state_digest_before`. Missing state digests produce `unknown`; the historical R4b trace lacks them and is not an independently replayed host-state finding.

The Demo 1 term **anti-morphism** is a useful label for directionally wrong or phase-invalid candidate moves. It is not automatically a morphism in the opposite category: reversing an arrow requires an actual admissible reverse map. A flat CUT, an increasing CUT, and a CUT with undeclared support have different host predicates and should not be collapsed into one error class. A model's `LIFT_REQUEST` is an observable request, not evidence that it knows the source of its own failure.

## Epistemic immune system: typed, bounded, and replayable

The proposed EIS is a host-side detector registry. It examines a submitted move **after** binding it to a host state and before any authority-bearing transition. A finding contains detector ID/version, fixture and state digests, raw candidate digest, checker reference, exact trigger, disposition, and any hint ID. A detector's words never substitute for its predicate.

| Detector | Host predicate | Response and near miss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AM-1-CUT-RESTATE` | CUT reproduces asserted values and exact defect is flat on a complete state | Reject as no progress; a different valid delta must pass. |
| `AM-2-CUT-INCREASE` | Fully defined CUT yields an exact higher defect | Reject with before/after values; no negative result if the host lacks required inputs. |
| `AM-3-PRECONDITION-INCOMPLETE` | CUT support intersects undeclared manifest IDs | Return the exact missing IDs; do not guess whether the model intended absolute targets. |
| `AM-4-STATIONARY-REPEAT` | Same canonical CUT and same state digest recur after a flat result | Ask for a different candidate or lift; do not fire when state changed. |
| `AM-FSP-2-WITNESS-INVALID` | Map is absent, label-only, has a fixed point, mismatched sign/weight, or does not cover declared `S0` | Reject the specific check; accept a distinct valid positivity certificate on its own route. |
| `AM-FSP-5-POST-COMPLETE-STALL` | Complete exact coverage and a host-ready route, followed by repeated nonprogress ASSERTs | Emit a phase cue; never diagnose this from incomplete coverage. |
| `AM-FSP-6-WRONG-ROUTE` | Candidate supplies a witness of the wrong type for its claimed route | Request the matching certificate or return UNKNOWN; no class label blacklist substitutes for a checker. |

Every detector needs negative, positive, and near-miss fixtures before admission. Hints must be minted from a particular host receipt, carry a stable hint ID, and be verified as bytes in the **next raw prompt**. Compare no cue, a diagnostic cue containing no solution, and a solution-bearing cue. Label the latter `ASSISTED`; it tests use of supplied information, not independent witness discovery. Wrong hints are a prompt-injection resilience control: the checker remains fixed and must reject any invalid proposal they induce.

The EIS returns `REJECT_LOCAL`, `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, or a scoped exact finite certificate. It does not convert one failed search path into a universal impossibility claim. This is the Alpha-to-Core architectural step suggested by the user: the Alpha engine supplies minimal typed distinctions and repair/lift obligations; the Core workflow accumulates state, receipts, detectors, and controlled learned hints. That is a design interpretation, not a derivation from Genesis primitives.

## Parallel rewrite search and the Gin-style index

Treat the search space as a directed graph. A node binds `(H specification, physical adapter, presentation, exact carrier/cochain packet, allowed move family, proof obligations, host receipt)`. Edges are typed operations such as a verified basis/gauge change, a regrouping with preserved signed sum, a justified projection/retraction, or a lift to a richer carrier. A node without a preservation proof can be explored as a proposal but cannot inherit its parent's physical authority. The canonical index key must include the Hamiltonian and parameters **and** basis, ensemble, representation, adapter/checker versions, and transformation family. A SQL `PARTITION BY state_orbit_hash` groups rows; it does not establish the relation defining those groups or make an index of all equivalent physics.

An MCTS-like scheduler may use model scores, UCB exploration, or prior hints to decide which frontier branch to expand next. It can run several independent candidate generators at once. Those scores are routing heuristics only. Each expansion is separately admitted by exact grammar, preconditions, and a preservation proof or marked speculative. The host should maintain per-branch and global fuel, depth, wall-clock and checker-cost caps; retain a reproducible frontier snapshot at exhaustion. Deduplicate by a canonical **established** presentation equivalence or explicit witness, not by text similarity or a model's class label. Equal-looking Hamiltonians with different ensembles or auxiliary fields stay distinct until a bridge is checked.

The index should store `CERTIFIED_IN_PRESENTATION`, `RESTRICTED_OBSTRUCTION`, `OPEN_FRONTIER`, `INVALID_REWRITE`, and `UNKNOWN_ADAPTER` as different records. A certificate is existential: one verified representation can establish the scoped sign-free construction. A negative result over all explored branches is only `UNKNOWN` unless the declared search family is finite, exhaustively enumerated, and every branch soundly checked. The fixed-basis diagonal ±1 gauge family offers such a complete finite control; arbitrary Hamiltonian presentations do not. General sign-problem hardness motivates budgeted search and honest `UNKNOWN`; it neither establishes that a particular Hubbard fixture is hard nor provides a five-second performance bound.

## Controlled comparisons and falsifiers

Each comparison changes one render-level treatment while reusing **identical host fixture bytes, checker, witness grammar, budget, and terminal rule**. The manifest lists frozen controls and candidate fixtures. Interleave arm order; pin a model revision and sampling settings. If a provider does not support a seed, record that limitation and use repeated runs without claiming paired stochastic identity. Validate semantic equivalence of prompt arms before measuring effects.

| Test | Contrast | Main measurement | A result that weakens the idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SYMBOL_RELABEL` | ASCII IDs versus a blinded bijective Greek relabeling | Host-verified witness rate after mapping IDs back; coverage and malformed rate | Similar rates with uncertainty spanning any useful effect, or purity gain without certificate gain. |
| `YAML_WRAPPER` | Plain JSON/English envelope versus typed YAML; CUT or witness semantics identical | Verified witness rate and parser refusal rate | YAML only changes formatting, or introduces duplicate-key/answer-leak failures. |
| `PHASE_CUE` | No cue versus a cue emitted **only after** complete host coverage | Premature VERIFY rate, checked terminal rate, turns after readiness | Cues increase premature or invalid VERIFY, or do not change verified outcomes. |
| `EIS_HINT` | None, diagnostic, solution-bearing after the same host-confirmed error | Checked repair conditional on receipt and verified delivery | No delivery, higher false-positive intervention rate, or success only with the solution supplied. |
| `BATCH_ATOMICITY` | Equivalent sequential moves versus one bounded list; poison the last item in a control | Final committed state and receipt equivalence; rollback on poison | Partial state survives a rejected batch or terminal depends on packaging. |
| `ROUTE_DISCRIMINATION` | Gauge, cochain, and meron fixtures with class labels removed | Correct explicit witness **type** and host-verified content | Label-only prose, identity for sign reversal, or universal obstruction claims on restricted failures. |
| `FRONTIER_SEARCH` | Exact enumeration of the small diagonal sign-gauge family versus budgeted heuristic frontier expansion | Witness recall, soundness, duplicate suppression, and `UNKNOWN` calibration at a fixed node budget | Search reports global failure from a partial frontier, merges unproved-equivalent nodes, or beats the oracle only by changing the problem. |

Primary outcome is `valid_witness_within_fuel / eligible_runs`, with exact route-specific host evidence. Secondary outcomes are attempts to first valid witness, invalid proposal rate by detector, complete coverage, delivered-hint efficacy, batch rollback correctness, indexed-node count, sound duplicate suppression, frontier recall at a fixed budget, token/cost use when reported, and calibrated `UNKNOWN` on fixtures lacking adapters. Count a malformed or refused attempt in the denominator. Do not count a model's self-reported `Phi_1`, `Gamma_2`, or class. Report per-fixture/per-arm counts and uncertainty; the initial small pilot is descriptive, with no population-level claim.

The publication threshold is higher than a persuasive demo. A research note is justified by a frozen manifest, raw prompts/replies, host decisions and hashes, runnable independent checks, negative controls, counterexamples, and a scoped claim ledger. A paper claiming FSP classification additionally needs a justified Hamiltonian-to-representation adapter, a proof of preservation of relevant physics, explicit completeness domain, independent replay on held-out fixtures, and a theorem or certificate covering that domain. If those are missing, publish a useful **method and failure-mode note** rather than promoting a local terminal into a general result. A Zenodo deposit is a distribution decision, not a mathematical validator.

## Stop and unknown rules

Stop on a host-verified finite terminal, bounded fuel exhaustion, malformed unrecoverable input, or missing adapter/checker. `UNKNOWN_NOT_CERTIFIED` is the correct outcome for the historical R2 free-fermion map and for general Hubbard/classification requests lacking a path or determinant checker. `RestrictedSignGaugeObstruction` means no diagonal ±1 gauge in the pinned basis; it is not a physical no-go theorem. Preserve failed and positive receipts with equal fidelity. No model response, YAML field, detector hint, or paper-like wording may set the authoritative terminal itself.
