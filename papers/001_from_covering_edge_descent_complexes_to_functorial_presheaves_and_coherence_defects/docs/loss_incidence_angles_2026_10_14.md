This working supplement closes three selected DAG goals under explicit scopes.
It has not been redeposited. Historical source archives, September manifests,
and the earlier October 14 receipts remain unchanged.

The flat-loss projection is positive, normalized, contractive and idempotent,
yet erases a protected question. Its actual curvature is zero. The sharp norm
floors in §4.7b require independently checked direct collapse and composed-path
answer separation. The Rust triangle verifier computes the path product;
neither a record count nor the cost-two convention is used as a measurement.

the independence needed for intermediate extension/restriction. A separate
endpoint-action theorem handles degree zero. A full varying endpoint-Hom block
embedding and graded Jacobi remain explicit formal obligations; the native
