## Corrections and exact witnesses

| Finding | Resolution and evidence boundary |
|---|---|
| A flat background preserving all cover maps was asserted without path independence. | A contractive diamond has path products $1/4$ and $1/6$, so no such background exists. `decompose_normal_form` now invokes the existing Path A hypothesis check and refuses this input. |
| The normal form asserted $K=-A_{\mathrm{outer}}$ for all triples. | Expand all mixed terms. On a four-node chain the exact values $K=1/15$ and $-A_{\mathrm{outer}}=1/40$ disagree. The shorter cover-cover specialization is retained. |
| The operator cocycle was promoted to lax 2-presheaf coherence. | Retain the exact four-term operator comparison. Arbitrary rectangular $K$ does not type an endomorphism $I+K$ or construct compositor 2-cells. Full higher coherence remains open. |
| A degree-specific derivation identity was promoted to an all-degree curved algebra. | Retain the stated identity and require a separate graded product/all-degree construction for that promotion. |
| A charge assigned as $2m$ was described as an independently measured curvature lower bound. | Identify the declared convention and legacy field. A supplied measurement check does not derive its mathematical interpretation. |
| Rational face-poset fixtures were identified with Regge deficit angles. | Retain the finite operator cocycle. The fixtures need not be orthogonal and contain no metric edge lengths or dihedral angles. |
| Flat Bell marginal maps were equated with an LHV model. | A PR box has operator-flat marginals and positive global probability-gluing distance. Its stacked four-context $L_1$ distance is $2$, independently of $K=0$. |
| The enclosure for $2(\sqrt2-1)$ was called transcendental and cited a missing test. | It is an algebraic example. Exact endpoint squares certify the interval; a shifted endpoint is rejected. No arbitrary transcendental input algorithm is supplied. |
| Any orientation/order mutation was said to create a nonzero Bianchi residual. | Limit sensitivity to selected noncommuting fixtures. Identity or commuting loops can hide such mutations. Distinguish exact multiplicative closure from its generally nonzero additive residual. |
| Duplicate or undeclared restrictions could be silently accepted. | Refuse duplicate declarations, nonidentity self-maps, unknown self-pairs, and invalid empty/unknown chain queries, with regression controls. |

The Bell gluing distinction follows the global-probability-section formulation in [Abramsky and Brandenburger (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264). The package's exact LP checks use rational empirical models; an irrational-input quantum LP is not replayed.
