Abstract
A generated claim, a record of its evaluation, and permission to act on it are different objects. Confusing
them allows an output to appear verified without the supporting process. We call this failure counterfeit
descent: a claimed evaluation status unsupported by the declared checks and evidence. This paper proposes a
contract for recording proposals, provenance, evaluators, unresolved obligations, and authorization decisions.
Its mathematical content is narrower: finite counterexamples to composition of local acceptance, a char-
acterization of zero conditional failure rate, and elementary verification-backlog accounting. In particular,
zero local-pass/global-fail rate requires acceptance soundness on the accepted population; it does not require
a boundary representation to retain every distinction relevant elsewhere. We apply these distinctions to
research-paper ingestion, where file integrity, equation fidelity, proof validity, and empirical support require
separate records. The reference software checks finite examples and packet consistency. It does not establish
the truth of arbitrary claims, authenticate institutional authority, or measure deployment safety.
Why a researcher should care. A readable paper and a reproducible release need a trace from each
assertion to its assumptions and evidence. A format conversion can change an equation even when the
source was correct; a byte-perfect copy can preserve an incorrect equation. Both defects need identifiable
locations and different remedies.

1. Introduction and Related Work
Generative systems can produce assertions together with citations, reports, and descriptions of checks. Those
descriptions may be useful, but they are not evidence that the checks occurred. The relevant question is
concrete: which procedure evaluated which version of which object, under which assumptions, and what did
it establish?
Separation of privilege provides a related security principle [1]. Software provenance systems such as in-toto
bind artifacts to recorded supply-chain steps [2]. Risk-management frameworks require contextual assessment
and accountable use [3]. These approaches motivate explicit evidence and decision boundaries; they do not
make a cryptographic digest a truth certificate. Work on agent evaluation also distinguishes benchmark
results from reliable performance in deployed workflows [4,5].
The contribution here is a small vocabulary and a set of finite checks for that boundary. The seven re-
sponsibilities below are a proposed design, not a theorem that every trustworthy system must have seven
components. A system may combine responsibilities, use other designs, or refuse every action. Its guarantees
depend on the actual contract and implementation.

Definition 2.1 (Unsupported evaluation status). A packet has an unsupported status when its claimed
outcome is not justified by the declared procedure and evidence. This definition covers an unsupported claim
of success as well as a fabricated claim that a review or refusal occurred. It does not establish intent to
deceive.
A hash binds recorded bytes under a collision-resistance assumption. It does not show that an experiment
occurred, a cited source supports a sentence, a verifier is sound, or the recorder is independent. Those are
separate obligations. A proof checked against a formal statement also requires an explicit correspondence
between that statement and the manuscript claim.

3.1 Local acceptance and global acceptance
Let (Ω, Pr) be a finite probability space. Let 𝐸𝐴 , 𝐸𝐵 , 𝐸𝐺 ∶ Ω → {0, 1} denote two local evaluators and a
global evaluator on the same composed trials. Write 𝐿 = {𝐸𝐴 𝐸𝐵 = 1}. The gluing diagnostic is


                                        Δ𝐸 = 𝐸𝐺 − 𝐸𝐴 𝐸𝐵 ∈ {−1, 0, 1}.

The event Δ𝐸 = −1 means both local checks passed and the global check failed. When Pr(𝐿) > 0, define


                                            NGD = Pr(𝐸𝐺 = 0 ∣ 𝐿).

If no trial is locally accepted, the conditional rate is undefined. Reporting zero would conceal the absence
of an evaluated population.

Proposition 3.1 (Zero conditional failure)
If Pr(𝐿) > 0, then NGD = 0 if and only if local acceptance implies global acceptance almost surely:


                                            Pr(𝐿 ∩ {𝐸𝐺 = 0}) = 0.

Proof. The conditional probability is the displayed numerator divided by the positive number Pr(𝐿). It
vanishes exactly when its numerator vanishes. On a finite space with positive probability at every point,
this is equivalent to the pointwise implication 𝐸𝐴 𝐸𝐵 = 1 ⇒ 𝐸𝐺 = 1. With zero-probability points it only
constrains the support. □
This corrects the earlier two-condition equivalence. Information preservation is useful for implementing a
global evaluator from a boundary message, but is not necessary for zero NGD on a restricted accepted
population.

3.2 Three different suﬀiciency conditions
Write 𝛾𝐴 for the first trace, 𝑏 = 𝜋(𝛾𝐴 ) for its boundary message, and 𝛾𝐵 for the continuation. Three questions
must be kept separate:
1. Does the continuation law depend on the first trace only through 𝑏?
2. Can global evaluation be recovered from (𝑏, 𝛾𝐵 )?
3. Do the local acceptance predicates imply global acceptance on the evaluated support?
The second question is a function-factorization question for deterministic evaluators. It holds exactly when
𝐸𝐺 is constant on each fiber of (𝛾𝐴 , 𝛾𝐵 ) ↦ (𝑏, 𝛾𝐵 ). It is neither implied by the first question nor required by
the third.
Example 3.2 (Lost hidden bit). Let 𝐻 and 𝐵 be independent fair bits, let 𝛾𝐴 = 𝐻, 𝑏 = ⋆, and 𝛾𝐵 = 𝐵.
Both local evaluators always accept; the global evaluator accepts exactly when 𝐻 = 𝐵. The continuation
law depends only on the constant boundary, but two of four trials fail globally, giving NGD = 1/2.
Example 3.3 (Information factorization without soundness). Let both local evaluators always accept
and let 𝐸𝐺 = 0 everywhere. Global evaluation factors through every boundary map, but NGD = 1.
Example 3.4 (Zero NGD without information factorization). Let 𝐻 be a fair bit, let 𝑏 = ⋆, and
fix the continuation. Set 𝐸𝐴 = 1 − 𝐻, 𝐸𝐵 = 1, and 𝐸𝐺 = 1 − 𝐻. Local acceptance occurs only at 𝐻 = 0,
where the global check also passes. Thus Pr(𝐿) = 1/2 and NGD = 0. Nevertheless, global evaluation
differs between the two traces sharing the same boundary and continuation. It cannot factor through that
boundary.

A cohort fraction counts completed evaluations of a fixed cohort. A window ratio divides completions during
a time window by arrivals during that window. The latter may exceed one while older work is cleared.

counts is necessary to interpret either statistic.

4. Worked Example: A Typed Research Paper
Consider a manuscript containing a theorem, its assumptions, a proof, a numerical example, and an empirical
interpretation. Give the paper a persistent identity and each edition a content-bound identity. Markdown,
TeX, PDF, and text are representations of that edition, each with its own file digest.
Each equation has an expression identity and each occurrence has a location. Repeated occurrences must
remain distinguishable: one may be correct in the source while a particular rendered occurrence gains a
minus sign. Claim identity must also include the declared domain and assumptions. Equal formula text in
two contexts does not establish equal meaning.
A review record should name the reviewed claim version, evaluator, method, evidence, result, and unresolved
scope. Useful failure reasons include sign drift, missing hypothesis, invalid inference, incorrect arithmetic,
unsupported citation, empirical mismatch, missing proof correspondence, and extraction failure. Each finding
should link to the affected occurrence and its proposed correction.
Example 4.1 (A transparent review fraction). Suppose a declared inventory contains 100 equally
weighted claims, of which 85 have supporting reviews and 15 have counterexamples. The report may say
“85 of 100 inventoried claims supported under the recorded methods; 15 refuted,” with links to every review.
It may not infer an 85% probability that the paper is true. If the other 15 are unreviewed, the report must



                                                         4
say so; absence of review is not refutation. If the inventory omitted assertions, the fraction describes only
the inventory.
Multiple file formats do not increase this denominator. Ten copies of a theorem are one claim with ten
occurrences. Nor should splitting one easy claim into many sentences inflate a quality score. Fix and
version the claim inventory and weighting policy before comparing editions, and report critical unresolved
dependencies separately from aggregate counts.

5. Implementation and Verification Scope
The proposed workflow records seven responsibilities: proposal intake, trace custody, evaluator declara-
tion, unresolved obligations, terminal decision, evidence attestation, and accountable ownership. These are
separable responsibilities rather than a mandated service topology.
The Python reference implementation checks packet structure, canonical proposal/context bindings, declared
residual tolerances, finite gluing counts, and rational backlog calculations. Its local receipt protocol is a model
of consistency checking. Caller-provided identity fields and a digest do not establish institutional legitimacy
or an independent source of truth. A returned failure of the exactness-fraud rule is a contract diagnostic,
not a legal finding of fraud.
The shipped Lean module proves finite hidden-bit counts, an unconditional-rejection count, natural-number
backlog positivity, a cleared-denominator share identity, and Boolean cases of the exactness-fraud rule. It
does not formalize arbitrary real probability spaces, empirical generation rates, source authenticity, or the
complete packet-to-world correspondence. Proposition 3.1 has the written proof above. Example 3.4 is
exercised by a regression test against the shipped gluing-rate function and by two Lean theorems: the
accepted population has zero defect, and no Boolean evaluator factors its varying global acceptance through
the constant boundary.
The companion typing tooling inventories parsed document elements and formula occurrences and compares
source formulas across text formats. A matching hash establishes the defined representation comparison,
not mathematical validity. Fresh rendering, PDF-byte binding, and page inspection address separate parts
of publication fidelity. PDF extraction that cannot recover a symbolic equation must remain explicitly
unresolved.

6. Discussion and Non-Claims
This framework supports a practical question: what evidence justifies this particular status for this par-
ticular object? Its usefulness should be evaluated through measured detection of incorrect transformations,
unsupported assertions, stale reviews, and composition failures, alongside the cost of review and false alarms.
It does not imply that large models cannot improve self-evaluation, that every self-critique is incorrect, or
that institutional aﬀiliation establishes truth. A model can propose a valid proof; an independently specified
checker can verify it. Human reviewers and institutions can also make errors. The relevant distinctions
concern procedure, evidence, scope, and accountable decisions.
The obstruction calculation applies only after a defensible encoding is supplied. The backlog model is
conditional accounting, not a measured law of scientific publishing. A high supported-claim fraction does
not compensate for a refuted central theorem. No result here authorizes action, establishes unconstrained
AI safety, or certifies an arbitrary external paper as fully understood.
