# Apartment 50: Paper 006

**AuthorityEffect:** None. The scene is fictional examination staging, not a
historical quotation, a reconstruction of Landau's examinations, or a source
of mathematical authority. Levels 0-7 are this companion's curriculum.

Landau asks what was actually retained. Woland asks who holds the anchor.
Behemoth removes the last line and rewrites the header. The record still
opens; the examiner requires an explanation of what that proves.

Start with the [study guide](study_guide.md), then attempt the [quiz](quiz.md)
without the [instructor key](instructor_key.md). The key includes the grading
standard and an exact-arithmetic replay. Read the [manuscript](../paper.md)
alongside the exercises; its historical source pin governs implementation
claims. A current checkout and a historical audit are distinct inputs.

The standard is a calculation, a concrete counterexample, or an explicitly
missing obligation. A stored refusal does not settle its mathematical meaning;
a digest does not authenticate its producer; an emitted decision record does
not show that a host acted on it. These are the questions to answer before
claiming reusable knowledge.
