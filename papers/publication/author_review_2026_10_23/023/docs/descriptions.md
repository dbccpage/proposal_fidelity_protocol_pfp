# Scientific Description: PFP: Task-bound claims and the scope of hallucination control

This deposit contains the complete research package for PFP: Task-bound claims and the scope of hallucination control (CODA-FOC), authored by Jeremy H. Carroll.

The monograph formalizes the calculus of finite obstruction and monotonic descent over discrete cochain complexes. In distributed systems, consensus protocols, and applied topology, local consistency conditions often fail to compose into globally realizable states. This work introduces computable defect certificates bounding obstruction mass under explicit boundary morphisms.

All computational claims are accompanied by exact rational arithmetic implementations in Python and machine-checked formalizations in Lean 4. The accompanying companion notebooks provide interactive walkthroughs of the worked examples and counterexample complexes.
