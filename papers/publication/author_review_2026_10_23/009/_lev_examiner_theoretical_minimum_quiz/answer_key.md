**AuthorityEffect: None.** This key provides practice feedback, not a credential.

   original (1,1,1) is refused as `DefectIncrease` and active claims remain
   unchanged. Name the native-backed checker, score definition, revision,
   command, and stored state. This is a proposed check until executed.
2. Assumption: declared graph and allowed potential move. Witness: proposed
   potential. Receipt: observed execution record. Obligation: missing native

   to the digest. The theorem needs proof/check under stated hypotheses.
   Custody does not authenticate the producer or establish truth.

## Level 5

## Level 7

19. Necessary conditions: eligible candidate, no applicable blockers, and an
    accepted checker receipt bound to the candidate. A declared carrier also
    requires verified exact joint zeros. With no declared carrier, that zero
    requirement does not apply. A supplied zero alone is insufficient. This
    describes the pinned correction's source, not a fresh gate execution.
20. Binding identifies bytes; authentication identifies a producer; correctness
    establishes the checker implements the claimed property; critique validity
    requires a checked argument. The latter do not follow from digest equality
    or successful typed parsing.
21. “I inspected the pinned correction and read the historical reports of 66
    and 44 passing tests; I did not execute them.” A fresh claim needs the
    checkout, executable/dependency pins, named test commands, dated outcomes,
    and preserved outputs. Historical JSON inspection is a different operation.

## Level 8

22. (a) Supported within scope; (b) Refused claim; (c) Open obligation;
    (d) Not run. Different claims can have different dispositions.
23. Claim, revision, path/digest, assumptions, exact input, command, environment
    or executable pin, result, disposition, obligation. RFC 8785 also requires
    primitive serialization and other conditions. A zero-denominator fraction
    is undefined, represented by `None`, not zero or perfect performance.
24. Record unchanged residual, attempted move family, and invariant. New
    representations need new assumptions and checks. A trigger does not license
    protected input changes or demonstrate an effective search escape.
