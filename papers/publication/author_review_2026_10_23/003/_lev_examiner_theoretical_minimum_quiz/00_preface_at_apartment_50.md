# Apartment 50: Paper 003

**AuthorityEffect:** None. This is fictional examination staging, not a historical quotation or a source of mathematical authority.

Examiner points at the declared carrier. Adversary asks which claim its checker can establish. Gauge Breaker asks for the smallest valid near miss. Their task in this companion is **detector admission and valid near misses**.

Begin with the [study guide](study_guide.md), then attempt the [quiz](quiz.md)
before opening the separate [instructor key](instructor_key.md). The eight
levels are an original teaching sequence, not a historical Examiner examination.
Declare the proposal's carrier, route, predicate, and action before treating a
pattern as a defect. A receipt verifies its stated finite check and grants no
authority to extend Genesis or make a physical claim.

The examiner's standard is a concrete calculation, an explicit counterexample, or a source-bound missing obligation. The manuscript [source reading](../paper.md#3-detector-objects-and-admission) governs the answer.
