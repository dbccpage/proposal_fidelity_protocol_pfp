# Papers

Two series, each numbered in the order a reader would meet the papers if they
were posted to arXiv one after another.

| Series | Folder | Papers | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal Fidelity Protocol | [`pfp/`](pfp/README.md) | 000–021 | [000 Introduction](pfp/000_pfp_introduction/README.md) |
| Finite obstruction calculus and authority | [`non_pfp/`](non_pfp/README.md) | 001–016 | [001 ℓ¹-native cohomology](non_pfp/001_l1_native_cohomology/README.md) |

The papers were renumbered on 2026-11-06. [RENUMBERING.md](RENUMBERING.md)
(and [renumbering.json](renumbering.json)) maps every old folder and number to
its new one. Folder contents were moved without changing a byte, so numbers
written inside the manuscripts and records are the old ones.

The PFP collection's build and check tools, deposit renderings and catalog
moved with it to [`pfp/tools/`](pfp/tools/), [`pfp/publication/`](pfp/publication/)
and [`pfp/publication_catalog.json`](pfp/publication_catalog.json).
