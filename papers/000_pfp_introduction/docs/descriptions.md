# Description: The Symbol Game — A First Game with S(n), MSS-N, and Basic Packets

A proposed solution and a verified referee receipt are distinct computational objects. This foundational monograph in the Proposal Fidelity Protocol (PFP) suite establishes the architectural firewall between untrusted generation and independent, deterministic validation. The paper formalizes the proposal-and-check workflow over discrete symbolic systems, demonstrating that linguistic fluency cannot substitute for an exact checking receipt, and that an admitted proposal does not validate unverified claims within an author's explanation.

The setting begins with the most elementary nontrivial discrete configuration: a two-mark carrier $\{a, b\}$. The primary challenge is to construct an irreflexive directed separation from $a$ to $b$ and expose its checking rule. To prevent category errors in symbolic AI and mathematical design, the monograph establishes strict boundaries across three non-interchangeable layers:
1. **Genesis derivation stages $S(n)$:** An internal deductive progression from ground void $S(0) = \langle\rangle$ through observational collapse $S(2)$, directed separation $S(3)$, finite task closure $S(4)$, and extension with retraction $S(6)$, governed by active obstruction registers.
2. **Minimal Symbol System atlas MSS-N:** An external, human-designed specification taxonomy of representation obligations and verification gates. The monograph emphasizes that atlas categories do not equal derivation states; in particular, $\text{MSS-2} \neq S(2)$.
3. **The Symbol Game execution runtime:** The operational proposal-and-referee cycle executed under an explicit, fail-closed wire vocabulary $\Sigma_{\text{Landau}} = \{0\text{--}9, a\text{--}z, <, >\}$ with matched angle brackets, zero delimiter tokens, and strict rational arithmetic over $\mathbb{Q}$ with no floating-point approximations.

Eight progressive educational modules lead the reader through foundational primitives: empty ground presentation, observational separation versus collapse, compact wire syntax, permutation-invariant renaming, typed input-output schemas, bounded turn sessions with explicit fuel budgets, and extension round trips requiring conservative predecessor retraction. An accompanying Jupyter notebook and self-contained Python reference make every worked derivation reproducible offline. The monograph also includes the Lev Landau Theoretical Minimum examination companion, providing a staged pedagogical crucible with an examination paper, study guide, and instructor answer key.

**Evidentiary Scope and Claim Hygiene:**
- **What it supports:** The implementation and tests demonstrate in this toy setting that a deterministic host can separate candidate generation from rule checking, enforce strict irreflexivity without floating-point arithmetic, and bound state progression to certified receipts.
- **What it does not support:** This paper does not demonstrate general automated reasoning, does not show universal properties of large language models, does not solve infinite-horizon combinatorial games, and does not claim that finite task closure at $S(4)$ resolves external future obstructions.
- **Narrowest defensible claim:** On the declared finite carrier $\{a, b\}$ with vocabulary $\Sigma_{\text{Landau}}$, the supplied Python referee deterministically verifies whether a proposed relation satisfies the declared irreflexive separation rule within a bounded fuel budget.
- **Next test needed:** Expanding the referee to degree-$k$ hypergraphs and evaluating adversarial proposer-verifier dynamics under automated budget exhaustion.
