# Paper 017: Glyphs, rewrites, logits and attention as checked proposal channels

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit, v0.7.0  
**Folder:** [`papers/pfp/017_pfp_proposal_channels_and_internal_interventions`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/pfp/017_pfp_proposal_channels_and_internal_interventions)

## In brief

Candidates can come from natural-language prompts, glyph games, operad prompt decompositions or model-internal logit and attention interventions. The channel changes what is proposed; the host's validity check stays fixed.

## What it covers

- Twelve finite results, starting with channel-independent soundness of host checking.
- Includes a logit-mask viability result and guarded slot retry with abstention.
- Internal glyph, logit and attention experiments remain unexecuted proposals.
