## In brief

How the separation between generating and checking survives across Python objects, canonical encodings, a Rust process, exact solver replay and portable outcome records.

## What it covers

- A four-vertex fixture walks the full path: rebuild the carrier, recompute identities, replay the exact solve, compare the proposed normal form.
- The same result is still refused admission because three conformance laws are unchecked and its attestation is unsigned.
- Distinguishes the repository's sorted-JSON profiles from RFC 8785.
