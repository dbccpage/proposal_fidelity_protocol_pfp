# Paper 016: Compression that preserves future operations and their cost

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit  
**Folder:** [`papers/pfp/016_pfp_future_faithful_compression_and_cost`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/pfp/016_pfp_future_faithful_compression_and_cost)

## In brief

Equal current answers need not allow equal future choices.

## What it covers

- For a finite deterministic transition table, characterises an exact operation-and-cost quotient and builds the coarsest stable refinement.
- Counterexamples to output-only compression and to treating the fewest operations as the cheapest.
