**Record:** [Zenodo 23115542](https://zenodo.org/records/23115542)

## In brief

Adding a symbol, checking a finite example and proving a semantic theorem are different achievements. This paper records the difference in the MSS grammar and its evidence pipeline.

## What it covers

- The MSS-0 to MSS-1 maps preserve the single old packet and return a typed non-descent receipt for new structure.
- That round trip shows injectivity on its declared domain, not a general tower or conservative extension.
- A census of 50 challenges sorts them into delivered solver packages, model measurements and specifications.
