# Paper 024: Recursive re-entry with evidence scope and shared fuel

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit  
**Folder:** [`papers/024_pfp_recursive_reentry_and_evidence_scope`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/024_pfp_recursive_reentry_and_evidence_scope)

## In brief

A returned answer, rejection rule or operator can become the next proposal. That only works if its task, evidence, transport and resource bindings survive.

## What it covers

- A finite composition theorem that separates soundness from solution coverage.
- A rejection rule that is sound for its source task but unsound after a task change.
- Positive charged costs bound execution without showing the original problem is solved.
