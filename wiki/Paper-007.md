# Paper 007: PFP VII: Designing executable lessons with explicit evidence boundaries

**Status:** Published preprint, v0.1.0 (2026-10-02)

**Record:** [Zenodo 23114712](https://zenodo.org/records/23114712)

## In brief

A lesson can run successfully and still teach the wrong claim. This guide builds lessons from five parts: a finite example, a worked calculation, a learner prediction, a changed-input probe and an explicit evidence boundary.

## What it covers

- Maps a 20-notebook integration route and works one triangle in detail.
- Adds a companion notebook using exact fractions only, and a pinned Lean project with the two formal files cited.
- Reports no learner study or teaching-effect result.
