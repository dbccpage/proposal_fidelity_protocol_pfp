# Paper 010: PFP X: Exact host checking of model verdicts on small finite algebraic challenges

**Status:** Published preprint, v0.1.0 (2026-10-02)  
**Folder:** [`papers/010_pfp_holonomy_checks_and_model_pilots`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/010_pfp_holonomy_checks_and_model_pilots)  
**Record:** [Zenodo 23115720](https://zenodo.org/records/23115720)

## In brief

Exact host checking separates a model's proposed verdict from a verified certificate, shown on finite-group lattice holonomy and six formal-mathematics pilots.

## What it covers

- A complete Python flatness checker over a validated multiplication table.
- Fresh replay reproduces the historical verdicts on all 15 retained solution records.
- Erratum (v2.0.1): the pilot prompts named the expected result for 36 of 60 cases, so the pilot counts are not measures of unaided performance.
