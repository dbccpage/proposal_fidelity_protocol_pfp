# Paper 022: PFP XX: Identity prefixes share the braid action and the mean defect 23/1200

**Status:** Retained draft, v0.1.0  
**Folder:** [`papers/022_pfp_frontier_research`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/022_pfp_frontier_research)  
**Record:** [Zenodo 23120020](https://zenodo.org/records/23120020)

## In brief

Extends PFP to tournaments on a topological quantum compilation task. The host referee checks orthogonality and unit determinant and computes an exact commutation defect with rational arithmetic.

## What it covers

- Shows that two schedules' prefixes act as the identity, so they share the same braid action.
- Model-labelled tables need raw execution provenance before they can support a performance comparison.
- The papers README notes the displayed L1/max-norm identification should not be inherited.
