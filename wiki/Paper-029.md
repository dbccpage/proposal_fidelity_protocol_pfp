## In brief

An agent can propose changes to the software that organises its own attempts. Whether that improves research is an experiment; whether a given result passes a checker is a separate verification.

## What it covers

- An experimental re-entry path that keeps candidate ancestry, isolates execution, reserves cost against one budget and separates public feedback from private grading.
- A frozen protocol caps a future campaign at $40.
- The paid campaign was deferred on 2026-10-23: no new model trials, improvements or transfer results are claimed.
