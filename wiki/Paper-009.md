# Paper 009: PFP IX: Reviewing claims against code and evidence

**Status:** Published preprint, v0.1.0 (2026-10-02)  
**Folder:** [`papers/009_pfp_adversarial_dialectics_and_terminal_reality_gates`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/009_pfp_adversarial_dialectics_and_terminal_reality_gates)  
**Record:** [Zenodo 23115634](https://zenodo.org/records/23115634)

## In brief

A review protocol for deciding what a technical claim is actually supported by. "Adversarial" means testing plausible ways the claim could fail, not hostility or agreement among models.

## What it covers

- The unit of review is a claim together with its inputs, implementation and evidence.
- A worked reading of a reported catch rate from a retained ten-trial trace.
- An appendix applies the procedure to a terminal gate in the source code, with four falsifiers replayed.
