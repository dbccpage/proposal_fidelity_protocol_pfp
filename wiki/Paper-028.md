# Paper 028: Operadic PFP: typed composition and bounded DDC repair

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit, v0.2.0; implementation study  
**Folder:** [`papers/028_pfp_operadic_composition_and_bounded_ddc_repair`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/028_pfp_operadic_composition_and_bounded_ddc_repair)

## In brief

How the proposal/check/permission split is organised across subsystems: capability definitions and typed composition (operads), immune observations (EIS), and evolution and lineage (PCIV), with execution kept separate.

## What it covers

- A provider-free code-repair example: a supplied patch passes six source-bound operations on an isolated working copy.
- It repairs one branch while preserving one negative control.
- The result is finite test evidence, not a theorem about the program or a model-performance measure.
