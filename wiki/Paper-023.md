# Paper 023: Task-bound claims and the scope of hallucination control

## In brief

A certificate establishes a declared property of a declared task, not the prose around it.

## What it covers

- A finite claim-scoping rule that admits only claims discharged by independent checks.
- A counterexample to promoting a correct witness into a correct explanation.
