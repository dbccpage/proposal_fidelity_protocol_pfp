# Paper 025: Local witnesses and global compatibility in agent composition

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit, v0.2.0  
**Folder:** [`papers/025_pfp_local_to_global_agent_composition`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/025_pfp_local_to_global_agent_composition)

## In brief

Independently checked local answers can disagree on shared variables.

## What it covers

- A finite gluing theorem for explicit assignment tables, keeping overlap disagreements as obstruction evidence.
- A three-agent cycle where every local task is satisfiable but the joint task is not.
- No general sheaf theorem or automatic multi-agent reliability is claimed.
