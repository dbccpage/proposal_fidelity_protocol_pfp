# Paper 011: PFP shape, review findings and the next test campaign

**Status:** Author-review draft; not an external deposit  
**Folder:** [`papers/011_pfp_the_minimum_theorem`](https://github.com/dbccpage/proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp/tree/main/papers/011_pfp_the_minimum_theorem)

## In brief

States the strongest defensible shape of PFP: a typed graph of proposals and checked transformations that preserve what a declared task needs. Model stages search; the host owns admission, replay and permission to act.

## What it covers

- A representation is useful only relative to the questions it preserves.
- Two execution specifications for future test campaigns (Demo 001 rounds 26 to 100, Demo 002 rounds 1 to 100), not records of new runs.
