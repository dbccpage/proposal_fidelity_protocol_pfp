# Paper 002: PFP II: Signed certificates, resumable search and a restricted path-pairing obstruction

**Status:** Published preprint, v0.1.0 (2026-10-02)

**Record:** [Zenodo 23114127](https://zenodo.org/records/23114127)

## In brief

When a search fails to find a sign transformation, it may not exist, the search may be unfinished, or the allowed moves may be too narrow. This paper keeps those three apart.

## What it covers

- A finite signed graph has either a checkable vertex-sign assignment or an odd closed-walk obstruction.
- A bounded search records what it has not yet examined and replays earlier segments before continuing.
- An exact Fock-space example rules out one specific pairing operation, not every representation of the Hamiltonian.
