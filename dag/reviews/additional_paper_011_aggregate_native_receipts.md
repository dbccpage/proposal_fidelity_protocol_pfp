**P011-AGG-F001 — Conflicting duplicate source-map keys make two historical JSON bindings ambiguous**

**P011-AGG-F002 — R7 promotes refusal plus adversary challenge to an unchecked obstruction terminal**

The captured R7 gate always returns UNKNOWN_NOT_CERTIFIED/admitted=false because its functional/physical adapter is unavailable. Nevertheless any VERIFY_SIGN_FREE proposal followed by adversary CHALLENGE sets caught_am=true and terminal_state=OpenSignObstruction. A direct OPEN_SIGN_OBSTRUCTION proposal gets the same terminal. Neither branch checks an obstruction witness, so this terminal and hallucination label cannot establish a mathematical obstruction or an independently measured adversary success.

**P011-AGG-F003 — Scripted scores and narrow commitments cannot certify general repair**

run_refactoring_packet(max_steps=1) unconditionally reports PASS_TO_MASTER with supplied phi=5.4,gamma=2,active_count=5; run_problem forces its final score to zero independent of problem content. Three evidence commitments hash only task ID, step count and final phi, so distinct source/proposal histories sharing those fields receive the same commitment without a SHA collision. Dialogue speeches assert solver/schema/test checks without performing them. The archive/current runner differences inspected here concern names/imports, not these branches.

For seven aggregate runs (affected_v2,v3,v4,final_v1,immune_extension_v1,original_live_ast_extension_v1,original_topology_extension_v1), each archived aggregate receipt records capability.json and job.json raw digests that do not match its same-directory archived member bytes.
