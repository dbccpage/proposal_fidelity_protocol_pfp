4.   The  Seven  Mortal  Sins  of  the  Paper  000  Examinee
Landau  stepped  to  the  slate  blackboard  by  the  window  and,  with  three  savage
screeching  strokes  of  his  railway  chalk,  engraved  the  Seven  Mortal  Sins:
================================================================================
                THE SEVEN MORTAL SINS OF THE PAPER 000 EXAMINEE
================================================================================
1. THE CONFLATION OF S(n) AND MSS-N:
    Asserting that a Genesis derivation stage S(n) is identical to an atlas
    entry MSS-N. S(n) is an internal derivation governed by unresolved
    obstructions; MSS-N is an atlas of representation obligations. MSS-2 != S(2).
    Penalty: Koroviev laughs continuously into the examinee's ear for 15 minutes.

2. THE PROPOSER-CHECKER COLLUSION:
    Assuming that proposing a relation implies that the relation holds. A proposal
    packet is untrusted text; only an independent referee receipt confers status.
    Penalty: Hella confiscates the candidate's pen.

3. THE SELF-LOOP BLASPHEMY:
    Writing a relation where a mark relates to itself (x ~ x). Irreflexivity is
    declared for this finite teaching board; reflexive equality is not forbidden.
    Penalty: Behemoth overturns the primus stove.

4. THE UNCHECKED APPROXIMATION:
    Replacing the exact integer/fraction calculations in the capstones with
    rounded values and presenting them as exact certificates.
    Penalty: Automatic score = -infinity.

A  short  worked  prediction
On   carrier   p,q   with   goal   p   to   q   and   label separates,   the   candidate ["q",
"separates", "p"] is a well-shaped irreflexive triple.  It still misses the directed
goal.     The   defect   is   not   its   syntax   or   a   self-loop;   it   is   the   specific   requested
direction.   This  distinction  makes  the  refusal  useful.
Similarly,  a  two-column observation row["red", "red"]  supplies no  separator.
The  claim  concerns  that  supplied  row.    It  does  not  exclude  every  observation
you  could  later  declare.   A  different  spelling  for  q  changes  neither  value.

Keep  identity,  replay,  and  trust  separate
The teaching identity uses sorted dictionary keys and compact JSON. Key order
does   not   matter   under   that   profile;   list   order   does.      It   is   not   an   RFC   8785
canonicalization  claim.   The  digest  is  not  a  signature.
Replay  can  catch  a  receipt  changed  without  the  corresponding  input  and  check.
It  can  also  accept  a  whole  self-consistent  return  for  an  unintended  question.   A
consumer  therefore  needs  an  expected  problem  identity  retained  independently
of  the  returned  packet.    It  still  trusts  the  selected  checker  and  needs  separate
evidence  if  producer  authentication  matters.
