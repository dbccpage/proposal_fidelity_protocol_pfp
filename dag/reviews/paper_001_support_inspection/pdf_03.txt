Proposed  companion  for  the  v0.1  preprint,  reviewed  2026-10-02.    This  key  fol-
lows  the  corrected  quiz  supplied  with  this  proposal.    It  does  not  attest  to  an

Students  should  attempt  each  problem  before  consulting  its  answer.   Give  feed-

rather  than  conceal  the  later  learning  material.   Use  exact  integers  and  fractions
throughout  the  assessed  calculations.

A finite fuel budget guarantees at most that many attempts when every attempt
returns or times out.  Exhaustion with a positive residual means unfinished work.
A  forest-count  cap  is  a  count  bound,  not  an  elapsed-time  bound.   A  fixed  cost
lattice   with   a   positive   spacing   and   a   lower   bound   can   bound   admitted   strict
descents,  but  rejected  attempts  and  hanging  calls  still  need  limits.

Problem  3.4
According   to   the   manuscript,   the   local   statements   concern   pair   cancellation
under  supplied  sign  and  weight  hypotheses;  impossibility  of  assigning  triangle
vertex   signs   making   all   three   edge   products   −1;   and   a   one-value   policy   type

They  do  not  imply  a  polynomial-time  general  graph  algorithm,  correctness  of

The  narrow  claim  is  that  the  named  local  files,   when  built  with  their  pinned
