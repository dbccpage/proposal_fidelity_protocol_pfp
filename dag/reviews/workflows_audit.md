## Dependencies and finite permutations

Existing family edges are **documented design dependencies**, not proof implications or executable dispatch edges. Containment edges place pilots and tasks in their owning groups. Retained variations remain settings on their original families. Exact source edges and all nodes are in the JSON registry.

`FreezeTask → OptionalGuidance → Candidate → Check → BoundedRepair? → OptionalChallenge → ClaimScopedRecord`

Use a preregistered finite grammar with typed ports, bounded agents/stages/repairs, and only topological schedules satisfying task/schema/hypothesis preservation. Unroll repair loops by attempt number. Reject incompatible task presets, missing checkers, changing target assumptions, answer-bearing feedback and identity-only permutations. Normalize equivalent schedules. A future ten-agent research round should propose and audit these typed adapters; it should not treat every family as a solver or every ordering as meaningful.

## Findings and coverage

- **WF-A01 (scope):** Only WF1-WF5 have public family runners; WF6-WF49 are documented proposals. Nested historical sources are not absent, but do not make the proposed family design executable as published.
- **WF-A02 (benchmark_blocker):** No inventoried existing workflow supplies a verified checker for every obligation of the unchanged grad-QM seed. WF1-WF3 can transport schema_only; WF4 demands integer_sum_v1 and WF5 recognizes only a complete finite exact-sum language.
- **WF-A03 (provenance):** Historical pilot imports require omega_engine_* private/runtime owners; relabelled names and paths may invalidate retained hashes. Source presence and historical receipts do not prove portable replay.
- **WF-A04 (scope):** The cleaned checkout includes the retired finite-QM programme, absent at the initial audit base. Its P0-P5 contracts explicitly retain unreviewed obligations and require task-applicable independent checkers; source/layout/provider dependencies remain.
- **WF-A05 (checker_limits):** Challenge documentation discloses C027 dual-check limit, C049 independent checker accepting 31/48 mutants, C041 external Lean dependency, C052 missing package tests, and C058 sampled rather than exhaustive checking above 8 bits. These are disclosed historical/current-source limits, not newly reproduced test results.
- **WF-A06 (evidence_limits):** WF26 and WF27 historical improvements used answer-leaking/full-repair hints; WF28 and WF48 include reference self-scores. Do not pool them with independent model accuracy.
- **WF-A07 (benchmark_blocker):** The older finite-QM prompt compiler prints computed expected values and requests JSON extraction; its gate engine matches expected certificates and some failure strings include expected values. This is extraction/finite-control checking, not blind derivation, and cannot supply answer-free repair feedback unchanged. The nested v3 programme expressly supersedes the legacy exercises.
- **WF-A08 (configuration_blocker):** C001 attention-mask pipeline declares tokenizer_json_sha256 and prompt_template_digest UNSET; its pin note explicitly says NOT_READY. A pipeline file is not evidence of a ready experiment.
