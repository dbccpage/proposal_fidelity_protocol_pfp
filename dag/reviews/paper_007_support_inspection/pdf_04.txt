Scoring  and  remediation
The   key  gives four explicit points for each problem.   Total and level scores locate
work  to  revisit;   this  rubric  is  a  proposed  teaching  aid,   not  a  validated  certifi-
cation  standard.    Treat  a  wrong  coeﬀicient  system,   silent  change  of  carrier  or
claims,  unrecorded  first  response,  or  promotion  of  a  replay  into  learner  evidence
as  an  error  in  that  problem’s  scope  point.    Do  not  erase  a  correct  calculation
elsewhere.
Use  the  guide  to  repair  the  corresponding  skill,  then  try  a  changed  instance  and
retain   both   attempts.     No   grade   grants   authority   to   publish   a   learning-effect
claim.
