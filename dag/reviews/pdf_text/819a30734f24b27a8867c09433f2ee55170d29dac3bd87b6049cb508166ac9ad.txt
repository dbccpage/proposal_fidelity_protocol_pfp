Candidate selection with residual and constraint diagnostics
Jeremy H. Carroll
2026-10-23
V ersion 2.0.4
Abstract
We specify a finite candidate-selection rule using a quotient residual, constraint violations, a closure residual, interface
discrepancies and a secondary alignment statistic. The quotient and closure residuals satisfy an operator-norm inequal-
ity, so these channels are not algebraically independent. Candidates must satisfy literal non-increase of the quotient
residual and an exact ratio ceiling before entering a two-stage tolerance ranking. A separate inequality can screen
representation-expanding proposals. We establish the residual bound, explain the ranking rule and give counterexam-
ples to stronger interpretations: a ratio ceiling does not preserve constraints against deletion, and greedy candidate
selection does not establish convergence. The implementation provides exact rational decision routines alongside a
numerical interface.
1. Introduction
A repair proposal can improve one diagnostic while worsening another. A selection rule must therefore state which
comparisons are mandatory and which are permitted trade-offs. The rule studied here is a finite filtering and ranking
procedure. Its acceptance conditions are definitions of a policy; they are not theorems that selected proposals solve
the underlying problem. The geometric setup uses a finite cochain complex, while the optimization ingredients are
finite-dimensional norm minimization and ordered comparisons [1,2].

5. Implementation and formal verification
The exact rational methods of code/mod_constraint_regulation_core.py implement signature validation, the cross-
multiplied ratio comparison, the expansion condition and the global ranking rule. They reject malformed rational inputs.
The numerical interface uses floating-point diagnostics and rejects nonfinite states or scores. Numerical evaluations
are not exact proofs about the field from which those diagnostics are said to originate.
lean4/Regulate.lean verifies rational comparison statements, selected ranking cases and classifications of rejected can-
didates. It does not construct a general cochain complex or prove Proposition 2.1; that proposition has the prose proof
in Section 2. The bundled census records the checked declaration set. Tests exercise the reference implementation,
including invalid inputs and the constraint-deletion limitation.
6. Limitations and discussion
Selection uses a finite supplied candidate set. It does not establish that this set contains a feasible repair. The policy
does not certify the diagnostic computation, preserve constraints against deletion or guarantee termination. Even literal
non-increase of a real residual allows repeated equal-score steps and does not imply finite termination. Applications
needing an absolute closure bound must specify one separately from the ratio ceiling.
