Study guide: PFP II
AuthorityEffect: None. Start with the fictional Apartment 50 preface . This
guide prepares the 14-problem quiz ; complete an attempt before opening the
separate worked instructor key . All required readings and checks are included
in this deposit.
What you need and what you should be able to do
Prerequisites are addition modulo two, finite graph walks, matrix multiplication,
exact fractions, a spanning-tree argument, factorials, and the power series for the
exponential. For the Fock example, use the manuscript’s explicit occupation and
hopping rules; no prior many-body simulation experience is required. Review
those rules before attempting the integration and length bounds.
By the end, produce and independently check both witness types; derive the
matrix adapter; distinguish a complete classifier from an unfinished enumeration;
replay a contiguous receipt chain; and explain exactly which pairing operation
the Fock example excludes.

1. Local equations and the global witness
Practice first on a tree: root one vertex at zero and propagate its bits. There
is no cycle constraint to violate. Add a closing edge and compare the required
endpoint bit with the propagated bit. A disagreement supplies an odd closed
walk through the tree.
For a gauge, check every edge equation, including loops and repeated endpoints.
For an obstruction, check every edge ID and endpoint, continuity between steps,
final closure, and the XOR of its bits. Reversing a traversal does not change a
binary edge bit. Give each parallel edge its own identity.

2. The matrix bridge
The adapter assigns a bit one to a positive nonzero off-diagonal entry and zero
to a negative one. This bit is the constraint needed to make the transformed
matrix entry negative; the graph sign (−1)𝑐𝑒 follows that convention. Zero
entries impose no equation.
Compute an entry of 𝐷𝐻𝐷 as 𝐷𝑖𝑖𝐻𝑖𝑗𝐷𝑗𝑗 before applying a general rule. A
successful gauge proves stoquasticity under those diagonal signs in the declared
basis. Other basis changes require a different grammar.
For exponential positivity, shift the diagonal so 𝐵 = 𝑎𝐼 −𝐻 ′ is entrywise nonneg-
ative. Powers of 𝐵 remain nonnegative, and the convergent power series gives
the result for nonnegative 𝛽. This establishes matrix-entry positivity; sampling
cost and observable-estimator variance require additional analysis.

4. Cancellation and the infinite family of lengths
A pairing is a map on a closed paired subset. Check involution, absence of fixed
points, opposite signs, and equal magnitudes. Separate unpaired survivors from
that subset. A nonnegative total does not by itself supply a pairing.
For the Fock example, keep the six-mode order fixed. Count occupied modes
strictly between a hop’s endpoints for its sign and doubly occupied sites for its
energy. A constant-energy three-hop sequence has magnitude 𝑒−𝛽𝐸𝛽3/3!.
The proof has two different parts: exact enumeration establishes the special
facts for length three, while inequalities cover every other length. For 𝑛 ≥ 4 ,
use
𝛽𝑛+1/(𝑛 + 1)!
𝛽𝑛/𝑛! = 𝛽
𝑛 + 1 < 1 (𝛽 = 1/4).
This controls an individual sequence. Counting and summing many sequences
is a separate task.
For 𝑥 ∈ [0, 1], odd truncations of the alternating series for 𝑒−𝑥 are lower bounds
and even truncations are upper bounds. Compare rational enclosures instead of
rounded decimals. The target lies above every long-path upper bound and below
every relevant positive short-path lower bound, excluding an equal-magnitude
partner.

Complete the quiz, mark it against the separate key, and write down the rea-

study or automatic authority is claimed.
