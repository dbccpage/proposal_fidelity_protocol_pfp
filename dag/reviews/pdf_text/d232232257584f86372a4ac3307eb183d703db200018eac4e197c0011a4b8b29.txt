2. Degree Separation Theorem
Theorem — Degree Separation for Γ2
The general 𝑑𝑘 ∶ 𝐶 𝑘 → 𝐶 𝑘+1 statement is schematic: it follows the same typing logic, but this paper
establishes and replays only the 𝑑1 ∶ 𝐶 1 → 𝐶 2 / Γ2 instance.
In the presence of nontrivial higher-degree structure, the diagnostic information carried by 𝑑𝑘 ∶ 𝐶 𝑘 → 𝐶 𝑘+1
is not recoverable from 𝐶 𝑘−1-data alone without additional structure.
Proof sketch.
• 𝑑0 maps 𝐶 0 → 𝐶 1. Its image im (𝑑0) is the space of exact 1-cochains. When a 2-complex is specified, 𝐻 1
is defined as ker (𝑑1)/im(𝑑0). Otherwise, obstruction is measured operationally via Φ1.
• 𝑑1 maps 𝐶 1 → 𝐶 2. The closure defect Γ2 = ‖𝑑 1ℎ1‖1 depends on the 2-cell incidence operator 𝑑1. It is
therefore invisible to a graph-only model that supplies 𝐶 1 but no explicit 2-cell structure.
Consequence. The diagnostic hierarchy Φ1, Γ2 requires operating at multiple cochain degrees simultane-
ously. A system restricted to 𝐶 0 cannot represent arbitrary cycle obstruction. A system restricted to 𝐶 1
cannot evaluate closure failure unless an explicit 2-cell complex and 𝑑1 are supplied.
