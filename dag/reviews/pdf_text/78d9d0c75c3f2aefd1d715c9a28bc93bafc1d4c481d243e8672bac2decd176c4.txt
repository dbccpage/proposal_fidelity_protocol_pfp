1. Proposal channels and the Three-Layer PFP host boundary
Fix a finite task 𝑃 , a candidate language 𝑊 , a solution relation 𝑅(𝑃 , 𝑤), and a deterministic host

3. Exact glyph transport
For the Boolean task 𝑥 + 𝑦 = 1, encode (𝑥, 𝑦)by the string <xy>. The four domain values have four
distinct strings: <00>, <01>, <10>, <11>. The decoder checks the brackets, length, and binary digits;
it is inverse to the encoder on this declared domain. Hence checking a decoded string and checking
its assignment are the same mathematical obligation. The strings use only a subset of A–Z, 0–9,
and angle brackets.
More generally, a finite domain admits an injective finite-word code with a declared partial inverse.
Proof is by indexing its elements and assigning distinct fixed-length binary words (with a separate
empty-domain case). This is representation capacity. It does not show that an LLM can recover the
desired word, solve every encoded question, or supply a missing domain interpretation. The typed
prompt and required response matter because they fix that interpretation and the host’s checking
obligation.

Research extension: evolving the proposal harness
Prompt, workflow, search-policy and context-policy rewrites are additional proposal-channel inte
rventions. The host’s task and checking obligations remain fixed. A strategy label, root-cause
explanation or compressed context can change the distribution of submitted candidates without
changing what a valid certificate must satisfy. This is the operational use of channel-independent
checking; it does not establish a theorem about empirical improvement.
The new campaign uses named Anthropic deployments with frozen role configurations. Its interface
supplies adaptive-thinking and output limits; it does not expose provider seeds, arbitrary logit
interventions or attention weights. Local seeds reproduce scheduling choices only. Every paid
attempt, including an unusable response, is bound to the original budget and retained raw evidence.
Changed candidate code is executed only within the declared isolated surface.
The historical mechanism trials and finite proofs above retain their own source and evidence iden-
tities. They are not training data or measured outcomes for the new held-out panel. Paper 029
supplies the separate protocol, ablations and exploratory ignition design. Those new model effects
