Q5. Balance theorem and construction (10 points)
Necessity . XOR the endpoint equations around any closed walk, counting
occurrences with multiplicity. Every arrival matches a departure around the
closed walk, so the vertex terms cancel, including for a loop or a repeated edge.
The edge XOR must be zero.
Suﬀiciency . Choose a root in each component and set its bit to zero. For each
other vertex, define its bit as the parity of a root-to-vertex path. Any two such
paths give a closed walk by reversing the second, so their parities agree. Append
an edge 𝑢𝑣 to a root-to- 𝑢 path; path independence proves 𝛼𝑣 = 𝛼 𝑢 ⊕ 𝑐𝑢𝑣. This
proves every edge equation. Isolated vertices are free; an odd loop or disagreeing
parallel edges contradict the premise directly.
Breadth-first construction stores adjacency, assigned bits, and tree parents. Pro-
cess each vertex and edge a bounded number of times. The first conflicting edge
