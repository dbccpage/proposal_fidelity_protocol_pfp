Why a researcher should care
Least squares ( 𝑝 = 2 ) spreads error globally over all edges. 𝐿1 quotient minimization instead localizes
defect on sparse cycle representatives while retaining an exact rational dual certificate. The complete 𝐿𝑝
family connects sparse polyhedral localization ( 𝑝 = 1 ), strictly unique nonlinear 𝑝-Laplacian projections
(𝑝 ∈ (1, ∞)), classical Hilbertian Hodge theory ( 𝑝 = 2), and maximal-error Chebyshev suppression ( 𝑝 = ∞,
under its separate weighted endpoint convention). The certificate presentation connects these classical graph
and convex constructions with machine-checkable finite witnesses.
Falsifiability
The prose propositions and the separately scoped Lean theorems have the hypotheses stated in their sections
and claim records. A mathematical conclusion would fail if a positive-weight finite instance has no attained
minimum for some 𝑝 ∈ [1, ∞], if primal and dual values differ for an optimal pair, if data satisfying all
stated hypotheses of Theorem L1C-T4 or LpC-T3 fail their conclusions, if for 𝑝 ∈ (1, ∞)two distinct gauge-
fixed potentials (one fixed vertex per component) both minimize the quotient objective, if the nonlinear
𝑝-Laplacian Euler-Lagrange equation fails at an optimal potential, if some class lacks an 𝐿1 optimum with
support at most the cycle rank, if an integral instance has no integral 𝐿1 optimum, if the 𝑝 → 1+ trajectory
fails to converge to an 𝐿1 minimizer, if a 2-simplicial complex has non-vanishing field curvature 𝑑1(𝑑0𝑓) ≠ 0
for a pure gauge potential, or if a finite quantum state violates the off-diagonal Schatten- 𝑝 or trace coherence
bound. The companion code and tests check solver success, primal/dual conditions, support bounds, exact-
shift invariance, nilpotency, and quantum coherence bounds across enumerated instances; it also includes an
instance on which an 𝐿2 surrogate reports the wrong 𝐿1 value.

9. Cohomological classification and topological obstructions
For a general cell complex, 𝐻 1(𝐾; ℝ) =ker(𝑑1)/im(𝑑0). The quotient norm Φ𝑝 is defined on 𝐶1/ im(𝑑0)and
descends to 𝐻 1 only after restricting to closed cochains. On a graph, 𝐶2 = 0 and this distinction disappears:
1. Exact 1-Cochains ( [𝜔] = 0): Φ𝑝([𝜔]) = 0if and only if 𝜔 ∈ 𝐵 1 = im 𝑑0. The cochain is a pure gradient
potential ( 𝑟 = 0), corresponding to a trivial gauge connection with zero holonomy along all cycles.
2. Closed Cohomology Defect ( [𝜔] ≠ 0 on 1-graphs): On a 1-dimensional graph complex ( 𝑑1 = 0 ),
all cochains are cocycles ( 𝑍1 = 𝐶 1). When Φ𝑝([𝜔]) > 0, 𝜔 represents a non-trivial cohomology class in
𝐻 1(𝐺; ℝ) ≅ ℝ𝛽. The defect represents a topological obstruction that cannot be eliminated by any choice
of vertex potential.
3. Simplicial Curvature Obstructions (2-complexes): On a 2-simplicial complex 𝐾, the face cobound-
ary 𝑑1𝜔 ∈ 𝐶2(𝐾)measures discrete field curvature (magnetic flux). If 𝑑1𝜔 ≠ 0, 𝜔 is not closed ( 𝜔 ∉ 𝑍1),
indicating localized curvature defect on 2-simplices. By Theorem L1C-T7 (simplicial Bianchi nilpotency),
any exact gauge adjustment 𝑑0𝑓 has zero curvature ( 𝑑1(𝑑0𝑓) = 0), so the curvature 𝑑1𝜔 is strictly gauge-
invariant.
This taxonomy classifies discrete difference fields by their topological obstructions, distinguishing exact gauge
transformations, non-contractible cycle circulations, and non-vanishing face curvatures.

11. Scope and concluding remarks
This monograph investigates finite-dimensional quotient spaces of discrete cell complexes under the family
of weighted 𝐿𝑝 norms. We summarize the precise boundaries of these results:
1. Discrete vs. Continuum: The results herein apply to finite directed graphs and finite 2-simplicial
complexes. They do not constitute an infinite-dimensional Hodge-de Rham theorem on manifolds, nor
do they address continuous Sobolev or 𝐿𝑝 cohomology on non-compact domains.
2. Static vs. Dynamical: The bounds on quantum density matrix coherence (Theorems LpC-T8 and L1C-
T8) apply to fixed states in a chosen basis; they do not characterize continuous unitary time-evolution or
open quantum system master equations.
3. F ormal V erification: Lean 4 mechanizes general_finite_certificate_optimality and the named finite-
model theorems for L1C-T1, L1C-T2, L1C-T3, L1C-T4, L1C-T5, and L1C-T7. The general 𝐿𝑝 results,
exactness characterization, cycle-rank bound, total unimodularity, and Schatten statements have prose
