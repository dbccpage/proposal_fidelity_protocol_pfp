AuthorityEffect: None. Read the fictional Apartment 50 preface , then at-
tempt these questions before opening the worked study guide .
These eight learning rungs, L0 to L7, are a teaching sequence. They are separate
from MSS stage numbers and the evidence grades NONE, SYNTAX and COM-
PUTED. Use exact integers or rational fractions. A graph edge is a declared
constraint; no filled face is implicit.
Each question earns two points: one for the calculation or concrete disposition,
and one for its evidence boundary. The total of 32 is a self-study score. Record
the failed obligation even when the arithmetic is correct.

L4: Bind the instance
Q09. Edge indices 0, 1, 2 are 𝐴 → 𝐵 = 2 , 𝐵 → 𝐶 = 3 , 𝐴 → 𝐶 = 6 . Set
