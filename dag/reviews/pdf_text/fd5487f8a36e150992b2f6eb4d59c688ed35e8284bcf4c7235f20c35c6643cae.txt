1. Retained information and a genuine external event
The sink records information supplied by its caller. A caller may label a refusal
PROTECTED_PATH, but the sink does not inspect ownership rules and recompute
that decision. Store identity and decision correctness are separate checks. A
native-result claim needs the actual verifier, its source or binary, input/output
bytes, and an evaluator for their relationship.
Similarly, the literal marker "None" must be present under the declared contract.
Its correctness is a field check. It is not a signature or a grant of authority. A

refusal can be useful even when the cause is absent infrastructure rather than
an invalid mathematical object.
2. Normalize before reasoning about hashes
At the audited commit, fractions become rational strings; mapping keys become
strings; tuples become lists. This is a storage profile with admitted uses, not an
injective encoding of every Python value. For a new example, Fraction(3, 4)
and the text "3/4" have the same stored representation. No collision attack is
involved: the difference is removed before hashing.
Compact sorted JSON determines bytes for the exercised values. Full JCS con-
formance also requires its primitive serialization and UTF-16 key order. Read
RFC 8785 , especially Sections 3.2.2 and 3.2.3. Python’s sorted keys alone do
not meet that contract. The manuscript reports the older encoder; subsequent
source changes must be named by their own source pin and receipt.
3. Three distinct comparisons
Let the payload of entry i include its index and predecessor h_(i-1), with the
first predecessor equal to the fixed genesis marker. The sink computes h_i =
SHA256(encode(payload_i)). A header states count n and head h_(n-1).
1. Local verification checks the file against its own header and links.
2. Retained snapshot comparison compares a verified count/head with
a previously held independent pair.
3. Raw-file comparison compares a separately held digest of the exact file
bytes, including formatting and the header.
Work a fresh three-entry example on paper. Deleting the last entry while leaving
count three is locally inconsistent. Deleting it and changing the header to count
two can be locally consistent, but differs from the retained three-entry anchor.
Adding entry four changes the head too; that inequality does not distinguish
legitimate extension from tampering. Verify the whole new chain and compare
its entry at retained index two with the old head.
An attacker who controls both the record and the only supposed anchor can
replace both. Independent custody is an assumption that must be specified. A
self-hash on an anchor does not authenticate its author. Header schema, genesis
metadata, whitespace, and normalized type aliases also require care: a matching
normalized count/head need not mean matching raw bytes.
4. Read the API’s order of effects
emit_tail_receipt uses memory. Reopen, or successfully verify the persisted
file, before using a receipt as a disk comparison. The check assumes controlled
custody and one writer; it does not make a racing writer safe.

The append method updates memory before replacing the file. If replacement
fails, memory and disk may diverge. A failure is not an acknowledged append
and is not a guaranteed rollback. Reload or discard the old object, establish
persisted state, and reconcile the attempted event before deciding on a retry.
The package test injects one replacement error; it does not simulate every power
failure or filesystem behavior.
Frozen dataclasses protect assignment to their fields, not recursive mutation
of contained dictionaries. A later write can expose such mutation by failing
verification. API discipline is not immutable media.
For four equally sized 10-byte records, individually rewriting the history writes
(1+2+3+4)*10 = 100 entry bytes; the final snapshot contains 40. This idealized
count excludes headers and does not measure actual performance. Bound input
bytes, memory, total retained data, writer coordination, and failure recovery
separately.
5. Read a candidate catalog as a dataset
Use the snapshot provenance and the package’s catalog tests to reproduce file
counts offline. The candidate and active fields are labels in a historical file.
A negative-fixture field containing a comment is not an executable failing test.
A digest of identifying text is not a bound reproduction receipt.
Keep the evidence ladder explicit: retained event, independently checked re-
production, proposed detector, valid near-miss check, admission for a declared
scope/action, and actual host application. Each transition needs its own evi-
dence. The paper proposes this procedure; it reports no integrated semantic
miner or measured discovery yield. A predecessor chain supplies an order, not
arbitrary dependency edges or semantic equivalence.
The display a>a illustrates why scope matters. Under a declared irreflexive
relation it is a defect. Under a separately declared loop-permitting grammar
it can be valid. The classroom example needs no other paper or repository
worksheet to define those two contracts.
6. Interpret maintenance records carefully
Clonal checking has three outcomes: a panel match yields a kill record; no match
yields no record; an invalid expression yields a typed refusal. Looking only at
the Boolean loses the distinction between a clean check and a refused check.
Neither a kill record nor a regulatory record proves that a consumer mutated
the repertoire or applied an enforcement policy.
For an explicit list of four pairs with one pattern-only case, toxicity is 1/4,
which meets the inclusive 1/5 threshold. Pairs with neither signal still count in
that interface’s denominator. In benign corpus mode, two matches among eight
prompts produce two pattern-only triggers divided by two total triggers, hence

7. Exact decay has a separate expiry policy
With fixed positive tau, one idle advance from t to t’ applies k = floor(t'/tau)
- floor(t/tau) halvings. For a score 3/7, idle zero, tau four, and 17 further
idle runs, k is four and the new score is 3/112. With expiry at 20 runs, the rule
remains active. Expiry and the spacing of score halvings are different parameters
when tau is supplied separately.
Calls of 5, 5, and 7 runs cross one, one, and two new intervals. Their sum is
four because intermediate floor terms cancel. This proof assumes fixed tau, a
nonnegative initial score, and no intervening stimulation or reset. Stimulation
with boost 1/5 changes 3/112 to 127/560, resets idle to zero, and returns an
active rule. Arithmetic maintenance does not establish admission.
