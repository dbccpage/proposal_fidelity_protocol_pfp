## Findings

- **EDU-001 (medium): Non-increase is not strict descent.** Phi(after)<=Phi(before) admits equality, whereas strict descent requires <. Calling these the same obscures stationary steps and termination arguments. Constant positive Phi is an immediate counterexample to strict progress. Action: Use non-increase/monotonicity for <=; state a separate strict-progress or stationary/refusal rule and any well-founded termination assumptions. Evidence: `wiki/Core-Concepts.md:42-44`.

- **EDU-002 (low): Survivor-product analytic bound needs a unit-disk condition.** The stated a_d<=C M^d and |z|<1/M do not suffice if 0<M<1. One degree-one generator satisfies the bound with C=2,M=1/2, but its geometric series diverges at z=3/2 despite |z|<2. The formal Euler product remains valid. Action: Declare M>=1, replace it by max(1,M), or require |z|<min(1,1/M). Evidence: `education/004_oepfp/math/07_math_filtered_diagrams_persistence_and_survivors.md:111-116`.

- **EDU-003 (high): EIS educational implementation contradicts its exact-affinity curriculum.** The level prohibits arbitrary float boosts; the companion returns floating token-overlap ratios and raises affinity_threshold by 0.05. Its tests validate that boost. These classes cannot serve as executable evidence for the rational P/(P+F) specification. Action: Either align the companion with exact counts/Fraction and candidate custody, or label it explicitly as a separate synthetic simulation and remove verification equivalence claims. Evidence: `education/009_oeeis/levels/level_07_clonal_selection_and_affinity.md:76-86`; `education/009_oeeis/code/eis_core.py:220-237`; `education/009_oeeis/code/tests/test_eis_levels.py:166-177`.

The local companion runs only size/keyword screening and never invokes its stored thymus. This does not support the page's absolute cryptographic fencing claim. Production authenticated.rs is a different path and was separately reviewed by the parent team; this finding is not a production signature-bypass claim. Action: Show the complete trusted-verifier/role/scope/time/custody binding path or mark the fragment as digest binding only and link the actual authenticated owner and negative tests. Evidence: `education/009_oeeis/levels/level_09_external_admission_boundary.md:93-115`; `education/009_oeeis/levels/level_09_external_admission_boundary.md:128-138`; `education/009_oeeis/code/eis_core.py:262-285`.
