Capstone  —  Recount,  perturb,  and  report  (8  points)
Predict   the   counts   before   running python tools/review_capstone.py   from
the  package  root.   Complete  one  point  for  each  item:
    1. Verify  the  three  retained  record  digests  against  their  bundled  provenance.
    2.  Check  ten  distinct  conditions:   two  lattices  times  five  interaction  values.
    3.  Reconstruct  the  four  cells  and  four  exact  descriptive  fractions.
    4.  Change  one  refused  CHALLENGE  to  CONCUR  in  a  copy.    Predict  and
        recount.
    5.  Change  only  the  caught  flag  in  a  copy.   Predict  whether  cells  change.
    6.  Group  all  five  gate  probe  records  into  four  defect  families.
    7.  Preserve  originals  and  identify  the  altered  copy  as  a  control,   not  a  new
        trial.
    8. Write  source  identity,  command,  observed  outcome,  scope,  and  next  obli-
        gation.
The   tool   checks   custody,   condition   census,   and   recount   controls.     It   does   not
reexecute  models,   native  checkers,   or  the  historical  gate.    A  matching  output
does  not  automatically  grade  the  written  explanations.

Practice  rubric
Each  question  earns  0–3  points:   one  for  the  result,  one  for  calculation  or  reason,
and  one  for  evidence  scope  or  remaining  obligation.   The  24  questions  total  72
points;  the  capstone  adds  eight,  for  80  possible.   Use  scores  to  find  material  to
revisit.   No  score  establishes  competence  or  publication  authority.
