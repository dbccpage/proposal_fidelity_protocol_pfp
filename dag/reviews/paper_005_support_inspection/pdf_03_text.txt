Theoretical minimum: Paper 005
AuthorityEffect: None. Read the fictional Apartment 50 preface . The source
reading supplies the definitions; the study guide contains a separate instructor
key.
Use the ordered directed edges (ab,bc,ca), rational claims c=(1,1,1), positive
weights w=(1,2,3), and ordinary additive potentials unless a question explicitly
changes them. The symbol a>b>c>a sketches edges; it declares no filled face.
Give exact calculations and identify the move or obligation being checked. All
implementation questions refer to this package’s patched governor, not every
repository surface.
The examination has eight levels, four questions each. Attempt it without the
instructor key. The budget in Question 22 is a separately declared exercise
policy; UNKNOWN is not a terminal status implemented by this four-move
governor.
Level 0: Declare the instance
1. List the vertices, ordered edges, coeﬀicient system, claims, weights, units,
allowed changes, and cell declarations needed to reproduce this example.
Which datum would an undisclosed wraparound-clock convention change?
2. Is a weight evidence that one claim is true? What additional modeling
decision is needed before replacing a measured edge claim?
3. Compute the score D_c(0) and the minimum Phi_w(c). Explain why six
and three can both be correct reports about the same unchanged claims.
4. Compute df and r for f=(0,-2,-1). Contrast selecting this potential with
replacing claims by c’=(-2,1,1). State each resulting cycle sum and mini-
mum.
Level 1: Certify the small calculation
5. Prove the lower bound three for every potential, and give a witness attain-
ing it. Distinguish that proof from testing a few potentials.
6. Let q=(1,1,1). Show q dot df=0 and |q_e|<=w_e, then use q dot c to
obtain a lower bound on the weighted discrepancy. What completes the
optimality argument?
7. Replace f by f+(t,t,t), with t rational. What changes? Does the unique
cheapest edge make the absolute potential unique?
8. Reverse ca into ac. Give the correctly transformed third claim and poten-
tial difference. What false conclusion could result from keeping the claim
+1 after reversing its arrow?

Level 3: Identify the checked obligation
13. Use a section CUT to move the original all-zero potential to (0,-2,-1),

unchanged?
14. Install the consistent claims (-2,1,1) while the stored section is zero. Com-

15. Try EDGE_MATCH:d,e, EDGE_MATCH:a,c, and the malformed

16. A CUT supplies both a section and replacement claims. Another supplies

Level 4: Respect the execution route
17. The native calculator is unavailable. What can the hand argument, the

18. A native executable is configured but refuses during projection after a re-

20. Does scoring a graph with a duplicate vertex or duplicate directed edge
produce a defensible minimum? Identify the check boundary in this pack-
age. Distinguish duplicate directed edges from the separately declared
reverse edge (b,a).

21. Compare the harness inputs "1/10", integer 1, JSON number 0.1, and
JSON true. Which are accepted as exact claim values? Why is converting
a float to Fraction insuﬀicient?
22. Under a separate budget of two proposal attempts, both fail and candi-

24. Change the weights to (1,1,1). Give two different minimizing residuals for

Local grading rubric
Award two points per question: one for the exact calculation or correct imple-
mentation outcome, and one for the declared scope or missing obligation. A
level passes at seven of eight points with no unsupported acceptance. This
threshold is an instructional choice, not an empirically validated measure of
reasoning ability.
Do not pass the corresponding level if an answer silently changes the carrier,
treats omitted replacement claims as zeros, replaces a configured native refusal
with an accepted Python value, promotes an unfinished search to impossibil-
ity, or treats a checksum as semantic proof. Mark an unsupported step as an
open obligation; return the implementation’s actual refusal for a violated in-
put contract. Keep mathematical obstruction, runtime refusal, and unavailable
execution evidence distinct.
