What  you  should  be  able  to  do
A  detector’s  output  is  a  finding  under  its  executable  predicate.    Admission  al-
lows  a  frozen  policy  to  act  on  that  finding  within  a  declared  route.   Verification
asks  whether  a  witness  satisfies  the  route’s  contract.   Those  are  three  different
operations.   A  valid  proposal  can  contain  a  flagged  phrase;  an  internally  consis-
tent  receipt  can  describe  a  wrong  verdict;  a  missing  checker  can  refuse  without
judging  the  proposed  mathematics.

1.   Build  the  contract  before  interpreting  a  finding
Write  down  the  subject  schema,  route  and  checker,  allowed  transitions,  detector
predicate,  defect  code,  and  proposed  action.   A  positive  control  must  reproduce
the  named  defect  with  the  checker.     A  valid  near  miss  must  satisfy  the  same
applicable  contract  while  challenging  the  detector’s  boundary.
For   example,   a   contract   requiring   a   field   named witness   can   reject   a   record
without   it.     A   nearby   record   containing   the   field   with   an   unusual   permitted
value  is  a  useful  negative  control  if  the  checker  accepts  it.   A  record  legal  only
under  a  different  schema  demonstrates  a  scope  boundary;  it  cannot  substitute
for  a  negative  control  inside  the  admitted  schema.
Freeze  predicate,  action,  schema,  route,  checker,  and  fixture  identities  together.
Hashing  an  unchecked  assertion  preserves  the  assertion’s  bytes;  admission  still
requires  its  semantic  check.    One  valid  near  miss  is  a  regression  control,  not  a
distributional  safety  estimate.

3.   Keep  two  forms  of  state  in  the  transaction  trace
Semantic   state   consists   of   coverage,   proposed   witness,   and   phase.      Attempt
accounting  includes  consumed  fuel  and  receipt  history.    A  refused  transaction
restores  the  former  while  recording  the  latter.
Try  a  package  host  with  fuel  3.    First  cover  an  element  in  a  valid  batch:    fuel
becomes 2.  Next try an unknown move:  its semantic changes are rolled back and
fuel  becomes  1.   A  final  VERIFY  reaches  the  missing  port,  spends  the  last  unit,
and  ends  the  package  host  in  TERMINAL.  No  route  witness  has  been  accepted.
The  canonical  baseline  records  exhaustion  on  the  next  attempt  instead.
This fuel bound is a  bound on charged batches.   Serialization can inspect a  large
batch   before   refusal;   further   terminal   calls   can   still   produce   receipts.      Byte,
move,  time,  and  receipt-retention  limits  require  their  own  declarations.

4.   Treat  source  pins  as  part  of  the  answer
The  deposit  has  three  distinct  kinds  of  source:   its  standalone  illustrative  code;
the   canonical   host   at   the   paper’s   baseline;   and   the   older   immune-system   au-
dit/correction  history  in  Appendix  A.  List  each  separately  in  a  replay.    A  his-
torical  claim  about  a  pinned  commit  remains  a  historical  claim  even  when  the
default  branch  has  moved.
The   package   receipt   checker   recomputes   chains   and   refuses   certificate   labels,
but  does  not  replay  fuel  or  arm  scripts.   Its  canonical  counterpart  checks  more
protocol  fields.    Both  need  an  independently  held  anchor  to  detect  a  complete
rewrite;  neither  unkeyed  hash  chain  authenticates  a  producer.    Hash  coverage,
semantic  replay,  and  producer  authentication  are  separate  properties.
Record  the  exact  command,  tool  version,  input  digests,  exit  status,  and  skipped
scope.      “Not   run”   and   “unavailable”   are   results   of   the   audit,   not   successful
verification.   The  archive  verifier  checks  file  custody  and  structure.

5.   Derive  the  Hill  threshold  using  exact  arithmetic
Aggregate  only  positive  entries:


                                𝜃  =  ∑    𝑠𝑖,               𝐻(𝜃) =            𝜃𝑛𝐾𝑛 + 𝜃𝑛 .
                                     𝑠𝑖 >0

For 0<t<1,   activation   is   equivalent   to theta^n (1-t) >= t K^n.     This   form
avoids  computing  a  root.   At t=1/2  the  boundary  is  simply theta>=K.

As  a  practice  example, n=3,K=3,t=1/2  with  signals [1,1/2]  gives theta=3/2
and H=1/9,  so  it  does  not  activate.    One  signal  of  3  does  activate.    A  cap  of  1
on  every  entry  would  instead  require  at  least  three  positive  entries.    Counting
distinct  independent  witnesses  would  also  require  identities,  deduplication,  and
provenance.
The mathematical proof allows positive realn.  The exact package helper accepts
positive  integer n,  and  integer  or  Fraction  signals  and  scales.   Python’s  Boolean
subtype of integer does not makeTrue an admissible coeﬀicient.  Negative signals
are  ignored  here;  they  do  not  veto  a  positive  sum.    Check  threshold  endpoints
separately.

6.   Prove  the  screen’s  property  before  measuring  it
Let P  be  the  screened  predicate, E  the  executed  predicate,  and S  the  valid  self
panel.   Admission  means  no  member  of S  satisfies P.  Panel  soundness  means  an
admitted  detector  never  fires  under E  on S.
If E implies P,  admission  makes  firing  on  the  panel  impossible.   If  that  impli-
cation  fails  at  an  input x,  the  singleton S={x}  is  an  immediate  counterexample.
Avoiding   rejection   of   a   harmless   rule   requires   the   reverse   implication.      Both
properties  together  require E=P.
Even   equality   does   not   supply   missing   valid   inputs   to   the   panel.     With E=P
matching onlynovel and a panel containing onlyknown, admission says nothing
about whether an independent route checker would acceptnovel.  Executing the
right  predicate  and  covering  valid  cases  are  separate  obligations.    Duplicating
panel  rows  improves  neither  coverage  nor  independence.

7.   Keep  the  three  physics  questions  separate
A  diagonal  sign  gauge  asks  for  vertex  signs  making  a  particular  real  symmetric
matrix’s  nonzero  off-diagonal  entries  nonpositive.   Its  signed  cycle  product,  not
the  graph’s  odd-cycle  topology  alone,  is  the  obstruction.
The   standard   local   particle-hole   transformation   on   a   real-hopping   Hubbard
graph  requires  opposite  local  signs  on  every  bond.    That  requirement  is  about
bipartiteness.    It  differs  from  the  gauge  condition  because  the  transformation
rule  fixes  an  opposite-sign  constraint  regardless  of  the  bond’s  sign.
Attractive   Hubbard   determinant   QMC   instead   uses   equal   real   spin-sector   de-
terminants  under  the  stated  spin-independent  and  spin-balanced  assumptions:
their  product  is  a  square.   These  conditions  can  hold  on  a  non-bipartite  lattice.
This proves neither the particle-hole symmetry above nor a generic eﬀicient solu-
tion  to  the  NP-hard  sign-problem  class.   Majorana  and  meron  methods  require
their   own   model-specific   conditions;   changing   notation   or   matching   a   phrase
does  not  check  them.   See  the  manuscript’s  primary  references.

8.   Choose  denominators  before  looking  at  outcomes
Effectiveness   is   verified-witness   runs   divided   by   all   allocated   runs   under   the
common   budget.     Keep   refusals,   unknowns,   timeouts,   and   exhaustion   visible.
False  suppression  is  blocked  independently  valid  eligible  proposals  divided  by
all  independently  valid  eligible  proposals.    Invalid  proposals  cannot  make  that
fraction  smaller.
For  zero  failures  in  independent  Bernoulli  opportunities,  solve (1-p)^n=alpha
to  get  upper  bound 1-alpha^(1/n).   At  a  fixed  one-sided  95%  confidence  level,
alpha=0.05.    Repeated  proposals  from  a  shared  fixture  can  be  dependent,   so
the  number  of  stored  records  is  insuﬀicient  to  select  n.   Route-specific  intervals
also  do  not  automatically  form  a  joint  95%  interval;  the  analysis  must  declare
its  multiplicity  and  dependence  handling.
The  principal  experiment  compares  D  with  matched  generic  reflection  R.  Freeze
the  detector  bank  before  held-out  collection.    Keep  discovery,  admission,  pilot,
and   confirmatory   instances   disjoint   beneath   surface   relabeling.      An   assisted
arm  establishes  performance  with  that  assistance.   The  missing  route  seam  still
prevents  this  deposit’s  demonstrations  from  supplying  a  treatment  effect.
