+## Research extension: custody across harness replacement
+
+The new research path adds custody identities for the experiment, candidate,
+parent, checker, partition, model, configuration and original budget. Raw provider
+bytes are retained before interpretation. A private verdict binds the submitted
+program and the actual outputs on the frozen private cases. Offline replay
+rechecks those outputs; it does not infer success from a stored status string.
+A changed reply, stale parent or substituted private case cannot inherit an
+existing evaluation receipt. These are additional implementation obligations,
+not claims about the historical source archive audited above.
+
+Budget custody is also persistent. Every request reserves its maximum supported
+liability before transport. A malformed answer may still contain valid billable
+usage, while a missing or ambiguous usage report retains its reservation and
+halts dispatch. A child harness receives no ledger, provider credential or
+permission to choose its own budget identity. Experimental incumbent replacement
+therefore cannot reset expenditure.
+
+A chained local journal detects inconsistent edits under a retained anchor. It
+does not authenticate a hostile host capable of replacing every object and anchor.
+Native executable identity, source identity and source-to-binary reproducibility
+remain separate claims. The live Linux containment probe and paid experiment
+have not run; the new author-review package must not present local Windows tests
+as observations of those environments.
+
+The source and page references for this extension are collected in
+[Paper 029](../029_pfp_evidence_bound_harness_evolution/paper.md) and its
+[source-to-claim map](../029_pfp_evidence_bound_harness_evolution/docs/source_to_claim_map.md).
+The author-review rendering does not replace a historical deposit or archive.
