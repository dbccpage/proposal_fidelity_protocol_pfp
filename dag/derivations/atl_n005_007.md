## Claim

If $\|a\|_2=\|b\|_2$ then $\|a-b\|^2\|a+b\|^2=4(\|a\|^2\|b\|^2-\langle a,b\rangle^2)$.

## Hypotheses

- Integer vectors of equal length and equal squared norm

## Derivation

With $s=\|a\|^2=\|b\|^2$, $\|a\mp b\|^2=2s\mp2\langle a,b\rangle$; the product is $4(s^2-\langle a,b\rangle^2)$.
