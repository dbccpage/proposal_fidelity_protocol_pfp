## Claim

For a phrase detector P and sentence s containing phrase p, the negated sentence containing 'not' + p also matches.

## Hypotheses

- Substring/regex phrase matcher

## Derivation

A substring matcher depends only on presence of p in s; inserting a negator outside p preserves presence, so the match result is unchanged; one fixture pair instantiates it.
