AuthorityEffect: None.

- **Archive ID**: `ATL-P016-001`

- **Archive statement**: "Exact quotient: a report \(q\) admits an exact quotient of the action table iff states in one fibre share terminal labels and, per action, enablement, enabled cost and successor fibre; legal words, reports, costs and best resolution cost are then preserved."
- **Prior partial lineage**: `RCT-C21` (`narrows`: covered report-respecting congruence and quotient factorization for total uncosted Moore machines without partial action domains or positive rational action costs).

## 2. Hypotheses

1. **Declared finite model**: \(X\) is a finite state set, \(A\) is a finite action alphabet, \(q_0 : X \to R_0\) is an initial protected report, \(T_{\mathrm{term}} \subseteq X\) is the set of terminal states (included in the protected report), and for each \(a \in A\):
   - \(D_a \subseteq X\) is the domain on which \(a\) is enabled,
   - \(t_a : D_a \to X\) is a deterministic transition, and
   - \(c_a : D_a \to \mathbb{Q}_{>0}\) assigns an exact positive rational step cost on \(D_a\).
2. **Surjective quotient map**: \(q : X \to Z\) is a report map onto its image \(Z = q(X)\) refining \(q_0\) (including terminal labels).

## 3. Step-by-Step Derivation

### Step 1 — Definition of exact quotient descent and reflection

An abstract costed partial table \((\hat{D}_a, \hat{t}_a, \hat{c}_a, \hat{r}, \hat{T}_{\mathrm{term}})\) on \(Z = q(X)\) is an *exact quotient* of \(X\) along \(q\) iff for every \(x \in X\) and \(a \in A\):
1. \(\hat{r}(q(x)) = q_0(x)\) and \(q(x) \in \hat{T}_{\mathrm{term}} \iff x \in T_{\mathrm{term}}\),
2. \(x \in D_a \iff q(x) \in \hat{D}_a\) (enablement descent and reflection), and
3. whenever \(x \in D_a\), \(\hat{c}_a(q(x)) = c_a(x)\) and \(\hat{t}_a(q(x)) = q(t_a(x))\).

### Step 2 — Necessity of the fibre conditions

Assume an exact quotient exists on \(Z = q(X)\), and let \(x, y \in X\) satisfy \(q(x) = q(y) = z\). Then:
- **Protected report and terminal label**: \(q_0(x) = \hat{r}(z) = q_0(y)\), and \(x \in T_{\mathrm{term}} \iff z \in \hat{T}_{\mathrm{term}} \iff y \in T_{\mathrm{term}}\).
- **Per-action enablement**: \(x \in D_a \iff z \in \hat{D}_a \iff y \in D_a\).
- **Enabled cost and successor fibre**: Whenever \(x \in D_a\) (hence \(y \in D_a\)), \(c_a(x) = \hat{c}_a(z) = c_a(y)\) and \(q(t_a(x)) = \hat{t}_a(z) = q(t_a(y))\).

Thus every fibre \(q^{-1}(z)\) shares terminal labels, reports, per-action enablement, enabled step cost, and successor fibre.

### Step 3 — Sufficiency and word-level preservation

Conversely, assume the fibre conditions hold for all \(x, y \in X\) with \(q(x) = q(y)\). For each \(z \in Z\), pick any representative \(x_z \in q^{-1}(z)\) and define:
- \(\hat{r}(z) = q_0(x_z)\) and \(z \in \hat{T}_{\mathrm{term}} \iff x_z \in T_{\mathrm{term}}\),
- \(z \in \hat{D}_a \iff x_z \in D_a\), and for \(z \in \hat{D}_a\), \(\hat{c}_a(z) = c_a(x_z)\) and \(\hat{t}_a(z) = q(t_a(x_z))\).

By the fibre assumptions, every clause is independent of the choice of representative \(x_z \in q^{-1}(z)\), so properties (1)–(3) hold for every \(x \in X\).

Now prove by induction on word length \(|w|\) for \(w \in A^*\) that for every \(x \in X\):
- **Base case \(w = \varepsilon\)**: Both \(x\) and \(q(x)\) legally execute \(\varepsilon\) with accumulated cost \(0\), reaching \(x\) and \(q(x)\), which share protected report \(q_0(x) = \hat{r}(q(x))\) and terminal membership \(x \in T_{\mathrm{term}} \iff q(x) \in \hat{T}_{\mathrm{term}}\).
- **Inductive step \(w = a \cdot u\)**: By property (2), \(a\) is enabled at \(x\) iff \(a\) is enabled at \(q(x)\). If disabled, \(a \cdot u\) is illegal from both. If enabled, property (3) gives equal first-step cost \(c_a(x) = \hat{c}_a(q(x))\) and successor \(\hat{t}_a(q(x)) = q(t_a(x))\). Applying the induction hypothesis to \(u\) at \(t_a(x)\) shows that \(u\) is legal from \(t_a(x)\) iff legal from \(q(t_a(x))\), with equal tail cost \(C(t_a(x), u) = \hat{C}(q(t_a(x)), u)\), equal final report, and equal terminal status; adding \(c_a(x)\) gives \(C(x, a \cdot u) = \hat{C}(q(x), a \cdot u)\).

Consequently, for any \(x, y\) in one fibre (\(q(x) = q(y)\)), the sets of legal words \(L(x) = \hat{L}(q(x)) = L(y)\) and resolving words \(R_{\mathrm{res}}(x) = \hat{R}_{\mathrm{res}}(q(x)) = R_{\mathrm{res}}(y)\) coincide with identical word costs \(C(x, w) = C(y, w)\). Therefore the optimal finite resolution cost

\[
C^*(x) = \inf_{w \in R_{\mathrm{res}}(x)} C(x, w) \in \mathbb{Q}_{\ge 0} \cup \{+\infty\}
\]

is identical on \(x\), \(y\), and \(q(x)\) (including \(C^*(x) = +\infty\) when \(R_{\mathrm{res}}(x) = \emptyset\)).
