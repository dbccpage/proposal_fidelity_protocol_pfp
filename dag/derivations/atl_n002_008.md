## Claim

$\Phi_w(\omega_1+\omega_2)=\Phi_w(\omega_1)+\Phi_w(\omega_2)$ iff some $z\in Z_w=\{d_0^*z=0,|z_e|\le w_e\}$ maximizes both.

## Hypotheses

- finite scalar graph complex
- positive weights
- LP strong duality $\Phi_w(\omega)=\max_{z\in Z_w}\langle z,\omega\rangle$

## Derivation

If $z$ is common, $\Phi(\omega_1+\omega_2)\ge\langle z,\omega_1+\omega_2\rangle=\Phi(\omega_1)+\Phi(\omega_2)$; subadditivity gives equality. Conversely an optimal $z$ for the sum gives two nonnegative gaps $\Phi(\omega_i)-\langle z,\omega_i\rangle$ summing to zero.
