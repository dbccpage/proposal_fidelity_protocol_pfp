## 1. Claim Statement

Let $(G, \cdot, e, (\cdot)^{-1})$ be any group and let $\mathcal{L}$ be an admissible $L \times M$ rectangular lattice in `code/holonomy.py` ($L, M \ge 2$ when `periodic=False`; $L, M \ge 3$ when `periodic=True`). Let $e_0 \in E^+$ be any interior positive edge (all $2LM$ positive edges on a periodic torus; $(L - 2)(M - 1)$ horizontal edges $((r, c), (r, c + 1))$ with $1 \le r \le L - 2$ plus $(L - 1)(M - 2)$ vertical edges $((r, c), (r + 1, c))$ with $1 \le c \le M - 2$ on an open lattice). For any non-identity element $a \in G \setminus \{e\}$, the single-defect connection $U^{(e_0, a)}$ assigning $U_{e_0} = a$ and $U_e = e$ for all $e \in E^+ \setminus \{e_0\}$ satisfies:
1. **Exact two-plaquette coboundary support**: $e_0$ belongs to the boundary of two distinct plaquettes $P_+, P_-$ (once in forward orientation with product $a \neq e$ and once in reversed orientation with product $a^{-1} \neq e$), while every remaining plaquette has four identity links $(e, e, e, e)$ and product $e$. Hence `check_flatness` returns `verdict == "NOT_FLAT"` with `nonflat_plaquettes == 2`.
2. **Open-boundary vs. periodic-torus contrast**: On an open lattice, the $2L + 2M - 4$ boundary edges each bound a single plaquette, so setting one boundary edge to $a \neq e$ yields `NOT_FLAT` with `nonflat_plaquettes == 1`. On a periodic torus ($L, M \ge 3$), $\partial \mathcal{L} = \varnothing$, so zero single-link connections achieve `nonflat_plaquettes == 1`; moreover, across *all* $G^{2LM}$ torus connections, surface boundary cancellation forces any sole non-flat plaquette holonomy $W_*$ to lie in the commutator subgroup $[G, G]$ ($W_* \notin [G, G]$ is algebraically excluded on the torus, whereas every $a \in G \setminus \{e\}$ is realized as a sole plaquette defect on an open boundary edge).

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Exact Edge–Plaquette Coboundary Incidence and Product Evaluation
In `Lattice.plaquette(p, q)` (`holonomy.py` lines 241–244), plaquette $P_{p,q}$ ($0 \le p < L_{\mathrm{pl}}, 0 \le q < M_{\mathrm{pl}}$, where $(L_{\mathrm{pl}}, M_{\mathrm{pl}}) = (L, M)$ on a torus and $(L - 1, M - 1)$ on an open grid) traverses four directed edges in order:
$$\begin{aligned}
s_0(p, q) &= (p, q) \to (p \oplus 1, q) && \text{(positive vertical edge } e_v(p, q)\text{, forward)}, \\
s_1(p, q) &= (p \oplus 1, q) \to (p \oplus 1, q \oplus 1) && \text{(positive horizontal edge } e_h(p \oplus 1, q)\text{, forward)}, \\
s_2(p, q) &= (p \oplus 1, q \oplus 1) \to (p, q \oplus 1) && \text{(positive vertical edge } e_v(p, q \oplus 1)\text{, reversed)}, \\
s_3(p, q) &= (p, q \oplus 1) \to (p, q) && \text{(positive horizontal edge } e_h(p, q)\text{, reversed)}.
\end{aligned}$$

Case analysis on an interior positive edge $e_0 \in E^+$ with $U_{e_0} = a \neq e$ and $U_e = e$ for $e \neq e_0$:
1. **Interior horizontal edge $e_0 = e_h(r, c) = ((r, c), (r, c \oplus 1))$**:
   - On an open grid, "away from the boundary" means $1 \le r \le L - 2$ and $0 \le c \le M - 2$ (requiring $L \ge 3$); on a periodic torus ($L, M \ge 3$), every $0 \le r < L, 0 \le c < M$ is interior.
   - Comparing $e_h(r, c)$ against $s_0, s_1, s_2, s_3$ over all $(p, q) \in \mathrm{Origins}(\mathcal{L})$:
     - $e_h(p \oplus 1, q) = e_h(r, c) \iff (p, q) = ((r - 1) \bmod L,\, c)$, where $P_{(r-1)\bmod L,\, c}$ has link tuple $(e, a, e, e)$ and product $W_+ = e \cdot a \cdot e \cdot e = a \neq e$.
     - $e_h(p, q) = e_h(r, c) \iff (p, q) = (r, c)$, where $P_{r, c}$ has link tuple $(e, e, e, a^{-1})$ and product $W_- = e \cdot e \cdot e \cdot a^{-1} = a^{-1} \neq e$.
   - Because $L \ge 3$ in both cases, $(r - 1) \bmod L \neq r$, so $P_{(r-1)\bmod L,\, c} \neq P_{r, c}$ are two distinct plaquettes, and neither plaquette contains $e_h(r, c)$ on more than one side.
   - For row-major inspection in `check_flatness` (`holonomy.py` lines 380–391): when $r \ge 1$ (including $e_h(1, 1) = ((1, 1), (1, 2))$ in `test_single_non_identity_link_is_not_flat`, lines 141–147), $r - 1 < r$ visits $P_{r-1, c}$ before $P_{r, c}$, yielding `witness_plaquette == P_{r-1, c}` and `witness_holonomy == a`; on a torus when $r = 0$, $P_{0, c}$ precedes $P_{L-1, c}$, yielding `witness_plaquette == P_{0, c}` and `witness_holonomy == a^{-1}`.
2. **Interior vertical edge $e_0 = e_v(r, c) = ((r, c), (r \oplus 1, c))$**:
   - On an open grid, "away from the boundary" means $0 \le r \le L - 2$ and $1 \le c \le M - 2$ (requiring $M \ge 3$); on a periodic torus ($L, M \ge 3$), every $0 \le r < L, 0 \le c < M$ is interior.
   - Comparing $e_v(r, c)$ against $s_0, s_1, s_2, s_3$:
     - $e_v(p, q) = e_v(r, c) \iff (p, q) = (r, c)$, where $P_{r, c}$ has link tuple $(a, e, e, e)$ and product $W_+ = a \neq e$.
     - $e_v(p, q \oplus 1) = e_v(r, c) \iff (p, q) = (r, (c - 1) \bmod M)$, where $P_{r, (c-1)\bmod M}$ has link tuple $(e, e, a^{-1}, e)$ and product $W_- = a^{-1} \neq e$.
   - Because $M \ge 3$, $(c - 1) \bmod M \neq c$, so $P_{r, (c-1)\bmod M}$ and $P_{r, c}$ are two distinct plaquettes.
3. **Remaining plaquettes**: Every $(p, q)$ outside $\{P_+, P_-\}$ has link factors $(e, e, e, e)$, which evaluates to $e$ (and increments `skipped_identity_plaquettes` by $N_{\mathrm{pl}} - 2$ when `skip_identity_links=True`). Thus `nonflat_plaquettes == 2` and `verdict == "NOT_FLAT"`.

### 3.2 Boundary Partition on Open Grids vs. Closed-Surface Cancellation on Tori
1. **Open-grid edge partition ($L, M \ge 2$)**:
   $|E^+_{\mathrm{open}}| = L(M - 1) + (L - 1)M = 2LM - L - M$ splits disjointly into:
   - $|E^+_{\mathrm{int}}| = (L - 2)(M - 1) + (L - 1)(M - 2) = 2LM - 3L - 3M + 4$ interior edges, each lying in $2$ plaquettes (`nonflat_plaquettes == 2`), and
   - $|E^+_{\partial}| = 2(M - 1) + 2(L - 1) = 2L + 2M - 4$ boundary edges ($e_h(0, c)$, $e_h(L - 1, c)$, $e_v(r, 0)$, $e_v(r, M - 1)$), each lying in $1$ plaquette (`nonflat_plaquettes == 1`, as seen in C017 cases `pois_01`, `base_01`, and `base_13` in `paper.md` Table 1, lines 289–294). On $2 \times 2$ open, $|E^+_{\mathrm{int}}| = 0$ and $|E^+_{\partial}| = 4$.
2. **Periodic-torus single-link and general surface cancellation ($L, M \ge 3$)**:
   - On a torus, $|E^+_{\partial}| = 0$ and $|E^+_{\mathrm{int}}| = 2LM$, so every single-link non-identity connection yields `nonflat_plaquettes == 2` (and `skipped_identity_plaquettes == LM - 2`); zero single-link torus connections produce `nonflat_plaquettes == 1`.
   - More generally, for *any* connection $U \in G^{2LM}$ on the unwrapped fundamental rectangle $[0, L] \times [0, M]$ of the torus, gluing the $LM$ elementary plaquettes in row-by-row strips cancels all interior horizontal and vertical edges in opposite-orientation pairs, expressing the boundary commutator $B A B^{-1} A^{-1}$ of the fundamental cycles $B = \operatorname{hol}((0,0)\to\cdots\to(L,0))$ and $A = \operatorname{hol}((0,0)\to\cdots\to(0,M))$ as an ordered product of basepoint-conjugated plaquette holonomies:
     $$\prod_{m=1}^{LM} \gamma_m W_{r_m, c_m} \gamma_m^{-1} = B A B^{-1} A^{-1} \in [G, G].$$
     Passing to the abelianization $\pi_{\mathrm{ab}} : G \to G / [G, G]$ cancels all conjugators and commutators:
     $$\prod_{r=0}^{L-1} \prod_{c=0}^{M-1} \pi_{\mathrm{ab}}(W_{r,c}) = e_{G/[G, G]}.$$
     Consequently, if $LM - 1$ plaquettes on the torus are flat ($W_{r,c} = e$), the remaining plaquette holonomy $W_*$ satisfies $\gamma_* W_* \gamma_*^{-1} = B A B^{-1} A^{-1}$, so $W_* \in [G, G]$ is mandatory:
     - For abelian $G$ ($[G, G] = \{e\}$), no torus connection in $G^{2LM}$ can have `nonflat_plaquettes == 1`.
     - For $S_3$ ($[S_3, S_3] = \{\text{"012"}, \text{"120"}, \text{"201"}\}$, parity homomorphism $\mathrm{sgn} : S_3 \to \{\pm 1\}$), $\prod_{r,c} \mathrm{sgn}(W_{r,c}) = +1$ holds identically across all $6^{2LM}$ torus connections, so a sole non-flat plaquette with odd-permutation holonomy (`"021"`, `"102"`, `"210"`) is excluded on the torus, whereas a single open-boundary edge $U_{e_{\partial}} = \text{"021"}$ realizes it immediately.
     - For $Q_8$ ($[Q_8, Q_8] = \{\text{"e"}, \text{"-e"}\}$, $Q_8 / [Q_8, Q_8] \cong \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$), a sole non-flat plaquette with order-$4$ holonomy (`"i"`, `"-i"`, `"j"`, `"-j"`, `"k"`, `"-k"`) is excluded across all $8^{2LM}$ torus connections, whereas `"-e"` is realized as a sole non-flat plaquette by multi-link non-commuting seam connections (`AUDIT-P010-013`).

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Complements `PAPER010-RECTANGULAR-HOLONOMY-CLASSIFIER`**: This derivation supplies the exact edge–plaquette coboundary support count (`nonflat_plaquettes == 2`) for interior single-link defects and the open-boundary vs. torus surface-cancellation dichotomy; global spanning-tree gauge classification of flat connections remains in `PAPER010-RECTANGULAR-HOLONOMY-CLASSIFIER`.
2. **Interior-Edge Dimension Requirement on Open Lattices**: Although `Lattice(2, 2, False)` is admitted by `code/holonomy.py`, a $2 \times 2$ open grid has zero interior edges ($|E^+_{\mathrm{int}}| = 0$); open interior horizontal defects require $L \ge 3$ and open interior vertical defects require $M \ge 3$.
3. **Multi-Link Torus Defects**: While single-link torus defects always yield `nonflat_plaquettes == 2` and non-commutator sole plaquette defects $W_* \notin [G, G]$ are excluded on the torus, multi-link non-abelian torus connections can achieve `nonflat_plaquettes == 1` when $W_* \in [G, G] \setminus \{e\}$ (realized by the seam family of `AUDIT-P010-013`).

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Derives `verdict == "NOT_FLAT"` and `nonflat_plaquettes == 2` (with products $a$ and $a^{-1}$) for every single non-identity interior edge on open and periodic rectangular lattices, establishes `nonflat_plaquettes == 1` on open boundary edges, and derives $\prod_{r,c} \pi_{\mathrm{ab}}(W_{r,c}) = e_{G/[G,G]}$ on periodic tori.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not claim `nonflat_plaquettes == 2` for boundary edges on open grids, and does not claim `nonflat_plaquettes == 1` is excluded on non-abelian tori when multiple links carry non-commuting elements with $W_* \in [G, G] \setminus \{e\}$.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: On any open ($L, M \ge 2$) or periodic ($L, M \ge 3$) rectangular lattice, setting one non-boundary positive edge to $a \in G \setminus \{e\}$ and all other positive edges to $e$ yields `NOT_FLAT` with two non-flat plaquettes (carrying products $a$ and $a^{-1}$), whereas open boundary single-link defects affect one plaquette.
4. **Next test needed**: If higher-dimensional cubical complexes ($d \ge 3$) are introduced, replace the $2$-plaquette valence law with $(2d - 2)$-plaquette interior coboundary valence and discrete cube Bianchi identities.
