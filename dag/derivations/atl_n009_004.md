## Claim

For a finite carrier over $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$ with the $\ell^1$ norm, the infimum defining $\Phi$ is attained.

## Hypotheses

- finite $C^0,C^1$
- scalars $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$
- $\ell^1$ norm

## Derivation

$\Phi(\omega)=\min\{\mathbf 1^\top t: -t\le\omega-d_0\alpha\le t\}$ is a feasible LP bounded below by $0$, so an optimal vertex exists (fundamental theorem of LP), and over $\mathbb{Q}$ the optimum is rational.
