## 1. Claim Statement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a connected finite graph with strictly positive weights $\mathbf{w} \in (0, \infty)^E$, and let $\mathcal{M}_1 \subset F$ denote the non-empty compact convex set of gauge-fixed $L^1$ quotient minimizers for a given cochain $\boldsymbol{\omega} \in C^1(G)$. For each $p \in (1, 2]$, let $f_p^* \in F$ be the unique minimizer of the $L^p$ Dirichlet energy $J_p(f) = \frac{1}{p} \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}^p$. Under these hypotheses:

1. Every cluster point $f_1^* = \lim_{k \to \infty} f_{p_k}^*$ as $p_k \to 1^+$ belongs to $\mathcal{M}_1$.
2. The first-order asymptotic expansion of the objective as $p \to 1^+$ on a bounded set $K \subset F$ containing the trajectory is:
   $$\frac{1}{p} \sum_{e \in E} w_e |r_f(e)|^p = \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} + (p - 1)\left[E(f) - \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}\right] + R_p(f),$$
   where $E(f) = \sum_{e \in E} w_e |r_f(e)| \log |r_f(e)|$ (with the continuous convention $0 \log 0 = 0$) and $\sup_{f \in K} |R_p(f)| \le C (p-1)^2$.
3. The functional $E(f)$ is strictly convex on $\mathcal{M}_1$, so the weighted entropy minimizer:
   $$f_{\text{ent}}^* = \arg\min_{f \in \mathcal{M}_1} \sum_{e \in E} w_e |r_f(e)| \log |r_f(e)|$$
   is uniquely determined in $\mathcal{M}_1$, and the entire trajectory converges:
   $$\lim_{p \to 1^+} f_p^* = f_{\text{ent}}^*.$$

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Graph Topology**: Connected finite 1-dimensional cell complex $G = (V, E)$.
- **Positive Weights**: Weights $w_e > 0$ on each edge $e \in E$.
- **Gauge-Fixed Subspace**: $F = \{f \in C^0(G) : f(v_0) = 0\}$ for a designated root vertex $v_0 \in V$, ensuring injectivity of $d_0|_F$.
- **Residual Operator**: $r_f = \boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f \in C^1(G)$.
- **$L^1$ Optimal Face**: $\mathcal{M}_1 = \{f \in F : \|r_f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} = \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])\}$, which forms a non-empty compact convex polytope.
- **Conjugate Range**: Exponent $p \in (1, 2]$ approaching $1^+$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Uniform Boundedness of the Primal Trajectory
1. Let $W = \sum_{e \in E} w_e$ and $M_0 = \max_{e \in E} |\omega_e|$.
2. By weighted Hölder inequality and optimality of $f_p^*$ for $J_p$ relative to $f = 0$:
   $$\|r_{f_p^*}\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \le W^{1 - 1/p} \|r_{f_p^*}\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \le W^{1 - 1/p} \|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \le W M_0.$$
3. By the triangle inequality:
   $$\|d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \le \|r_{f_p^*}\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} + \|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \le W M_0 + \|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}.$$
4. Because $d_0|_F: F \to C^1(G)$ is injective on the finite-dimensional space $F$, there exists a constant $C_F < \infty$ such that $\|f\|_\infty \le C_F \|d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ for all $f \in F$.
5. This places the entire trajectory $\{f_p^*\}_{p \in (1, 2]}$ and the optimal face $\mathcal{M}_1$ in a common compact set $K \subset F$. On $K$, residuals are uniformly bounded: $\sup_{f \in K, e \in E} |r_f(e)| \le M < \infty$.

### 3.2 Uniform Taylor Expansion and $\Gamma$-Convergence
1. For any scalar $x \in [-M, M]$, the Taylor expansion of $t \mapsto \frac{1}{p} |x|^p$ around $p = 1$ yields:
   $$\frac{1}{p} |x|^p = |x| + (p - 1)\left[|x| \log |x| - |x|\right] + R(p, x),$$
   under the continuous extension $0 \log 0 = 0$, with remainder $|R(p, x)| \le C_1 (p-1)^2$ uniformly for $x \in [-M, M]$.
2. Multiplying by positive weights $w_e > 0$ and summing over $E$:
   $$J_p(f) = \frac{1}{p} \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}^p = \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} + (p - 1)\left[E(f) - \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}\right] + R_p(f),$$
   where $\sup_{f \in K} |R_p(f)| \le W C_1 (p-1)^2$.
3. As $p \to 1^+$, $J_p(f) \to \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ uniformly on $K$, establishing $\Gamma$-convergence. Consequently, every cluster point $f_1^*$ of $f_p^*$ minimizes $\|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$, placing $f_1^* \in \mathcal{M}_1$.

### 3.3 Asymptotic Selection on the Optimal Face
1. Let $g \in \mathcal{M}_1$ be an arbitrary gauge-fixed $L^1$ minimizer, so $\|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 g\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} = \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$.
2. By optimality of $f_p^*$ for $J_p$, $J_p(f_p^*) \le J_p(g)$.
3. Expanding both sides:
   $$\|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} + (p - 1)\left[E(f_p^*) - \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}\right] + R_p(f_p^*) \le \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) + (p - 1)\left[E(g) - \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])\right] + R_p(g).$$
4. Because $\|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \ge \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$, the zeroth-order difference $\|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} - \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \ge 0$ is non-negative. Subtracting this non-negative quantity gives:
   $$(p - 1)\left[E(f_p^*) - \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_p^*\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}\right] + R_p(f_p^*) \le (p - 1)\left[E(g) - \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])\right] + R_p(g).$$
5. Dividing by $p - 1 > 0$ and letting $p \to 1^+$ along any sequence converging to $f_1^* \in \mathcal{M}_1$:
   $$E(f_1^*) - \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \le E(g) - \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \implies E(f_1^*) \le E(g).$$
6. Thus, every cluster point $f_1^*$ minimizes $E(f)$ on $\mathcal{M}_1$.

### 3.4 Orthant Localization and Strict Convexity of Weighted Entropy
1. No two $L^1$-optimal residuals $r_1, r_2 \in \{r_f : f \in \mathcal{M}_1\}$ can have opposite nonzero signs on any coordinate: if $(r_1)_e > 0$ and $(r_2)_e < 0$, then $|\frac{1}{2}(r_1)_e + \frac{1}{2}(r_2)_e| < \frac{1}{2}|(r_1)_e| + \frac{1}{2}|(r_2)_e|$.
2. With positive weights $w_e > 0$, the midpoint would satisfy $\|\frac{1}{2}(r_1 + r_2)\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} < \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$, which is inconsistent with the definition of $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$.
3. Therefore, all optimal residuals in $\{r_f : f \in \mathcal{M}_1\}$ lie in a single closed coordinate orthant.
4. On $[0, \infty)$, the scalar function $\eta(t) = t \log t$ is strictly convex with $\eta''(t) = 1/t > 0$ for $t > 0$.
5. Because $d_0|_F$ is injective, $f \mapsto r_f$ is an affine injection. Hence $E(f)$ is strictly convex on $\mathcal{M}_1$.
6. A strictly convex continuous function on a non-empty compact convex set attains its minimum at an unconditionally unique point $f_{\text{ent}}^* \in \mathcal{M}_1$.
7. Because every cluster point of the compact trajectory $\{f_p^*\}_{p \in (1, 2]}$ must equal $f_{\text{ent}}^*$, the entire trajectory converges: $\lim_{p \to 1^+} f_p^* = f_{\text{ent}}^*$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Exponent Regime**: Applies to the one-sided limit $p \to 1^+$. It does not address the limit $p \to \infty$ (Chebyshev selection).
- **Graph Carrier**: Formulated for connected finite graphs with strictly positive weights; does not generalize to infinite graphs or graphs with zero/negative weights.
- **Physical Non-Claim**: Secondary entropy selection is a variational selection mechanism for non-strictly convex finite optimization problems; it does not represent thermodynamic entropy generation or physical dissipation.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports convergence of the unique $p$-Laplacian minimizer trajectory $f_p^*$ to the unique weighted entropy minimizer $f_{\text{ent}}^*$ on the $L^1$ optimal face $\mathcal{M}_1$ as $p \to 1^+$ for finite connected graphs with positive weights.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support uniqueness of potentials without gauge fixing; does not support selection on non-compact domains or under zero/negative weights.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For a finite connected graph with positive weights and root gauge fixing, the $p$-Dirichlet minimizers converge as $p \to 1^+$ to the unique minimizer of $\sum w_e |r_e| \log |r_e|$ on $\mathcal{M}_1$.
