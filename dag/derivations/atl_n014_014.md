## Claim

On a finite index set, if local colour is constant on relation $\sim$ and the joint (local, global) colour is not, some $i\sim j$ has equal local colour and different verdict.

## Hypotheses

- finite index set
- $\ell(i)=\ell(j)$ whenever $i\sim j$

## Derivation

Non-constancy of $(\ell,g)$ on $\sim$ gives $i\sim j$ with $(\ell(i),g(i))\ne(\ell(j),g(j))$; since $\ell(i)=\ell(j)$, $g(i)\ne g(j)$.
