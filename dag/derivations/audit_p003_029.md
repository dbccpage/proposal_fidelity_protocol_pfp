## 1. Claim Statement

In the Paper 003 Appendix A oracle module (`atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/code/detector_lemmas.py`), the exact threshold and signal helpers (`positive_sum`, `hill_activates`, `sum_threshold_activates`, `minimum_contributors`, and `gate`) reject Boolean and floating-point values, non-integer Hill exponents, and non-positive scales or bounds with `LemmaInputError`, while the panel and predicate helpers (`admitted`, `fires_on_self`, `sound_for_every_panel`, and `rejects_no_harmless_rule`) reject Boolean, floating-point, or negative bitmasks and reject predicate masks carrying bits at or above `1 << universe_bits`.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Exact Type-Identity Primitives (`_count` and `_exact`)
`detector_lemmas.py` defines `LemmaInputError(TypeError)` (line 38) and two low-level guards:
1. `_count(value, label)` (lines 42–45) checks `if type(value) is not int or value < 0: raise LemmaInputError(...)`. Because `type(True) is bool`, `_count` rejects `True` and `False` as well as `float`, `Fraction`, and negative integers.
2. `_exact(value, label)` (lines 48–53) admits only `type(value) is Fraction` or `type(value) is int` (converted via `Fraction(value)`), raising `LemmaInputError` on `bool`, `float`, strings, or `None`.

### 3.2 Threshold, Exponent, Scale, and Signal Refusals
Inspection and execution of `detector_lemmas.py` (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestExactHelperInputBoundaries::test_threshold_helpers_reject_boolean_and_inexact_coefficients_and_signals`) establish:
- **Hill coefficient `n`**: Both `hill_activates` (lines 124–125) and `sum_threshold_activates` (lines 137–138) enforce `type(n) is not int or n < 1`, raising `LemmaInputError` on `True`, `False`, `1.5`, `Fraction(3, 2)`, `0`, and `-1`.
- **Half-activation scale `k` and per-sensor `bound`**: `hill_activates` (lines 126–128), `sum_threshold_activates` (lines 139–141), and `minimum_contributors` (lines 151–153) pass `k` and `bound` through `_exact` and require strict positivity (`> 0`), raising `LemmaInputError` on `2.0`, `True`, `False`, `0`, and `-1`.
- **Threshold `t` and signal entries**: `positive_sum` (lines 111–118) applies `_exact` to every entry of `signals`, rejecting `[0.5]` and `[True]`; `t` is validated by `_exact(t, "t")` (rejecting `0.5` and `True`), and `sum_threshold_activates` (lines 142–143) additionally enforces `0 < t_exact < 1` (rejecting `t = 0` and `t = 1`).
- **Two-signal count helper**: `gate` (lines 65–72) applies `_count` to `signal1_count` and `defect_count`, rejecting `gate(True, None)` and `gate(1, ("HIGH", True))`.

### 3.3 Panel Bitmask and Universe-Bound Refusals
Inspection and execution of the Lemma C bitmask helpers (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestExactHelperInputBoundaries::test_panel_helpers_reject_boolean_float_and_negative_masks` and `::test_panel_soundness_helpers_reject_bits_outside_declared_universe`) establish:
- **Entry-point mask guards**: `admitted(screened, panel)` (lines 169–171) and `fires_on_self(executed, panel)` (lines 174–176) validate both arguments via `_count`, raising `LemmaInputError` on `True`, `False`, `1.0`, `0.5`, `Fraction(1, 2)`, and `-1`.
- **Declared universe bit bound**: `_validate_predicates(executed, screened, universe_bits)` (lines 162–166), called at the start of `sound_for_every_panel` (line 181) and `rejects_no_harmless_rule` (line 190), computes `size = _count(universe_bits, "universe_bits")` (rejecting `universe_bits = True`, `2.0`, or `-1`) and checks `_count(value, name) >= 1 << size`. For `universe_bits = 2` (`1 << 2 == 4`), passing `executed = 4` or `screened = 4` (bit index $2 \ge 2$) raises `LemmaInputError` (`"... has bits outside the declared universe"`).
- **Valid exact domain control**: Valid exact `int`/`Fraction` parameters and maximum in-universe masks `(1 << size) - 1` execute without raising (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestExactHelperInputBoundaries::test_falsification_valid_exact_inputs_and_in_range_masks_succeed`).

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Package Oracle Scope**: Validation applies to `code/detector_lemmas.py` within the Paper 003 deposit; it does not audit input parsing across unrelated repository subsystems.
2. **Unbounded Two-Argument Bitmask Helpers**: `admitted(screened, panel)` and `fires_on_self(executed, panel)` validate non-negative integer bitmasks via `_count` without taking a `universe_bits` parameter; universe-size truncation checks reside specifically in `sound_for_every_panel` and `rejects_no_harmless_rule`.
3. **Integer Hill Exponent Restriction**: While the mathematical proof of Lemma B holds for real $n > 0$, `hill_activates` and `sum_threshold_activates` deliberately restrict `n` to positive `int` values so exact `Fraction` powers avoid irrational roots.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that the threshold, count, and bitmask helpers in `code/detector_lemmas.py` raise `LemmaInputError` on `bool`, `float`, fractional Hill exponents, non-positive scales/bounds, negative masks, and predicate bits at or above `1 << universe_bits`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not establish a repository-wide runtime input audit or exact rational evaluation for non-integer Hill exponents.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: `code/detector_lemmas.py` enforces exact `int`/`Fraction` type-identity guards via `_count` and `_exact` and enforces `mask < (1 << universe_bits)` in `sound_for_every_panel` and `rejects_no_harmless_rule`.
4. **Next test needed**: Property-based fuzzing over arbitrary Python scalar and container types (`Decimal`, `complex`, `bytes`, `numpy` scalars) to verify every entry point in `detector_lemmas.py` fails closed with `LemmaInputError`.
