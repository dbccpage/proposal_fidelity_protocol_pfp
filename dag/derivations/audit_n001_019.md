## 1. Claim Statement

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be an $N$-dimensional Hilbert space with orthonormal basis $\{|v\rangle\}_{v \in V}$, and let $\rho$ be a density operator on $\mathcal{H}$ ($\rho = \rho^\dagger$, $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho) = 1$, $\rho \succeq 0$). Let $G_\rho = (V, E_\rho)$ be the support graph of non-vanishing off-diagonal coherences, with $E_\rho = \{(u, v) \in V \times V : u \ne v,\, \rho_{uv} \ne 0\}$. Under these hypotheses:

1. The trace distance between $\rho$ and its diagonal dephased state $\rho_{\text{diag}} = \sum_{v \in V} \rho_{vv} |v\rangle\langle v|$ is bounded by half the total $\ell_1$-norm of off-diagonal coherence:
   $$\|\rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}\|_{\text{tr}} = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Tr}|\rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}| \le \sum_{u < v} |\rho_{uv}| = \frac{1}{2} C_{\ell_1}(\rho),$$
   where $C_{\ell_1}(\rho) = \sum_{u \ne v} |\rho_{uv}|$.
2. For each edge $(u, v) \in E_\rho$, positive semidefiniteness enforces the principal minor inequality:
   $$|\rho_{uv}| \le \sqrt{\rho_{uu} \rho_{vv}} \le \frac{1}{2}(\rho_{uu} + \rho_{vv}).$$
3. For any chosen real phase lift $\boldsymbol{\omega} \in C^1(E_\rho; \mathbb{R})$ satisfying $\rho_{uv} = |\rho_{uv}| e^{i \omega(u, v)}$, and for any nonzero cycle circulation $C \in Z_1(G_\rho; \mathbb{R})$, the $L^1$ quotient norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ satisfies the lower bound:
   $$\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \ge \frac{|\langle C, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle|}{M_C}, \quad \text{where } M_C = \max_{e \in E_\rho: C_e \ne 0} \frac{|C_e|}{w_e}.$$
4. Under diagonal unitary transformations $U_\theta = \operatorname{diag}(e^{-i \theta_v})$, entries transform as $(U_\theta \rho U_\theta^\dagger)_{uv} = e^{i (d_0 \theta)_{uv}} \rho_{uv}$. Principal branch argument extraction $\operatorname{Arg}(\cdot) \in (-\pi, \pi]$ introduces integer period shifts $\mathbf{k} \in \mathbb{Z}^{E_\rho}$, demonstrating that the real quotient norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ is lift-dependent under branch-cut wrapping, while the multiplicative $U(1)$ cycle holonomy:
   $$\operatorname{hol}_{U(1)}(C) = \prod_{(u, v) \in C} \left( \frac{\rho_{uv}}{|\rho_{uv}|} \right)^{C_e} = \exp(i \langle C, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle)$$
   is invariant under all diagonal unitaries.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Hilbert Space and State**: Finite dimension $N = |V| \ge 2$; state operator $\rho$ satisfies Hermiticity, unit trace, and positive semidefiniteness.
- **Support Graph**: $G_\rho = (V, E_\rho)$ records edges with $\rho_{uv} \ne 0$. Edges with vanishing coherences are excluded because their complex argument is undefined.
- **Trace Distance**: $\|\rho - \sigma\|_{\text{tr}} = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Tr}\sqrt{(\rho - \sigma)^\dagger (\rho - \sigma)}$.
- **Quotient Space**: $Q^1(G_\rho) = C^1(G_\rho; \mathbb{R}) / \operatorname{im}(d_0)$, with norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) = \inf_{f \in C^0(G_\rho)} \|\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Trace Distance and Off-Diagonal Coherence Bound
1. Let $\Delta = \rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}$. Then $\Delta$ is hollow (all diagonal entries are zero) and Hermitian ($\Delta_{uv} = \overline{\Delta_{vu}}$).
2. The trace norm is the Schatten-1 norm: $\|\Delta\|_{\text{tr}} = \frac{1}{2} \|\Delta\|_{S_1} = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{j=1}^N \sigma_j(\Delta)$, where $\sigma_j$ are the singular values.
3. For any matrix $A$, the Schatten-1 norm is bounded by the entrywise $\ell_1$ norm:
   $$\|A\|_{S_1} \le \sum_{u, v} |A_{uv}|.$$
4. Applying this to $\Delta$:
   $$\operatorname{Tr}|\Delta| = \|\Delta\|_{S_1} \le \sum_{u, v} |\Delta_{uv}| = \sum_{u \ne v} |\rho_{uv}| = C_{\ell_1}(\rho).$$
5. Multiplying by $1/2$ yields:
   $$\|\rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}\|_{\text{tr}} \le \frac{1}{2} C_{\ell_1}(\rho) = \sum_{u < v} |\rho_{uv}|.$$
6. For each pair $(u, v)$, the $2 \times 2$ principal submatrix of $\rho$ is:
   $$\begin{pmatrix} \rho_{uu} & \rho_{uv} \\ \rho_{vu} & \rho_{vv} \end{pmatrix}.$$
   Positive semidefiniteness implies non-negativity of its determinant: $\rho_{uu} \rho_{vv} - |\rho_{uv}|^2 \ge 0$, which yields $|\rho_{uv}| \le \sqrt{\rho_{uu} \rho_{vv}}$. The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality then gives $\sqrt{\rho_{uu} \rho_{vv}} \le \frac{1}{2}(\rho_{uu} + \rho_{vv})$.

### 3.2 Dual Circulation Lower Bound on Quotient Norm
1. For any edge weights $\mathbf{w} \in (0, \infty)^{E_\rho}$, the weighted $L^1$ quotient norm is:
   $$\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) = \inf_{f \in C^0(G_\rho)} \sum_{e \in E_\rho} w_e |(\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f)_e|.$$
2. By linear programming duality, the dual formulation is:
   $$\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) = \max_{\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^{E_\rho}} \langle \mathbf{z}, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle \quad \text{subject to } d_0^\top \mathbf{z} = \mathbf{0}, \quad |z_e| \le w_e \quad \forall e \in E_\rho.$$
3. Let $C \in Z_1(G_\rho; \mathbb{R})$ be any nonzero cycle circulation, so $d_0^\top C = \mathbf{0}$.
4. Define the scaling factor $M_C = \max_{e \in E_\rho: C_e \ne 0} \frac{|C_e|}{w_e} > 0$.
5. Consider the normalized vector $\mathbf{z} = \operatorname{sgn}(\langle C, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle) \frac{C}{M_C}$.
6. Then $d_0^\top \mathbf{z} = \mathbf{0}$, and for every edge $e$, $|z_e| = \frac{|C_e|}{M_C} \le w_e$.
7. Thus $\mathbf{z}$ is dual-feasible. By weak duality:
   $$\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \ge \langle \mathbf{z}, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle = \frac{|\langle C, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle|}{M_C}.$$

### 3.3 Unitary Transformation Sign, Branch Winding, and $U(1)$ Holonomy
1. Let $U_\theta = \sum_{v \in V} e^{-i \theta_v} |v\rangle\langle v|$. The rotated matrix entries are:
   $$(U_\theta \rho U_\theta^\dagger)_{uv} = \langle u | U_\theta \rho U_\theta^\dagger | v \rangle = e^{-i \theta_u} \rho_{uv} e^{i \theta_v} = e^{i (\theta_v - \theta_u)} \rho_{uv}.$$
2. Under the incidence convention $(d_0 \theta)(u, v) = \theta_v - \theta_u$, the phase shifts by $+(d_0 \theta)_{uv}$.
3. When extracting principal arguments in $(-\pi, \pi]$, if $\theta_v - \theta_u + \omega(u, v)$ leaves $(-\pi, \pi]$, an integer multiple of $2\pi$ is added:
   $$\operatorname{Arg}((U_\theta \rho U_\theta^\dagger)_{uv}) = \omega(u, v) + (d_0 \theta)_{uv} + 2\pi k_{uv}, \quad k_{uv} \in \mathbb{Z}.$$
4. In the 3-cycle setting with $\boldsymbol{\omega} = (0, 0, 0)$ and rotation $\theta = (0, 3\pi/4, 3\pi/2)$:
   - $(d_0 \theta)_{01} = 3\pi/4$, $(d_0 \theta)_{12} = 3\pi/4$, $(d_0 \theta)_{20} = -3\pi/2$.
   - Principal branch wrapping shifts $-3\pi/2$ to $\pi/2 = -3\pi/2 + 2\pi$ (with $k_{20} = 1$).
   - The resulting principal cochain is $(3\pi/4, 3\pi/4, \pi/2)$ with cycle sum $2\pi$, producing $\Phi_1 = 2\pi$.
5. However, computing the multiplicative holonomy:
   $$\exp\left( i \sum_{e \in C} \omega'_e \right) = \exp(i \cdot 2\pi) = 1.0,$$
   which matches the original holonomy $\exp(i \cdot 0) = 1.0$.
6. This establishes that the real lift quotient norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ depends on the chosen real branch, whereas the multiplicative $U(1)$ holonomy is gauge-invariant under all diagonal unitaries.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Static Combinatorial Setting**: This derivation establishes static inequalities for density matrix entries on finite graphs; it does not model Hamiltonian time evolution or decoherence dynamics.
- **Branch Dependence**: The real quotient norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ is defined for a fixed real lift $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ and is not invariant under integer $2\pi$ branch wrapping; only the multiplicative $U(1)$ holonomy and the extended integer-shift quotient $\Phi_1^{U(1)}$ possess full diagonal unitary invariance.
- **No Continuous Limit**: Results apply to finite-dimensional systems and do not address infinite-dimensional fields.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the trace distance coherence bound $\|\rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}\|_{\text{tr}} \le \frac{1}{2} C_{\ell_1}(\rho)$, dual circulation lower bounds for fixed real lifts, and the distinction between lift-dependent real quotient norms and gauge-invariant $U(1)$ holonomy.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support unitary invariance of real lifts without integer period minimization; does not model dynamical quantum processes.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For a finite-dimensional density operator on a support graph of non-zero coherences, off-diagonal trace distance is bounded by $C_{\ell_1}(\rho)/2$, and dual circulations lower-bound $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ for any fixed real lift.
4. **Next test needed**:
   Verification of branch cut winding versus multiplicative $U(1)$ holonomy under diagonal unitary conjugation.
