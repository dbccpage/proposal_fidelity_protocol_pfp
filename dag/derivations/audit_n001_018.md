## 1. Claim Statement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite directed graph with strictly positive edge weights $\mathbf{w} \in (0, \infty)^E$, and let $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{C}^d$ be a finite-dimensional Hilbert space. For operator-valued cochains $H \in C^1(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))$ taking values in the Schatten-$p$ ideal with $p \in [1, \infty]$:

1. The Schatten-$p$ quotient defect:
   $$\Phi_p([H]) = \inf_{F \in C^0(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))} \|H - d_0 F\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \inf_{F \in C^0(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))} \left( \sum_{e \in E} w_e \|H_e - (d_0 F)_e\|_{S_p}^p \right)^{1/p}$$
   satisfies non-commutative weak duality:
   $$|\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H \rangle| \le \Phi_p([H])$$
   for every dual operator circulation $Y \in C_1(G, \mathcal{B}_q(\mathcal{H}))$ satisfying the divergence-free condition $d_0^* Y = 0$ and dual norm constraint $\|Y\|_{q, \mathbf{w}^*} \le 1$, where $1/p + 1/q = 1$ and $\mathbf{w}^*$ denotes conjugate weights.
2. For any operator $A \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$, non-commutative Hölder duality $|\operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger B)| \le \|A\|_{S_p} \|B\|_{S_q}$ is sharply attained by the polar dual maximizer $B_{\text{opt}} = U \Sigma^{p-1} V^\dagger / \|\Sigma\|_p^{p-1}$ when $A = U \Sigma V^\dagger$.
3. For any $d \times d$ density matrix $\rho$ with diagonal pointer state $\rho_{\text{diag}}$ and off-diagonal coherence matrix $A = \rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}$:
   - For $1 \le p \le 2$: $\|A\|_{S_p} \le \left( \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}|^p \right)^{1/p}$.
   - For $2 \le p \le \infty$: $\|A\|_{S_p} \le d^{1 - 2/p} \left( \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}|^p \right)^{1/p}$.
   In particular, at $p = 1$, the trace distance is bounded by the total off-diagonal $\ell_1$ coherence: $\|\rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}\|_1 \le C_{\ell_1}(\rho) = \sum_{u \ne v} |\rho_{uv}|$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Graph Topology**: Finite directed graph $G = (V, E)$ with $|V| = n, |E| = m$.
- **Hilbert Space**: $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{C}^d$ with standard trace inner product $\langle A, B \rangle_{\operatorname{Tr}} = \operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger B)$.
- **Schatten-$p$ Norm**: $\|A\|_{S_p} = (\operatorname{Tr}(|A|^p))^{1/p}$ for $p \in [1, \infty)$, and $\|A\|_{S_\infty} = \|A\|_{\text{op}}$, where $|A| = \sqrt{A^\dagger A}$.
- **Conjugate Exponents**: $p, q \in [1, \infty]$ with $1/p + 1/q = 1$.
- **Operator Coboundary**: For 0-cochain $F \in C^0(G, \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}))$, $(d_0 F)_e = F_v - F_u$ for edge $e = (u, v)$.
- **Operator Adjoint Divergence**: $(d_0^* Y)_u = \sum_{e=(u, v)} Y_e - \sum_{e=(v, u)} Y_e$.
- **Circulation Space**: $\ker d_0^* = \{Y \in C_1(G, \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})) : d_0^* Y = 0\}$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Non-Commutative Hölder Inequality and Polar Attainment
1. Let $A \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$ have singular value decomposition $A = U \Sigma V^\dagger$ with singular values $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots \ge \sigma_d \ge 0$.
2. By the non-commutative Hölder inequality (von Neumann / Schatten), for any $B \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$:
   $$|\operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger B)| \le \sum_{i=1}^d \sigma_i(A) \sigma_i(B) \le \|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p} \|\sigma(B)\|_{\ell^q} = \|A\|_{S_p} \|B\|_{S_q}.$$
3. When $A \ne 0$, define the candidate dual operator:
   $$B_{\text{opt}} = U \operatorname{diag}\left( \frac{\sigma_i^{p-1}}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{p-1}} \right) V^\dagger.$$
4. Its singular values are $\sigma_i(B_{\text{opt}}) = \sigma_i^{p-1} / \|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{p-1}$.
5. Computing the Schatten-$q$ norm of $B_{\text{opt}}$ (recalling $q(p-1) = p$):
   $$\|B_{\text{opt}}\|_{S_q}^q = \sum_{i=1}^d \frac{\sigma_i^{q(p-1)}}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{q(p-1)}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^d \sigma_i^p}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^p} = 1 \implies \|B_{\text{opt}}\|_{S_q} = 1.$$
6. Evaluating the trace pairing:
   $$\operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger B_{\text{opt}}) = \operatorname{Tr}\left( V \Sigma U^\dagger U \frac{\Sigma^{p-1}}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{p-1}} V^\dagger \right) = \frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\Sigma^p)}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{p-1}} = \frac{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^p}{\|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p}^{p-1}} = \|\sigma(A)\|_{\ell^p} = \|A\|_{S_p}.$$
7. This achieves equality in the non-commutative Hölder inequality.

### 3.2 Operator Cochain Weak Duality
1. Let $H \in C^1(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))$ and let $Y \in C_1(G, \mathcal{B}_q(\mathcal{H}))$ satisfy the divergence-free condition $d_0^* Y = 0$.
2. For any operator potential $F \in C^0(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))$, consider the pairing:
   $$\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H - d_0 F \rangle = \sum_{e \in E} \operatorname{Tr}(Y_e^\dagger (H_e - (d_0 F)_e)) = \sum_{e \in E} \operatorname{Tr}(Y_e^\dagger H_e) - \sum_{e \in E} \operatorname{Tr}(Y_e^\dagger (d_0 F)_e).$$
3. By adjoint rearrangement across the directed incidence structure:
   $$\sum_{e=(u, v)} \operatorname{Tr}(Y_e^\dagger (F_v - F_u)) = \sum_{v \in V} \operatorname{Tr}\left( \left( \sum_{e=(\cdot, v)} Y_e - \sum_{e=(v, \cdot)} Y_e \right)^\dagger F_v \right) = -\sum_{v \in V} \operatorname{Tr}((d_0^* Y)_v^\dagger F_v).$$
4. Because $d_0^* Y = 0$, this boundary term vanishes identically:
   $$\sum_{e \in E} \operatorname{Tr}(Y_e^\dagger (d_0 F)_e) = 0 \implies \operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H - d_0 F \rangle = \operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H \rangle.$$
5. Applying non-commutative Hölder on each edge with weights $w_e > 0$:
   $$|\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H \rangle| = |\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H - d_0 F \rangle| \le \sum_{e \in E} \|Y_e\|_{S_q} \|H_e - (d_0 F)_e\|_{S_p}.$$
6. Rewriting with weights $w_e^{1/p} \cdot w_e^{-1/p} = 1$:
   $$\sum_{e \in E} w_e^{-1/p} \|Y_e\|_{S_q} \cdot w_e^{1/p} \|H_e - (d_0 F)_e\|_{S_p} \le \left( \sum_{e \in E} w_e^{-q/p} \|Y_e\|_{S_q}^q \right)^{1/q} \left( \sum_{e \in E} w_e \|H_e - (d_0 F)_e\|_{S_p}^p \right)^{1/p}.$$
7. Since $\|Y\|_{q, \mathbf{w}^*} \le 1$, taking the infimum over all $F \in C^0(G, \mathcal{B}_p(\mathcal{H}))$ yields:
   $$|\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H \rangle| \le \Phi_p([H]).$$

### 3.3 Off-Diagonal Coherence Bounds
1. Let $A = \rho - \rho_{\text{diag}}$ have zero diagonal ($A_{uu} = 0$).
2. For $p = 1$, by the triangle inequality on $2 \times 2$ blocks or polar decomposition:
   $$\|A\|_{S_1} = \operatorname{Tr}|A| \le \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}| = C_{\ell_1}(\rho).$$
3. For $p = 2$, the Schatten-2 norm is the Frobenius norm:
   $$\|A\|_{S_2} = \sqrt{\operatorname{Tr}(A^\dagger A)} = \left( \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}|^2 \right)^{1/2}.$$
4. By complex interpolation (Riesz-Thorin / Clarkson), for all $1 \le p \le 2$:
   $$\|A\|_{S_p} \le \left( \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}|^p \right)^{1/p}.$$
5. For $p \ge 2$, singular values satisfy $\|A\|_{S_p} \le \|A\|_{S_2} = \|A\|_{\ell^2}$. By discrete Hölder on the $d(d-1)$ off-diagonal entries:
   $$\|A\|_{\ell^2} \le (d(d-1))^{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{p}} \|A\|_{\ell^p} \le d^{1 - 2/p} \left( \sum_{u \ne v} |A_{uv}|^p \right)^{1/p}.$$

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Finite Dimension**: Formulated for finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{C}^d$. Generalization to infinite-dimensional trace-class and Hilbert-Schmidt ideals requires topology and domain considerations.
- **Static vs. Dynamic Non-Claim**: These bounds represent kinematic properties of off-diagonal coherences on finite graphs. They do not constitute dynamical master equations (Lindblad / GKLS) or continuous time-evolution invariants.
- **Duality Attainment**: Weak duality holds unconditionally for all dual circulations; strong duality attainment requires optimal dual witness construction via operator polar decomposition.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports non-commutative weak Hölder duality $|\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\langle Y, H \rangle| \le \Phi_p([H])$ for divergence-free operator circulations, exact polar witness attainment, and off-diagonal coherence bounds across $p \in [1, \infty]$ on finite graphs.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support gauge invariance for non-divergence-free test fields; does not support omission of dimension scaling factors $d^{1 - 2/p}$ for $p > 2$.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For finite-dimensional operator cochains, pairing with a unit-norm divergence-free dual circulation lower-bounds the Schatten-$p$ quotient defect, and off-diagonal coherence bounds hold with the stated dimension factors.
