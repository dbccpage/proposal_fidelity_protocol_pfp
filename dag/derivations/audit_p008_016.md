## 1. Claim Statement

Across the eight archived $K_4$ session traces in `docs/source_archive/k4_traces/` (`n = 1` trace per `{qwen/qwen-2.5-7b-instruct, meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct} × {baseline, partially_masked, scratchpad, hinted}` cell at temperature $0.0$ and 12-turn cap), every file matches the byte length, SHA-256 digest, and Git blob SHA-1 recorded in `docs/source_archive/source_index.json` (`revision = "52cb5457f6c485ba548cfd292f6012a941ed22cf"`). Extracting all `CUT` proposals and replaying exact weighted $L^1$ coboundary distance $\Phi_1$ across every turn matches 100% of embedded host prefix logs (`Turns 1..T-1`) and confirms that the tree-preserving coboundary repair `{"a->b": 1, "b->c": 2, "c->a": -3, "b->d": -3, "d->a": 2, "c->d": -5}` appears in `scratchpad` and `hinted` (whose prompts embed the target chord values `-3, -3, -5`) and in neither `baseline` nor `partially_masked`, while unassisted runs terminate at $\Phi_1 = 8, 6$ (Qwen) and $\Phi_1 = 5, 7$ (Llama).

In `docs/source_archive/source_index.json` (`capture_review_base = "23893fc44162555fa12ceda3e1665982e3d7a14e"`), eight entries under `docs/source_archive/k4_traces/` bind origin paths from `research/mssn_sn_tower/demos/demo_1/traces/` at `revision = "52cb5457f6c485ba548cfd292f6012a941ed22cf"`. Recomputing byte length, SHA-256, and Git blob SHA-1 (`blob <len>\0<bytes>`) over the admitted archive bytes matches all eight entries (`5f0d9fcd...`, `fda84228...`, `26bba61c...`, `5701d90e...`, `f8b4a22f...`, `9c62957c...`, `648934f0...`, `eea768f6...`), partitioning `{qwen/qwen-2.5-7b-instruct, meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct} × {baseline, partially_masked, scratchpad, hinted}` with one trace per cell ($n = 1$).

### 3.2 Raw Row Schema, Unstored Final Turn Result, and Prompt-Supplied Targets
Every JSONL row (`schema = "symbol_game.trace.v2"`) logs the pre-dispatch state (`phi1_before: null`, `phi1_after: null`, `admission_status: "pending"`, `admitted: false`). Host outcomes for Turns $1 \dots t-1$ are serialized in Turn $t$'s `user_turn` history block (`Turn k: <MOVE> -> <Status>. Phi_1=<int>.`), leaving the final row $T$ with $T - 1$ embedded history lines and no stored post-move host record.

Inspecting `user_turn` text across conditions after the six edges are asserted confirms the prompt-assistance boundary:
- **`baseline` and `partially_masked`**: Neither prompt contains `"(-2) - 1 = -3"`, `"(-2) - 3 = -5"`, `"target=-3"`, `"target=-5"`, or `'"c->d": -5'`.
- **`scratchpad`**: Supplies spanning-tree potentials $f(a)=0, f(b)=1, f(c)=3, f(d)=-2$ together with worked arithmetic containing `"(-2) - 1 = -3"` and `"(-2) - 3 = -5"`.
- **`hinted`**: Supplies `"target=-3"`, `"target=-5"`, and a complete JSON `CUT` repair payload containing `'"c->d": -5'`.

### 3.3 Exact Weighted $L^1$ Replay and Proposal Extraction
Evaluating $\Phi_1(c)$ over all $\binom{6}{3} = 20$ edge triples ($16$ spanning trees of $K_4$) gives initial defect $\Phi_1(1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 1) = 8$ and tree-preserving coboundary repair $c^* = \{a\to b: 1,\; b\to c: 2,\; c\to a: -3,\; b\to d: -3,\; d\to a: 2,\; c\to d: -5\}$ with $\Phi_1(c^*) = 0$. Replaying every `parsed_move` matches 100% of embedded prefix lines (`Turns 1..T-1`) across all eight traces and yields:

Here the benchmark loop count reproduces the historical script rule (`paper.md` lines 606–610): a turn increments the counter iff its host status is `IdempotentStationary`, `DefectIncrease`, or `MoveViolation`, or it is an `ASSERT` move at turn index $> 6$.

### 3.4 Partial `CUT` Zero-Claim Loophole and Single-Chord Monotonicity Controls
1. **Partial `CUT` Omission Semantics**: On Llama `baseline` Turn 9 and `scratchpad` Turn 12, the model emits `{"c->a": 0, "b->d": 0, "c->d": 0}`, omitting tree edges `a->b`, `b->c`, and `d->a`. Because whole-map replacement defaults omitted edges to `0`, the host scores the all-zero cochain ($\Phi_1(0) = 0$) and emits `ExactRepair` / `IdempotentStationary`. Had the asserted tree claims $(1, 2, 2)$ been preserved alongside zero chords, minimum spanning-tree distance would be $\Phi_1 = 5 > 0$ (attained at zero potential via spanning tree $\{c\to a, b\to d, c\to d\}$ with edge residuals $1 + 2 + 2 = 5$).
2. **Single-Chord Monotonicity Refusal**: Updating any single chord of initial claims $(1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 1)$ to its coboundary target in isolation raises $\Phi_1$ above $8$—setting `c->a = -3` yields $\Phi_1 = 10$, `b->d = -3` yields $\Phi_1 = 12$, and `c->d = -5` yields $\Phi_1 = 14$—causing the $\Delta \Phi_1 \le 0$ gate to reject every single-chord step with `DefectIncrease`.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Single-Trial Sample ($n = 1$)**: One deterministic trace per model-condition cell on a single 4-vertex graph supports case-level replay only, not population-level model accuracy or convergence-rate estimates.
2. **Assisted Conditions**: Because `scratchpad` and `hinted` prompts contain `-3, -3, -5`, their zero-defect outcomes show reproduction of prompt-supplied values rather than unaided coboundary synthesis.
3. **Unstored Final Host Field and Zero-Wipe Acceptance**: Terminal $\Phi_1$ values come from deterministic host replay of Turn $T$ (`phi1_after` is `null` in raw JSONL rows), and host acceptance checks $\Phi_1 = 0$ on the proposed map without requiring preservation of earlier tree assertions.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Verifies byte-exact SHA-256 and Git blob custody for all eight $K_4$ traces, confirms prompt-supplied repair values in `scratchpad` and `hinted`, and reproduces all prefix logs and terminal $\Phi_1$ scores (`8, 6, 0, 0` for Qwen; `5, 7, 0, 0` for Llama) with $c^*$ proposed exclusively in `scratchpad` and `hinted`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not establish statistical rates beyond $n = 1$ per cell, unaided nullspace solving by 7B/8B models, or preservation of asserted tree edges on Llama `scratchpad` Turn 12.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: Across the eight hash-bound $K_4$ traces ($n = 1$ per cell), exact coboundary repair $c^*$ appears in `scratchpad` and `hinted` (whose prompts supply `-3, -3, -5`) and not in `baseline` or `partially_masked`, which end under exact host replay at $\Phi_1 = 8, 6$ (Qwen) and $5, 7$ (Llama) with no post-move host field stored on the final raw JSONL row.
4. **Next test needed**: Require `CUT` moves to declare values on all graph edges or enforce tree-anchor preservation when benchmarking coboundary repair, and persist post-move host receipts on every turn including Turn $T$.
