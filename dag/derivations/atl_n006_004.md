## Claim

On a finite connected graph without 2-cells, $\Phi_1(\omega)=0$ iff $\sum_{e\in C}\omega_e=0$ for every cycle $C$.

## Hypotheses

- finite connected graph
- attainment of $\Phi_1$

## Derivation

($\Rightarrow$) $\Phi_1=0$ and attainment give $\omega=d_0f$; cycle sums of $d_0f$ telescope to $0$. ($\Leftarrow$) integrate $\omega$ along a spanning tree to get $f$; each chord equation holds because its fundamental-cycle sum is $0$; so $\omega=d_0f$ and $\Phi_1=0$.
