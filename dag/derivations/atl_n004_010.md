## Claim

Curvature does not factor through the payload quotient when two payload-identical packets have different curvature.

## Hypotheses

- finite packet set
- payload map $q$
- curvature observable $K$

## Derivation

By fibre-constancy, $K$ factors through $q$ iff $q(x)=q(y)\Rightarrow K(x)=K(y)$; one payload-equal pair with $K(x)\ne K(y)$ refutes it, and when curvature is preserved by $q$ the quotient function is well defined.
