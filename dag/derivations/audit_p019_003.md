## 3. Step-by-Step Derivation

### Step 1 — Structural invariant on `IncumbentDecisionV1`

In `operads/domain/autopoiesis/research.py` (lines 200–227), `IncumbentDecisionV1.__post_init__` enforces:
1. `self.selected in (self.previous, self.proposed)` (`FOREIGN_INCUMBENT`, line 224), and
2. `if self.selected != self.previous and (not self.selection or not self.confirmation): raise ValueError("FRESH_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED")` (lines 225–226).

Thus no `IncumbentDecisionV1` instance can select `proposed != previous` with an empty selection or confirmation tuple.

### Step 2 — Freshness and binding checks in `decide` (`pciv/use_cases/research_selection.py:44-88`)

Given `(experiment, incumbent, candidate, selection, confirmation, *, controls_pass)`:
1. Every receipt in `all_rows = (*selection, *confirmation)` must match `(experiment.experiment_id, experiment.source, experiment.checker, experiment.split, experiment.configuration, experiment.budget_id)` (`FOREIGN_EVALUATION_BINDING`), belong to `{incumbent.candidate_id, candidate.candidate_id}` (`FOREIGN_CANDIDATE`), and carry exact `parent` and `experiment.inner_model` (`STALE_PARENT_OR_MODEL`, lines 46–67).
2. `candidate.parent == incumbent.candidate_id` is required (`STALE_CANDIDATE_PARENT`, lines 68–69).
3. `occurrences = [r.occurrence for r in all_rows]` must have `len(set(occurrences)) == len(occurrences)` (`CONFIRMATION_NOT_FRESH`, lines 70–72), preventing selection receipts from being replayed as confirmation receipts.
4. Replacement occurs (`selected = candidate.candidate_id`, `reason = "CONFIRMED_IMPROVEMENT"`) iff `controls_pass and improved(*split_rows(selection)) and improved(*split_rows(confirmation))` (lines 77–88); otherwise `incumbent.candidate_id` is retained (`RETAIN_INCUMBENT`).

### Step 3 — Paired Pareto dominance and per-family non-regression in `improved` (`lines 9-41`)

For a single split `(candidate_rows, incumbent_rows)`:
1. Empty lists return `False`; mismatched sorted `(task, family, repetition)` tuples raise `UNPAIRED_EVALUATIONS` (lines 19–23); any `"INCOMPLETE"` or `"INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR"` status returns `False` (lines 24–25).
2. Exact aggregate metrics `correctness = Fraction(sum(r.private_pass for r in rows), len(rows))` and `cost = sum(r.cost for r in rows)` are partitioned by `ParetoFrontSelectorV1().partition_fronts` and ordered by `(-correctness, cost)` (lines 26–34). Equal metrics (`c == i`) or incumbent membership ahead of candidate on front 0 returns `False` — i.e., `candidate` must strictly increase `correctness` or achieve equal `correctness` at strictly lower `cost`.
3. **Per-family non-regression guard** (lines 35–40): for every task `family` present in the split,

\[
\mathrm{correctness}_f(\mathrm{candidate}) < \mathrm{correctness}_f(\mathrm{incumbent}) \implies \mathrm{improved} = \mathrm{False}.
\]

Consequently, a candidate that raises aggregate correctness across two families (e.g., family `A`: \(0/2 \to 2/2\), family `B`: \(1/1 \to 0/1\), aggregate \(1/3 \to 2/3\)) is deterministically rejected (`RETAIN_INCUMBENT`), as is any candidate that improves on `selection` but fails to improve on fresh `confirmation`, or fails `controls_pass`.
