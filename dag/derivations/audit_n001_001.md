## 1. Claim Statement

For any finite graph $G = (V, E)$ with strictly positive edge weights $\mathbf{w} \in (0, \infty)^E$, any cochain $c \in C^1(G)$, and any exponent $p \in [1, \infty]$, the quotient infimum:
$$\Phi_p([c]) = \inf_{f \in C^0(G)} \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}$$
is attained at some potential $f^* \in C^0(G)$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Graph Topology**: $G = (V, E)$ is a finite 1-dimensional cell complex with $|V| \ge 1$ vertices and $|E| \ge 0$ edges.
- **Edge Weights**: Each edge $e \in E$ has an assigned weight $w_e > 0$.
- **Cochain Spaces**:
  - 0-cochains $C^0(G) = \mathbb{R}^V$ (vertex potentials).
  - 1-cochains $C^1(G) = \mathbb{R}^E$ (edge reports / differential forms).
- **Coboundary Operator**: $d_0: C^0(G) \to C^1(G)$ with $(d_0 f)_{uv} = f(v) - f(u)$ for oriented edge $(u, v)$.
- **Norm Definition**: For $r \in C^1(G)$:
  - $\|r\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \left( \sum_{e \in E} w_e |r_e|^p \right)^{1/p}$ for $p \in [1, \infty)$.
  - $\|r\|_{\infty, \mathbf{w}} = \max_{e \in E} w_e |r_e|$ for $p = \infty$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Gauge Fixing and Kernel Triviality
1. Let $G$ have $k$ connected components $C_1, \dots, C_k$.
2. The kernel of the coboundary operator $\ker d_0$ is the space of locally constant vertex potentials, having dimension $k$.
3. Fix a root vertex $v_j \in C_j$ for each component and define the gauge-fixed subspace:
   $$F = \{f \in C^0(G) : f(v_j) = 0 \text{ for each } j \in \{1, \dots, k\}\}.$$
4. The space $C^0(G)$ decomposes as a direct sum:
   $$C^0(G) = F \oplus \ker d_0.$$
5. The restricted coboundary operator $d_0|_F: F \to C^1(G)$ is strictly injective:
   if $d_0 f = 0$ for $f \in F$, then $f$ is constant on each component and vanishes at each root $v_j$, so $f \equiv 0$.

### 3.2 Coercivity on the Gauge-Fixed Subspace
1. The gauge-fixed space $F$ has finite dimension $|V| - k$.
2. In finite-dimensional vector spaces, all norms are equivalent, and an injective linear map is bounded below.
3. Therefore, for every $p \in [1, \infty]$ and positive weight vector $\mathbf{w}$, there exists a constant $c_{p, \mathbf{w}} > 0$ such that:
   $$\|d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \ge c_{p, \mathbf{w}} \|f\|_\infty \quad \text{for all } f \in F.$$
4. By the reverse triangle inequality on $C^1(G)$:
   $$\|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \ge \|d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} - \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \ge c_{p, \mathbf{w}} \|f\|_\infty - \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}.$$
5. Consequently, as $\|f\|_\infty \to \infty$ in $F$, the objective function $\|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \to \infty$. The objective is coercive on $F$.

### 3.3 Compactness of Sublevel Sets and Existence of Minimizers
1. Evaluate the objective at the origin $f = 0 \in F$:
   $$\|c - d_0(0)\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}.$$
2. Consider the sublevel set:
   $$S = \{f \in F : \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \le \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}\}.$$
3. For any $f \in S$:
   $$c_{p, \mathbf{w}} \|f\|_\infty - \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \le \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \le \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} \implies \|f\|_\infty \le \frac{2 \|c\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}}{c_{p, \mathbf{w}}}.$$
4. The sublevel set $S$ is closed (by continuity of $f \mapsto \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}$) and bounded in $F$, hence compact by the Heine-Borel theorem.
5. By the Weierstrass extreme value theorem, a continuous real-valued function on a non-empty compact set attains its global minimum.
6. Thus there exists $f^* \in S \subset F$ such that:
   $$\|c - d_0 f^*\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \min_{f \in F} \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}.$$
7. Because any $g \in C^0(G)$ decomposes as $g = f + k$ with $f \in F$ and $k \in \ker d_0$, and $d_0 g = d_0 f$, the infimum over $C^0(G)$ coincides with the minimum over $F$:
   $$\inf_{g \in C^0(G)} \|c - d_0 g\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \min_{f \in F} \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}} = \|c - d_0 f^*\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}.$$
8. The quotient infimum is attained.

### 3.4 Verification and Test Witness Reference
Supported by executable tests in `tests/test_note_001_quotient_norm.py`:
- `test_coercivity_constants_positive`: checks explicit coercivity constant $c_{p, w} > 0$ on triangle, cycle, and complete graph instances across $p \in \{1, 2, \infty\}$.
- `test_lp_attainment_triangle`: evaluates exact quotient infimum attainment for $p=1$ (simplex extreme point), $p=2$ (Moore-Penrose Laplacian pseudoinverse), and $p=\infty$ (Chebyshev active edge balance).
- `test_sublevel_set_boundedness`: demonstrates that all potentials with objective below $\|c\|_{p, w}$ lie strictly within the compact radius $\frac{2 \|c\|_{p, w}}{c_{p, w}}$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Finite Graphs Only**: The derivation holds for finite graphs. Infinite lattices, non-compact spaces, and continuous manifolds are not addressed.
- **Positive Weights**: Weights must be strictly positive ($w_e > 0$). If zero weights are permitted, coercivity on $F$ can fail.
- **No Uniqueness for $p=1$ or $p=\infty$**: The derivation establishes existence of a minimizer. For $p=1$ and $p=\infty$, minimizers are generally non-unique. Strict uniqueness holds for $p \in (1, \infty)$ by uniform convexity.
- **Classical Analysis**: The argument relies on finite-dimensional real analysis and compactness of sublevel sets.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that for any finite graph with positive edge weights and any $p \in [1, \infty]$, the quotient infimum $\inf_f \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}$ is attained on the gauge-fixed complement $F$ by compactness of sublevel sets under coercivity.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support claims of uniqueness for $p=1$ or $p=\infty$; does not extend to infinite complexes, continuous manifolds, or graphs with zero or negative edge weights.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For finite graphs with strictly positive weights and $p \in [1, \infty]$, the function $f \mapsto \|c - d_0 f\|_{p, \mathbf{w}}$ attains its infimum on $C^0(G)$.
