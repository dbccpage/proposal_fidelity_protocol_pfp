## 1. Claim Statement

The stored live pilot receipt `demos/demo_3/results/live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json` does not establish the frozen randomized study specified in Paper 003 Section 6.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Source Reference**:
  - Receipt file: `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/demos/demo_3/results/live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json`.
  - Manifest design: `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/demos/demo_3/experiment_manifest.yaml`.
  - Paper exposition: `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/paper.md`, Section 6 (lines 437–460) and Section 8 (lines 522–529).
  - Paper claim ledger: row P3-19 (line 34).
- **Hypotheses**:
  - The frozen experimental design is specified by Paper 003 Section 6 and the corresponding manifest.
  - The review report preserves the inventory SHA-256 of the receipt and records its LF Git blob SHA-256 separately. The regression uses the LF form so it applies on both Windows and Linux.
- **Obligations**:
  - Demonstration of any structural omission—such as missing experimental arms, unrandomized arm assignments, sample sizes below prespecified design thresholds, or absence of raw proposal text bindings—suffices to establish insufficiency.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Comparison of Experimental Arms
The frozen study design in Paper 003 Section 6 (lines 450–454) prespecifies six distinct experimental arms:
1. **P**: Plain proposal
2. **T**: Typed protocol
3. **C**: Structured coverage guidance
4. **R**: Generic reflection
5. **D**: Admitted detector policy with feedback
6. **A**: Solution-bearing assistance

Inspection of `live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json` (line 13–16) shows:
```json
"arms": [
  "D",
  "R"
]
```
Four of the six prespecified arms (`P`, `T`, `C`, and `A`) are entirely absent from the receipt.

### 3.2 Model Reserve Deficit
Paper 003 Section 6 (lines 445–446) requires reserving task families and a second model for held-out evaluation:
> "Reserve task families and a second model for held-out evaluation."

Inspection of the receipt (lines 3–5) demonstrates that only a single model was evaluated:
```json
"models": [
  "openai/gpt-4o-mini"
]
```
No secondary model or held-out model transfer evaluation is recorded.

### 3.3 Sample Size and Replicate Deficit
The manifest (`demos/demo_3/experiment_manifest.yaml`, line 60) prespecifies:
```yaml
pilot_repetitions_per_arm: 8
```
Furthermore, Section 7 derives that $n \ge 299$ independent opportunities are required to bring the 95% one-sided upper error bound below 1%.

In `live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json`:
- `total_trials`: 30.
- Arm allocations: $n_D = 15$ and $n_R = 15$ across 5 fixtures and 3 seeds (`42, 43, 44`).
- This allocates exactly 1 trial per (fixture, seed, arm) combination, rather than 8 independent replicates per condition.
- A sample size of $n = 15$ leaves an exact 95% Bernoulli upper bound of $1 - 0.05^{1/15} \approx 0.1810$ (exceeding 18%), which is insufficient for confirmatory statistical precision.

### 3.4 Missing Randomization Records
The manifest specifies:
```yaml
randomize_arm_order: true
```
The trial receipt contains no randomization seed for arm sequencing, no timestamped record of randomized arm assignment, and no counterbalancing schedule across model calls.

### 3.5 Incomplete Raw Byte Custody and Terminal Provenance
Paper 003 Section 8 (lines 522–528) documents:
> "The historical pilot file in this package, `demos/demo_3/results/live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json`, contains descriptive arm counts and provider fields for one model and two arms. Its raw request/source bindings do not establish the frozen randomized experiment above, and its trial records report terminal certificates from an earlier runner that the audited host cannot issue."

Direct inspection of `live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json` confirms:
1. Calls contain only truncated `raw_completion_snippet` text fields rather than full verbatim byte sequences.
2. Terminal records carry labels (`RestrictedSignGaugeRepair`, `RestrictedSignGaugeObstruction`) issued under an older schema (`symbol-game-demo3-experiment/live-matrix-receipt-v1`), which the audited canonical host rejects as authoritative.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Descriptive Pilot Value**: The receipt remains an informative preliminary engineering artifact documenting exploratory trial executions. The claim of insufficiency addresses only its adequacy as a confirmatory randomized study.
2. **No Claim on Model Competence**: Insufficiency of the trial receipt does not imply that the evaluated model (`gpt-4o-mini`) is incapable of solving the underlying fixtures under appropriate protocols.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Demonstrates that `live_openrouter_trial_receipt.json` lacks four of six experimental arms, omits a second model for held-out evaluation, has sample sizes below confirmatory thresholds, lacks arm order randomization logs, and withholds raw proposal byte bindings.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not support claims about model capability in general or the outcome of future confirmatory trials.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The historical pilot receipt does not satisfy the structural criteria of the frozen six-arm randomized study design.
4. **Next test needed**: Execution of a complete preregistered trial following all six arms with independent arm randomization and complete raw byte logging.
