## Claim

$(d_2K(s))(a,b,c,d)=K_{c,b,a}(r_{d,c}s_d)-K_{c,b,a}(s_c)$, vanishing when the lower triple is flat or $r_{d,c}s_d=s_c$.

## Hypotheses

- finite poset
- linear restrictions
- alternating coboundary convention of the source

## Derivation

Expand the alternating sum of $K(s)_{xyz}=K_{z,y,x}s_z$ over the four faces of $(a,b,c,d)$ and cancel with the four-term identity $r_{b,a}K_{d,c,b}+K_{d,b,a}=K_{d,c,a}+K_{c,b,a}r_{d,c}$; the residue is the stated difference, which vanishes if $K_{c,b,a}=0$ or $r_{d,c}s_d=s_c$.
