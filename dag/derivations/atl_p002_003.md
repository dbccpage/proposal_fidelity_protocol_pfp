## Claim

For a finite real matrix $H$ with $H_{ij}\le0$ for $i\ne j$ and $\beta\ge0$, $e^{-\beta H}$ is entrywise nonnegative.

## Hypotheses

- Finite real matrix
- Off-diagonal nonpositive
- $\beta\ge0$

## Derivation

Let $c=\max_i H_{ii}$ and $M=cI-H$, entrywise nonnegative. Then $e^{-\beta H}=e^{-\beta c}e^{\beta M}=e^{-\beta c}\sum_k \beta^kM^k/k!$, a convergent sum of nonnegative matrices.
