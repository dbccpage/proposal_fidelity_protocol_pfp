## Claim

Exact pairwise cancellation of a signed multiset into equal-weight pairs requires a fixed-point-free involution pairing opposite signs with equal magnitudes; nonnegative total alone does not suffice.

## Hypotheses

- Finite signed weights
- Cancellation means partition into zero-sum pairs

## Derivation

A zero-sum pair $\{a,b\}$ with $a+b=0$ has opposite signs and $|a|=|b|$; the pairing is an involution. For $\{+2,-1,-1\}$ the total is $0$ but no element equals $-2$, so no pairing exists.
