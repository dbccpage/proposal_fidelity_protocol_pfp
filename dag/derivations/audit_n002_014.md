## 1. Claim Statement

Let stalks of an operator-valued presheaf be finite-dimensional operator algebras $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ evaluated under the Schatten-1 trace norm. Every surjective trace-norm-preserving linear automorphism
$$T: \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n) \to \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$$
that is completely positive (CP) and preserves the positive state cone ($T(\mathcal{B}_1^+) \subseteq \mathcal{B}_1^+$) is a unitary conjugation:
$$T(A) = U A U^*,$$
for some unitary operator $U \in U(n)$. The faithful action on operator stalks is the projective unitary group:
$$PU(n) = U(n) / U(1).$$

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Trace-Class Stalks**: $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ denotes the space of $n \times n$ complex matrices equipped with the Schatten-1 trace norm $\|A\|_1 = \mathrm{Tr}\sqrt{A^* A}$.
- **Surjective Isometry**: $T: \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n) \to \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ is a surjective linear map satisfying $\|T(A)\|_1 = \|A\|_1$ for all $A$.
- **Kadison-Sourour Isometry Classification (Imported)**: Every surjective linear isometry on $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ has the form $T(A) = U A V$ or $T(A) = U A^T V$, where $U, V \in U(n)$ are unitary operators.
- **Complete Positivity (CP)**: For every integer $k \ge 1$, the tensor product map $I_k \otimes T: \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^k \otimes \mathbb{C}^n) \to \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^k \otimes \mathbb{C}^n)$ maps positive semidefinite operators to positive semidefinite operators.
- **State-Cone Preservation**: $T$ maps positive semidefinite operators to positive semidefinite operators ($T(A) \succeq 0$ whenever $A \succeq 0$). In particular, the identity operator $I$ is mapped into the positive semidefinite cone ($T(I) \succeq 0$).

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Kadison-Sourour Surjective Isometry Classification
1. By the Kadison isometry theorem and its extension to Schatten ideals by Sourour, every surjective linear isometry of the trace-class algebra $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ decomposes into one of two branches:
   - Untransposed branch: $T(A) = U A V$,
   - Transposed branch: $T(A) = U A^T V$,
   where $U$ and $V$ are unitary operators on $\mathbb{C}^n$.

### 3.2 Complete Positivity Excludes the Transpose Branch
1. Suppose $T$ belongs to the transposed branch: $T(A) = U A^T V$.
2. Test complete positivity by evaluating the amplified map on a bipartite system with $k = n \ge 2$.
3. Let $|\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{i=1}^n |i\rangle \otimes |i\rangle$ be the standard maximally entangled state vector, with rank-one density projector $\rho = |\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+| \succeq 0$.
4. The amplified transformation evaluates to:
   $$(I_n \otimes T)(\rho) = (I_n \otimes U) \cdot (I_n \otimes \mathrm{transpose})(\rho) \cdot (I_n \otimes V).$$
5. The partial transpose $(I_n \otimes \mathrm{transpose})(\rho)$ equals $n^{-1} S$, where $S = \sum_{i,j=1}^n |i\rangle\langle j| \otimes |j\rangle\langle i|$ is the swap operator.
6. For $n \ge 2$, the swap operator possesses antisymmetric eigenvectors $|\psi_-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|12\rangle - |21\rangle)$ with eigenvalue:
   $$S |\psi_-\rangle = -1 \cdot |\psi_-\rangle.$$
7. The spectrum of $(I_n \otimes \mathrm{transpose})(\rho)$ contains the strictly negative eigenvalue $-1/n < 0$.
8. Since unitary transformations $(I_n \otimes U)$ and $(I_n \otimes V)$ preserve the singular values and eigenvalues of the operator, $(I_n \otimes T)(\rho)$ has a strictly negative eigenvalue.
9. Thus the transposed branch fails complete positivity for all $n \ge 2$, and is excluded by the CP premise.

### 3.3 State-Cone Preservation Forces $V = U^*$
1. The remaining admissible branch has the untransposed form:
   $$T(A) = U A V.$$
2. Evaluating $T$ on the identity operator $I \in \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$:
   $$T(I) = U I V = U V.$$
3. The identity operator $I$ is strictly positive definite ($I \succ 0$). State-cone preservation requires $T(I)$ to be positive semidefinite:
   $$T(I) \succeq 0.$$
4. Define $W := U V$. Since $U$ and $V$ are unitary, $W$ is a unitary operator:
   $$W^* W = (U V)^* (U V) = V^* U^* U V = V^* I V = I.$$
5. A unitary operator $W$ is positive semidefinite if and only if all of its eigenvalues are nonnegative real numbers.
6. The eigenvalues of any unitary operator lie on the complex unit circle $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| = 1\}$. The only nonnegative real number on the unit circle is $+1$.
7. Therefore, every eigenvalue of $W$ equals $1$, forcing:
   $$W = I \implies U V = I \implies V = U^{-1} = U^*.$$
8. Substituting $V = U^*$ into the transformation gives:
   $$T(A) = U A U^*.$$

### 3.4 Projective Gauge Invariance $PU(n) = U(n)/U(1)$
1. Suppose two unitaries $U_1, U_2 \in U(n)$ produce identical transformations on all operators:
   $$U_1 A U_1^* = U_2 A U_2^* \quad \forall A \in \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n).$$
2. Rearranging terms:
   $$(U_2^* U_1) A = A (U_2^* U_1) \quad \forall A \in \mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n).$$
3. By Schur's lemma, any matrix commuting with all operators in $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ is a scalar multiple of the identity:
   $$U_2^* U_1 = e^{i\theta} I \implies U_1 = e^{i\theta} U_2,$$
   for some phase angle $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$.
4. The global phase center $U(1) \cdot I \subset U(n)$ acts trivially on $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$.
5. The faithful group of local stalk automorphisms is the projective unitary group $PU(n) = U(n)/U(1)$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Frame Relabelling Symmetry Only**: This derivation restricts the group of admissible local observer frame relabellings on trace-class operator stalks. It does not derive a physical gauge field, Yang-Mills action, or spacetime gauge theory.
- **Physical Axiom Load**: Complete positivity is an explicit physical premise imported from quantum information theory. The Banach space $\ell^1$ topology alone does not exclude the transpose branch.
- **Dimension Restriction**: For $n = 1$, $U(1)$ conjugation is trivial ($PU(1) \cong \{1\}$) and transposition coincides with the identity map. The result applies non-trivially to $n \ge 2$.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that surjective trace-norm-preserving linear automorphisms that are completely positive and state-cone preserving are unitary conjugations $A \mapsto U A U^*$, acting faithfully as $PU(n)$.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support the existence of dynamic physical gauge bosons, Yang-Mills equations, or continuous spacetime fields.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   On $\mathcal{B}_1(\mathbb{C}^n)$ with $n \ge 2$, surjective trace-norm isometries preserving complete positivity and the positive state cone are unitary conjugations $A \mapsto U A U^*$.
