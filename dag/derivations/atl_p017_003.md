## Claim

Non-faithful prompt trees form a two-sided operad ideal; in the quotient a composite is nonzero iff every leaf span is byte-identical to its slice.

## Hypotheses

- planar prompt-tree operad
- faithfulness by byte-identical leaf spans

## Derivation

Leaf spans of a composite are the union of the components' leaf spans, so the composite is faithful iff every component is; hence composing a non-faithful tree on either side stays non-faithful, and the quotient sends the ideal to the null tree.
