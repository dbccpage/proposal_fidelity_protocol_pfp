## Claim

Under edge obligations, $b_0\circ\cdots\circ b_{n-1}$ decodes checked $P_n$ solutions to $P_0$; forward maps compose in reverse; $b_i\circ f_i=\mathrm{id}$ composes; with costs $\ge\delta>0$ against budget $B$ at most $\lfloor B/\delta\rfloor$ operations run.

## Hypotheses

- Each $b_i$ maps $\mathrm{Sol}(P_{i+1})\to\mathrm{Sol}(P_i)$
- Each $f_i$ maps $\mathrm{Sol}(P_i)\to\mathrm{Sol}(P_{i+1})$
- One shared budget

## Derivation

Induction on $n$ for decoding and coverage. $(b_0\circ b_1)\circ(f_1\circ f_0)=b_0\circ(b_1\circ f_1)\circ f_0=b_0\circ f_0=\mathrm{id}$. If $k$ operations ran, $k\delta\le B$, so $k\le\lfloor B/\delta\rfloor$.
