   - **Constant shift invariance**: Shifting every vertex height by $+7/5$ leaves the coboundary differences equal to $df$.
   - **Claim negation**: Replacing $h$ with $-h$ at fixed $f$ yields $-h - df = (-2/3, -2/3, 1/3)$.
