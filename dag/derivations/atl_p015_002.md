## Claim

Over $\{0,1\}$, $x=y$, $y=z$, $z\ne x$ are each satisfiable but jointly unsatisfiable; with $z=x$ the solutions are exactly $(0,0,0),(1,1,1)$.

## Derivation

$x=y=z$ contradicts $z\ne x$. With $z=x$ all three equal, giving two solutions. Confirm by enumerating the 8 assignments.
