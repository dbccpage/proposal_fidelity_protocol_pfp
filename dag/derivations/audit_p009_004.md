## 1. Claim Statement

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **No Search Strategy or Escape Rate**: `StagnationRecord` neither selects moves nor measures how often stalled searches recover after review.
2. **No Score or History Provenance**: `consecutive_stagnant_rounds`, `current_defect_norm`, and `locked_gauge` are caller-supplied fields; `StagnationRecord` does not check whether prior rounds were actually stagnant or whether `current_defect_norm` equals a graph obstruction.
