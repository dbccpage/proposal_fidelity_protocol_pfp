## 1. Claim Statement

Across the three-grade stage status taxonomy $\mathcal{G} = \{\text{NONE}, \text{SYNTAX}, \text{COMPUTED}\}$ (with zero $\text{LEAN}$ or $\text{GATED}$ grades), Paper 008's audited baseline contract (`research/mssn_sn_tower/code/python/stage_status.py` at external commit `52cb5457f6c485ba548cfd292f6012a941ed22cf`) defines $\text{SYNTAX}$ by round-tripping a stage-$N$ packet and refusing it at stage $N - 1$, and defines $\text{COMPUTED}$ by executing a `CheckerBinding` across positive, stage-negative, and empty fixtures on the six bound stages $\{0, 1, 23, 35, 36, 48\}$. In the current `proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp` checkout, the underlying finite syntax and mathematical checkers execute those positive, negative, and empty fixture checks in `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` (and pinned target checkers enforce `ROW_SHA256`, `OWN_NEGATIVE`, and empty/flag-only probes in `pfp/infrastructure/tower_research/reach/targets.py`), whereas `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` retains the closed grade tuple `GRADES = ("NONE", "SYNTAX", "COMPUTED")` and six-stage `BINDINGS` tuple (`{0, 1, 23, 35, 36, 48}`) as a static lookup stub (`if level in COMPUTED_LEVELS` / `elif level <= 21`) that does not invoke `CheckerBinding.run`.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Closed Three-Grade Taxonomy & Six-Stage Census Partition
In `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` (lines 11–67):
1. `GRADES = ("NONE", "SYNTAX", "COMPUTED")` (line 11) contains three strings; neither `"LEAN"` nor `"GATED"` belongs to `GRADES`, matching Paper 008's statement that formal Lean declarations and gate policies are not encoded as stage-status grades.
2. `BINDINGS` (lines 35–42) declares six `CheckerBinding` entries:
   $$L_{\text{comp}} = \{0\ (\text{checks.validate\_stage@0}),\; 1\ (\text{checks.validate\_stage@1}),\; 23\ (\text{checks.glue\_finite\_sections}),\; 35\ (\text{checks.check\_markov}),\; 36\ (\text{checks.check\_bayes}),\; 48\ (\text{checks.check\_spin\_z})\}.$$
3. Over the 101 catalog stages $N \in \{0, 1, \dots, 100\}$, `census()` (lines 61–67) partitions $\{0, \dots, 100\}$ into three pairwise-disjoint level lists (`dict[str, list[int]]`):
   - $|\text{census}[\text{"COMPUTED"}]| = |L_{\text{comp}}| = 6$,
   - $|\text{census}[\text{"SYNTAX"}]| = |\{0, \dots, 21\} \setminus \{0, 1\}| = 20$ (stages $2 \le N \le 21$),
   - $|\text{census}[\text{"NONE"}]| = |\{22, \dots, 100\} \setminus \{23, 35, 36, 48\}| = 79 - 4 = 75$,
   with $6 + 20 + 75 = 101$.

### 3.2 Executed Syntax and Finite Checker Fixtures in `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py`
The concrete positive, stage-negative, and empty fixture obligations described in Paper 008 Section 4 (lines 177–210) and Study Guide Q06 (`study_guide.md:56`) are implemented as deterministic functions in `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py`:
- **Stages 0–21 syntax round-trip and one-stage-early refusal (`validate_stage`, lines 48–83, with wire round-trip `parse_wire` / `emit_wire`)**:
  - Positive validation and wire round-trip: stage `0` admits `Diff()` (`"<> "[:2]` -> `"STRUCTURAL_ONLY"`), stage `1` admits binary atoms (`Atom("0")`, `Atom("1")`) and first-lift node `application(0, Diff(), Atom("0"), Atom("1"))`, and each opcode in `FIRST_STAGE` (lines 32–40) is admitted at stage `k = FIRST_STAGE[code]` while wire serialization `parse_wire(emit_wire(node)) == node` round-trips cleanly.
  - Stage-negative refusal: passing a stage-$1$ node (`Atom("0")` or `application(0, Diff(), Atom("0"), Atom("1"))`) to stage `0` raises `WireError("MSS-0 admits only <>")`, passing a non-binary atom `Atom("a")` to stage `1` raises `WireError("inactive MSS-1 data atom")`, and passing any `FIRST_STAGE` opcode node at stage `FIRST_STAGE[code] - 1` raises `WireError("opcode not admitted at this stage")`.
  - Empty wire refusal: `parse_wire("")` raises `WireError`.
- **Four higher-stage mathematical checkers (stages 23, 35, 36, 48)**:
  - Stage `23` (`glue_finite_sections`, lines 334–343): glues compatible finite-cover sections `cover = [{"a", "b"}, {"b", "c"}]`, `sections = [{"a": "0", "b": "1"}, {"b": "1", "c": "0"}]` to `{"a": "0", "b": "1", "c": "0"}`; refuses conflicting overlap `[{"a": "0", "b": "1"}, {"b": "0", "c": "0"}]` with `WireError("overlap disagreement")`; refuses empty cover/section lists `[], []` with `WireError("cover data mismatch")`.
  - Stage `35` (`check_markov`, lines 289–295): parses canonical rational string kernels (`[["1/2", "1/2"], ["1/3", "2/3"]]`) into `Fraction` tuples and checks row non-negativity and unit row sums; refuses noncanonical rationals (`"2/4"`), non-string/float inputs, negative entries, row sums $\ne 1$, and empty matrices `[]` (`WireError("finite square Markov kernel required")`).
  - Stage `36` (`check_bayes`, lines 298–304): computes exact posterior rationals `format_rat(p_i * lik_i / evidence)` from canonical rational strings (`prior = ["1/2", "1/2"]`, `likelihood = ["3/4", "1/4"]` $\mapsto$ `("3/4", "1/4")`); refuses zero marginal evidence (`likelihood = ["0/1", "0/1"]`), prior sums $\ne 1$, likelihoods $> 1$, noncanonical/float inputs, and empty lists `[], []` (`WireError("Bayes domain mismatch")`).
  - Stage `48` (`check_spin_z`, lines 307–313): verifies exact real-rational two-component normalized spin states (`("3/5", "4/5")` $\mapsto$ `{"up": "9/25", "down": "16/25", "expectation_z": "-7/25", "authority_effect": "None"}`); refuses unnormalized states (`("1/2", "1/2")`) and zero state (`("0/1", "0/1")`) with `WireError("state is not normalized")`, noncanonical/float inputs, and empty tuple `()` (`WireError("spin half needs two basis components")`).

In parallel, `pfp/infrastructure/tower_research/reach/targets.py` (`load_checker`, `run`, `probe_battery`, lines 86–191) enforces pinned-byte checker execution requiring `STAGE`, `ROW_SHA256`, `CODES`, `OWN_NEGATIVE`, `OWN_NEGATIVE_CODE`, `STRUCTURE_MAPS`, and `check`, converting any raised exception to `("REFUSED", "R_EXCEPTION:...")` and generating empty and flag-only probes (`R_EMPTY_INPUT`, `R_VERDICT_FIELD`).

### 3.3 Current-Checkout Divergence in `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py`
Inspection of `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` (lines 14–58) in `proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp` reveals an architectural divergence from the historical `52cb5457` execution contract:
1. `CheckerBinding` (lines 14–22) defaults `run: Callable[[Any], Any] = field(default=lambda x: x, compare=False)` and sets `positive: Any = None`, `stage_negative: Any = None`, `empty: Any = None` across all six entries of `BINDINGS` (lines 35–42).
2. `stage_status(level: int)` (lines 47–58) evaluates:
   - `if level in COMPUTED_LEVELS: grade = "COMPUTED"` (`COMPUTED_LEVELS = frozenset({0, 1, 23, 35, 36, 48})`),
   - `elif level <= 21: grade = "SYNTAX"`,
   - `else: grade = "NONE"`.
3. Consequently, in current `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py`:
   - `CheckerBinding.run` is never called during `stage_status(level)` or `census()`; replacing a binding's `run` callback with a function that increments a counter and raises `RuntimeError` leaves the call count at `0` while still returning `grade == "COMPUTED"`.
   - `elif level <= 21` branches on the integer level comparison without upper/lower catalog bounds or `validate_stage` invocation, returning `StageStatus(level=-1, grade="SYNTAX", ...)` for negative integer `level = -1`.
4. By contrast, a genuine three-fixture gate evaluator $G(b)$ over a binding $b = (\text{run}, x_{+}, x_{-}, x_{\varnothing})$ assigns $\text{COMPUTED}$ if and only if $\text{run}(x_{+})$ succeeds and both $\text{run}(x_{-})$ and $\text{run}(x_{\varnothing})$ raise `WireError`; under $G$, any hollow checker that accepts $x_{-}$ or $x_{\varnothing}$ (the Q06 changed-input control) drops out of $\text{COMPUTED}$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Unarchived Historical Baseline File**: `research/mssn_sn_tower/code/python/stage_status.py` at commit `52cb5457f6c485ba548cfd292f6012a941ed22cf` is not checked out in `proposal_fidelity_protocol_pfp`; direct code verification covers the in-repo modules `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py`, `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py`, and `pfp/infrastructure/tower_research/reach/targets.py`.
2. **Static Dispatch in Current Domain Stub**: Current `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` reports static membership in `COMPUTED_LEVELS` and integer inequality `level <= 21` rather than executing `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` at call time.
3. **Finite Toy Fixture Scope**: Even when the finite checkers in `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` are executed on positive, stage-negative, and empty fixtures, passing three finite fixtures establishes bounded syntactic/algebraic discrimination on those inputs, not full semantic soundness of a stage theory.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that `GRADES = ("NONE", "SYNTAX", "COMPUTED")` excludes `"LEAN"` and `"GATED"`, verifies the 6/20/75 partition across stages `0..100`, executes positive, stage-negative, and empty checks across `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` and `reach/targets.py`, and documents that current `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` short-circuits to static set/threshold lookup without invoking `CheckerBinding.run`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not replay unarchived historical `research/mssn_sn_tower/code/python/stage_status.py` at `52cb5457`, does not claim current `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` executes fixtures dynamically, and does not infer general stage semantics from finite fixtures.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The grade set `("NONE", "SYNTAX", "COMPUTED")` contains no `LEAN` or `GATED` grade; `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` and `reach/targets.py` execute positive, negative, and empty fixture checks for the bound stages, whereas current `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` assigns `COMPUTED` and `SYNTAX` from `COMPUTED_LEVELS` and `level <= 21` without calling `CheckerBinding.run`.
4. **Next test needed**: Wire `pfp/domain/tower/stage_status.py` to execute `pfp/domain/tower/checks.py` on non-`None` `(positive, stage_negative, empty)` fixtures with explicit `0 <= level <= 100` bounds so hollow or raising bindings cannot retain `COMPUTED` or `SYNTAX` grades.
