## 1. Claim Statement

Each of the four package phrase detectors fires on its corresponding constructed fixture sentence and on none of the three clean control sentences, including the true attractive-U statement.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Candidate Phrase Detectors**:
  - Implemented in `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/code/canaries.py`.
  - Four candidate detectors based on lower-case substring matching:
    1. `AM-FSP-8-UNRESTRICTED-SOLVER-CLAIM` (Unrestricted polynomial-time sign-free method asserted).
    2. `AM-FSP-2-FRUSTRATED-OBSTRUCTION` (Particle-hole symmetry asserted on non-bipartite lattices).
    3. `AM-FSP-7-UNVERIFIED-MAJORANA-LIFT` (Unverified Majorana representation or lift asserted).
    4. `AM-FSP-4-ORBIT-SUM-MISMATCH` (Meron-cluster cancellation asserted outside valid regimes).
- **Constructed Fixtures and Controls**:
  - Evaluated in `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/code/tests/test_demo_3_host.py` and `test_danger_gate.py`.
  - Four constructed fixture sentences tailored to trigger exactly one detector each.
  - Three clean control sentences containing mathematical and physical discourse that should not trigger any detector.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

   - Sentence: `"Modern neural network quantum states and tensor networks have overcome this limitation, providing an unrestricted polynomial-time sign-free solution for all fermion systems without requiring P = NP."`
   - Matched pattern: `"unrestricted polynomial-time"`.
   - Result: `[CanaryFinding(canary_id="AM-FSP-8-UNRESTRICTED-SOLVER-CLAIM", ...)]`.
   - Other detectors firing: 0.

2. **Fixture for `AM-FSP-2`**:
   - Sentence: `"For attractive U < 0, particle-hole symmetry holds on any lattice including the triangle and pentagon."`
   - Matched pattern: `"particle-hole symmetry holds on any lattice"`.
   - Result: `[CanaryFinding(canary_id="AM-FSP-2-FRUSTRATED-OBSTRUCTION", ...)]`.
   - Other detectors firing: 0.

3. **Fixture for `AM-FSP-7`**:
   - Sentence: `"Continuous-time Majorana decomposition secures positive Monte Carlo weights."`
   - Matched pattern: `"majorana decomposition secures positive"`.
   - Result: `[CanaryFinding(canary_id="AM-FSP-7-UNVERIFIED-MAJORANA-LIFT", ...)]`.
   - Other detectors firing: 0.

4. **Fixture for `AM-FSP-4`**:
   - Sentence: `"Meron cluster algorithms eliminate sign oscillations across arbitrary non-bipartite parameter regimes, ignoring orbit-cancellation breakdowns."`

Evaluating each of the 4 fixtures against all 4 detectors confirms that each fixture triggers its designated detector and zero others.

### 3.2 Evaluation on Clean Controls
Scanning the three clean control sentences with `scan_for_canaries` yields zero detections across all detectors:

1. **Control 1 (Discrete Cochain Boundary)**:
   - Text: `"We compute the discrete cochain boundary of a 1-chain on a bipartite square lattice over Q."`
   - Findings: `[]`.

2. **Control 2 (Sign Gauge Transformation)**:
   - Text: `"We test whether diagonal sign gauge S = diag(1, -1, 1) eliminates negative signs on this triangle."`
   - Findings: `[]`.

3. **Control 3 (True Attractive-U Statement)**:
   - Text: `"For attractive U < 0 with equal spin populations, determinant QMC is sign-problem-free on any lattice."`
   - Findings: `[]`.
   - Note: Earlier phrase lists matching naive substrings like `"attractive u < 0"` would flag this true statement. The v2 detector patterns in `canaries.py` refine the triggers, allowing this true physical theorem to pass without detection.

### 3.3 Resolution of Open Obligation Regarding Real Outputs
The ledger recorded the obligation: "Constructed fixtures do not measure detector rates on real outputs."
- As documented in Paper 003 Section 3.1, this test demonstrates that the phrase detectors behave as programmed on these seven specific sentences.
- Naive phrase matching cannot parse grammar, syntax, or logical negation. For example, the sentence:
  `"There is no particle-hole symmetry on triangle plaquettes."`
  still triggers `AM-FSP-2-FRUSTRATED-OBSTRUCTION` because the phrase `"particle-hole symmetry on triangle"` is present as a substring despite the explicit semantic denial.
- Consequently, these results do not establish accuracy, precision, or false-suppression rates on open-domain LLM outputs.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Unadmitted Candidate Status**: These four phrase detectors are unadmitted research candidates, not production-certified guards.
2. **Substrings Lack Semantic Awareness**: The detectors perform literal substring matching. They cannot distinguish assertions from denials, citations, counterfactual hypotheses, or questions.
3. **No General Invariance**: Clean controls demonstrate lack of firing only on the three tested sentences; they do not establish that valid scientific claims in other domains will avoid false triggers.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that `scan_for_canaries` in `canaries.py` produces an exact diagonal match across the four declared fixture sentences, produces zero findings on the three clean controls, and tolerates the true attractive-U statement.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not support claims that these phrase detectors reliably identify false mathematical statements in unconstrained text or handle grammatical negation.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: On the declared evaluation matrix consisting of the four constructed fixture sentences and three clean controls in `test_demo_3_host.py`, `scan_for_canaries` triggers exactly the single corresponding detector for each fixture and returns empty findings for each clean control.
4. **Next test needed**: Evaluating phrase detector false-positive rates on a benchmark of syntactically complex and negated statements from peer-reviewed physics literature.
