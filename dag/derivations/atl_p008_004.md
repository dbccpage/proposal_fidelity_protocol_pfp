## Claim

Blocks $[b(N),b(N)+8)$ with $b(N)=128+8(N-22)$ for $N\ge 22$ are pairwise disjoint and lie above the source opcodes ($<128$).

## Hypotheses

- Integer $N\ge22$
- Source opcodes $<128$

## Derivation

For $N<M$, $b(M)-b(N)=8(M-N)\ge 8$, so $b(N)+7<b(M)$. Also $b(N)\ge128$. Finite check over the loaded catalog $22\le N\le100$.
