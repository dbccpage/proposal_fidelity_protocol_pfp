## 1. Claim Statement

Let $P$ and $Q$ be finite cell complexes with 0-cochain spaces $C^0(P), C^0(Q)$ and 1-cochain spaces $C^1(P), C^1(Q)$ equipped with stalkwise $\ell^1$ norms. If an aggregation map pair $(\pi_*, A)$ satisfies the coboundary commutation identity
$$A \circ d_0^P = d_0^Q \circ \pi_*$$
and the 1-cochain aggregation map is contractive,
$$\|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} \le 1,$$
then for every 0-cochain section $s \in C^0(P)$, the coarse defect is bounded by the fine defect:
$$\|d_0^Q \pi_* s\|_1 \le \|d_0^P s\|_1.$$

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Finite Complexes**: $P$ and $Q$ have finite vertex sets $V_P, V_Q$ and finite oriented edge sets $E_P, E_Q$.
- **0-Cochain Map**: $\pi_*: C^0(P) \to C^0(Q)$ maps fine vertex potentials to coarse potentials.
- **1-Cochain Map**: $A: C^1(P) \to C^1(Q)$ maps fine edge values to coarse edge values.
- **Coboundary Commutation**: $d_0^Q (\pi_* s) = A (d_0^P s)$ for every $s \in C^0(P)$.
- **Operator Contraction**: $\|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} = \sup_{x \ne 0} \frac{\|A x\|_1}{\|x\|_1} \le 1$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Operator Norm Application
1. Let $s \in C^0(P)$ be any section. By coboundary commutation:
   $$d_0^Q (\pi_* s) = A (d_0^P s).$$
2. Taking the $\ell^1$ norm of both sides:
   $$\|d_0^Q \pi_* s\|_1 = \|A (d_0^P s)\|_1.$$
3. By the definition of the subordinate $\ell^1 \to \ell^1$ operator norm:
   $$\|A (d_0^P s)\|_1 \le \|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} \|d_0^P s\|_1.$$
4. Imposing the contraction hypothesis $\|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} \le 1$:
   $$\|d_0^Q \pi_* s\|_1 \le 1 \cdot \|d_0^P s\|_1 = \|d_0^P s\|_1.$$

### 3.2 Cancellation and Defect Collapse
1. Monotonicity allows strict inequality: aggregation can cancel opposing fine coboundary differences, strictly reducing the defect.
2. In particular, a total collapse map that identifies all vertices into a single vertex has $d_0^Q \pi_* s = 0$, giving $\|d_0^Q \pi_* s\|_1 = 0 \le \|d_0^P s\|_1$ even when $d_0^P s \ne 0$.
3. Coarse defect suppression is monotonic under these assumptions, but zero coarse defect does not certify zero fine defect in the absence of a faithfulness condition.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Contraction Premise**: Monotonicity requires $\|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} \le 1$. If $A$ amplifies edge cochains ($\|A\| > 1$), coarse defect can exceed fine defect.
- **Commutation Premise**: Requires exact commutation $A \circ d_0^P = d_0^Q \circ \pi_*$.
- **No Faithfulness Claim**: Monotonicity does not assert injectivity or faithfulness. Zero coarse defect does not certify zero fine defect.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that under coboundary commutation and $\ell^1$ operator contraction, the coarse coboundary defect cannot exceed the fine coboundary defect.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support defect monotonicity when $A$ is non-contractive or fails commutation; does not assert that coarse zero defect certifies fine zero defect.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   If $A \circ d_0^P = d_0^Q \circ \pi_*$ and $\|A\|_{\ell^1 \to \ell^1} \le 1$, then $\|d_0^Q \pi_* s\|_1 \le \|d_0^P s\|_1$ for all sections $s$.
