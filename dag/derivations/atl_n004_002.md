## Claim

Contractive covering-edge maps whose saturated-chain composites are path-independent extend uniquely to a functorial Banach presheaf.

## Hypotheses

- finite poset P
- contractive maps on covering edges
- path-independence of saturated-chain composites

## Derivation

For $a\le b$ set $r_{b,a}$ to the composite along any saturated chain from $a$ to $b$; this is well defined by path-independence, $r_{a,a}=\mathrm{id}$ is the empty chain, and concatenating saturated chains $a..b$ and $b..c$ gives one for $a..c$, so $r_{c,a}=r_{b,a}r_{c,b}$. Contractivity follows from $\|ST\|\le\|S\|\|T\|$. Any functorial extension must equal the chain composite, giving uniqueness.
