# AUDIT-N002-020: Admission Check for Nilpotency $d_1 d_0 = 0$ Before Degree-2 Inference

## 1. Claim Statement

Let $K$ be a supplied finite cell complex or discrete presheaf system with declared 0-cells $V$, 1-cells $E$, and 2-cells $F$, equipped with rational coboundary matrices $d_0 \in \mathbb{Q}^{E \times V}$ and $d_1 \in \mathbb{Q}^{F \times E}$. Under these hypotheses:

1. Over a general partially ordered set presheaf, coboundary nilpotency $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ is not an automatic identity; it requires presheaf flatness ($r_{b, a} \circ r_{c, b} = r_{c, a}$), which was declared as an unproved axiom in the archive manuscript rather than derived.
2. Rather than relying on an ungrounded global axiom, any system evaluating degree-2 inferences must execute an exact rational admission check:
   $$P = d_1 d_0 \in \mathbb{Q}^{F \times V}.$$
3. The complex is admitted if and only if $P = \mathbf{0}_{F \times V}$ identically over $\mathbb{Q}$, with zero numerical tolerance.
4. Under the 1-dimensional graph truncation where $C^2(K) = \{0\}$ and $F = \emptyset$, $d_1$ is the zero linear map (represented by an empty $0 \times |E|$ matrix), and the admission check $d_1 d_0 = 0$ holds vacuously.
5. If the admission check fails ($P \ne \mathbf{0}_{F \times V}$), the complex is refused and all downstream degree-2 curvature evaluations ($\Gamma_2$, 2-cell closures) are blocked.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Supplied Complexes**: Finite cell complexes with $V$ vertices, $E$ edges, and $F$ faces.
- **Rational Arithmetic**: Coboundary matrices $d_0 \in \mathbb{Q}^{E \times V}$ and $d_1 \in \mathbb{Q}^{F \times E}$ are represented with exact rational entries.
- **Axiom Origin**: Table row C18 of `l1_obstruction_framework.tex` §2.1 and claim ledger line 56 record $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ as a declared axiom via presheaf functoriality, noting: "Not proved in this package. Nilpotency is not automatic over a poset. Trivial under the §4 graph truncation $C^2 := 0$."
- **Admission Gate**: An algorithmic guard that checks matrix product nilpotency before constructing higher-order quotient spaces or curvature observables.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Axiom Replacement by Finite Computational Admission
1. In standard algebraic topology on CW complexes, $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ follows from boundary geometric cancellation $\partial_0 \circ \partial_1 = 0$, where each directed edge has one source and one target, and each face boundary is a closed oriented cycle.
2. In presheaf theory over a poset $(P, \le)$, the restriction maps between stalks must satisfy functoriality:
   $$r_{b, a} \circ r_{c, b} = r_{c, a} \quad \text{for } a \le b \le c.$$
   When the presheaf possesses non-vanishing composition curvature $K_{c, b, a} = r_{b, a} \circ r_{c, b} - r_{c, a} \ne 0$, the composition $d_1 \circ d_0$ fails to vanish.
3. Because general poset sheaves do not automatically satisfy flatness, declaring $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ as an axiom introduces unverified obligations into degree-2 inferences.
4. The operational derivation replaces this unchecked declaration with an admission receipt:
   - Input: Matrices $d_0 \in \mathbb{Q}^{E \times V}, d_1 \in \mathbb{Q}^{F \times E}$.
   - Evaluation: Compute $P_{f, v} = \sum_{e \in E} (d_1)_{f, e} (d_0)_{e, v}$ using exact rational arithmetic.
   - Decision rule:
     $$\text{AdmissionStatus}(d_0, d_1) = \begin{cases} \text{ADMITTED} & \text{if } P_{f, v} = 0 \quad \forall f \in F, v \in V, \\ \text{REFUSED} & \text{if } \exists (f, v) \text{ such that } P_{f, v} \ne 0. \end{cases}$$
5. Only complexes receiving `ADMITTED` are permitted to execute degree-2 evaluations.

### 3.2 Graph Truncation Vacuous Attainment
1. When the carrier is a 1-dimensional graph complex, $C^2 = \{0\}$.
2. The number of 2-cells is $|F| = 0$.
3. The matrix $d_1$ has 0 rows and $|E|$ columns.
4. The matrix product $P = d_1 d_0$ has size $0 \times |V|$, containing zero entries.
5. Every entry in the empty set trivially equals zero.
6. Therefore, on 1-graphs, the nilpotency check passes vacuously, consistent with Note 002 §2.1.

### 3.3 Falsification via Face Orientation Inversion
1. Consider an oriented triangular 2-cell $f_0$ bounded by edges $e_0 = (0, 1)$, $e_1 = (1, 2)$, $e_2 = (2, 0)$.
2. The standard incidence is $d_0(0) = -e_0 + e_2$, $d_0(1) = e_0 - e_1$, $d_0(2) = e_1 - e_2$.
3. The valid boundary operator for $f_0$ has $(d_1)_{f_0, e_0} = 1$, $(d_1)_{f_0, e_1} = 1$, $(d_1)_{f_0, e_2} = 1$.
4. Evaluating $d_1 d_0$:
   - Vertex 0: $1 \cdot (-1) + 1 \cdot 0 + 1 \cdot 1 = 0$.
   - Vertex 1: $1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot (-1) + 1 \cdot 0 = 0$.
   - Vertex 2: $1 \cdot 0 + 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot (-1) = 0$.
   All entries vanish, producing `ADMITTED`.
5. Now invert the orientation of edge $e_2$ in the face boundary declaration, setting $(d_1)_{f_0, e_2} = -1$:
   - Vertex 0: $1 \cdot (-1) + 1 \cdot 0 + (-1) \cdot 1 = -2 \ne 0$.
   - Vertex 2: $1 \cdot 0 + 1 \cdot 1 + (-1) \cdot (-1) = 2 \ne 0$.
6. The product $P \ne \mathbf{0}$, triggering `REFUSED` and blocking subsequent inference.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Discrete Verification**: This mechanism checks finite rational matrices supplied to the system; it does not constitute a proof of flatness for infinite or abstract sheaves.
- **Refusal Semantics**: When $d_1 d_0 \ne 0$, the check refuses the complex rather than attempting automated repair.
- **Dimension Ceiling**: Stated for the sequence $C^0 \to C^1 \to C^2$; higher-order compositions $d_{k+1} d_k = 0$ for $k \ge 2$ require separate admission checks if higher cells are declared.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports exact rational verification of $d_1 d_0 = 0$ as a necessary admission condition on supplied finite complexes prior to degree-2 curvature inference.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support presheaf flatness for arbitrary sheaves; does not prove nilpotency when matrices fail the algebraic check.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   Evaluates the exact matrix product $d_1 d_0 = 0$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ and refuses complexes that fail, preventing invalid degree-2 inferences.
4. **Next test needed**:
   Rejection of complexes with perturbed face orientations.
