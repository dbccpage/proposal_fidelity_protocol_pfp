## 1. Claim Statement

Let $(G, \cdot, e, (\cdot)^{-1})$ be any group, and let a lattice connection assign $U_{uv} \in G$ to each positively oriented edge $(u, v) \in E^+$ with reversed-edge transport $U_{vu} = U_{uv}^{-1}$. For any elementary plaquette $P_{r,c} = (v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3)$ with directed step factors $u_m = U_{v_m v_{(m+1)\bmod 4}}$ ($m \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) and canonical based holonomy $W = u_0 u_1 u_2 u_3$:
1. **Orientation reversal at basepoint $v_0$**: Traversing the plaquette in the opposite cyclic order $(v_0, v_3, v_2, v_1)$ yields ordered product $W_{\mathrm{rev}} = u_3^{-1} u_2^{-1} u_1^{-1} u_0^{-1} = W^{-1}$.
2. **Cyclic starting-vertex shift**: Shifting the starting vertex by $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ steps along forward orientation to $(v_k, v_{k+1}, v_{k+2}, v_{k+3})$ (indices modulo $4$) conjugates $W$ by the prefix path transport $P_k = u_0 \cdots u_{k-1}$ ($P_0 = e$):
   $$W^{(k)} = u_k u_{k+1} u_{k+2} u_{k+3} = P_k^{-1} W P_k.$$
   In particular, for $k = 1$, starting at $v_1$ yields $W^{(1)} = u_1 u_2 u_3 u_0 = u_0^{-1} W u_0 = U_{v_0 v_1}^{-1} W U_{v_0 v_1}$.
3. **Order-three and order-four non-self-inverse witnesses in $S_3$ and $Q_8$**: Both `symmetric_group_s3()` ($|S_3| = 6$) and `quaternion_group_q8()` ($|Q_8| = 8$) in `code/holonomy.py` contain elements of order $\ge 3$ ($w \neq w^{-1}$), realized as plaquette holonomies $W \neq W^{-1}$ on open and periodic lattices.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Orientation Reversal Inverts Plaquette Holonomy
In `Lattice.plaquette(r, c)` (`holonomy.py` lines 241–244), the canonical vertex listing is $(v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3) = ((r, c), (r_1, c), (r_1, c_1), (r, c_1))$ with $r_1 = (r + 1) \bmod L$ and $c_1 = (c + 1) \bmod M$. Setting $v_4 = v_0$, `Connection.cycle_holonomy((v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3))` (`holonomy.py` lines 297–301) evaluates left-associated multiplication (`FiniteGroup.product`, lines 117–121) along $v_0 \to v_1 \to v_2 \to v_3 \to v_0$:
$$W = U_{v_0 v_1} U_{v_1 v_2} U_{v_2 v_3} U_{v_3 v_0} = u_0 u_1 u_2 u_3.$$
Note that $v_0 \to v_1$ and $v_1 \to v_2$ are positive edges in `Lattice.positive_edges()`, whereas $v_2 \to v_3$ and $v_3 \to v_0$ traverse the positive edges $(v_3, v_2)$ and $(v_0, v_3)$ backwards, so $u_2 = U_{v_3 v_2}^{-1}$ and $u_3 = U_{v_0 v_3}^{-1}$.

For any $x, y \in G$, $(xy)(y^{-1} x^{-1}) = x(y y^{-1})x^{-1} = x e x^{-1} = e$ and $(y^{-1} x^{-1})(xy) = e$, so $(xy)^{-1} = y^{-1} x^{-1}$ by uniqueness of two-sided inverses; in particular $(z^{-1})^{-1} = z$ because $z z^{-1} = z^{-1} z = e$. Applying $(xy)^{-1} = y^{-1} x^{-1}$ three times gives:
$$W^{-1} = (u_0 u_1 u_2 u_3)^{-1} = u_3^{-1} (u_0 u_1 u_2)^{-1} = u_3^{-1} u_2^{-1} (u_0 u_1)^{-1} = u_3^{-1} u_2^{-1} u_1^{-1} u_0^{-1}.$$
Now consider the basepoint-preserving reversed cycle $(v_0, v_3, v_2, v_1)$, which traverses $v_0 \to v_3 \to v_2 \to v_1 \to v_0$. For each step, edge inversion $U_{ab} = U_{ba}^{-1}$ gives:
- $U_{v_0 v_3} = (U_{v_3 v_0})^{-1} = u_3^{-1}$ (which equals the stored positive link $U_{(r,c)\to(r,c_1)}$),
- $U_{v_3 v_2} = (U_{v_2 v_3})^{-1} = u_2^{-1}$ (which equals the stored positive link $U_{(r,c_1)\to(r_1,c_1)}$),
- $U_{v_2 v_1} = (U_{v_1 v_2})^{-1} = u_1^{-1}$,
- $U_{v_1 v_0} = (U_{v_0 v_1})^{-1} = u_0^{-1}$.

Multiplying in traversal order yields:
$$\operatorname{hol}(v_0 \to v_3 \to v_2 \to v_1 \to v_0) = U_{v_0 v_3} U_{v_3 v_2} U_{v_2 v_1} U_{v_1 v_0} = u_3^{-1} u_2^{-1} u_1^{-1} u_0^{-1} = W^{-1}.$$

### 3.2 Cyclic Starting-Vertex Shift Conjugates Plaquette Holonomy
For $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, let $P_k = u_0 \cdots u_{k-1}$ be the forward prefix product from $v_0$ to $v_k$ (with $P_0 = e$) and let $S_k = u_k \cdots u_3$ be the remaining suffix product from $v_k$ back to $v_0$ (with $S_0 = W$), so that $W = P_k S_k$. The $k$-shifted forward cycle $(v_k, v_{k+1}, v_{k+2}, v_{k+3})$ traverses $v_k \to \cdots \to v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k$ and therefore has ordered product:
$$W^{(k)} = S_k P_k = (P_k^{-1} P_k) S_k P_k = P_k^{-1} (P_k S_k) P_k = P_k^{-1} W P_k.$$
For $k = 1$, $P_1 = u_0 = U_{v_0 v_1}$, recovering the exact identity tested at `test_holonomy_checker.py` lines 186–188:
$$W^{(1)} = u_1 u_2 u_3 u_0 = u_0^{-1} W u_0 = \operatorname{conjugate}(G,\, U_{v_0 v_1}^{-1},\, W),$$
where `conjugate(group, g, x)` computes $g x g^{-1}$ (`(u_0^{-1}) W (u_0^{-1})^{-1} = u_0^{-1} W u_0`).

Across all eight dihedral listings generated by `_plaquette_listings(lattice)` (`holonomy.py` lines 477–485):
- Forward rotations `cycle[k:] + cycle[:k]` ($k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) have holonomy $P_k^{-1} W P_k$.
- Reversed rotations `tuple(reversed(cycle))[k:] + tuple(reversed(cycle))[:k]`, where `reversed(cycle) = (v_3, v_2, v_1, v_0)`, start at $v_{(3-k)\bmod 4}$ and traverse backwards, yielding $(W^{((3-k)\bmod 4)})^{-1} = P_{(3-k)\bmod 4}^{-1} W^{-1} P_{(3-k)\bmod 4}$.

Because $g e g^{-1} = e$ and $e^{-1} = e$, every one of the eight dihedral boundary listings evaluates to $e$ if and only if $W = e$. When $W \neq e$, `verify_claim` (`holonomy.py` lines 504–513) accepts any of the eight listings if and only if `claim["holonomy"]` equals `connection.cycle_holonomy(listed)` for that specific starting vertex and orientation.

### 3.3 Existence and Exact Census of $W \neq W^{-1}$ in $S_3$ and $Q_8$
In any group $G$, $w = w^{-1} \iff w^2 = e \iff \operatorname{ord}(w) \in \{1, 2\}$, so $w \neq w^{-1} \iff \operatorname{ord}(w) \ge 3$.
1. **In $S_3$ (`symmetric_group_s3()`, `holonomy.py` lines 150–170)**:
   - Elements: `("012", "021", "102", "120", "201", "210")` with identity `"012"`.
   - Orders: `"012"` has order $1$; transpositions `"021"`, `"102"`, `"210"` have order $2$ ($w = w^{-1}$); 3-cycles `"120"` and `"201"` have order $3$ with `"120"^{-1} = "201" \neq "120"$.
   - Plaquette realization: Setting $U_{(0,0)\to(1,0)} = \text{"120"}$ and all other positive edges to `"012"` on any admissible open ($L, M \ge 2$) or periodic ($L, M \ge 3$) lattice gives $W_{0,0} = \text{"120"}$ and reversed holonomy $W_{0,0}^{-1} = \text{"201"} \neq W_{0,0}$. Across all $6^4 = 1,296$ connections on the $2 \times 2$ open lattice, bijectivity of left multiplication by $u_0 \in S_3$ for each fixed triple $(u_1, u_2, u_3) \in S_3^3$ distributes $W_{0,0}$ uniformly ($216$ connections per element), giving $2 \times 216 = 432$ connections with $W_{0,0} \neq W_{0,0}^{-1}$.
2. **In $Q_8$ (`quaternion_group_q8()`, `holonomy.py` lines 181–202)**:
   - Elements: `("e", "-e", "i", "-i", "j", "-j", "k", "-k")` with identity `"e"`.
   - Orders: `"e"` has order $1$; `"-e"` is the unique element of order $2$ (`"-e"^{-1} = "-e"`); the six imaginary units `{"i", "-i", "j", "-j", "k", "-k"}` have order $4$ with $x^{-1} = -x \neq x$.
   - Plaquette realization: Setting $U_{(0,0)\to(1,0)} = \text{"i"}$ and all other positive edges to `"e"` gives $W_{0,0} = \text{"i"} \neq \text{"-i"} = W_{0,0}^{-1}$. Across all $8^4 = 4,096$ connections on the $2 \times 2$ open lattice, each element of $Q_8$ occurs $8^3 = 512$ times as $W_{0,0}$, giving $6 \times 512 = 3,072$ connections with $W_{0,0} \neq W_{0,0}^{-1}$ (and $512$ non-flat connections with $W_{0,0} = \text{"-e"} = W_{0,0}^{-1}$, such as the commutator plaquette $i j (-i) (-j) = \text{"-e"}$ highlighted in `study_guide.md` lines 26–27 and 38–39).

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Complements `PAPER010-WITNESS-ACCEPTANCE`**: This derivation establishes the exact algebraic reversal and basepoint-shift laws and finite $S_3 / Q_8$ non-self-inverse witnesses; certificate parsing and host-level claim dispatch remain scoped to `PAPER010-WITNESS-ACCEPTANCE`.
2. **Order-2 Invariance Caveat**: Reversal changes the plaquette product if and only if $\operatorname{ord}(W) \ge 3$; when $W$ has order $2$ (transpositions in $S_3$, `"-e"` in $Q_8$, or any non-identity element in $\mathbb{Z}_2^k$), $W^{-1} = W$, so reversal alone does not alter the group element.
3. **Implementation Scope**: Group multiplication in `code/holonomy.py` is validated dynamically over finite string-keyed tables (`FiniteGroup._check_axioms`), not mechanically formalized in Lean 4.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Derives $W_{\mathrm{rev}} = W^{-1}$ and $W^{(k)} = P_k^{-1} W P_k$ for all groups under edge inversion $U_{vu} = U_{uv}^{-1}$, characterizes all eight dihedral plaquette listings in `_plaquette_listings`, and verifies exact non-self-inverse plaquette counts ($432 / 1,296$ in $S_3$ and $3,072 / 4,096$ in $Q_8$ on $2 \times 2$ open lattices, plus periodic lattices).
2. **What it does not support**: Does not imply that every non-flat plaquette has $W \neq W^{-1}$ (order-$2$ holonomies satisfy $W = W^{-1} \neq e$), and does not recover unretained C017 Arm 1 / Arm 2 certificate products.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: Under $U_{vu} = U_{uv}^{-1}$ in any group, reversing a plaquette's traversal order at its basepoint inverts its ordered product ($W \mapsto W^{-1}$), shifting the starting vertex by $k$ edges conjugates $W$ by the prefix product $P_k$ ($W \mapsto P_k^{-1} W P_k$), and both $S_3$ and $Q_8$ admit plaquette holonomies of order $\ge 3$ satisfying $W \neq W^{-1}$.
4. **Next test needed**: If additional finite groups (such as $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{F}_3)$ of order $24$ referenced in C017) are added to `code/holonomy.py`, extend the exhaustive dihedral plaquette identity suite to their constructors.
