## 1. Claim Statement

On the oriented 3-cycle $C_3$, the integer $\ell^1$ distance from the 1-cochain $\omega = (1, 0, 0)$ to the coboundary subspace $\mathrm{im}\,d_0$ is $1$ with or without a 2-cell:
$$\Phi_1(\omega) = \min_{f \in \mathbb{Z}^V} \|\omega - d_0 f\|_1 = 1.$$
Meanwhile, the closure defect $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h\|_1$ is undefined without the 2-cell ($C^2$ is absent) and equals $1$ when a single oriented 2-cell is attached.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Oriented Triangle**: Directed 3-cycle $C_3 = (V, E)$ with vertices $V = \{0, 1, 2\}$ and edges $e_0 = (0, 1)$, $e_1 = (1, 2)$, $e_2 = (2, 0)$.
- **Integer Cochains**: 0-cochains $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$ and 1-cochains $\omega \in \mathbb{Z}^E$.
- **Coboundary Operator**: $d_0: \mathbb{Z}^V \to \mathbb{Z}^E$ defined by $(d_0 f)(u, v) = f(v) - f(u)$.
- **Quotient Distance Metric**: $\Phi_1(\omega) = \min_{f \in \mathbb{Z}^V} \|\omega - d_0 f\|_1$, where $\|x\|_1 = \sum_{e \in E} |x_e|$.
- **Optional Single 2-Cell**: When absent, $C^2$ is empty and $d_1$ does not exist. When present, an oriented 2-cell $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$ has boundary $\partial \sigma = e_0 + e_1 + e_2$, giving $(d_1 \omega)_\sigma = \omega(e_0) + \omega(e_1) + \omega(e_2)$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Circulation Sum and Lower Bound
1. For any 0-cochain $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$, the circulation sum along the cycle $C_3$ vanishes:
   $$\sum_{e \in C_3} (d_0 f)(e) = (f(1) - f(0)) + (f(2) - f(1)) + (f(0) - f(2)) = 0.$$
2. For the 1-cochain $\omega = (1, 0, 0)$, the circulation sum is:
   $$\sum_{e \in C_3} \omega(e) = 1 + 0 + 0 = 1.$$
3. Applying the triangle inequality for any integer potential $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$:
   $$\|\omega - d_0 f\|_1 = \sum_{e \in E} |\omega(e) - (d_0 f)(e)| \ge \left| \sum_{e \in E} (\omega(e) - (d_0 f)(e)) \right| = |1 - 0| = 1.$$
4. At $f = (0, 0, 0)$, $d_0 f = (0, 0, 0)$, and:
   $$\|\omega - d_0 f\|_1 = |1 - 0| + |0 - 0| + |0 - 0| = 1.$$
5. Because the lower bound is attained at an integer potential, $\Phi_1(\omega) = 1$.

### 3.2 Invariance Under 2-Cell Attachment
1. The quotient distance $\Phi_1$ is evaluated entirely on the 1-skeleton $(V, E, d_0)$.
2. The definition and image of $d_0$ depend solely on 0-cells and 1-cells; attaching or omitting 2-cells leaves $V$, $E$, and $d_0$ unchanged.
3. Therefore, $\Phi_1(\omega) = 1$ in both the graph-only model and the cell complex model.

### 3.3 Closure Defect $\Gamma_2$ Divergence Across Configurations
1. In the graph-only model, $C^2$ is undeclared. The coboundary operator $d_1$ is absent, so $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h\|_1$ is undefined. Attempting to evaluate $d_1$ raises a typed error (`MissingComplex2Error`).
2. When the 2-cell $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$ is declared, $d_1: \mathbb{Z}^E \to \mathbb{Z}^F$ maps $h = \omega - d_0 f^* = (1, 0, 0)$ to:
   $$d_1 h = h(e_0) + h(e_1) + h(e_2) = 1 + 0 + 0 = 1.$$
3. Thus $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h\|_1 = |1| = 1$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Integer $C_3$ Scope**: This derivation establishes degree separation on the specific integer triangle instance $C_3$.
- **Schematic General-$k$ Statement**: General-$k$ degree separation for higher degree complexes ($d_k: C^k \to C^{k+1}$) is schematic only and is not established here.
- **Minimization Domain**: Minimization is evaluated over integer cochains; real-valued or continuum optimization is outside this derivation.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that on the integer 3-cycle $C_3$, $\Phi_1(1, 0, 0) = 1$ independently of whether a 2-cell is declared, while $\Gamma_2$ is undefined in the 1-skeleton and equals $1$ in the 2-complex.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support degree separation on general polyhedral complexes or general degrees $k > 1$.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For $(1, 0, 0)$ on integer $C_3$, the integer $\ell^1$ distance to $\mathrm{im}\,d_0$ is $1$ with or without a 2-cell, whereas $\Gamma_2$ is undefined without a 2-cell and $1$ with it.
