## Claim

A family $(s_i:S_i\to D)$ glues to a unique assignment on $\bigcup_iS_i$ iff $s_i|_{S_i\cap S_j}=s_j|_{S_i\cap S_j}$ for all $i,j$; if $\bigcup_iS_i=V$ and permission is local, compatible families biject with global solutions.

## Hypotheses

- Finite scopes
- Constraints each contained in some $S_i$

## Derivation

Define $s(v)=s_i(v)$ for $v\in S_i$; well defined by pairwise agreement, unique since determined pointwise. Conversely restrictions of a global $s$ agree. Restriction and gluing are mutually inverse.
