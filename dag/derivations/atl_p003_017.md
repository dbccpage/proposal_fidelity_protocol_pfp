## Claim

With 0 failures in n independent Bernoulli(p) trials, the one-sided 95% upper confidence bound on p is $1-0.05^{1/n}$.

## Hypotheses

- n independent trials with common failure probability p

## Derivation

$P(0\text{ failures})=(1-p)^n$, decreasing in $p$. The bound is the largest $p$ with $(1-p)^n\ge 0.05$, i.e. $p\le 1-0.05^{1/n}$ (approximately $3/n$).
