## Claim

For a plaquette word $U=u_1u_2u_3u_4$, reversal gives $U^{-1}$ and a cyclic start shift gives $u_1^{-1}Uu_1$.

## Hypotheses

- Any group

## Derivation

Reversal traverses links backwards: $(u_1u_2u_3u_4)^{-1}=u_4^{-1}u_3^{-1}u_2^{-1}u_1^{-1}$. Starting at the second vertex gives $u_2u_3u_4u_1=u_1^{-1}(u_1u_2u_3u_4)u_1$. Finite witness: some $U\neq U^{-1}$ in $S_3$ and $Q_8$.
