## Claim

With $d_i=\alpha+v_i$ and $F(t)=\frac{\alpha}{n}\sum_i(d_i-t)^{-1}$, the unique $\tau\in[0,d_{\min})$ with $F(\tau)=1$ equals $\lambda_{\min}(\alpha(I-P)+\mathrm{diag}(v))$ and lower-bounds $\lambda_{\min}(L_0+\mathrm{diag}(v))$.

## Hypotheses

- $n\ge2$
- $v_i\ge0$
- $\alpha>0$
- $L_0\succeq\alpha(I-P)$

## Derivation

$F$ is increasing from $F(0)\le1$ to $+\infty$, so $\tau$ is unique. Weighted Cauchy gives $\langle x,(\alpha(I-P)+D_v-tI)x\rangle\ge(1-F(t))\sum(d_i-t)x_i^2\ge0$ for $t\le\tau$; the vector $x_i=(d_i-\tau)^{-1}$ attains equality. Loewner order transfers the bound to $L_0$.
