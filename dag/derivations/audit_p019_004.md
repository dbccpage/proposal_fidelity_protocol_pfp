## 3. Step-by-Step Derivation

### Step 1 — Fixed `$40` ceiling and six-phase partition (`operads/domain/autopoiesis/research.py:11-18, 83-111`)

`PHASE_CAPS` partitions the campaign ceiling in integer micro-USD (`1 USD = 1_000_000` micro-USD):
- `preflight`: `2_000_000` (`$2`)
- `discovery`: `16_000_000` (`$16`)
- `transfer`: `10_000_000` (`$10`)
- `ablations`: `6_000_000` (`$6`)
- `ignition`: `4_000_000` (`$4`)
- `retry`: `2_000_000` (`$2`)

Summing `PHASE_CAPS.values()` gives exactly `40_000_000` micro-USD (`$40`), and `ExperimentSpecV1.__post_init__` refuses any `ceiling != sum(PHASE_CAPS.values())` (`CAMPAIGN_CEILING_FIXED`) or `budget_id != campaign_id` (`ORIGINAL_BUDGET_REQUIRED`).

### Step 2 — Singleton SQLite campaign binding and phase/concurrency guards (`pfp/infrastructure/adapters/harness_budget.py:11-56`)

`HarnessBudgetLedger` subclasses `SqliteBudgetLedger` (`ops/adversary/infrastructure/providers/budget_ledger.py`), opening `budget.sqlite` with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` write serialization:
1. **Singleton binding lock** (lines 19–32): creates `campaign_binding (singleton INTEGER PRIMARY KEY CHECK(singleton=1), binding TEXT NOT NULL)`, refuses any changed `ExperimentSpecV1` JSON with `ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_BINDING_CHANGED`, and refuses any foreign row in `provider_budget` with `FOREIGN_BUDGET` — preventing wallet replacement or ceiling reset on restart.
2. **Reservation guard** (`guard`, lines 34–52): executed atomically inside `BudgetController.reserve` before dispatch:
   - refuses unknown attempt states with `UNRECONCILED_COST`,
   - enforces `len(pending) < 2` (`CAMPAIGN_CONCURRENCY_LIMIT` when 2 reservations are unsettled concurrently across threads), and
   - sums settled charges plus unsettled maximum-liability holds for `self.phase`, raising `CAMPAIGN_PHASE_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` whenever `charged + quote.cost_microusd > PHASE_CAPS[self.phase]`, in addition to `BudgetController`'s global `40_000_000` micro-USD ceiling across all phases and candidate descendants.

### Step 3 — Shared outer/inner routing and restart persistence (`pfp/infrastructure/adapters/harness_provider.py:97-138`)

Both `role="outer"` (candidate proposal) and `role="inner"` (task evaluation) instantiate `HarnessBudgetLedger(self.store.root / "budget.sqlite", spec=self.spec, phase=phase)` with `BudgetController(self.spec.ceiling, ledger=ledger, reservation_guard=ledger.guard)` (lines 133–134). Re-instantiating `HarnessProvider` after a simulated process restart reads the persisted SQLite `attempts` and `settlements` tables, blocks immediately with `UNRECONCILED_COST` if any prior attempt remained unsettled (`budget.pending_attempts()`, lines 136–137), and refuses `phase == "retry"` unless separately authorized (`RETRY_REQUIRES_SEPARATELY_REVIEWED_INFRASTRUCTURE_RECEIPT`, lines 100–101).
