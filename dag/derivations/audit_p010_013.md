## 1. Claim Statement

On any periodic $L \times M$ torus ($L, M \ge 3$, and specifically $3 \times 4$) over a group $(G, \cdot, e, (\cdot)^{-1})$, let $U^{(a,b)}$ be the seam connection (`seam_connection(group, lattice, a, b)`) assigning $a \in G$ to every wrapping horizontal link $((r, M - 1), (r, 0))$, $b \in G$ to every wrapping vertical link $((L - 1, c), (0, c))$, and $e$ to all remaining $2LM - L - M$ positive edges. Then for every $(a, b) \in G \times G$ (and exhaustively across all $36$ pairs in $S_3 \times S_3$ and $64$ pairs in $Q_8 \times Q_8$):
1. **Exact plaquette evaluation**: All $LM - 1$ non-corner plaquettes have product $e$, while the unique seam-corner plaquette $P_{L-1, M-1}$ ($P_{2,3} = ((2,3), (0,3), (0,0), (2,0))$ on $3 \times 4$) has product $W_{L-1, M-1} = b a b^{-1} a^{-1}$.
2. **Biconditional flatness criterion**: $U^{(a,b)}$ is flat if and only if $ab = ba$ (split into `FLAT_TRIVIAL` when $a = b = e$ and `FLAT_NONTRIVIAL` when $ab = ba$ with $(a, b) \neq (e, e)$); when $ab \neq ba$, `check_flatness` returns `NOT_FLAT` with `nonflat_plaquettes == 1`, `witness_plaquette == P_{L-1, M-1}`, and `witness_holonomy == b a b^{-1} a^{-1}`.
3. **Cycle products and simultaneous basepoint conjugation**: When $ab = ba$, the based fundamental cycles `loop_a` and `loop_b` at $(0, 0)$ have products $(\operatorname{hol}(\text{loop\_a}), \operatorname{hol}(\text{loop\_b})) = (a, b)$, and under any vertex gauge $g : V \to G$ transform to $(g_{(0,0)} a g_{(0,0)}^{-1},\, g_{(0,0)} b g_{(0,0)}^{-1})$ conjugated by the single basepoint element $g_{(0,0)}$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Four-Region Plaquette Evaluation on the Seam Family $U^{(a,b)}$
In `seam_connection(group, lattice, a, b)` (`test_holonomy_checker.py` lines 36–46), positive links are:
$$U_{(r, c) \to (r, (c+1)\bmod M)} = \begin{cases} a & \text{if } c = M - 1, \\ e & \text{if } 0 \le c < M - 1, \end{cases} \qquad U_{(r, c) \to ((r+1)\bmod L, c)} = \begin{cases} b & \text{if } r = L - 1, \\ e & \text{if } 0 \le r < L - 1. \end{cases}$$
Partition the $LM$ plaquettes $P_{r,c} = ((r, c), (r_1, c), (r_1, c_1), (r, c_1))$ ($r_1 = (r + 1) \bmod L$, $c_1 = (c + 1) \bmod M$) into four pairwise-disjoint subsets (with counts $(6, 2, 3, 1)$ summing to $12$ when $L = 3, M = 4$):
1. **Interior non-seam plaquettes ($0 \le r < L - 1,\; 0 \le c < M - 1$, count $(L - 1)(M - 1)$)**:
   Neither $r = L - 1$ nor $c = M - 1$ holds, so all four boundary steps carry $e$ or $e^{-1} = e$:
   $$W_{r,c} = e \cdot e \cdot e \cdot e = e.$$
2. **Column-seam non-corner plaquettes ($0 \le r < L - 1,\; c = M - 1$, count $L - 1$)**:
   - $(r, M - 1) \to (r + 1, M - 1)$ carries $e$ ($r < L - 1$),
   - $(r + 1, M - 1) \to (r + 1, 0)$ traverses horizontal seam $((r + 1, M - 1), (r + 1, 0))$ forward, carrying $a$,
   - $(r + 1, 0) \to (r, 0)$ traverses interior vertical edge $((r, 0), (r + 1, 0))$ backward, carrying $e^{-1} = e$,
   - $(r, 0) \to (r, M - 1)$ traverses horizontal seam $((r, M - 1), (r, 0))$ backward, carrying $a^{-1}$.
   Multiplying in order gives opposite-orientation cancellation of $a$:
   $$W_{r, M-1} = e \cdot a \cdot e \cdot a^{-1} = a a^{-1} = e.$$
3. **Row-seam non-corner plaquettes ($r = L - 1,\; 0 \le c < M - 1$, count $M - 1$)**:
   - $(L - 1, c) \to (0, c)$ traverses vertical seam $((L - 1, c), (0, c))$ forward, carrying $b$,
   - $(0, c) \to (0, c + 1)$ carries $e$ ($c < M - 1$),
   - $(0, c + 1) \to (L - 1, c + 1)$ traverses vertical seam $((L - 1, c + 1), (0, c + 1))$ backward, carrying $b^{-1}$,
   - $(L - 1, c + 1) \to (L - 1, c)$ carries $e^{-1} = e$.
   Multiplying in order gives opposite-orientation cancellation of $b$:
   $$W_{L-1, c} = b \cdot e \cdot b^{-1} \cdot e = b b^{-1} = e.$$
4. **Unique seam-corner plaquette ($r = L - 1,\; c = M - 1$, count $1$; $P_{2,3} = ((2,3), (0,3), (0,0), (2,0))$ on $3 \times 4$)**:
   - $(L - 1, M - 1) \to (0, M - 1)$: vertical seam forward $= b$,
   - $(0, M - 1) \to (0, 0)$: horizontal seam forward $= a$,
   - $(0, 0) \to (L - 1, 0)$: vertical seam backward $= b^{-1}$,
   - $(L - 1, 0) \to (L - 1, M - 1)$: horizontal seam backward $= a^{-1}$.
   Multiplying in order gives the commutator word:
   $$W_{L-1, M-1} = b \, a \, b^{-1} \, a^{-1}.$$

### 3.2 Biconditional Flatness, Cycle Holonomies, and Gauge Conjugation
Because $W_{r,c} = e$ holds identically on all $LM - 1$ non-corner plaquettes for every $(a, b) \in G \times G$, $U^{(a,b)}$ is flat if and only if $W_{L-1, M-1} = e$. Right-multiplying $b a b^{-1} a^{-1} = e$ by $a$ and then by $b$ (both reversible via $b^{-1}$ and $a^{-1}$) yields:
$$b a b^{-1} a^{-1} = e \iff b a b^{-1} = a \iff b a = a b \iff a b = b a.$$
- **Non-commuting branch ($ab \neq ba$)**: $W_{L-1, M-1} = b a b^{-1} a^{-1} \neq e$ is the sole non-flat plaquette (`nonflat_plaquettes == 1`, `witness_plaquette == ((2, 3), (0, 3), (0, 0), (2, 0))` on $3 \times 4$, `witness_holonomy == b a b^{-1} a^{-1}`, `verdict == "NOT_FLAT"`). Under any gauge $g$, `moved = gauge_transform(U^{(a,b)}, g)` retains `verdict == "NOT_FLAT"` and `nonflat_plaquettes == 1` (`AUDIT-P010-007`), with corner holonomy $g_{(L-1, M-1)} (b a b^{-1} a^{-1}) g_{(L-1, M-1)}^{-1} \neq e$.
- **Commuting branch ($ab = ba$)**: Every plaquette has product $e$. Evaluating `Lattice.loop_a()` (`(0,0) -> (0,1) -> ... -> (0,M-1) -> (0,0)`, `holonomy.py` lines 246–250) and `Lattice.loop_b()` (`(0,0) -> (1,0) -> ... -> (L-1,0) -> (0,0)`, lines 252–256) on $U^{(a,b)}$ gives:
  $$A = \operatorname{hol}_{U^{(a,b)}}(\text{loop\_a}) = \underbrace{e \cdots e}_{M-1} \cdot a = a, \qquad B = \operatorname{hol}_{U^{(a,b)}}(\text{loop\_b}) = \underbrace{e \cdots e}_{L-1} \cdot b = b.$$
  Under any vertex gauge $g : V \to G$, both `loop_a` and `loop_b` are closed paths based at the same vertex $v_0 = (0, 0)$. By closed-path gauge covariance (`AUDIT-P010-007`), intermediate vertex factors $g_{(0,c)}^{-1} g_{(0,c)}$ along row $0$ and $g_{(r,0)}^{-1} g_{(r,0)}$ along column $0$ cancel, conjugating *both* cycle holonomies by the single element $g_0 = g_{(0,0)}$:
  $$(A', B') = \bigl(\operatorname{hol}_{U'}(\text{loop\_a}),\, \operatorname{hol}_{U'}(\text{loop\_b})\bigr) = \bigl(g_0 a g_0^{-1},\; g_0 b g_0^{-1}\bigr).$$
  Note that $A' B' = g_0 a g_0^{-1} g_0 b g_0^{-1} = g_0 (ab) g_0^{-1} = g_0 (ba) g_0^{-1} = B' A'$, and $(A', B') = (e, e) \iff (a, b) = (e, e)$ (`FLAT_TRIVIAL` for $(e, e)$ and `FLAT_NONTRIVIAL` for the remaining commuting pairs).

### 3.3 Exact Class-Equation Census over $S_3 \times S_3$ and $Q_8 \times Q_8$
For any finite group $G$ with $k(G)$ conjugacy classes, the orbit-stabilizer identity $|G| = |\mathrm{cl}(a)| \cdot |C_G(a)|$ implies:
$$|\{(a, b) \in G \times G : ab = ba\}| = \sum_{a \in G} |C_G(a)| = \sum_{C \in \mathrm{Cl}(G)} \sum_{a \in C} \frac{|G|}{|C|} = k(G) \cdot |G|.$$
1. **$S_3 \times S_3$ ($|S_3| = 6$, $36$ seam pairs, $k(S_3) = 3$ conjugacy classes)**:
   - Centralizer sizes: $|C_{S_3}(\text{"012"})| = 6$, three transpositions (`"021"`, `"102"`, `"210"`) each have $|C_{S_3}(\tau)| = 2$, and two $3$-cycles (`"120"`, `"201"`) each have $|C_{S_3}(\sigma)| = 3$.
   - Commuting (flat) pairs: $6 + 3(2) + 2(3) = 3 \times 6 = 18$ ($1$ `FLAT_TRIVIAL` at `("012", "012")` + $17$ `FLAT_NONTRIVIAL`).
   - Non-commuting (`NOT_FLAT`) pairs: $36 - 18 = 18$, with corner commutator $b a b^{-1} a^{-1} \in \{\text{"120"}, \text{"201"}\}$ ($9$ pairs each).
2. **$Q_8 \times Q_8$ ($|Q_8| = 8$, $64$ seam pairs, $k(Q_8) = 5$ conjugacy classes)**:
   - Centralizer sizes: central elements `"e"` and `"-e"` each have $|C_{Q_8}(\pm e)| = 8$, and the six order-$4$ elements (`"i"`, `"-i"`, `"j"`, `"-j"`, `"k"`, `"-k"`) each have $|C_{Q_8}(x)| = |\{\text{"e"}, \text{"-e"}, x, -x\}| = 4$.
   - Commuting (flat) pairs: $2(8) + 6(4) = 5 \times 8 = 40$ ($1$ `FLAT_TRIVIAL` at `("e", "e")` + $39$ `FLAT_NONTRIVIAL`).
   - Non-commuting (`NOT_FLAT`) pairs: $64 - 40 = 24$, with corner commutator $b a b^{-1} a^{-1} = \text{"-e"}$ on all $24$ pairs.
3. **Combined $100$-pair family (`study_capstone.py` lines 56–75)**: $36 + 64 = 100$ connections partition into $2$ `FLAT_TRIVIAL`, $56$ `FLAT_NONTRIVIAL` ($58$ flat), and $42$ `NOT_FLAT`.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Complements `PAPER010-RECTANGULAR-HOLONOMY-CLASSIFIER`**: This derivation establishes the converse flatness condition, corner commutator formula $b a b^{-1} a^{-1}$, simultaneous $(0, 0)$ gauge conjugation of $(a, b)$, and exact $58 / 42$ census on the $100$-member seam family; general torus homotopy reduction on arbitrary connections remains scoped to `PAPER010-RECTANGULAR-HOLONOMY-CLASSIFIER`.
2. **Subfamily Scope**: On the $3 \times 4$ torus ($|E^+| = 24$), the seam family tests $|G|^2$ connections ($36$ for $S_3$ out of $6^{24}$, $64$ for $Q_8$ out of $8^{24}$), representing one canonical cross-section of $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}^2, G)$ together with its non-commuting seam extensions rather than all connections on the lattice (`paper.md` lines 227–231).
3. **Basepoint Convention**: Simultaneous conjugation by a single group element $g_{(0,0)}$ relies on `loop_a` and `loop_b` sharing the common basepoint $(0, 0)$; choosing disjoint basepoints $u_A \neq u_B$ would conjugate the two loop products independently by $g_{u_A}$ and $g_{u_B}$.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Derives $W_{r,c} = e$ on all $LM - 1$ non-corner plaquettes and $W_{L-1, M-1} = b a b^{-1} a^{-1}$ at the seam corner of $U^{(a,b)}$, establishes flatness iff $ab = ba$, derives $(A', B') = (g_0 a g_0^{-1}, g_0 b g_0^{-1})$ under any vertex gauge $g$, and verifies the exact $18 / 18$ ($S_3^2$) and $40 / 24$ ($Q_8^2$) flat/non-flat counts on `code/holonomy.py`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not exhaust all $6^{24}$ or $8^{24}$ connections on a $3 \times 4$ torus, and does not claim cycle products are gauge-invariant as raw group elements when $G$ is non-abelian (only their joint $G$-conjugacy class is gauge-invariant).
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: On the $3 \times 4$ torus seam family over $S_3$ and $Q_8$, all $11$ non-corner plaquettes cancel to $e$ and $P_{2,3}$ evaluates to $b a b^{-1} a^{-1}$, so the connection is flat iff $ab = ba$ ($18$ of $36$ in $S_3^2$, $40$ of $64$ in $Q_8^2$), with flat cycle products $(a, b)$ conjugated simultaneously by $g_{(0,0)}$ under any gauge transformation.
4. **Next test needed**: Extend the exhaustive $G \times G$ seam-pair check to a non-Hamiltonian group of order $\ge 12$ (such as $A_4$ or $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbb{F}_3)$) where commutators span multiple non-identity elements of differing orders.
