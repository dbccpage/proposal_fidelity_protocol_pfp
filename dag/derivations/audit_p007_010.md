## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **No Fresh Kernel Replay**: The repository audit inspects and counts the retained reports and verifies their consistency against the release validation receipt. It does not invoke a fresh `lake build` or Lean kernel check when the Lean toolchain is absent from the host environment.
2. **Whitelist Membership vs. Usage**: `Classical.choice` is approved in the report whitelist but is not listed as used by any of the 64 declarations.
3. **Census Boundaries**: The 64 declarations belong strictly to package-local definitions and their generated sub-declarations; imported core Lean library declarations are outside the package census.

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that retained evidence records a Lean 4.34.0 build, an explicit 21-declaration selected axiom report, and a 64-declaration package-local census without unapproved axioms.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not assert fresh kernel verification on hosts lacking Lean/Lake, nor does it make general claims about human cognitive workloads or arbitrary Lean environments.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: Companion 007 retains reports documenting a Lean 4.34.0 build with 21 named declarations (19 axiom-free, 2 using `propext` and `Quot.sound`) and 64 total package-local declarations.
