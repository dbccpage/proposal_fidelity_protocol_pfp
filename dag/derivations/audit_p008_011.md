Parsing `docs/historical_census_inventory.json` (`revision == "a623568eb0f853b968bc419bf06dfc6af256ab02"`) yields 50 challenge records indexed consecutively from `C001` through `C050` across five category tree SHAs (`a_mechanism`: `6dbeb232...`, `b_cohomology`: `4e1d27e8...`, `c_quantum`: `dc2d8b71...`, `d_physics_mtheory`: `b4db689e...`, `e_formal_math`: `e74d6eb3...`):

1. **Category A ($\mathcal{A} = \{\text{C011}, \dots, \text{C050}\}$, $|\mathcal{A}| = 40$)**:
   - Every entry $c \in \mathcal{A}$ carries `"category": "A"`, a 40-hex `"tree_sha"`, $\text{len}(\text{reference\_solver\_files}) \ge 3$ (ranging from $3$ to $7$ `.py` files with recorded SHA-1 blob digests), and $\text{len}(\text{checker\_files}) = 2$ (under `judge_packet/checkers/` for `C011`–`C046` and `judge_packet/` for `C047`–`C050`).
2. **Category B ($\mathcal{B} = \{\text{C001}, \text{C005}\}$, $|\mathcal{B}| = 2$)**:
   - Both entries carry `"category": "B"`, a 40-hex `"tree_sha"`, `reference_solver_files == []`, and `checker_files == []`, matching their empirical model-measurement role (`C001` Qwen2.5-0.5B harness without retained run output; `C005` hosted decoding controls).

Set-theoretic verification over $\mathcal{U} = \{\text{C001}, \dots, \text{C050}\}$:
$$\mathcal{A} \cap \mathcal{B} = \varnothing, \quad \mathcal{A} \cap \mathcal{C} = \varnothing, \quad \mathcal{B} \cap \mathcal{C} = \varnothing, \quad \mathcal{A} \cup \mathcal{B} \cup \mathcal{C} = \mathcal{U}, \quad 40 + 2 + 8 = 50.$$
