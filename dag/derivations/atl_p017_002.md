## Claim

On a directed $k$-cycle, $\Phi_1(c)=|s|$ with $s=\sum_ic_i$, equal to the dual maximum.

## Hypotheses

- directed $k$-cycle
- unit weights

## Derivation

Coboundaries are exactly $\{\sum c'_i=0\}$ (telescoping, rank $k-1$). For any residual $r=c-d_0u$, $\sum|r_i|\ge|\sum r_i|=|s|$, attained by putting $s$ on one edge. The circulation $z=\mathrm{sgn}(s)\mathbf 1$ gives $\langle z,c\rangle=|s|$.
