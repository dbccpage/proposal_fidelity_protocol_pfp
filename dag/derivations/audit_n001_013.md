## 1. Claim Statement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a connected finite directed graph with edge weights $\mathbf{w} \in (0, \infty)^E$, and let $\boldsymbol{\omega} \in C^1(G)$ be a 1-cochain. The weighted discrete Chebyshev quotient problem is defined by:
$$\Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) = \inf_{f \in C^0(G)} \max_{e \in E} w_e |(\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f)_e|.$$
Under these hypotheses:

1. The infimum $\Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ is attained at some potential $f_\infty^* \in C^0(G)$, coinciding with the Chebyshev approximation radius of $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ with respect to the coboundary subspace $\operatorname{im}(d_0)$.
2. An optimum potential $f_\infty^*$ admits a dual circulation vector $\mathbf{z}^* \in \mathbb{R}^E$ satisfying the conservation law $d_0^\top \mathbf{z}^* = \mathbf{0}$, the unit weighted $\ell_1$ dual constraint $\sum_{e \in E} \frac{|z_e^*|}{w_e} \le 1$, and the complementary slackness alignment:
   $$\langle \mathbf{z}^*, \boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_\infty^* \rangle = \Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}]).$$
3. The dual circulation $\mathbf{z}^*$ is supported on the active peak edges:
   $$\operatorname{supp}(\mathbf{z}^*) \subseteq \left\{ e \in E : w_e |(\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_\infty^*)_e| = \Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}]) \right\},$$
   with sign condition $\operatorname{sgn}(z_e^*) = \operatorname{sgn}((\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f_\infty^*)_e)$ on active edges where $z_e^* \ne 0$.
4. The discrete Aronsson partial differential equation remains withdrawn for arbitrary cochains on graphs.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Graph Topology**: $G = (V, E)$ is a finite 1-dimensional cell complex with vertices $V$ and oriented edges $E$.
- **Positive Weights**: Each edge $e \in E$ has weight $w_e > 0$.
- **Cochain Spaces**:
  - Vertex potentials $C^0(G) = \mathbb{R}^V$.
  - Edge forms $C^1(G) = \mathbb{R}^E$.
- **Coboundary Operator**: $d_0: C^0(G) \to C^1(G)$ given by $(d_0 f)_{uv} = f(v) - f(u)$ for oriented edge $(u, v) \in E$.
- **Subgradient and Duality**: The dual space to $(C^1(G), \|\cdot\|_{\infty, \mathbf{w}})$ is $(C_1(G), \|\cdot\|_{1, 1/\mathbf{w}})$, with duality pairing $\langle \mathbf{z}, r \rangle = \sum_{e \in E} z_e r_e$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Epigraph Formulation as a Linear Program
1. The Chebyshev quotient minimization is expressed as:
   $$\min_{f \in C^0(G)} \max_{e \in E} w_e |(\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 f)_e|.$$
2. Introducing a scalar upper bound variable $t \in \mathbb{R}$, this is equivalent to the linear program:
   $$\min t$$
   subject to the linear inequality constraints for each edge $e = (u, v) \in E$:
   $$-t \le w_e \left( \omega_e + f(v) - f(u) \right) \le t.$$
3. Dividing by $w_e > 0$:
   $$f(u) - f(v) - \frac{t}{w_e} \le \omega_e,$$
   $$f(v) - f(u) - \frac{t}{w_e} \le -\omega_e.$$
4. Gauge fixing at a chosen reference vertex $v_0$ ($f(v_0) = 0$) renders the constraint matrix of full column rank. Because the feasible region for $t \ge 0$ is non-empty (for example, $f = \mathbf{0}, t = \max_e w_e |\omega_e|$) and bounded below by $t \ge 0$, linear programming duality over $\mathbb{Q}$ establishes that an optimal solution $(t^*, f_\infty^*)$ exists with finite rational value $t^* = \Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$.

### 3.2 Dual Circulation Problem and Complementary Slackness
1. Assign non-negative Lagrange multipliers $\lambda_e^+, \lambda_e^- \ge 0$ to the constraints:
   $$w_e(\omega_e + (d_0 f)_e) \le t \implies \lambda_e^+,$$
   $$-w_e(\omega_e + (d_0 f)_e) \le t \implies \lambda_e^-.$$
2. The Lagrangian is:
   $$\mathcal{L}(t, f, \lambda^+, \lambda^-) = t + \sum_{e \in E} \lambda_e^+ \left[ w_e (\omega_e + (d_0 f)_e) - t \right] + \sum_{e \in E} \lambda_e^- \left[ -w_e (\omega_e + (d_0 f)_e) - t \right].$$
3. Collecting coefficients:
   $$\mathcal{L} = t \left( 1 - \sum_{e \in E} (\lambda_e^+ + \lambda_e^-) \right) + \sum_{e \in E} w_e (\lambda_e^+ - \lambda_e^-) \omega_e + \sum_{v \in V} f(v) \left( d_0^\top [ \mathbf{w} \odot (\boldsymbol{\lambda}^+ - \boldsymbol{\lambda}^-) ] \right)_v.$$
4. Vanishing of derivatives with respect to primal variables requires:
   - For $t$: $\sum_{e \in E} (\lambda_e^+ + \lambda_e^-) \le 1$.
   - For $f$: $d_0^\top [ \mathbf{w} \odot (\boldsymbol{\lambda}^+ - \boldsymbol{\lambda}^-) ] = \mathbf{0}$.
5. Setting $z_e = w_e (\lambda_e^+ - \lambda_e^-)$, we obtain:
   $$d_0^\top \mathbf{z} = \mathbf{0},$$
   and $|z_e| = w_e (\lambda_e^+ + \lambda_e^-)$ when at most one of $\lambda_e^+, \lambda_e^-$ is non-zero, so:
   $$\sum_{e \in E} \frac{|z_e|}{w_e} = \sum_{e \in E} (\lambda_e^+ + \lambda_e^-) \le 1.$$
6. The dual objective is:
   $$\sum_{e \in E} w_e (\lambda_e^+ - \lambda_e^-) \omega_e = \langle \mathbf{z}, \boldsymbol{\omega} \rangle.$$
7. Complementary slackness requires that if $z_e > 0$, then $\lambda_e^+ > 0$ and $w_e (\omega_e + (d_0 f_\infty^*)_e) = t^*$. If $z_e < 0$, then $\lambda_e^- > 0$ and $w_e (\omega_e + (d_0 f_\infty^*)_e) = -t^*$.
8. Consequently, $\mathbf{z}^*$ is non-zero only on active edges where the weighted residual magnitude equals $t^* = \Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$, and the signs match.

### 3.3 Status of the Discrete Aronsson Equation
1. In continuous infinity-Laplacian analysis, $L^\infty$ variational problems are connected to the Aronsson equation $\Delta_\infty u = 0$.
2. On general discrete graphs, a point-wise discrete Aronsson PDE does not hold for arbitrary offset cochains $\boldsymbol{\omega}$, as stated in Section 4.4 of the reference manuscript.
3. The derivation here relies on finite linear programming duality and active-edge complementary slackness; the Aronsson PDE remains omitted.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Non-Uniqueness**: Because the $L^\infty$ norm is not strictly convex, the primal potential $f_\infty^*$ and the active-edge circulation $\mathbf{z}^*$ are not uniquely determined.
- **Finite Graph Restriction**: This formulation applies to finite graphs with positive edge weights; it does not extend to infinite networks without compactness assumptions.
- **No PDE Extension**: This result establishes linear programming duality and subgradient alignment; it does not construct a discrete Aronsson operator.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports discrete linear programming duality, exact rational attainment, and active-edge subgradient circulation pairings for $\Phi_\infty([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$ on finite directed graphs with positive weights.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support uniqueness of minimizers; does not support a discrete Aronsson equation for arbitrary cochains.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For a finite directed graph with positive edge weights, $\Phi_\infty$ minimizers are characterized by dual circulations supported on active edges with matching signs.
4. **Next test needed**:
   Falsification testing of non-active dual edge perturbations.
