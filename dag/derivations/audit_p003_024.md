## 1. Claim Statement

At historical commit `30609de2c` (`30609de2c3928238715375a9bac608256fa381d8`), the allostery gate evaluates the sum threshold of Lemma B in IEEE-754 floating point. Under its defaults ($n = 5/2 = 2.5$, $K = 2.0$, $t = 0.5$) it activates at a positive signal sum of 2, including on a single sensor `[2.0]`. The two-signal comment at line 68 has no per-sensor upper bound or sensor-identity check behind it. At threshold $t = 0$ the gate activates on an empty signal list `[]`. [Historical gate](https://github.com/dbccpage/the_symbol_game/blob/30609de2c3928238715375a9bac608256fa381d8/omega_engine_epistemic_immune_system/domain/allostery.py#L67-L79), [historical Hill formula](https://github.com/dbccpage/the_symbol_game/blob/30609de2c3928238715375a9bac608256fa381d8/omega_engine_epistemic_immune_system/domain/value_objects.py#L121-L128)

This node supplies only the residual historical audit closing the gap left by `PAPER003-HILL-SUM-THRESHOLD` (mathematical sum-threshold equivalence) and `SYS-EIS-002` (current exact bounded-rational gate with per-sensor cap and sensor-name keying).

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Floating-Point Sum Threshold and Single-Sensor Activation at Sum 2
At commit `30609de2c`, `eis/domain/allostery.py` (lines 74–79) sums strictly positive entries $\theta = \sum_{s_i > 0} s_i$ in `float` and calls `HillParameters.calculate_cooperativity(theta)`:
$$H_{\text{float}}(\theta) = \begin{cases} 0.0 & \text{if } \theta \le 0.0 \\ \dfrac{\theta^{n}}{K^{n} + \theta^{n}} & \text{if } \theta > 0.0 \end{cases}$$
activating when $H_{\text{float}}(\theta) \ge t$. At the historical defaults $(n = 2.5, K = 2.0, t = 0.5)$ documented in `paper.md` (lines 721–723) and `eis/tests/unit/test_allostery_exact.py` (line 38):
- By `PAPER003-HILL-SUM-THRESHOLD` (Lemma B(2)), at $t = 1/2$ the half-activation sum threshold equals $\theta^* = K = 2$ regardless of $n > 0$.
- Evaluating on a single-sensor list `[2.0]` gives $\theta = 2.0$ and $H_{\text{float}}(2.0) = 2.0^{2.5} / (2.0^{2.5} + 2.0^{2.5}) = 0.5 \ge 0.5$, activating with one sensor (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestHistoricalAllosteryGate::test_historical_float_gate_activates_at_sum_two_with_single_sensor`).
- Evaluating on `[1.999]` gives $\theta = 1.999 < 2.0$ and $H_{\text{float}}(1.999) < 0.5$ (not activated). Evaluating on `[1.0, 0.5, -7.0]` discards $-7.0$, leaving $\theta = 1.5 < 2.0$ (not activated).

### 3.2 Unenforced Two-Signal Comment at Line 68
Line 68 of historical `eis/domain/allostery.py` carried the comment that $K = 2$ `"requires 2 equivalent unit signals"` (`paper.md`, lines 789–791). By Lemma B(3), a sum threshold $\theta \ge 2$ implies at least two contributing entries only when every signal satisfies $s_i \le 1$, and implies two distinct sensor names only when inputs are keyed by sensor identifier rather than passed as a raw list (`eis/tests/unit/test_allostery_exact.py`, lines 8–9 and 126–128). Because `30609de2c` enforced neither a per-sensor cap nor sensor-key uniqueness, both `[2.0]` (single entry) and `[1.0, 1.0]` (duplicate entries from one caller) activated the gate (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestHistoricalAllosteryGate::test_historical_two_signal_comment_unenforced_without_cap_or_keying`).

### 3.3 Empty-List Activation at $t = 0$
On an empty input list `[]`, the positive sum is $\theta = 0$ and the Hill response is $H(0) = 0$. Because activation compares $H(\theta) \ge t$ non-strictly without an open-interval guard $0 < t < 1$, setting `activation_threshold = 0` evaluates $0.0 \ge 0.0$ in floating point (and `Fraction(0) >= Fraction(0)` in `detector_lemmas.hill_activates([], n, k, 0)`), returning `True` with zero signals (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestHistoricalAllosteryGate::test_historical_zero_threshold_activates_on_empty_list`).

### 3.4 Boundary Contrast with Repaired `SYS-EIS-002`
In the repaired implementation (`eis/domain/allostery.py` and `eis/domain/value_objects.py` from commit `fdc0035a8`), `HillParameters(2.5, 2)` raises `AllosteryRefusal("NOT_EXACT")`, `AllostericConsensusGate(activation_threshold=0)` raises `AllosteryRefusal("THRESHOLD_OUT_OF_RANGE")`, unkeyed `[2]` raises `AllosteryRefusal("SIGNALS_NOT_KEYED")`, and `{"s1": 2}` under default `per_sensor_cap = Fraction(1)` raises `AllosteryRefusal("SIGNAL_ABOVE_SENSOR_CAP")` (`tests/test_w4_w1_shard_a.py::TestHistoricalAllosteryGate::test_falsification_repaired_gate_refuses_float_exponent_zero_threshold_and_list`).

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. The linked `the_symbol_game` source supplies the historical line references and defaults. The local tests replay the cited formula and counterexamples as reconstructions. The real-valued threshold identity does not establish behavior across floating-point overflow or rounding boundaries.
2. **Non-Duplication of Current Gate**: Current exact allostery semantics and sensor-name quorum bounds belong to `SYS-EIS-002`; general Lemma B threshold algebra belongs to `PAPER003-HILL-SUM-THRESHOLD`.
3. **Independence Boundary**: Even after adding sensor-name keying and a per-sensor cap in `fdc0035a8`, distinct sensor identifiers establish entry-name distinctness rather than statistical or causal independence of upstream observers.

1. **What it supports**: Confirms from the Paper 003 Appendix A audit, exact Lemma B boundary evaluation, and `test_allostery_exact.py` regression records that the `30609de2c` floating-point Hill gate activated at default sum 2 on a single sensor `[2.0]`, left its two-signal comment unenforced, and activated on `[]` at $t = 0$.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not claim that the current `eis/domain/allostery.py` retains floating-point arithmetic, unkeyed list inputs, or $t = 0$ silence activation, nor does it inspect live git blob bytes of `30609de2c` inside this repository's truncated history.
