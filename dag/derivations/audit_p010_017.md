## 1. Claim Statement

Because pre-split commit `3d3492f20d839a3c032bbf15eb803733b12599fe` holding the historical CRLF judge manifest resides outside this split checkout (while in-repo `workflows/tasks/certified_challenges/c017_nonabelian_lattice_holonomy/judge_packet/manifest.json` was regenerated on `2026-09-29` with `CRLF_TO_LF`), `AUDIT-P010-017` narrows `ATL-P010-017` (`partial`, `relation: narrows`): historical CRLF judge-manifest equality rests on `code/data/c017/provenance.json`'s retained attestation, while local LF byte custody and CRLF digest computation are verified directly across each of the five retained C017 case files $f \in \{\text{pois\_01.json}, \text{base\_01.json}, \text{base\_02.json}, \text{base\_03.json}, \text{base\_13.json}\}$ in `atlas/archive/pfp/010_pfp_holonomy_checks_and_model_pilots/code/data/c017/`:
1. The LF byte sequence $B_{\text{LF}}(f)$ stored in Git at `78bc23b35` and packaged inside `010_pfp_holonomy_checks_and_model_pilots_2026-10-02.zip` is byte-identical to the retained challenge fixture in `workflows/tasks/certified_challenges/c017_nonabelian_lattice_holonomy/judge_packet/cases/` (`poisoned/pois_01.json` and `base/base_{01,02,03,13}.json`), with $\operatorname{SHA256}(B_{\text{LF}}(f))$ equal to `provenance.json["cases"][i]["sha256"]` in `code/data/c017/provenance.json`, `MANIFEST.sha256`, `SHA256SUMS`, and the `CRLF_TO_LF`-regenerated in-repo `judge_packet/manifest.json`.
2. Applying the line-ending map $\tau_{\text{CRLF}}(B_{\text{LF}}(f)) = B_{\text{LF}}(f)[\text{\texttt{\textbackslash n}} \mapsto \text{\texttt{\textbackslash r\textbackslash n}}]$ yields $\operatorname{SHA256}(\tau_{\text{CRLF}}(B_{\text{LF}}(f)))$ equal to `provenance.json["cases"][i]["judge_manifest_sha256_crlf"]` in `code/data/c017/provenance.json` and to the working-tree digest recorded in `dag/file_inventory.json` under `core.autocrlf=true` checkout.

### 3.2 CRLF Digest Identity & `.gitattributes` Working-Tree Boundary
For each of the five LF blobs, $|B_{\text{CRLF}}(f)| = |B_{\text{LF}}(f)| + N_{\text{nl}}(f)$, where $N_{\text{nl}}(f) \in \{102, 112, 140, 198, 176\}$ is the LF newline (`\n`) count (`103, 113, 141, 199, 177` lines via `splitlines()`, since none of the 5 LF blobs ends with a trailing `\n`), and $\operatorname{SHA256}(B_{\text{LF}}(f).\text{replace}(\text{\texttt{b"\textbackslash n"}}, \text{\texttt{b"\textbackslash r\textbackslash n"}}))$ equals `provenance.json["cases"][i]["judge_manifest_sha256_crlf"]` in `code/data/c017/provenance.json` (lines 10, 18, 26, 34, 42), implementing the exact check in `code/tests/test_c017_replay.py` (lines 43–52).

Two repository-level custody details govern local replay:
1. **Regenerated Upstream Judge Manifest**: In-repo `workflows/tasks/certified_challenges/c017_nonabelian_lattice_holonomy/judge_packet/manifest.json` was regenerated on `2026-09-29` with `"digest_normalization": "CRLF_TO_LF"`, so its `files` map (`judge_packet/cases/poisoned/pois_01.json` and `judge_packet/cases/base/base_{01,02,03,13}.json`) records the five LF SHA-256 values directly rather than the historical CRLF manifest values preserved in `provenance.json["cases"][i]["judge_manifest_sha256_crlf"]`.
2. **Checkout Line-Ending Asymmetry**: Repository `.gitattributes` pins `code/** text eol=lf` (line 18) and `workflows/** text eol=lf` (line 27) at repository root, leaving `atlas/archive/**` unpinned for `.json` files. Under `core.autocrlf=true`, `workflows/tasks/certified_challenges/c017_nonabelian_lattice_holonomy/judge_packet/cases/**` checks out as LF, whereas `atlas/archive/pfp/010_pfp_holonomy_checks_and_model_pilots/code/data/c017/*.json` checks out as CRLF (matching `dag/file_inventory.json`). Consequently, `test_c017_replay.py::test_case_files_match_recorded_digests` (`assert b"\r\n" not in data`, line 48) succeeds when pointed at the LF Git (`78bc23b35`) / ZIP bytes and raises `AssertionError` on raw `core.autocrlf=true` working-tree bytes (corroborating the checkout custody note on `PAPER010-REPLAY-AND-RECOUNT-CEILING`).

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **External Pre-Split Commit (`partial`, `relation: narrows`)**: Commit `3d3492f20d839a3c032bbf15eb803733b12599fe` cited in `code/data/c017/provenance.json` resides outside this standalone split checkout's Git object database, while in-repo `judge_packet/manifest.json` reflects the `2026-09-29` `CRLF_TO_LF` regeneration; therefore this derivation narrows `ATL-P010-017` because historical CRLF judge-manifest equality rests on `code/data/c017/provenance.json`'s retained attestation (`provenance.json["cases"][i]["judge_manifest_sha256_crlf"]`).
2. **Working-Tree `core.autocrlf` Sensitivity**: Without an `eol=lf` rule covering `atlas/archive/**/*.json`, working-tree reads under `core.autocrlf=true` yield CRLF bytes rather than the LF bytes stored in Git (`78bc23b35`) and the deposit ZIP.
3. **Custody Scope**: Byte identity and digest agreement attest fixture transport integrity across the five retained C017 JSON files; they do not evaluate holonomy verdicts or model replies.
