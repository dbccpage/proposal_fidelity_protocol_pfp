## Claim

If $\mathcal O:C^1\to C^1_{\mathcal O}$ is linear, $\ell^1$-nonexpansive and maps $B^1$ into $B^1_{\mathcal O}$, then $\Phi_{\mathcal O}(\mathcal O\omega)\le\Phi(\omega)$.

## Hypotheses

- H1 finite deterministic quotient
- H2 $\mathcal O(B^1)\subseteq B^1_{\mathcal O}$
- H3 $\|\mathcal O x\|_1\le\|x\|_1$

## Derivation

$\Phi_{\mathcal O}(\mathcal O\omega)=\inf_{b'}\|\mathcal O\omega-b'\|_1\le\inf_{b\in B^1}\|\mathcal O(\omega-b)\|_1\le\inf_b\|\omega-b\|_1=\Phi(\omega)$ using H2 then H3.
