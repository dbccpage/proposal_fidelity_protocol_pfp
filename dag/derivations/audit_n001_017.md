## 1. Claim Statement

On oriented cell complexes modeling discrete gauge geometry—specifically the 2-simplicial triangle complex, the 4-vertex square lattice plaquette cell, and the 5-cycle loop complex:

1. The algebraic coboundary composite vanishes identically:
   $$d_1 \circ d_0 = 0,$$
   and its adjoint boundary composite satisfies $D_1 \circ D_2 = 0$.
2. For every 0-cochain (vertex potential) $\phi \in C^0$, the pure gauge cochain $d_0 \phi \in C^1$ produces identically zero curvature through faces:
   $$d_1(d_0 \phi) = 0.$$
   Consequently, pure gauge connections lie strictly in the cocycle subspace $Z^1 = \ker(d_1)$.
3. For every 1-cycle $z \in C_1$ satisfying $D_1 z = 0$ (Kirchhoff circulation) and every 0-cochain $\phi \in C^0$, the dual pairing vanishes:
   $$\langle z, d_0 \phi \rangle = 0,$$
   ensuring gauge invariance of the quotient norm: $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 \phi]) = \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Oriented 2-Simplicial Triangle Complex**:
  - Vertices: $V = \{0, 1, 2\}$.
  - Directed edges: $E = \{(0,1), (1,2), (0,2)\}$.
  - Oriented face: $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$ with boundary $D_2 \sigma = e_{01} + e_{12} - e_{02}$.
- **Oriented Square Plaquette Cell**:
  - Vertices: $V = \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$.
  - Directed edges: $E = \{(0,1), (1,2), (2,3), (0,3)\}$.
  - Plaquette face: boundary $D_2 \tau = e_{01} + e_{12} + e_{23} - e_{03}$.
- **Oriented 5-Cycle Complex**:
  - Vertices: $V = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
  - Directed edges: $E = \{(0,1), (1,2), (2,3), (3,4), (0,4)\}$.
  - Loop face: boundary $e_{01} + e_{12} + e_{23} + e_{34} - e_{04}$.
- **Boundary / Coboundary Operators**:
  - $D_1: C_1 \to C_0$, $(D_1 e) = v - u$ for directed edge $e = (u, v)$.
  - Coboundary $d_0 = D_1^\top: C^0 \to C^1$, $(d_0 \phi)(u, v) = \phi_v - \phi_u$.
  - Coboundary $d_1 = D_2^\top: C^1 \to C^2$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Simplicial Triangle Nilpotency
1. For the oriented triangular 2-simplex $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$, the simplicial boundary is:
   $$D_2 \sigma = e_{01} + e_{12} - e_{02}.$$
2. Applying the vertex boundary operator $D_1$:
   $$D_1 (D_2 \sigma) = D_1(e_{01}) + D_1(e_{12}) - D_1(e_{02}) = (v_1 - v_0) + (v_2 - v_1) - (v_2 - v_0) = 0.$$
3. Taking adjoints over $\mathbb{Z}$:
   $$d_1 \circ d_0 = (D_1 \circ D_2)^\top = 0^\top = 0.$$
4. For any potential $\phi \in C^0$, $(d_1(d_0 \phi))(\sigma) = (\phi_1 - \phi_0) + (\phi_2 - \phi_1) - (\phi_2 - \phi_0) = 0$.

### 3.2 Square Plaquette Nilpotency
1. For the 4-vertex plaquette cell with boundary $D_2 \tau = e_{01} + e_{12} + e_{23} - e_{03}$:
2. Applying $D_1$:
   $$D_1 (D_2 \tau) = (v_1 - v_0) + (v_2 - v_1) + (v_3 - v_2) - (v_3 - v_0) = 0.$$
3. Taking adjoints: $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$.
4. For any lattice gauge potential $\phi \in \mathbb{R}^4$:
   $$(d_1(d_0 \phi))(\tau) = (\phi_1 - \phi_0) + (\phi_2 - \phi_1) + (\phi_3 - \phi_2) - (\phi_3 - \phi_0) = 0.$$

### 3.3 5-Cycle Loop Nilpotency
1. For the 5-cycle loop with face boundary $D_2 \gamma = e_{01} + e_{12} + e_{23} + e_{34} - e_{04}$:
2. Applying $D_1$:
   $$D_1 (D_2 \gamma) = (v_1 - v_0) + (v_2 - v_1) + (v_3 - v_2) + (v_4 - v_3) - (v_4 - v_0) = 0.$$
3. By adjoint duality, $d_1(d_0 \phi) = 0$ for any vertex potential $\phi \in \mathbb{R}^5$.

### 3.4 Cycle-Coboundary Orthogonality
1. For any 1-chain $z \in C_1$ satisfying $D_1 z = 0$ (Kirchhoff circulation) and any 0-cochain $\phi \in C^0$:
   $$\langle z, d_0 \phi \rangle = \langle z, D_1^\top \phi \rangle = \langle D_1 z, \phi \rangle = \langle 0, \phi \rangle = 0.$$
2. Therefore, coboundaries $d_0 \phi$ lie in the orthogonal complement $(\ker D_1)^\perp = \operatorname{im} d_0$.
3. For any cochain $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ and any gauge shift $d_0 \phi$, $\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 \phi$ represents the identical coset in $Q^1 = C^1 / \operatorname{im} d_0$, establishing gauge invariance of the quotient norm $\Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega} + d_0 \phi]) = \Phi_1([\boldsymbol{\omega}])$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Orientation Convention**: Nilpotency strictly requires coherent relative orientation of face boundaries. Flipped edge signs destroy boundary cancellation.
- **Physical Non-Claim**: Vanishing of discrete curvature $d_1(d_0 \phi) = 0$ is an exact combinatorial/algebraic property of coboundary complexes. It does not establish continuum Maxwell or Yang-Mills equations, which require Hodge stars and metric geometries.
- **Topological Obstruction**: Closed cochains ($d_1 \boldsymbol{\omega} = 0$) are not necessarily exact ($\boldsymbol{\omega} = d_0 \phi$); non-trivial 1-homology $H_1(K) \ne 0$ admits non-zero circulation flux.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports identity $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ and $D_1 \circ D_2 = 0$ across the triangle 2-simplex, 4-vertex square plaquette, and 5-cycle loop complexes, ensuring pure gauge cochains yield zero field curvature and are orthogonal to 1-cycles.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support vanishing curvature for non-gauge connections ($d_1 \boldsymbol{\omega} \ne 0$ in general); does not assert continuum differential forms or dynamic wave propagation.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   On the triangle, 4-vertex square plaquette, and 5-cycle oriented cell complexes, the discrete coboundary composite vanishes identically ($d_1 d_0 = 0$).
