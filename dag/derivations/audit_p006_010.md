## 1. Claim Statement

The canonical epistemic immune self panel (`pfp/domain/immune/self_panel.json`) comprises 1,000 stored example entries across four declared categories, but contains exactly 40 distinct base contents once synthetic comment trailers are normalized.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Source Implementation**:
  - Corpus: `pfp/domain/immune/self_panel.json`.
  - Panel loader and trailer normalization: `pfp/domain/immune/thymus.py`.
- **Target Lineage**:
  - Paper 006 Section 7 (lines 352–354) and `paper_claim_ledger.md` row P6-10.
- **Normalization Invariant**:
  - The self panel augments repeated base examples with synthetic comment trailers of the form `-- index NNN hash_mod_M`.
  - The base content is the canonical text obtained by removing this synthetic trailer (`SelfPanel.base_content(entry.content)`).
- **Diversity Metric**:
  - The effective diversity ratio of the panel is the exact rational fraction $\frac{40}{1000} = \frac{1}{25}$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Census of Canonical Panel Rows and Distinct Base Contents
Loading the canonical panel through `SelfPanel.load_canonical()`:
1. Total entry count: $|P| = 1{,}000$ rows.
2. Distinct base content count: $|\text{distinct\_base\_contents}(P)| = 40$.
3. Exact diversity fraction:
   $$\text{Diversity}(P) = \frac{40}{1000} = \frac{1}{25}$$

### 3.2 Category-Wise Partitioning
The 1,000 rows partition into four declared functional categories of 250 rows each:
- `clean_lean4_proof`: 250 rows, containing exactly 10 distinct base contents.
- `clean_architecture_port`: 250 rows, containing exactly 10 distinct base contents.
- `clean_prompt_template`: 250 rows, containing exactly 10 distinct base contents.
- `clean_physics_expression`: 250 rows, containing exactly 10 distinct base contents.

Summing the per-category distinct base contents yields:
$$10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 40$$

### 3.3 Synthetic Trailer Removal Logic
In `pfp/domain/immune/thymus.py:28-34, 185-195`:
```python
_SYNTHETIC_TRAILER = re.compile(r"\s*--\s*index\s+\d+\s+hash_mod_\d+\s*$", re.MULTILINE)

@staticmethod
def base_content(content: str) -> str:
    """Content with the synthetic ``-- index NNN hash_mod_M`` trailer removed."""
    return _SYNTHETIC_TRAILER.sub("", content)

def distinct_base_contents(self) -> frozenset[str]:
    return frozenset(self.base_content(entry.content) for entry in self.entries)
```
Genuine language comments (such as `-- a real Lean comment`) do not match the index pattern and remain intact.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Effective Coverage**: The 1,000 entries do not constitute 1,000 independent samples; the panel's effective epistemic coverage is bounded by the 40 distinct underlying fixtures.
2. **Category Scope**: This derivation assesses only the canonical self panel fixture stored in `self_panel.json`; it does not claim completeness for all conceivable non-defective source files.
3. **No Generalization Beyond Declared Categories**: The count of 10 distinct items per category reflects the specific design of the test corpus rather than an inherent property of Lean 4 or Python.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that `pfp/domain/immune/self_panel.json` contains 1,000 stored entries across four categories and exactly 40 distinct base contents when normalized via `thymus.py`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not support describing the 1,000 rows as 1,000 independent test fixtures.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The canonical self panel has 40 distinct base contents distributed evenly (10 each) across four categories among 1,000 stored rows.
4. **Next test needed**: Expansion of the fixture corpus with distinct base cases to increase the diversity ratio beyond $1/25$.
