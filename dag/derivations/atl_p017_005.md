## Claim

With a sound and complete Stage-1 guard and conditionally independent retries of pass probability $p$, admission probability is $1-(1-p)^n$, Stage-2 precision is that of the admitted set, and savings are $(1-p)^n$ calls per task.

## Hypotheses

- sound and complete guard
- conditional independence

## Derivation

Failures over $n$ independent attempts have probability $(1-p)^n$; abstained tasks skip Stage 2.
