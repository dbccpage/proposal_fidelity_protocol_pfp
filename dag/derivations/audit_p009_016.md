## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Retained `F3` Call Sequence and Exact Coboundary Non-Annihilation
1. **Exact `F3` Replay Driver (`replay_adversary_gate_falsifiers.py:46-49, 128-143`)**:
   - Defines `sha(data)` as `"sha256:" + hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()` (`sha("f3") = "sha256:625e0f649de27800fc3bcf4c118ef79f69dcb762c2e73fbb1cfce0e7a86f6b80"`).
   - Calls `execute_act7_authorization_gate(None, "f3", [candidate(b"x")], [], {}, 1.0, 0.0, dual_witness={"(0, 1)": "1"}, exact_phi1="1").unwrap()`, reads `receipt = narrative.get("obstruction_non_vanishing_receipt", {})` and `refs = list(proposal.evidence_refs)`, and checks `refs[0] == sha("f3")`, `receipt.get("annihilates_coboundary")`, and `receipt.get("norm_bounded")`.
   - Retained `adversary_gate_falsifiers.json` (lines 48–53; checked at `test_review_counts.py:82`) records `"status": "BOUNDED_RESIDUAL"`, `"first_reference_is_sha256_of_run_key": true`, `"receipt_annihilates_coboundary": true`, and `"receipt_norm_bounded": true`.
2. **Exact Coboundary Pairing Refutation**:
   - A 1-chain / dual edge witness $\eta$ on a directed graph $G = (V, E)$ annihilates every coboundary $df$ ($(df)_{(u,v)} = f(v) - f(u)$) if and only if $\eta \in \ker(\partial_1)$, i.e., flow balance $\sum_{(w, v) \in E} \eta(w, v) - \sum_{(v, w) \in E} \eta(v, w) = 0$ holds at every vertex $v \in V$.
   - For `dual_witness = {"(0, 1)": "1"}` ($\eta(0, 1) = 1$, $\eta(e) = 0$ for $e \neq (0, 1)$), net vertex divergence is $(\partial_1 \eta)(0) = -1 \neq 0$ and $(\partial_1 \eta)(1) = +1 \neq 0$. Evaluating against $f = (0, 1, 0)$ (where `triangle_cycle_sum.edge_differences((Fraction(0), Fraction(1), Fraction(0))) == (Fraction(1), Fraction(-1), Fraction(0))`) gives
     $$\langle \eta, df \rangle = 1 \cdot (f(1) - f(0)) + 0 \cdot (f(2) - f(1)) + 0 \cdot (f(0) - f(2)) = 1 \neq 0.$$
   - Moreover, no capacity or edge-weight vector was passed in the `F3` call (`replay_adversary_gate_falsifiers.py:129-130`), so neither coboundary annihilation nor norm boundedness held on the `F3` input.
