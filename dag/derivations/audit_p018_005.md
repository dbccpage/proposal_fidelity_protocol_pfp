## 3. Step-by-Step Derivation

### Step 1 — Caller-supplied patch requirement and absence of candidate generation

1. `CodeRepairRequestV1` (`operads/contracts/ddc_code_repair_v1.py`, lines 54–62) requires an explicit non-empty `candidate_patch: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=65536)` and `checks: tuple[LocalCheckV1, ...] = Field(min_length=1, max_length=16)` at request construction; omitting or emptying `candidate_patch` fails Pydantic strict validation immediately.
2. AST inspection across `operads/contracts/ddc_code_repair_v1.py`, `operads/infrastructure/ddc_code_repair.py`, and `ops/adversary/infrastructure/ddc_code_repair.py` confirms zero imports of LLM gateways, HTTP clients (`urllib`, `httpx`, `requests`, `anthropic`, `openai`), search loops, or patch-synthesis heuristics. `CodeRepairRuntime` executes at most one caller-supplied patch in `evaluate` (`ops/adversary/infrastructure/ddc_code_repair.py`, lines 232–248) without retry or mutation.

### Step 2 — Zero model accuracy, cost, or OS-sandbox safety claims

1. **Zero model accuracy or cost measurements**: Neither `RepairStateV1`, `RepairTerminalV1`, nor `CodeRepairRuntime.run`'s `"oeop.ddc-code-repair-run.v1"` record contains token-usage, model-score, or API-cost fields; `docs/observed_execution.json` (line 20) explicitly records `"new_model_trials": 0`.
2. **Working-copy separation vs OS sandboxing**: Module docstring lines 3–6 of `ops/adversary/infrastructure/ddc_code_repair.py` state verbatim: *"This is working-copy isolation, not an OS security sandbox. Commands are trusted, host-selected argv vectors, without a shell."* `_checks` (`lines 176-178`) invokes `subprocess.run(check.argv, cwd=self.workspace, ...)` directly on the host OS without container, namespace, seccomp, or network isolation, confirming no safety-sandbox guarantee is claimed or implemented by the supplied-patch runner.
