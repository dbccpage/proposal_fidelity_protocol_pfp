## Claim

If $A,D$ are constant on classes of $\sim$ and $R$ meets every class, $D=A$ iff $D|_R=A|_R$; omitting a class admits a counterexample.

## Hypotheses

- $A,D$ class-constant
- $R$ a transversal

## Derivation

For $x$ choose $r\sim x$ in $R$: $D(x)=D(r)=A(r)=A(x)$. For an omitted class flip $D$ on it.
