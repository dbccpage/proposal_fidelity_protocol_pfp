## Claim

If $r$ is optimal with support $S$ and $\sum_{e\in S}w_e|h_e|<\sum_{e\notin S}w_e|h_e|$ for all nonzero $h\in\mathrm{im}B$, then $r$ is the unique optimal residual.

## Hypotheses

- finite graph
- positive weights

## Derivation

For $h\ne0$: $\|r-h\|_w\ge\|r\|_w-\sum_Sw|h|+\sum_{S^c}w|h|>\|r\|_w$.
