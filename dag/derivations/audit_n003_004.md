## 1. Claim Statement

For any graded cochain complex $(C^0, C^1, C^2, d_0, d_1)$ satisfying the simplicial nilpotency condition $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$ and any admitted projection $\pi_Z: C^1 \to C^1$ satisfying $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$ and $\pi_Z \circ d_0 = 0$:
- **Rule 1 (Exactness kills closure)**: For every 0-cochain $f \in C^0$, $d_1(d_0(f)) = 0$.
- **Rule 2 (Projection idempotence)**: For every 1-cochain $\omega \in C^1$, $\pi_Z(\pi_Z(\omega)) = \pi_Z(\omega)$.
- **Rule 3 (Exact component vanishes under projection)**: For every 0-cochain $f \in C^0$, $\pi_Z(d_0(f)) = 0$.
Additionally, the quotient defect $\Phi_1$ is invariant under exact shifts: $\Phi_1(\omega + d_0 f) = \Phi_1(\omega)$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Admitted Complex**: Graded cochain complex with linear coboundary operators $d_0: C^0 \to C^1$ and $d_1: C^1 \to C^2$ satisfying $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$.
- **Admitted Projection**: Declared operator $\pi_Z: C^1 \to C^1$ satisfying the admission contract identities $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$ and $\pi_Z \circ d_0 = 0$.
- **Quotient Defect**: $\Phi_1(\omega) = \min_{g \in C^0} \|\omega - d_0 g\|_1$.
- **Contract Lemmas**: The symbolic rewrite rules are derived directly as consequences of these admitted contract identities.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Direct Consequence Derivations
1. **Rule 1 Derivation**:
   Let $f \in C^0$ be an arbitrary 0-cochain.
   By the composition property of linear maps:
   $$d_1(d_0(f)) = (d_1 \circ d_0)(f).$$
   Since the complex is admitted, $d_1 \circ d_0 = 0$, giving:
   $$(d_1 \circ d_0)(f) = 0(f) = 0.$$
2. **Rule 2 Derivation**:
   Let $\omega \in C^1$ be an arbitrary 1-cochain.
   By definition of operator squaring:
   $$\pi_Z(\pi_Z(\omega)) = (\pi_Z \circ \pi_Z)(\omega) = \pi_Z^2(\omega).$$
   Since $\pi_Z$ is admitted, $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$, giving:
   $$\pi_Z^2(\omega) = \pi_Z(\omega).$$
3. **Rule 3 Derivation**:
   Let $f \in C^0$ be an arbitrary 0-cochain.
   Applying composition:
   $$\pi_Z(d_0(f)) = (\pi_Z \circ d_0)(f).$$
   Since $\pi_Z$ is admitted, $\pi_Z \circ d_0 = 0$, giving:
   $$(\pi_Z \circ d_0)(f) = 0(f) = 0.$$

### 3.2 Quotient Defect Shift Invariance
1. Consider the quotient distance $\Phi_1(\omega + d_0 f)$ for any fixed $f \in C^0$:
   $$\Phi_1(\omega + d_0 f) = \min_{g \in C^0} \|(\omega + d_0 f) - d_0 g\|_1 = \min_{g \in C^0} \|\omega - d_0(g - f)\|_1.$$
2. Substituting the variable $g' = g - f \in C^0$, the minimization domain $C^0$ remains bijective under translation by $f$:
   $$\min_{g \in C^0} \|\omega - d_0(g - f)\|_1 = \min_{g' \in C^0} \|\omega - d_0 g'\|_1 = \Phi_1(\omega).$$
3. Hence, exact cochain perturbations leave the quotient defect unchanged.

### 3.3 Unsoundness on Unadmitted Operators
1. The rewrite rules do not hold for general linear operators.
2. If an unadmitted operator $\Pi_{\mathrm{unadmitted}}$ is used, Rule 3 fails.
3. For example, the identity operator $I: C^1 \to C^1$ satisfies $I(d_0 f) = d_0 f$. Whenever $d_0 f \ne 0$, $I(d_0 f) \ne 0$, demonstrating that Rule 3 is invalid for unadmitted projections.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Soundness Dependent on Admission**: Rules 1-3 are valid only for complexes and operators that have undergone explicit admission checks. Applying these rules to unadmitted operators is unsound.
- **Contract Projection**: The derivation constitutes the algebraic consequence of the admitted contract, not a general theorem holding for arbitrary operators.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that Rules 1-3 and quotient defect shift invariance hold for any cochain complex with $d_1 d_0 = 0$ and any admitted projection satisfying $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$ and $\pi_Z d_0 = 0$.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support the application of rewrite rules to arbitrary or unadmitted projections.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   Under the declared assumptions $d_1 d_0 = 0$, $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$, and $\pi_Z d_0 = 0$, the rewrite rules $d_1 d_0 = 0$, $\pi_Z^2 = \pi_Z$, $\pi_Z d_0 = 0$, and $\Phi_1(\omega + d_0 f) = \Phi_1(\omega)$ hold.
