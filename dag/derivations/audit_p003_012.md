## 1. Claim Statement

The historical harvest corpus behind the failure detector identifiers contains 112 rows: 102 author-written scripted responses across six probe specifications and 10 responses from a single model (`qwen/qwen-2.5-7b-instruct`), labelled via prompt-specific regular expressions without an independent checker.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Harvest Corpus Record**:
  - Recorded in `research/mssn_sn_tower/demos/antimorphism_farm/harvest/antimorphism_harvest.jsonl` as cited in Paper 003 Section 3.2.
- **Probe Domain Specifications**:
  - Defined in `eis/infrastructure/mssn_domains.py`, establishing three physics domains and six probes in the `DOMAINS` structure.
- **Classifier Rule Sets**:
  - Implemented in `eis/infrastructure/mssn_classifier.py`, evaluating candidate text with prompt-specific regular expression patterns (`classify_response`).

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Author-Scripted Probe Specifications
In `eis/infrastructure/mssn_domains.py`:
- The framework defines three domain specifications:
  1. `PHYSICS | SIGN PROBLEM | MERON`
  2. `PHYSICS | SIGN PROBLEM | HUBBARD`
  3. `PHYSICS | SIGN PROBLEM | FULL FERMION SIGN PROBLEM`
- These contain six probe specifications (two per domain):
  1. `MERON-01` (targeting unconstrained solver claims)
  2. `MERON-02-ORBIT-CANCELLATION`
  3. `HUBBARD-01-ATTRACTIVE-VS-REPULSIVE`
  4. `HUBBARD-02-INVOLUTION-ON-ODD-CYCLES`
  5. `FULL-FERMION-01-COMPLEXITY-BARRIER`
  6. `FULL-FERMION-02-MAJORANA-AND-CONTINUOUS-TIME`
- Each probe includes hardcoded, author-scripted text in `scripted_hallucination` and `scripted_grounded` fields.

### 3.2 Prompt-Specific Regular Expression Classification
In `eis/infrastructure/mssn_classifier.py`:
- `classify_response(probe, response, model)` inspects `probe.probe_id` and executes hardcoded regex scans tailored solely to that single probe.
- No independent checker or ground-truth verification oracle validates the assigned labels.
- Detector `AM-FSP-6` is absent from active classifier dispatch; probe `MERON-01` emits `AM-FSP-8-UNRESTRICTED-SOLVER-CLAIM` instead.

### 3.3 Empirical Sensitivity and Specificity Boundary
- When evaluated on author-scripted grounded text, `classify_response` yields zero findings (`[]`) across all six probes.
- When key phrases in scripted hallucinations are paraphrased (e.g. replacing "particle-hole symmetry" with "fermionic charge-conjugation invariance"), the regex rules fail to fire, demonstrating lack of semantic generalization.

### 3.4 Census Invariant
The harvest corpus census decomposes into:
- 102 author-scripted rows drawn from the prompt specifications in `mssn_domains.py`.
- 10 model responses generated by `qwen/qwen-2.5-7b-instruct`.
- Totaling 112 harvest rows.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Provenance Only**: This derivation documents dataset provenance and classifier mechanics; it does not measure real-world model hallucination rates.
2. **No Semantic Generalization**: The regex classifier operates on prompt-specific lexical tokens and fails on paraphrased text.
3. **Unvalidated Labels**: Absence of an independent review process means detector label accuracy on unconstrained corpora is unestablished.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that the historical harvest consists of 112 rows (102 authored, 10 qwen), labelled by prompt-specific regex in `mssn_classifier.py` without an independent checker.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not support claims of general detector accuracy, precision, or recall on unconstrained model outputs.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The failure detector identifiers in Paper 003 derive from a 112-row dataset labelled by prompt-specific regex rules on six physics probes, with no external verification oracle.
4. **Next test needed**: Measuring classification agreement between the prompt-specific regex rules and human expert annotations on open-domain physics responses.
