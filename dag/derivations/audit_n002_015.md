## 1. Claim Statement

Let $G = (V, E)$ be a finite directed graph complex with strictly positive edge weights $\mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^E$, and let $\omega \in \mathbb{R}^E$ be an observed 1-cochain. If a potential $s \in \mathbb{R}^V$ minimizes the weighted $\ell^1$ coboundary residual
$$s \in \arg\min_{f \in \mathbb{R}^V} \|\omega - d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}},$$
then there exists a dual subgradient vector $\phi \in \partial \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ that is a circulation:
$$d_0^* \phi = 0.$$

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Finite Graph**: Finite vertex set $V$ and oriented edge set $E$, with coboundary $d_0: \mathbb{R}^V \to \mathbb{R}^E$ and adjoint $d_0^*: \mathbb{R}^E \to \mathbb{R}^V$.
- **Positive Weights**: $\mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}^E$, giving the weighted norm $\|r\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} = \sum_{e \in E} w_e |r_e|$.
- **Residual**: For potential $f$, $r(f) = \omega - d_0 f$.
- **Objective Function**: $J(f) = \|r(f)\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} = \|\omega - d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$.
- **Minimizer Premise**: $s$ satisfies $J(s) \le J(f)$ for all $f \in \mathbb{R}^V$.
- **Prior Sufficiency Result**: Prior node `PFPDOMAIN-CL07` established sufficiency: if $\exists \phi \in \partial \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ with $d_0^* \phi = 0$, then $s$ is an optimal potential. This derivation establishes the necessity direction.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Convex Subdifferential Chain Rule
1. The objective $J(f) = g(A f + b)$ is the composition of the continuous convex function $g(r) = \|r\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ with the affine mapping $r(f) = -d_0 f + \omega$ (where $A = -d_0$ and $b = \omega$).
2. Because $g$ is convex and everywhere finite on the finite-dimensional space $\mathbb{R}^E$, its domain has full dimension and contains the range of the affine map.
3. By the subdifferential chain rule for affine compositions (Moreau–Rockafellar):
   $$\partial J(s) = A^* \partial g(r(s)) = -d_0^* \partial \|\cdot\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}(\omega - d_0 s).$$

### 3.2 First-Order Optimality Condition
1. Because $J$ is a convex function on $\mathbb{R}^V$, $s$ is a global minimizer if and only if:
   $$0 \in \partial J(s).$$
2. Substituting the subdifferential chain rule:
   $$0 \in -d_0^* \partial \|\cdot\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}(\omega - d_0 s).$$
3. This implies the existence of a vector $\phi \in \partial \|\cdot\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}(\omega - d_0 s)$ such that:
   $$-d_0^* \phi = 0 \iff d_0^* \phi = 0.$$

### 3.3 Subgradient Structure
1. The coordinate subdifferential of $w_e |r_e|$ at $r_e = (\omega - d_0 s)_e$ is:
   $$\partial(w_e |r_e|) = \begin{cases} \{w_e\} & \text{if } r_e > 0, \\ \{-w_e\} & \text{if } r_e < 0, \\ [-w_e, w_e] & \text{if } r_e = 0. \end{cases}$$
2. Therefore, $\phi \in \partial \|\cdot\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}(r(s))$ satisfies:
   - Capacity constraint: $|\phi_e| \le w_e$ for all $e \in E$.
   - Complementary slackness: $\phi_e = w_e \operatorname{sgn}(r_e)$ whenever $r_e \ne 0$.
3. Combined with $d_0^* \phi = 0$, $\phi$ is a divergence-free circulation witness certifying the KKT condition.

### 3.4 Reconciled Equivalence with PFPDOMAIN-CL07
- Necessity (AUDIT-N002-015): $s \text{ minimizes } \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \implies \exists \phi \in \partial \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \text{ s.t. } d_0^* \phi = 0$.
- Sufficiency (PFPDOMAIN-CL07): $\exists \phi \in \partial \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}} \text{ s.t. } d_0^* \phi = 0 \implies s \text{ minimizes } \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$.
- Together, they form an exact KKT characterization of $\ell^1$ quotient minimality.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Non-Uniqueness at Zero Residual**: When residual coordinates vanish ($r_e = 0$), the certifying circulation $\phi$ is generally not unique.
- **Finite Graphs Only**: Uses finite-dimensional convex calculus; does not establish KKT necessity on infinite-dimensional Banach spaces.
- **Non-Differentiable Polyhedral Norm**: The objective is non-smooth; $\phi$ is a subgradient, not an ordinary derivative.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the necessity of the KKT circulation condition for any minimizer of the weighted $\ell^1$ coboundary residual on a finite graph with positive weights.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support uniqueness of the dual circulation witness when zero-residual coordinates are present; does not assert differentiability of the $\ell^1$ norm.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   If $s \in \arg\min_f \|\omega - d_0 f\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ on a finite graph with positive weights, then $\exists \phi \in \partial \|\omega - d_0 s\|_{1, \mathbf{w}}$ such that $d_0^* \phi = 0$.
