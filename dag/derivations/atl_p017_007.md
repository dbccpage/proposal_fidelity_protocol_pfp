## Claim

Chord-coordinate incidence of the fundamental cycle basis is $I_{b_1}$; the partial-guard false-green kernel has rank $|V|-1+b_1$.

## Hypotheses

- connected graph
- spanning tree $T$, $b_1\ge2$ chords

## Derivation

Each fundamental cycle contains exactly one chord, giving $I_{b_1}$. The rank follows by counting free tree potentials ($|V|-1$) and free chord residuals ($b_1$).
