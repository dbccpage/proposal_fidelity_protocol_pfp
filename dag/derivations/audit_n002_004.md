## 1. Claim Statement

For every $p \in [1, \infty]$ and unitary operators $U, V \in \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{H})$ on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, the Schatten-$p$ norm is unitarily invariant:
$$\|U X V\|_{S_p} = \|X\|_{S_p}$$
for all $X \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$. Conversely, by the classical Kadison–Sourour isometry classification, surjective linear isometries of $\mathcal{S}_p(\mathcal{H})$ ($p \ne 2$) are of the form $X \mapsto U X V$ or $X \mapsto U X^T V$. Imposing complete positivity (CP) excludes the transpose forms for $\dim \mathcal{H} \ge 2$, and requiring preservation of the state cone (unitality) forces $V = U^*$, restricting physical frame automorphisms to unitary conjugations $X \mapsto U X U^*$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Finite-Dimensional Stalk**: Operator space $\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}) \cong M_d(\mathbb{C})$ on a Hilbert space of dimension $d < \infty$.
- **Unitary Group**: $\mathcal{U}(\mathcal{H}) = \{U \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H}) : U^* U = U U^* = I\}$.
- **Schatten Norm**: $\|X\|_{S_p} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^d \sigma_i(X)^p\right)^{1/p}$ for $p \in [1, \infty)$ and $\|X\|_{S_\infty} = \sigma_1(X)$.
- **Isometry Classification Import**: Imported classical results of Kadison (1951, *Isometries of Operator Algebras*) and Sourour (1981, *Isometries of C_p*).
- **Physical Axioms**:
  - *Complete Positivity (CP)*: The map $\Phi \otimes \mathrm{id}_k$ is positive for all ancillary dimensions $k \ge 1$.
  - *State Cone Preservation*: $\Phi$ maps the positive semidefinite cone $\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})_+$ into itself and preserves trace/unitality $\Phi(I) = I$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Direct Invariance: Unitary Stability of Singular Values
1. Let $X \in \mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$ and let $U, V \in \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{H})$ be unitary.
2. Form the positive semidefinite operator:
   $$(U X V)^* (U X V) = V^* X^* U^* U X V = V^* (X^* X) V.$$
3. Because $V$ is unitary, $V^* (X^* X) V$ is unitarily similar to $X^* X$.
4. Similar matrices possess identical characteristic polynomials:
   $$\det\left(\lambda I - V^* (X^* X) V\right) = \det\left(V^* (\lambda I - X^* X) V\right) = \det(\lambda I - X^* X).$$
5. The eigenvalues of $(U X V)^* (U X V)$ coincide with the eigenvalues of $X^* X$.
6. Because singular values are the non-negative square roots of these eigenvalues:
   $$\sigma_i(U X V) = \sigma_i(X) \quad \text{for all } i = 1, \dots, d.$$
7. Since any Schatten-$p$ norm is a symmetric gauge function of singular values:
   $$\|U X V\|_{S_p} = \|\sigma(U X V)\|_{\ell^p} = \|\sigma(X)\|_{\ell^p} = \|X\|_{S_p}.$$

### 3.2 Converse Classification (Imported)
1. **Kadison (1951) and Sourour (1981)**: For $p \in [1, \infty]$ with $p \ne 2$, every surjective linear isometry $\Phi: \mathcal{S}_p(\mathcal{H}) \to \mathcal{S}_p(\mathcal{H})$ decomposes into one of two forms:
   $$\Phi(X) = U X V \quad \text{or} \quad \Phi(X) = U X^T V$$
   for some unitaries $U, V \in \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{H})$.
2. **CP Filtering**:
   - The matrix transposition map $T: X \mapsto X^T$ is positive, but not completely positive for $d \ge 2$.
   - Explicit witness: applying $(\mathrm{id}_2 \otimes T)$ to the 2-qubit maximally entangled Bell projection $|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|$ yields eigenvalues $\{1/2, 1/2, 1/2, -1/2\}$. The negative eigenvalue $-1/2$ refutes complete positivity on composite systems.
   - Therefore, the premise of complete positivity excludes the transpose branch $\Phi(X) = U X^T V$.
3. **State Cone Positivity and Unitality**:
   - Requiring preservation of the positive cone and the maximally mixed state implies unitality: $\Phi(I) = I$.
   - Evaluating on the identity: $U I V = U V = I \implies V = U^{-1} = U^*$.
   - Consequently, the only admissible frame automorphisms preserving Schatten norms, complete positivity, and state cones are unitary conjugations:
     $$\Phi(X) = U X U^*.$$

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Excluded Exponent $p = 2$**: For $p = 2$ (the Hilbert–Schmidt / Frobenius norm), $\mathcal{S}_2(\mathcal{H})$ is a Hilbert space isomorphic to $\mathbb{C}^{d^2}$. Its isometry group is the full unitary group $\mathcal{U}(d^2)$, which strictly contains the subgroup of elementary tensor unitaries $U \otimes \bar{V}$. The Kadison–Sourour decomposition applies for $p \ne 2$.
- **Imported Converse**: The classification of all isometries is imported with standard citations, while the direct invariance and CP filtering are verified directly.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that Schatten-$p$ norms are invariant under unitary operations, and that complete positivity together with state-cone preservation selects unitary conjugation as the admissible stalk automorphism.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support claims that all isometries of the Frobenius norm ($p=2$) factor as $U X V$; does not claim novel derivation of Kadison's 1951 theorem.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   For any unitaries $U, V$ and any $X \in \mathbb{C}^{d \times d}$, $\|U X V\|_{S_p} = \|X\|_{S_p}$ holds for all $p \in [1, \infty]$, and transposition fails complete positivity on $2$-qubit entangled states.
4. **Next test needed**:
   Application to gauge-fixing algorithms on non-commutative operator presheaves.
