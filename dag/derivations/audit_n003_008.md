## 1. Claim Statement

On the oriented 3-cycle cell complex $C_3$ with integer cochain data $\boldsymbol{\omega} = (1, 0, 0)$, the $\ell^1$ quotient distance from $\boldsymbol{\omega}$ to the coboundary subspace $\operatorname{im}(d_0)$ satisfies $\Phi_1(\boldsymbol{\omega}) = 1$ both with and without 2-cell attachment:
$$\Phi_1(\boldsymbol{\omega}) = \min_{f \in \mathbb{Z}^V} \|\boldsymbol{\omega} - d_0 f\|_1 = 1.$$
Meanwhile, the closure defect $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h_1\|_1$ is undefined without a 2-cell ($C^2$ is absent) and evaluates to $1$ when a single oriented 2-cell is attached, yielding closure ratio $R_{cl} = \Gamma_2 / \Phi_1 = 1$.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Oriented Triangle Carrier**: Directed 3-cycle $C_3 = (V, E)$ with vertices $V = \{v_0, v_1, v_2\}$ and directed edges $e_0 = (v_0, v_1)$, $e_1 = (v_1, v_2)$, $e_2 = (v_2, v_0)$.
- **Integer Cochains**: 0-cochains $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$ and 1-cochains $\boldsymbol{\omega} \in \mathbb{Z}^E$.
- **0-Coboundary Operator**: $d_0: \mathbb{Z}^V \to \mathbb{Z}^E$ defined on edges by $(d_0 f)(u, v) = f(v) - f(u)$.
- **$\ell^1$ Quotient Distance**: $\Phi_1(\boldsymbol{\omega}) = \min_{f \in \mathbb{Z}^V} \|\boldsymbol{\omega} - d_0 f\|_1$, where $\|x\|_1 = \sum_{e \in E} |x_e|$.
- **Optional Single 2-Cell**: When absent, $C^2$ is empty and $d_1$ does not exist. When present, an oriented 2-cell $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$ has boundary $\partial \sigma = e_0 + e_1 + e_2$, giving $d_1: \mathbb{Z}^E \to \mathbb{Z}^F$ with $(d_1 \boldsymbol{\omega})_\sigma = \omega(e_0) + \omega(e_1) + \omega(e_2)$.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Circulation Sum Identity and Lower Bound
1. For any 0-cochain $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$, the circulation sum of $d_0 f$ around the cycle $C_3$ vanishes:
   $$\sum_{e \in C_3} (d_0 f)(e) = (f(v_1) - f(v_0)) + (f(v_2) - f(v_1)) + (f(v_0) - f(v_2)) = 0.$$
2. For the 1-cochain $\boldsymbol{\omega} = (1, 0, 0)$, the circulation sum evaluates to:
   $$\sum_{e \in C_3} \omega(e) = 1 + 0 + 0 = 1.$$
3. Applying the triangle inequality for any integer potential $f \in \mathbb{Z}^V$:
   $$\|\boldsymbol{\omega} - d_0 f\|_1 = \sum_{e \in E} |\omega(e) - (d_0 f)(e)| \ge \left| \sum_{e \in E} (\omega(e) - (d_0 f)(e)) \right| = |1 - 0| = 1.$$
4. At potential $f = (0, 0, 0)$, $d_0 f = (0, 0, 0)$, and:
   $$\|\boldsymbol{\omega} - d_0 f\|_1 = |1 - 0| + |0 - 0| + |0 - 0| = 1.$$
5. Because the lower bound is attained at an integer potential, $\Phi_1(\boldsymbol{\omega}) = 1$.

### 3.2 Invariance Under 2-Cell Attachment
1. The quotient distance $\Phi_1$ is evaluated entirely on the 1-skeleton $(V, E, d_0)$.
2. The definition and image of $d_0$ depend solely on 0-cells and 1-cells; attaching or omitting 2-cells leaves $V$, $E$, and $d_0$ unchanged.
3. Therefore, $\Phi_1(\boldsymbol{\omega}) = 1$ in both the graph-only model and the 2-cell complex model.

### 3.3 Closure Defect $\Gamma_2$ Divergence Across Configurations
1. In the graph-only model, $C^2$ is undeclared. The coboundary operator $d_1$ is absent, so $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h_1\|_1$ is undefined. Attempting to evaluate $d_1$ raises a typed error (`MissingComplex2Error`), and `Gamma2Readout` reports `Gamma2Status.NO_COMPLEX2` with value `None`.
2. When the 2-cell $\sigma = (0, 1, 2)$ is declared, $d_1: \mathbb{Z}^E \to \mathbb{Z}^F$ maps $h_1 = \boldsymbol{\omega} - d_0(0) = (1, 0, 0)$ to:
   $$(d_1 h_1)_\sigma = h_1(e_0) + h_1(e_1) + h_1(e_2) = 1 + 0 + 0 = 1.$$
3. Thus $\Gamma_2 = \|d_1 h_1\|_1 = |1| = 1$, and $R_{cl} = \Gamma_2 / \Phi_1 = 1 / 1 = 1$.

### 3.4 Verification and Test Witness Reference
Supported by executable tests in `tests/test_w2w2_shard_c.py`:
- `test_audit_n003_008_worked_replay_c3_with_and_without_two_cell_positive`: evaluates $\Phi_1 = 1$ on both configurations, confirms $\Gamma_2$ is undefined without 2-cell and equals 1 with 2-cell, and verifies $R_{cl} = 1$.
- `test_audit_n003_008_negative_control_phi1_invariant_to_two_cell`: confirms that $\Phi_1$ carries zero information regarding the presence of 2-cells.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

- **Integer $C_3$ Scope**: This derivation establishes degree separation on the specific integer triangle instance $C_3$.
- **Minimization Domain**: Minimization is evaluated over integer cochains; continuous optimization over real numbers and general polyhedral complexes remain outside this scope.
- **Closure Ratio Scope**: $R_{cl} = 1$ is an empirical consequence of the integer fixture data, not an assertion for arbitrary cell complexes.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**:
   Supports the conclusion that on the integer 3-cycle $C_3$, $\Phi_1(1, 0, 0) = 1$ independently of whether a 2-cell is declared, while $\Gamma_2$ is undefined in the 1-skeleton and equals $1$ in the 2-complex.
2. **What it does not support**:
   Does not support continuous real-valued minimization or closure ratios on arbitrary cell complexes.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**:
   On the integer $C_3$ triangle fixture with $\boldsymbol{\omega} = (1, 0, 0)$, $\Phi_1 = 1$ with and without the 2-cell, and $\Gamma_2 = 1$ once the 2-cell is attached.
