## 1. Claim Statement

Complementary to `PAPER004-CONDITIONAL-LEAN-SLICE` (which established by source inspection that the six Lean modules in `004_pfp_native_verification_and_evidence_custody/lean4/` formalize finite Boolean, integer, incidence-column, quotient, and point-set topology lemmas rather than the native Rust host or protocol receipts), this residual derivation establishes by clean out-of-tree compilation and deterministic parser checks that:
1. All six Lake root modules (`TotalUnimodularity`, `IdentityTypes`, `Topology50`, `SparseLanding`, `SnTower`, `SparseLandingGeneral`) compile with exit status `0` under the unpacked `lean4/lean-toolchain` pin (`leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`).
2. `lean4/Census.lean` and `lean4/axiom_report.txt` enumerate every `theorem` declaration across the six modules (`5 + 3 + 60 + 10 + 5 + 3 = 86` theorems total), with 47 axiom-free theorems and 39 theorems whose dependencies lie within the approved Lean kernel axiom set `{propext, Quot.sound, Classical.choice}`.
3. Exact report-string replay (`tools/replay_lean_axiom_census.py --replay`) exits `0` under the report's recorded `2026-09-28` toolchain (`leanprover/lean4:v4.34.0-rc1`) and exhibits a three-theorem `Classical.choice` elaboration delta in `SparseLandingGeneral.lean` under `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Formal Scope Boundary**: As recorded in `PAPER004-CONDITIONAL-LEAN-SLICE` and `paper.md` Section 8, none of the 86 Lean theorems formalizes the Rust `core_symbol_game` binary, `PacketEnvelopeV2`, JSON canonicalization, or any execution receipt field.
2. **Version-Binding Split**: The unpacked `lean4/lean-toolchain` specifies `v4.35.0`, whereas `lean4/axiom_report.txt` records `v4.34.0-rc1`; exact `--replay` equality holds at `v4.34.0-rc1`, while `v4.35.0` adds `Classical.choice` to the three `SymbolGame.NSP` theorems without introducing non-standard axioms or `sorryAx`.
3. **Named Theorem Scope vs. Auxiliary Declarations**: `axiom_report.txt` lists the 86 `theorem` declarations; generated recursors, constructors, and `def`/`structure` constants are cataloged separately in `lean4/full_axiom_report.txt` (316 package-local declarations).

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Establishes that all six Lean modules build cleanly (`exit 0`) under both `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0` and `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.0-rc1`, that `Census.lean` and `axiom_report.txt` cover all 86 `theorem` declarations (47 axiom-free, 39 within `{propext, Quot.sound, Classical.choice}`), and that exact `--replay` parity holds under `v4.34.0-rc1` with a three-theorem `Classical.choice` shift under `v4.35.0`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not establish mechanized verification of the Rust host, Python adapters, wire serialization, or full matrix-level total unimodularity.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The six Lean modules of Paper 004 compile with exit code `0` under `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`, and `Census.lean` / `axiom_report.txt` enumerate all 86 package theorems within `{propext, Quot.sound, Classical.choice}`, matching `axiom_report.txt` identically under `v4.34.0-rc1` and differing only by `Classical.choice` on the three `SymbolGame.NSP` theorems under `v4.35.0`.
4. **Next test needed**: Regenerating or dual-pinning `axiom_report.txt` if strict `replay_lean_axiom_census.py --replay` equality under `v4.35.0` is required in future release bundles.
