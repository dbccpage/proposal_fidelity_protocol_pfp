## 1. Claim Statement

The package verify_receipt_bytes refuses empty input, {}, certificate labels, non-hex event bytes and unrecomputed digest edits, and does not check fuel or arm scripts.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Package Verifier Implementation**:
  - `verify_receipt_bytes(data: bytes) -> dict[str, Any]` in `atlas/archive/pfp/003_pfp_host_admitted_failure_detectors/code/host_engine.py` (lines 407–501).
- **Receipt Payload**:
  - Assembled 30-cell JSON dictionary (`canonical_json(assemble_receipt())`).

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Refusal Classes of Package Verifier
In `host_engine.py` lines 407–501:
1. **Empty and Blank Input**: Lines 415–416 raise `DemoRefusal("INVALID_RECEIPT", ...)` on `b""` or `b"{}"`.
2. **Certificate Labels**:
   - Lines 454–459 raise `DemoRefusal("FORGED_CERTIFICATE", ...)` if `cell.get("terminal_exit") in ("SCOPED_CERTIFICATE", HostExit.SCOPED_CERTIFICATE)`.
   - Lines 471–476 raise `DemoRefusal("FORGED_CERTIFICATE", ...)` if step `host_decision in ("ACCEPTED_CERTIFICATE", "SCOPED_CERTIFICATE")`.
3. **Non-Hex Event Bytes**: Lines 483–486 raise `DemoRefusal("MALFORMED_STEP", ...)` if `bytes.fromhex(ev_hex)` fails.
4. **Unrecomputed Digest Edits**: Lines 488–494 verify digest chains, raising `DemoRefusal("CHAIN_DISCONTINUITY", ...)` on mismatched prior digests or `DemoRefusal("HASH_CHAIN_MISMATCH", ...)` on mismatched new digests.

### 3.2 Omission of Fuel and Scripted Arm Checks
1. Source inspection of `host_engine.py` lines 407–501 shows that:
   - Neither step fuel nor initial fuel is validated.
   - The arm coordinate is validated only for membership in `{"P", "T", "C", "R", "D", "A"}`; step decisions are not matched against arm scripts (arm D `HOLD_DETECTOR_FEEDBACK` is never checked).
2. Executable demonstration:
   - Altering step fuel or injecting arbitrary fuel values with recomputed digests is accepted by `verify_receipt_bytes`.
   - Modifying arm D's decision from `HOLD_DETECTOR_FEEDBACK` to `TOLERATED` with a recomputed digest chain is accepted by `verify_receipt_bytes`.
3. This establishes the stated omission: the package receipt verifier enforces hash-chain continuity and rejects certificate labels, but does not check fuel or arm scripts.

## 4. Limits & Non-claims

1. **What it supports**: Confirms the exact refusal boundary of `verify_receipt_bytes` in `host_engine.py` (empty, `{}`, certificate labels, non-hex bytes, unrecomputed digests) and confirms that fuel and arm scripts are omitted from validation.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not assert that omitting fuel or arm checks is secure for production use; documents the package demonstration's design boundary.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: `verify_receipt_bytes` in `host_engine.py` raises `DemoRefusal` on empty input, `b"{}"`, certificate labels, invalid hex, and unrecomputed digest edits, but accepts receipts with modified fuel or arm decisions when digest chains are recomputed.
4. **Next test needed**: Measuring verification performance when scaling to multi-step long-horizon transcripts.
