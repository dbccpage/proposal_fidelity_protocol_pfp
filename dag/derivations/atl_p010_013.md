## Claim

On the $3\times4$ torus with seam links $a,b$ and all others $e$, the connection is flat iff $ab=ba$, and the seam-corner plaquette product is $bab^{-1}a^{-1}$.

## Hypotheses

- Finite groups $S_3$, $Q_8$

## Derivation

Only the corner plaquette contains both seam links; its product is $bab^{-1}a^{-1}$, which is $e$ iff $ab=ba$; plaquettes with one seam link contain it twice with opposite orientation. Exhaust all $36+64$ pairs.
