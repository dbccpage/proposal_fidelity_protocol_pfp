## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Exact Lattice, Link-Ratio, Plaquette, and Skippable Recount for Table 1
For an $L \times M$ rectangular lattice (`holonomy.py:206-256`), positive edges and elementary plaquettes satisfy:
$$|E_+| = \begin{cases} L(M - 1) + (L - 1)M, & \text{open boundary}, \\ 2LM, & \text{periodic boundary}, \end{cases} \qquad |\mathcal{P}| = \begin{cases} (L - 1)(M - 1), & \text{open boundary}, \\ LM, & \text{periodic boundary}. \end{cases}$$
In `check_flatness(connection, skip_identity_links=True)` (`holonomy.py:373-415`), a plaquette $P_{r,c} = ((r,c), (r+1,c), (r+1,c+1), (r,c+1))$ (indices modulo $(L, M)$ when periodic) has four directed boundary factors (`Connection.plaquette_links`, `holonomy.py:303-305`)
$$F(P_{r,c}) = \bigl(U_{(r,c)\to(r+1,c)},\; U_{(r+1,c)\to(r+1,c+1)},\; U_{(r+1,c+1)\to(r,c+1)},\; U_{(r,c+1)\to(r,c)}\bigr),$$
and increments `skipped_identity_plaquettes` if and only if $F(P_{r,c}) = (e, e, e, e)$ (for which $e \cdot e \cdot e \cdot e = e$ identically). Evaluating `host_rows()` (`replay_c017.py:24-47`) and `check_flatness` on all five retained fixtures yields exact Table 1 parity (`paper.md:287-294`):

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Complementary Scope**: Complements `PAPER010-DATA-LOCAL-VERSUS-GLOBAL-HOLONOMY` and `PAPER010-REPLAY-AND-RECOUNT-CEILING` without re-claiming model pilot replay beyond the five Table 1 fixtures.
2. **Gauge Dependence of Skippable Count**: As noted in `study_guide.md:56` and `paper.md:362-363`, `skipped_identity_plaquettes` counts four-identity boundary tuples in the supplied link presentation; a non-constant vertex gauge transformation changes `skipped_identity_plaquettes` (for example, `base_02` is `FLAT_TRIVIAL` with `0` skippable plaquettes because `28 of 31` positive links are non-identity) without altering flatness or conjugacy class.
3. **Historical vs Remediated Harness Checkers**: At the historical pilot commits (`7e639a0b`, `9e45ee50`, `5327cfcb`, `eb5fc971`), both C017 checkers imported plaquette enumeration and path multiplication from the reference solver (`paper.md:369-371`); current in-repo `algebraic_holonomy_checker.py` and `independent_checker.py` reflect the `2026-09-29` self-contained remediation.

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Confirms every lattice dimension, boundary mode, positive-edge ratio (`1/12`, `1/17`, `28/31`, `11/60`, `1/31`), plaquette count (`4, 6, 12, 30, 12`), skippable four-identity count (`3, 5, 0, 20, 11`), and host verdict in Table 1, plus 25/25 `harness_expected_verdict` agreement and upstream `solve` / `evaluate_dual` parity.
