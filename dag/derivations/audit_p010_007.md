## 1. Claim Statement

Let $(G, \cdot, e, (\cdot)^{-1})$ be any group, let $U$ be a connection on an open or periodic $L \times M$ lattice assigning $U_{uv} \in G$ to each positive edge $(u, v) \in E^+$ with $U_{vu} = U_{uv}^{-1}$ on reversed edges, and let $g : V \to G$ be any vertex gauge transforming positive edges by $U'_{uv} = g_u U_{uv} g_v^{-1}$. Then:
1. **Directional consistency**: Reversed edges automatically satisfy $U'_{vu} = (U'_{uv})^{-1} = g_v U_{vu} g_u^{-1}$, so $U'_{xy} = g_x U_{xy} g_y^{-1}$ holds on every directed lattice step $(x, y)$.
2. **Path telescoping and plaquette conjugation**: Along any directed path $\gamma = (v_0, v_1, \dots, v_k)$, $\operatorname{hol}_{U'}(\gamma) = g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(\gamma) g_{v_k}^{-1}$. In particular, for every plaquette $P_{r,c} = (v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3)$ based at its first vertex $v_0 = (r, c)$ ($v_4 = v_0$),
   $$W'_{r,c} = \operatorname{hol}_{U'}(P_{r,c}) = g_{v_0} W_{r,c} g_{v_0}^{-1}.$$
3. **Identity invariance**: $W_{r,c} = e \iff W'_{r,c} = e$, so local plaquette flatness and the total count of non-flat plaquettes are invariant under `gauge_transform`.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Compatibility of Stored Positive-Edge Gauge Action with Reversed-Edge Inversion
In `code/holonomy.py`, a `Connection` stores links only on `lattice.positive_edges()` ($E^+$), and `gauge_transform(connection, gauge)` (lines 315–323) computes:
$$U'_{uv} = (g_u \cdot U_{uv}) \cdot g_v^{-1} \qquad \text{for every } (u, v) \in E^+.$$
In each plaquette $P_{r,c} = (v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3)$, the first two steps $(v_0, v_1)$ and $(v_1, v_2)$ lie in $E^+$, whereas the third and fourth steps $(v_2, v_3)$ and $(v_3, v_0)$ traverse the positive edges $(v_3, v_2) \in E^+$ and $(v_0, v_3) \in E^+$ against their stored orientation. When `Connection.link(x, y)` (lines 284–289) is called on a reversed edge $(v, u)$ with $(u, v) \in E^+$, it returns `(U'_{uv})^{-1}`. Using the group anti-automorphism $(abc)^{-1} = c^{-1} b^{-1} a^{-1}$ and $(g_v^{-1})^{-1} = g_v$:
$$U'_{vu} = (U'_{uv})^{-1} = \bigl(g_u U_{uv} g_v^{-1}\bigr)^{-1} = (g_v^{-1})^{-1} U_{uv}^{-1} g_u^{-1} = g_v U_{vu} g_u^{-1}.$$
Consequently, the covariance formula $U'_{xy} = g_x U_{xy} g_y^{-1}$ holds uniformly for both forward and reversed directed lattice edges.

### 3.2 Induction Along Arbitrary Directed Paths and Specialization to $P_{r,c}$
Let $\gamma = (v_0, v_1, \dots, v_k)$ ($k \ge 1$) be any directed lattice path. We show by induction on $k$ that:
$$\operatorname{hol}_{U'}(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) = g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) g_{v_k}^{-1}.$$
- **Base case ($k = 1$)**: $\operatorname{hol}_{U'}(v_0 \to v_1) = U'_{v_0 v_1} = g_{v_0} U_{v_0 v_1} g_{v_1}^{-1} = g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to v_1) g_{v_1}^{-1}$ by Section 3.1.
- **Inductive step ($k \to k + 1$)**: Assuming the identity for $k$, left-folded multiplication in `FiniteGroup.product` (`holonomy.py` lines 117–121) gives:
  $$\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{hol}_{U'}(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_{k+1})
  &= \operatorname{hol}_{U'}(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) \cdot U'_{v_k v_{k+1}} \\
  &= \bigl(g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) g_{v_k}^{-1}\bigr)\bigl(g_{v_k} U_{v_k v_{k+1}} g_{v_{k+1}}^{-1}\bigr) \\
  &= g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) (g_{v_k}^{-1} g_{v_k}) U_{v_k v_{k+1}} g_{v_{k+1}}^{-1} \\
  &= g_{v_0} \bigl(\operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_k) U_{v_k v_{k+1}}\bigr) g_{v_{k+1}}^{-1} \\
  &= g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(v_0 \to \cdots \to v_{k+1}) g_{v_{k+1}}^{-1}.
  \end{aligned}$$

Setting $k = 4$ on the closed plaquette boundary $P_{r,c} = ((r,c), (r_1,c), (r_1,c_1), (r,c_1))$ with $v_4 = v_0 = (r, c)$ yields:
$$W'_{r,c} = \operatorname{hol}_{U'}(v_0 \to v_1 \to v_2 \to v_3 \to v_0) = g_{v_0} W_{r,c} g_{v_0}^{-1} = g_{(r,c)} W_{r,c} g_{(r,c)}^{-1},$$
matching `moved.plaquette_holonomy(r, c) == conjugate(group, gauge[(r, c)], connection.plaquette_holonomy(r, c))` in `test_holonomy_checker.py` (lines 227–229). The same closed-path specialization at $v_0 = (0, 0)$ shows that torus fundamental cycles `loop_a` and `loop_b` transform simultaneously by $(A', B') = (g_{(0,0)} A g_{(0,0)}^{-1},\, g_{(0,0)} B g_{(0,0)}^{-1})$.

### 3.3 Identity Invariance vs. Non-Central Element Conjugation
For any fixed $g_{v_0} \in G$, inner conjugation $\operatorname{Ad}_{g_{v_0}}(x) = g_{v_0} x g_{v_0}^{-1}$ has two-sided inverse $\operatorname{Ad}_{g_{v_0}^{-1}}$ since $g_{v_0}^{-1} (g_{v_0} x g_{v_0}^{-1}) g_{v_0} = x$, and satisfies $\operatorname{Ad}_{g_{v_0}}(e) = g_{v_0} e g_{v_0}^{-1} = e$. Therefore:
$$W_{r,c} = e \iff W'_{r,c} = g_{v_0} W_{r,c} g_{v_0}^{-1} = e.$$
Every flat plaquette remains flat and every non-flat plaquette remains non-flat under any vertex gauge $g$, preserving `FlatnessReport.nonflat_plaquettes` and the row-major position of `witness_plaquette` in `check_flatness` (`holonomy.py` lines 373–391). By contrast, whenever $W_{r,c} \notin Z(G)$ (for example any non-identity element of $S_3$, where $Z(S_3) = \{\text{"012"}\}$, or any order-$4$ unit $\pm i, \pm j, \pm k \in Q_8$, where $Z(Q_8) = \{\text{"e"}, \text{"-e"}\}$) and $g_{v_0} \notin C_G(W_{r,c})$, the transformed plaquette value $W'_{r,c} = g_{v_0} W_{r,c} g_{v_0}^{-1}$ differs from $W_{r,c}$.

## 4. Limits and Non-Claims

1. **Finite-Test Host Verification Boundary**: While the telescoping identity holds in every abstract group, execution of `code/holonomy.py` verifies host agreement on bounded finite lattices over $S_3$ and $Q_8$; `code/holonomy.py` is not formalized in Lean 4.
2. **Basepoint Dependence for Non-Abelian Groups**: Conjugation preserves the conjugacy class of $W_{r,c}$ (and hence $W_{r,c} = e$), not the raw group element $W_{r,c}$ itself when $G$ is non-abelian and $W_{r,c} \notin Z(G)$.
3. **Skippable-Plaquette Non-Invariance**: Although $W_{r,c} = e \iff W'_{r,c} = e$ is gauge-invariant, the optimization predicate `all(x == e for x in factors)` (`holonomy.py` line 382) is not gauge-invariant (`skipped_identity_plaquettes` can drop from $LM$ to $0$ under a pure gauge with $g_u \neq g_v$ on adjacent vertices).

## 5. Claim Hygiene Summary

1. **What it supports**: Derives $U'_{vu} = g_v U_{vu} g_u^{-1}$ on reversed edges, open-path endpoint covariance $\operatorname{hol}_{U'}(\gamma) = g_{v_0} \operatorname{hol}_U(\gamma) g_{v_k}^{-1}$, plaquette conjugation $W'_{r,c} = g_{(r,c)} W_{r,c} g_{(r,c)}^{-1}$, and flatness equivalence $W_{r,c} = e \iff W'_{r,c} = e$, backed by exhaustive $2 \times 2$ $S_3$ connection-by-gauge checks and multi-lattice $S_3 / Q_8$ tests against `code/holonomy.py`.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not imply gauge invariance of individual non-central plaquette elements $W_{r,c}$ or gauge invariance of `skipped_identity_plaquettes`, and does not provide a mechanical Lean formalization of Python runtime code.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: Under $U'_{uv} = g_u U_{uv} g_v^{-1}$ and $U_{vu} = U_{uv}^{-1}$ in any group, intermediate vertex gauge factors cancel along each plaquette boundary so that $W'_{r,c} = g_{(r,c)} W_{r,c} g_{(r,c)}^{-1}$, preserving $W_{r,c} = e \iff W'_{r,c} = e$.
4. **Next test needed**: Pair `gauge_transform` property checks with `SL(2, F_3)` tables if C017's third group is added to the archived host module.
