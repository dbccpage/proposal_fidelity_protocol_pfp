## 1. Claim Statement

verify_receipt_bytes recomputes each of the 30 cells' digest chains from the initial digest, checks fuel and scripted arm decisions, and refuses {}, schema changes, chain breaks and certificate claims.

## 2. Context & Hypotheses

- **Baseline Verifier Specification**:
  - Validates a 30-cell matrix spanning 5 declared fixtures (`KNOWN_FIXTURES`) and 6 arms (`P`, `T`, `C`, `R`, `D`, `A`).
  - Recomputes SHA-256 state digest chains starting from each cell's `initial_digest(fixture_id, arm)`.
- **Integrity Bounds**:
  - Enforces continuous step digests `chain_digest(expected_digest, event_bytes, host_decision)`.
  - Enforces fuel accounting consistency across batch steps.
  - Enforces scripted arm decisions (such as arm D `HOLD_DETECTOR_FEEDBACK` and standard moves ending in `NO_NATIVE_PORT`).
  - Refuses unpopulated receipts, schema mutations, chain discontinuities, and certificate claims.

## 3. Derivation / Evidence

### 3.1 Digest Chain Recomputation
1. For each cell in the 30-cell matrix:
   - The cell establishes initial digest $D_0 = \text{SHA256}(\text{"DEMO3\_INIT:"} \parallel f\_id \parallel \text{":"} \parallel arm)$.
   - For each step $j \in [0, k-1]$:
     - The verifier verifies that `prior_state_digest` equals $D_j$.
     - The verifier recomputes $D_{j+1} = \text{chain\_digest}(D_j, \text{event\_bytes}, \text{host\_decision})$.
     - The recorded `new_state_digest` must match $D_{j+1}$.
2. Discontinuous prior digests raise `CHAIN_DISCONTINUITY`.
3. Mismatched or tampered state digests raise `HASH_CHAIN_MISMATCH`.

### 3.2 Fuel and Arm Script Checks
1. The baseline verifier checks that step fuel reflects consistent accounting without unwarranted refunds or unrecorded fuel gains. Edited fuel numbers raise `FUEL_VERIFICATION_FAILED`.
2. The verifier checks scripted arm decisions across the matrix:
   - Arm D must record `HOLD_DETECTOR_FEEDBACK`.
   - Arms P, T, C, R, and A must match their scripted execution paths ending in `NO_NATIVE_PORT`.
   - Any divergence in arm decision triggers `ARM_SCRIPT_MISMATCH`.

### 3.3 Refusal Classes and Negative Controls
1. **Empty / Blank Input**: An empty payload `b""` or blank dictionary `b"{}"` triggers `INVALID_RECEIPT`.
2. **Schema Field Edits**: Receipts with missing coordinates, cell count not equal to 30, duplicate cell coordinates, or missing steps trigger `INCOMPLETE_MATRIX`, `MALFORMED_CELL`, or `DUPLICATE_CELL`.
3. **Chain Breaks**: Alterations to prior or new digests trigger `CHAIN_DISCONTINUITY` or `HASH_CHAIN_MISMATCH`.
4. **Certificate Claims**: A terminal exit claiming `SCOPED_CERTIFICATE` or a step decision claiming `ACCEPTED_CERTIFICATE` triggers `FORGED_CERTIFICATE`.

## 4. Limits & Non-claims

1. **What it supports**: Confirms that within the audited 30-cell matrix specification, the baseline receipt verifier recomputes all 30 digest chains from initial digests, validates fuel and arm scripts, and rejects empty inputs, schema deviations, broken chains, and uncertified claims.
2. **What it does not support**: Does not establish cryptographic non-repudiation or asymmetric producer signatures; digests authenticate internal state consistency across the known matrix, not author identity.
3. **Narrowest defensible claim**: The baseline verifier checks all 30 cells' SHA-256 digest chains from initial digests, enforces fuel and scripted arm decisions, and returns typed refusals for empty input, schema variations, chain breaks, and certificate labels.
4. **Next test needed**: Testing verifier performance on expanded long-horizon execution matrices beyond single-batch cells.
