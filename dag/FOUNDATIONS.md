# What the implemented mathematics supports

This is a working synthesis at commit `8050c66900899771766f33e8f5fdd200b7dd9120`, with source evidence and open obligations in `derivations_dag.json`. The substantive source audit is unfinished. The repository contains useful finite mathematics, operational protocols, and speculative extensions with different evidential status.

## PFP: a witness relation and bounded checking protocol

The basic mathematical object is a declared relation between input and witness, `R(x,w)`, together with a checker `V(x,w)`. PFP prepares a typed task, binds task/attempt/input identities, obtains a proposal, invokes host checkers, and records the outcome. A soundness implication `V(x,w)=accept => R(x,w)` needs a correct checker; the additional implication from `R` to the user's intended claim needs an adequate task encoding. Schemas, hashes, model agreement and terminal labels do not supply these implications.

One substantive implemented instance is the finite weighted graph repair problem

`min_u sum_e w_e |c_e - (u(head(e))-u(tail(e)))|`, with `w_e>0`.

The C011 checker verifies a primal repair `r=c-d0(u)`, a circulation `y` with `|y_e|<=w_e`, and equality between the primal objective and `sum_e c_e y_e`. For every competing potential `v`, circulation gives `y·d0(v)=0`, and the bounds give `y·c <= sum_e w_e |c_e-d0(v)_e|`. Attaining that lower bound certifies an optimum for this finite declared problem. This is useful mathematics; a translation from a QM derivation or natural-language argument into such a graph remains a separate obligation.

PFP Lab additionally implements bounded spanning-tree enumeration, exact rational defects, projection, and explicit ASSERT/CUT/VERIFY/RETRACT transitions. Some CUT transitions enforce nonincrease. ASSERT has an optional monotonicity flag, and RETRACT restores earlier states, so the entire interaction is not automatically a monotone convergence process. See `pfp/domain/cohomology/certificate_checker.py`, `validation.py`, and `pfp_lab/core/`.

## EIS: finite detector tests and explicit activation gates

EIS implements candidate matching, testing against supplied clean fixtures, and activation/suppression gates that require named signals and custody conditions. Its mathematical content includes Boolean conjunction, exact threshold sums, and finite support-graph selection. Zero firing on a finite clean panel is a statement about that panel. It does not by itself establish population false-positive rates, semantic completeness, independence of observers, or protection against unknown failures.

In particular, a maximum independent set over declared correlation keys is a graph-theoretic calculation; it is not evidence that the underlying sources are statistically independent. A two-signal gate requires an independently justified host-health signal if it is to mean more than two correlated model assertions. See `eis/domain/thymus.py`, `danger_gate.py`, `allostery.py`, `provisional_promotion.py` and the systems audit.

The completed 146-file declaration/evidence review checks the proposed detectors against their own examples. Among 54 regex/fixture pairs, ordinary matching separates the bad example from the good in only 10 pairs; 41 match neither and three match both. Broader case/dot matching changes the first two counts to 11 and 40. These are bounded fixture results, not population accuracy estimates. The 1,069-record catalog includes repeated templates, mined fragments and historical logs; every semantic record was covered through pinned bases and complete differences, with exact reconstruction independently checked. Repetition, a stored repair label or a successful process exit does not establish an effective detector or semantic mutation kill. See `reviews/additional_eis_artifacts_remaining.json` and `reviews/eis146_parent_integration.json`.

## PCIV: lineage, controlled mutation and metric-relative selection

PCIV supplies protected-span mutation, prompt lineage records, fitness calculations and Pareto-style selection. A candidate is nondominated only relative to the supplied objective vector and comparison convention. This does not prove improved performance on unseen tasks, convergence, or causal learning. A lineage seal binds recorded fields; it does not establish that the recorded causal event actually occurred.

The cochain profile is especially important: admission enforces `d1*d0=0`, and its residual is formed from `d0`. The corresponding `phi1` is therefore zero for every admitted input. That algebraic identity is valid, but cannot by itself discriminate good reasoning from bad reasoning. A useful semantic detector needs an independently justified extraction and target property. See `pciv/profile/cochain_profile.py`, `pciv/use_cases/fitness.py`, `genealogy.py`, `immunity.py` and `lamarckian_loop.py`.

## OPERADS: typed substitution with additional execution obligations

The core combinatorial structure is a collection of operations with ordered typed input ports and an output type, composed by inserting a compatible operation into a matching slot. The repository contains signature-level planar substitution and a separate callable composition layer. Type compatibility prevents some wiring errors; it does not establish that operation bodies compute the intended function or preserve a semantic query.

Associativity of executable composition additionally depends on body behavior and the laws of accumulated residual/effect values. The audit reproduced a counterexample when an accepted NaN risk enters residual accumulation. HoTT-labeled path metadata is not thereby a proof term in homotopy type theory. Semantic quotients require actual congruence/preservation witnesses; descriptive type names and scalar diagnostics cannot supply them. See `operads/domain/operad/planar.py`, `operads/skill_operad/domain/planar.py`, `evidence.py`, `hott.py` and `quotient_operad.py`.

The completed 578-file skills review preserves useful premise, custody, task-binding and both-polarity detector checks, but identifies mathematical errors in several proposed rules. An exact gauge shift cannot strictly decrease distance to the same repair image; dual feasibility alone is a lower bound rather than an optimality certificate; an arbitrary idempotent projection need not minimize L1 distance. Nilpotence, closedness, exactness and existence of global sections are distinct conditions. An explicit two-dimensional channel also refutes the stated unrestricted skew-information inequality. These findings concern the displayed rules and examples, not every runtime owner that uses related terminology. The report records 14 qualified claims, 129 findings and 104 observed independent controls, with one shell control unavailable. No skill workflow was invoked. See `reviews/additional_operad_skills_remaining.json`.

The finite ACSet/DPO executor is another substantive instance: it constructs bounded attributed-graph rewrites from explicit injective matches and identity-key interface inclusions, with dangling, protection and fuel checks. Independent judges then compare protected ordered prompt bytes or check every rational cochain residual against a supplied fixed potential. The separation is useful: a structurally valid rewrite can still change the protected query, and aggregate residual cancellation does not establish edgewise exactness. Both imported ACSet pilots already occur in WF21; their current legacy import/resource paths prevent an end-to-end run in this checkout. See `reviews/additional_imported_acset.json`.

The completed 153-file native slice also exposes gaps between labels and enforced predicates. A public replay-sealing API can promote caller-constructed comparison metadata into a `CertifiedIsomorphism` without a replay or key; a foundation interpreter executes a callback despite an empty capability slice; a Koszul-resolution flag depends on a presentation name. These are implementation counterexamples, not disproofs of operad or Koszul theory. Native EIS promotion can label a receipt `REVIEWED` without executing the holdout checks asserted in its reason, and finite-integer PCIV residual arithmetic can overflow. Thus an API label is not a mathematical certificate unless the actual path enforces its premises. Exact reproductions and scope limits are in `reviews/additional_native_systems_03.json`.

## What the formal sources establish

The ongoing Rust linear-algebra slice has fully reviewed 32 of 83 files. It contains substantive determinant, Pfaffian, PSD, image-membership, basis-change and cochain-map checks. Rational report invariance under repairs is enforced by the exact equation `R*d0=0`. Semiring lumpability checks `M*H=H*B`; associativity and distributivity then give `M^n*H=H*B^n`, preserving the declared endpoint-block aggregates. These mechanisms need lawful scalar operations, admitted shapes and an adequate application encoding. In contrast, the rank-nullity count wrapper can combine a reduced matrix and kernel from different sources; the separate public repair-space metadata counts duplicate vectors as rank. A semiring operation ceiling counts product terms while each term makes two scalar calls. Twenty-one independent finite controls support the bounded assessments; no original Rust execution is claimed. See `reviews/additional_rust_linear_algebra_remaining.json`.

The exact-field solver separately replays either a solution or a dual inconsistency witness. The centralizer is constructed as the kernel of the complete commutator equations, with every returned basis matrix checked against all generators. The pseudoinverse replays its full-rank factorization and all four Penrose identities. Berkowitz characteristic coefficients agree with independent permutation determinants in the finite controls; minimal polynomials agree with exhaustive monic search. These are meaningful algebraic constructions under their coefficient assumptions. Commutativity is required for the claimed ring extension, and an involution alone gives a Hermitian form rather than a positive norm. Smaller interface gaps remain: empty-axis extraction skips index admission, raw rational conversion omits canonical-value admission, and some composite functions restart their scalar-operation allowance at each phase. Per-step limits therefore must not be substituted for a whole-workflow cost cap.

The ordered-field and congruence owners make the additional positivity premises explicit. Elementary congruence operations track an invertible basis transform, with a separate verifier for the complete matrix equation. LDL produces either reconstructing positive-semidefinite factors or a vector with a negative quadratic form; 760 small symmetric rational controls replay those alternatives and agree with the all-principal-minor criterion. A stale documentation paragraph calls this PSD procedure incomplete using an impossible positive-definite example, while the implementation and later tests use the complete rule. Universal order and conjugation laws remain implementor obligations rather than consequences of checking a few sign values.

The completed 24-file Rust HoTT slice includes useful finite predicates alongside explicit shape-only interfaces. Equal finite carrier sizes make its one-sided inverse test sufficient for mutual invertibility. Its primitive-difference checker tests equivalence and constancy on quotient fibers, conditional on stable provider callbacks; its presentation evaluator detects strict refinement on well-formed answer tables. Public malformed tables and a mutable provider can bypass those premises. These are finite set and information-loss constructions, not a HoTT kernel or an implementation of univalence. See `reviews/additional_rust_hott_remaining.json`.

Three higher-algebra drafts in that slice are disabled by `cfg(any())`. Their defects must therefore remain separate from active runtime behavior. In particular, the dormant A-infinity formality checker treats a nonzero higher multiplication as sufficient for nonformality. An independent exact example over F2 has a nonzero third multiplication but explicit inverse A-infinity maps to a strict algebra; the control verifies every potentially nonzero basis identity and accounts for the remaining arities. The same draft drops higher morphism components during composition and applies a strict-DGA Massey representative without the required restriction or higher correction. These are concrete mathematical reasons to revise the draft, rather than evidence against the underlying theories. The slice contains 13 independent controls and no original Rust or proof-assistant execution.

The Rust category-theory review distinguishes substantive finite constructions from names and count records. Several owners admit explicit finite carriers and replay complete finite witnesses: query descent requires constancy on report fibers; Moore refinement computes indistinguishability under all finite input words of the admitted finite machine; partial-structure descent checks definedness, output classes and predicate values; presheaf cover checks distinguish absent, unique and ambiguous gluing. The validated finite nerve requires an admitted category and complete bounded enumeration or refusal. These are useful exact finite mechanisms. None supplies an independently justified map from natural-language reasoning or physical observations into its declared carrier. The detailed source premises are recorded in `reviews/additional_rust_category_remaining.json`.

Other owners omit the premises their names suggest. The older coend implementation uses a common-target equality where coend relations require a common source; the walking-arrow Hom bifunctor gives a concrete wrong quotient. The higher-category constructor returns success without validation. The rewrite checker can accept formal-inverse zigzags as local confluence even when two forward branches end in distinct irreducible terms, so its success does not establish the usual termination-plus-directed-local-confluence theorem. Numeric hom counts, capacity ledgers and caller-supplied coherence flags remain conditional metadata. The audit's independent finite controls establish these scoped countermodels; no original Rust build or test execution is claimed for this slice.

The reviewed Lean files make useful distinctions explicit. `FidelityAdequacy.lean` checks eight finite fields and exhibits separation from detector adequacy. Its positive and negative controls rule out a constant detector, but do not establish complete detection over an arbitrary domain. `OperationTreeFold.lean` proves internal effect closure for an admitted operation table whose entries are explicitly assigned no authority effect. The induction does not derive the interpretation of that table or a universal authority principle. A fresh Lean v4.35.0 build and axiom census passed for the nine kernel modules: 1,144 constants, 375 non-internal theorem constants (including generated lemmas and proposition-valued fields), with only `propext` and `Quot.sound` among their axiom dependencies. This checks the formal statements under their definitions and premises, not their intended runtime correspondence.

`TypedPacketShadow.lean` proves a useful conditional obstruction: if two source states have the same packet but different target values, no function of that packet alone recovers the target everywhere. Its concrete example intentionally omits one Boolean distinction; it does not prove that all packets are inadequate. `LiftPatchSemantics.lean` constructs a decreasing-budget, increasing-count lift under explicit positive-cost and applicability premises. It does not establish semantic knowledge gain. `TowerZeta.lean` uses affine natural-number towers and arbitrary integer-valued `zetaAt` functions, including the zero function; its names and Boolean admission gates are not an analytic zeta-function construction or a Riemann-hypothesis result.

The remaining `PrimitiveDifference.lean` and `ShadowBoundary.lean` developments make quotient and information-loss premises explicit. Factoring an operation through a zero-difference equivalence requires congruence; a supplied Boolean countermodel refutes automatic congruence. Recovering a target from a shadow requires constancy on its fibers, and recovering a lost distinction requires information that separates the conflicting states. Appending the target itself makes recovery a projection; it does not derive that target or verify an extraction procedure. These are conditional mathematical results under declared relations and maps, rather than a derivation of a physical or semantic foundation from an unqualified primitive. See `reviews/additional_lab_clients_formal.json`.

## Consequences for the benchmark

The completed 440-path PFP test/support review separates 415 full source reads from 25 bounded JSON assessments. It finds meaningful finite certificate and custody tests, but also tests that check comments, fixed labels or local stand-ins rather than the stated owner behavior. A standalone gauge-repair harness replaces a nonexact triangle cochain by zero although a coboundary shift cannot change its cycle sum. A higher-simplicial test calls graph cycle rank the first Betti number even when filled faces kill every cycle. Several negative tests violate multiple obligations simultaneously and therefore cannot establish enforcement of the named obligation. These are scoped test or test-local-helper findings; they do not establish that an unexecuted production owner has the same defect. The report contains 61 qualified claims, 126 findings and 81 bounded controls; no broad suite pass or fresh native/provider result is claimed. See `reviews/additional_pfp_tests_remaining.json` and `reviews/pfp_tests_440_parent_integration.json`.

The systems offer candidate-generation control, typed interfaces, finite exact certificates, failure screening and traceable experiments. They do not currently provide a verified end-to-end checker for the entire frozen graduate-QM seed. The experiment must distinguish ordinary prompt techniques from actual native-system integrations, mathematical correctness from schema acceptance, and a new proposed composition from an executable or empirically successful workflow.
