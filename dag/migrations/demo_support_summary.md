classification. Per-file reports state the depth of inspection; this is not a
full mathematical review of every body or a fresh validation of historical
model results.

The **300 moved files** contain 11,966,171 original bytes. private engine's 216 files
are now in `pilots/imported_pilots_support/`; PFP's 84 files are now in
`pilots/education/`. All 300 original paths are absent. private engine's move includes
202 tracked files and 14 incidental untracked bytecode caches.

Every destination was copied and verified before any source removal. A separate
recovery ZIP preserves every original byte and path; all members passed SHA-256,
size and CRC checks. Its SHA-256 is
`db851cc2bbe8b5b01f1a4d1cc565a224e8fb2a13a6da8efdb8add640e18d3805`.
The frozen plan SHA-256 is
`e35c7e476383487c0d24dd828d44be1a8350e7bb4d5ab3dbf57388d744f43724`.

Sixty-five consumer changes were applied separately: 13 in private engine and 52 in
PFP. Their originals have a second verified recovery archive. Navigation now
points to the relocated owners, and notebook outputs remain unchanged.
private engine's retained harness requires explicit pricing inputs and reports absent
historical certificate checkers before provider dispatch. It does not silently
bind the PFP corpus into a live campaign. Dedicated corpus tests travel with
their data; unrelated runtime tests remain in private engine.

Retained source owners are the live graph integration fixture, production
research profiles, provider regression evidence, reusable runtime guides and
ordinary curriculum. Their demo references do not make those owners pilot
corpora.
