## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF31 structures competitive multi-agent benchmarking using **held-out cross-validation**. When evaluating multiple model architectures, prompt configurations, or algorithmic agents, evaluation on public problem parameters often leads to overfitting.

WF31 presents each competing agent with an unmasked training sub-instance (e.g. 70% of graph edge constraints) to establish their proposed potential or solution, but evaluates and ranks their performance exclusively on a strictly held-out test partition (the remaining 30%) that the proposer never observed. A random-choice baseline serves as a persistent negative control.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Tournament Ingress:** Prepare a battery of mathematical instances (e.g. gauge potentials in $\operatorname{SO}(4N)$ or graph colorings).
2. **Train/Test Splitting:** For each instance, partition the constraints into an observed training share (70%) and a sealed held-out share (30%):
   $$X = X_{\text{train}} \cup X_{\text{held-out}}, \quad X_{\text{train}} \cap X_{\text{held-out}} = \emptyset$$
3. **Parallel Dispatch:** Dispatch $X_{\text{train}}$ to all competing model agents and the random baseline.
4. **Held-Out Adjudication:** The host evaluates the candidate solutions against $X_{\text{held-out}}$ using an exact rational referee.
5. **Budget-Normalized Ranking:** Rank competitors based on held-out score normalized by token and financial expenditure.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Exact Rational Referee

- **Special Orthogonal $\operatorname{SO}(4N)$ Gauge Consistency:**
  For rotation matrices $R \in \mathbb{Q}^{4N \times 4N}$:
  $$R^T R = I, \quad \det(R) = +1, \quad \|R v - w\|_1 \le \epsilon_{\text{tol}}$$
- **Held-Out Generalization Score:**
  $$\text{Score}_{\text{held-out}}(A) = \frac{1}{|X_{\text{held-out}}|} \sum_{e \in X_{\text{held-out}}} \mathbf{1}[\operatorname{check}(A, e) == \text{PASS}]$$
- **Negative Control Invariant:**
  If the random baseline scores $> 0$ on the held-out partition, the instance is flagged as unconstrained or redundant and discarded from tournament standings.

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Information Isolation:** The host guarantees under declared assumptions that $X_{\text{held-out}}$ bytes are never transmitted to competitor prompts.
- **Rational Arithmetic:** Evaluates matrix determinants and inner products in exact rational arithmetic.

2. **What the Evidence Does Not Support:**
   - Tournament ranking does not imply universal intelligence or general problem-solving ability outside the specific tournament matrix.
3. **Narrowest Defensible Claim:**
   - WF31 specifies a cross-validated tournament harness that ranks model proposals based on generalization to strictly held-out constraint subsets.
