## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF20 governs multi-round theoretical mathematical derivations where lower-dimensional formalisms encounter structural obstructions. When a derivation in an existing language cannot close a defect, the host issues an explicit **Lift Grant**—licensing the introduction of higher-dimensional structures (e.g. elevating 1-cochains to 2-cochains, or flat connections to curved gauge holonomies).

The core principle is **controlled structural escalation**: models are prohibited from inventing arbitrary mathematical machinery on the fly. Higher mathematical abstractions can only be invoked when granted by the host after proving that the lower-dimensional problem has a non-zero obstruction.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Named Obligations:** The host specifies a set of explicit formal obligations $\Omega = \{O_1, \dots, O_m\}$.
2. **Derivation Turn ($t$):** The model emits a derivation step attempting to satisfy $\Omega$ within the current licensed structure.
3. **Finite Witness Verification:** The host evaluates the candidate derivation against exact finite mathematical controls.
4. **Adversarial Negative Control:** The adversary checks whether the step holds vacuously or trivializes the problem.
5. **Lift Grant Evaluation:**
   - If the derivation closes the defect: Admitted.
   - If blocked by an obstruction: The host evaluates whether to grant a higher categorical or cochain lift (e.g. licensing 2-form curvature).
6. **Refinement Loop:** State advances to $(P_{t+1}, G_{t+1}, W_{t+1})$ based on the error residual. The process terminates at $t = 5$ or upon reaching the budget ceiling.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Lift Grants

- **Loop State Evolution:**
  $$\text{State}_t = (P_t, G_t, W_t)$$
  where $P_t$ is the current proposal, $G_t$ is the active lift grant, and $W_t$ is the verified witness set.
  $$P_{t+1} = \operatorname{refine}(P_t, \operatorname{residual}_t) \quad \text{for } t \le 5$$
- **Metric/Cochain Bridge Invariant:**
  In simplicial gravity derivations, additive combinatorial curvature deficits and metric Regge dihedral holonomies are evaluated by separate, non-communicating checkers to prevent circular definition.
- **Terminal Tripartite Halt Policy:**
  $$\text{Halt} \iff \text{Certificate Achieved} \lor \text{Cost} \ge \text{Limit} \lor t == 5$$

## 4. Team Structure & Panel Roles

- **Pattern:** `research_cell`
- **Normal Path Calls:** 5 calls per pilot campaign.
- **Roles:**
  - `deep_researcher` (Opus, count: $1$): Proposes theoretical derivations and formal proof steps.
  - `adversary` (Fable, count: $1$): Executes negative controls and stress-tests assumptions.
  - `coder` (Sonnet, count: $1$): Implements exact finite witnesses and stage validation harnesses.
  - `host_checker` (None, count: $1$): Deterministic host verifier; evaluates finite controls and regulates lift grants.
- **Boost Rule:** If a witness check fails twice consecutively, the subsequent derivation round must start directly from the adversary's explicit counterexample (limit 1).

---

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Independent Proof Review:** While finite controls are evaluated deterministically on the host, theoretical proofs remain classified as `ANALYTICALLY_UNVERIFIED` until verified by independent interactive theorem provers (e.g. Lean 4) or human audit.
- **Lift Enforcement:** Any submission utilizing mathematical objects outside the active lift grant is refused with `UNAUTHORIZED_LIFT_VIOLATION`.
