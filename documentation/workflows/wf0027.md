## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF27 extends bounded check-and-repair by incorporating a host-evaluated **stationary-loop detector**. In iterative repair, language models frequently oscillate between two identical incorrect configurations or reach a stationary plateau where proposed edits produce identical residual vectors $r_t$.

WF27 detects stationary cycles after 3 repetitions. When a stationary loop is confirmed, the host refuses further localized edits, forces a retraction to an earlier checkpoint, and triggers a structural reframe or coordinate lift.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Residual Feedback:** On each turn, the host injects the exact cochain residual vector $r_t$ and the set of violating cell identifiers into the turn context.
2. **Stationary Loop Tracking:** The host records the trajectory of residual vectors $(r_1, r_2, \dots, r_t)$.
3. **Loop Detection Trigger:** If $r_t == r_{t-1}$ or $r_t == r_{t-3}$, a stationary trap is flagged.
4. **Forced Retraction & Lift:**
   - Local edge adjustments are disabled.
   - The state is rolled back via `RetractMove` to the pre-loop state.
   - The host enforces a global topological inversion or path-integrated reframe (using tree-basis inversion $B_T^{-1}$ rather than edge-local adjustments).
5. **Turn Cap Halt:** Halts immediately if $\Phi_1 = 0$ is achieved or upon reaching the turn cap.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Path-Integrated Inversion

- **Cochain Residual Vector:**
  For edge claims $c_t \in C^1(X)$:
  $$r_t = \delta_1 c_t \in C^2(X)$$
- **Stationary Condition:**
  $$\operatorname{StationaryTrap}(t) \iff \exists k \in \{1, 2, 3\}, \quad r_t == r_{t-k} \ne 0$$
- **Global Tree-Basis Inversion:**
  Local adjustments on cycle edges often cancel out. WF27 forces a change of basis to a spanning tree $T \subset G$:
  $$c_{\text{tree}} = B_T^{-1} r_t$$
  solving the coboundary equation globally across fundamental cycle chords rather than guessing individual edge mutations.

---

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Residual Gate:** The run is admitted if and only if primary obstruction $\Phi_1 == 0$ is certified by exact rational linear programming.
- **Copying Prevention:** Assisted arms (where hints or tree bases are provided) are strictly partitioned from unassisted discovery arms to avoid conflating copying with autonomous solving.

## 6. Claim Hygiene Evaluation

1. **What the Evidence Supports:**
   - Pilot 27 demonstrates that monitoring residual vectors detects circular repair oscillation on discrete graphs.
2. **What the Evidence Does Not Support:**
   - Historical live runs providing full repair hints demonstrate that models can copy solutions, not that they can independently resolve stationary traps.
3. **Narrowest Defensible Claim:**
   - WF27 specifies an automated trap detector that halts oscillatory repair loops by tracking exact cochain residuals and triggering global tree-basis reframes.
4. **Next Test Needed:**
   - Test the stationary loop detector on 20 frustrated grid graphs with $L = 6$, confirming that 100% of 2-period and 3-period oscillations trigger the retraction gate.
