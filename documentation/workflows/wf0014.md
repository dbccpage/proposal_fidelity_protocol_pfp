## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF14 governs the lifecycle and admission of diagnostic detectors (antibodies) within the Epistemic Immune System (EIS). Rather than acting on a single task, WF14 operates as a **meta-workflow**: it analyzes observed failure traces from previous runs, extracts candidate error patterns, and subjects them to rigorous immune gating before allowing them to be added to the active screening library.

WF14 enforces two non-negotiable biological principles:
1. **Negative Selection (Self-Tolerance):** A detector must never fire on known-good outputs (the "Self" panel).
2. **Two-Signal Admission:** A detector cannot be admitted based on lexical pattern matching alone; it requires both a pattern match (Signal 1) and independent confirmation by a host defect check (Signal 2).

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Sealed Self Panel:** Compile and freeze a representative panel of known-valid model solutions and verified proofs under SHA-256 custody.
2. **Antibody Extraction:** Analyze historical execution failures to formulate candidate detector rules (regular expressions, AST checks, or negative constraints).
3. **Negative Selection Gate:** Execute the candidate detector against every member of the sealed Self panel. If it triggers on even one valid instance, it is immediately rejected as an autoimmune rule.
4. **Two-Signal Evaluation:** Test the detector against a panel of known failure cases. Verify that both Signal 1 (detector trigger) and Signal 2 (deterministic checker failure) coincide.
5. **Admission to Pharmacy Catalog:** Admitted antibodies are indexed in `eis/pharmacy_catalog.py` with explicit specificity scores.

## 3. Mathematical Model & Two-Signal Gate

- **Negative Selection Filter:**
  Let $\text{Self} = \{s_1, \dots, s_M\}$ denote the sealed panel of valid outputs. A candidate detector $d$ is retained if and only if:
  $$\forall s \in \text{Self}, \quad \operatorname{match}(d, s) < \theta_{\text{threshold}}$$
- **Self-Tolerance Ratio:**
  $$\text{Self-Tolerance}(d) = 1 - \frac{\sum_{s \in \text{Self}} \mathbf{1}[\operatorname{match}(d, s) \ge \theta]}{|\text{Self}|}$$
  Admission requires $\text{Self-Tolerance}(d) == 1.0$.
- **Two-Signal Admission Logic:**
  $$\operatorname{Admit}(d) \iff (\text{Signal}_1(d, x) == 1) \land (\text{Signal}_2(x) == \text{FAIL}) \land (\text{Self-Tolerance}(d) == 1.0)$$
  where $\text{Signal}_1$ is the detector's pattern match and $\text{Signal}_2$ is the host's exact defect check.

## 4. Team Structure & Panel Roles

- **Pattern:** `red_team_pair`
- **Normal Path Calls:** Per candidate detector.
- **Roles:**
  - `coder` (Sonnet, count: $1$): Implements candidate detectors and extraction logic.
  - `adversary` (Fable, count: $1$): Synthesizes adversarial "near-self" inputs (valid mathematical solutions phrased unusually) to trigger false positives.
  - `host_checker` (None, count: $1$): Evaluates the negative selection gate over the sealed Self panel.
- **Boost Rule:** If a candidate detector fires on Self, it is permanently discarded; Opus is authorized to propose one strictly narrower detector (limit 1).

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Admission Gate:** Automated execution of the detector suite across the entire local `eis/` self-fixture bank.
- **Autoimmune Refusal:** Any false positive on Self terminates admission with `AUTOIMMUNE_DETECTOR_REJECTED`.
