## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF25 addresses the context window and compute explosion that occurs when verifying large discrete spatial lattices or network graphs. On a large lattice (e.g. a $16 \times 16$ discrete torus), traversing every individual elementary plaquette consumes thousands of tokens and exhausts reasoning budgets.

WF25 introduces **hierarchical algebraic decimation**: contractible, trivially flat sub-blocks are decimated and collapsed at zero token cost. The model is asked to reason only over the non-contractible homology generators of the quotient complex.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Plaquette Flatness Decimation:** The host or pre-processing script identifies and certifies contractible planar sub-blocks that have zero internal curvature.
2. **Homological Quotienting:** Contractible regions are quotiented out, reducing the lattice to a coarse graph of macro-blocks and fundamental cycles.
3. **Targeted Non-Contractible Evaluation:** The model evaluates holonomies exclusively along the non-contractible cycles (e.g. the longitudinal and meridian cycles of a torus).
4. **Commutation & Residual Check:** The host checks whether the remaining cycle holonomies commute and satisfy topological invariants.
5. **Macro-Block Aggregation:** Optional second pass aggregates macro-blocks into an exact global invariant.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Homological Reduction

- **Triviality of Contractible Loops:**
  If a simply connected sub-complex $U \subset X$ has zero curvature everywhere:
  $$\forall \text{face } f \subset U, \quad \prod_{e \in \partial f} U(e) = \operatorname{id}_G$$
  then any closed path $\gamma \subset U$ satisfies $\operatorname{Hol}(\gamma) = \operatorname{id}_G$.
- **Quotient Complex Holonomy:**
  The holonomy of the full lattice $X$ factors completely through the fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$:
  $$\operatorname{Hol}: \pi_1(X) \to G$$
  For a 2-torus $T^2$, this reduces the infinite set of paths to two generators $(\alpha, \beta)$ subject to the relation $[\alpha, \beta] = e$.
- **Token Efficiency Invariant:**
  Decimation reduces the token requirement from $O(|V| + |E|)$ to $O(\operatorname{rank} H_1(X))$.

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Dual Checkers:** Evaluates cycle holonomies independently using sparse matrix products and topological quotient graphs.
- **Soundness Guarantee:** Decimation is proven sound by verifying that the boundary coboundaries of decimated regions vanish identically.
