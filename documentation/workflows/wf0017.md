## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF17 governs comparative claims between mathematical structures or algorithms. When an agent asserts that structure $A$ is equivalent to structure $B$, or that algorithm $A$ strictly dominates algorithm $B$, WF17 mandates an explicit epistemic receipt:
- Claims of **equivalence** require a formal **equivalence certificate** (e.g. an explicit bisimulation relation or isomorphism).
- Claims of **difference** require a concrete **distinguishing witness** (an input where behaviors diverge).
- Every asserted theorem must be paired with explicit countermodels establishing the necessity of its hypotheses.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Artifact Declaration:** Declare two formal objects, transition systems, or formulations $(A, B)$.
2. **Claim Hypothesis:** Propose either an equivalence relation $A \equiv B$ or a strict behavioral separation $A \not\equiv B$.
3. **Receipt Generation:**
   - For equivalence: Synthesize a bisimulation relation $R$ and verify that transitions match symmetrically.
   - For separation: Emit a finite input trace $x^*$ such that $A(x^*) \ne B(x^*)$.
4. **Adversarial Countermodeling:** Fable attempts to construct countermodels that satisfy the premises but violate the claimed relationship.
5. **Finite Instance Host Check:** The host evaluates the witnesses or bisimulation tables over finite test instances.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Duality Receipts

- **Bisimulation Equivalence Witness:**
  For transition systems $T_1 = (S_1, \to_1)$ and $T_2 = (S_2, \to_2)$, a relation $B \subseteq S_1 \times S_2$ is a bisimulation witness if:
  $$\forall (p, q) \in B, \quad p \xrightarrow{a}_1 p' \implies \exists q', \ q \xrightarrow{a}_2 q' \land (p', q') \in B$$
  and conversely for transitions from $q$.
- **Fenchel-Rockafellar Duality Certificate:**
  For convex optimization problems:
  $$\inf_{x \in X} \{ f(x) + g(Ax) \} = \sup_{y \in Y^*} \{ -f^*(A^T y) - g^*(-y) \}$$
  The certificate must provide primal $x^*$ and dual $y^*$ demonstrating equality within declared constraint qualifications.
- **Čech Cohomology Gluing:**
  Identifies whether difference witnesses represent trivial boundary artifacts ($\operatorname{im} \delta_0$) or true topological obstructions ($H^1(\mathcal{U}; \mathcal{F}) \ne 0$).

## 4. Team Structure & Panel Roles

- **Pattern:** `research_cell`
- **Normal Path Calls:** Research budget-capped.
- **Roles:**
  - `deep_researcher` (Opus, count: $2$): Derives theoretical equivalence theorems and formulates formal proofs.
  - `adversary` (Fable, count: $1$): Construct countermodels and searches for edge-case counterexamples.
  - `coder` (Sonnet, count: $2$): Implements finite instance simulators, bisimulation checkers, and test harnesses.
  - `host_checker` (None, count: $1$): Deterministic host witness and countermodel evaluator.
- **Boost Rule:** If a countermodel is confirmed by the host, the theoretical claim is immediately narrowed or withdrawn; Opus is authorized to restate the qualified claim once (limit 1).

---

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Witness Checking:** Distinguishing witnesses are replayed directly on the host against both artifacts; if outputs are identical, the separation claim is rejected.
- **Bisimulation Checking:** Bisimulation tables are verified exhaustively over finite state spaces.

## 6. Claim Hygiene Evaluation

1. **What the Evidence Supports:**
   - The pilot estate specifies rigorous criteria for pairing theoretical mathematical claims with concrete distinguishing witnesses.
2. **What the Evidence Does Not Support:**
   - Generating distinguishing witnesses for arbitrary infinite-state systems is undecidable in general.
3. **Narrowest Defensible Claim:**
   - WF17 provides an operational verification gate that requires mathematical equivalence or difference claims between discrete systems to be substantiated by host-checked witnesses or bisimulation certificates.
4. **Next Test Needed:**
   - Run WF17 on 10 pairs of finite state automata (5 equivalent, 5 non-equivalent) and confirm that all 5 non-equivalent pairs produce minimal distinguishing traces.
