---

## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF32 enforces the **principle of claim-scoped admission**. In complex multi-claim documents (such as mathematical papers, audit reports, or scientific proposals), verifying a single lemma or calculation frequently leads to the mistaken assumption that the entire document has been verified.

WF32 mandates that a receipt never certifies an entire document or model response wholesale. Instead, the receipt explicitly scopes and enumerates the exact subset of claims $J_{\text{checked}}$ that were individually subjected to deterministic host checkers. Any surrounding prose, unverified intermediate assertions, universal claims, or unquoted text remains explicitly designated as **`UNADMITTED_UNVERIFIED_CANDIDATE_MATERIAL`**.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Claim Extraction:** Parse the model's response into an explicit indexed dictionary of individual mathematical claims $J = \{j_1, \dots, j_m\}$.
2. **Independent Claim Checking:** Each claim $j_k$ is assigned an independent checker $C_k$ and evaluated against its specific witness:
   $$\operatorname{status}(j_k) = C_k(\operatorname{witness}(j_k))$$
3. **Receipt Partitioning:** Construct the receipt recording:
   - Admitted claims: $J_{\text{admitted}} = \{j \in J \mid \operatorname{status}(j) == \text{PASS}\}$.
   - Refused claims: $J_{\text{refused}} = \{j \in J \mid \operatorname{status}(j) == \text{FAIL}\}$.
   - Unaudited material: Prose descriptions, contextual claims, and speculative summaries.
4. **Quotation Grounding:** Any literal source quote must reference exact source bytes and span offsets.
5. **Receipt Sealing:** Seal the itemized claim receipt under SHA-256 custody.

---

## 3. Mathematical Model & Admission Calculus

- **Exact Claim Admission Map:**
  $$\operatorname{Admitted}(J) = \{ j \in J \mid C_j(\text{witness}_j) = \text{ACCEPT} \}$$
- **Non-Inference Rule (Zero Contagion of Truth):**
  Verification does not propagate across logical implication without an explicit host-checked proof step:
  $$j_1 \in \operatorname{Admitted}(J) \land (j_1 \implies j_2) \ \not\Longrightarrow \ j_2 \in \operatorname{Admitted}(J)$$
  Claim $j_2$ remains unadmitted until $C_2(\text{witness}_2) = \text{ACCEPT}$.
- **Span Grounding Contract:**
  For any quote $q$:
  $$\operatorname{bytes}(q) == \text{SourceFile}[\text{start\_offset} : \text{end\_offset}]$$

---

## 4. Team Structure & Panel Roles

- **Pattern:** `solo`
- **Normal Path Calls:** 1 call.
- **Roles:**
  - `coder` (Sonnet, count: $1$): Formulates the structured, claim-indexed response and attaches witnesses.
  - `host_checker` (None, count: $1$): Evaluates independent checkers for each indexed claim.
- **Boost Rule:** If an unchecked or ungrounded claim is detected in the response, it is automatically stripped from the admitted receipt without authorizing a retry (limit 0).

---

## 5. Host Verification Protocol

- **Per-Claim Checkers:** Each mathematical assertion (e.g. eigenvalue bounds, trace conservation, edge parity) executes its own isolated checker function.
- **Receipt Schema:** Emits `claim_scoped_receipt.json` explicitly enumerating which claims were verified and which remain unverified candidate material.

---

## 6. Claim Hygiene Evaluation

1. **What the Evidence Supports:**
   - Formal replay harnesses demonstrate that itemized claim indexing prevents blanket attestation of complex model responses.
2. **What the Evidence Does Not Support:**
   - Claim scoping does not verify whether the set of checked claims is logically sufficient to support the author's broader qualitative conclusions.
3. **Narrowest Defensible Claim:**
   - WF32 establishes a granular receipt protocol that restricts host acceptance exclusively to explicitly checked mathematical claims and witnesses, leaving all unverified surrounding text unadmitted.
4. **Next Test Needed:**
   - Generate a 5-claim mathematical paper draft and verify that the WF32 receipt indexes the 2 verified formulas while explicitly tagging the 3 unproven assertions as unadmitted.
