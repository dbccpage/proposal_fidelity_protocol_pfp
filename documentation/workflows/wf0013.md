## 1. Objective & Purpose

WF13 packages the 50 certified challenges of the PFP research estate into standalone, isolated test capsules. Each capsule targets an exact mathematical or physical domain—such as quantum error-correcting codes, gauge anomaly cancellation, Regge simplicial curvature, or finite group commutators.

WF13 explicitly rejects cross-domain aggregate percentage scores (e.g. "82% overall accuracy"). Results are reported strictly per domain, preventing high scores in elementary arithmetic from masking complete failure in higher algebra or gauge geometry.

---

## 2. Process Specification

1. **Capsule Extraction:** Select an individual certified challenge from the 50-problem catalog (`c011` to `c060`).
2. **Checker Registration:** The host registers and binds the exact Python or Lean verification checker before dispatch.
3. **Capsule Dispatch:** Dispatch a single-turn request under the WF1/WF7 contract.
4. **Independent Evaluation:** The registered domain checker evaluates the model response against exact algebraic invariants.
5. **Domain-Specific Reporting:** Record the outcome (Accepted, Refused, or Failed) under its specific task family.

## 3. Mathematical Domain Invariants

WF13 exercises exact, non-floating-point evaluation across distinct scientific domains:

- **Quantum Stabilizer Codes:**
  Stabilizer generators $g_i, g_j \in \mathcal{P}_n$ commute if and only if their binary symplectic inner product vanishes:
  $$\langle g_i, g_j \rangle_{\text{symp}} \equiv x_i \cdot z_j + z_i \cdot x_j \equiv 0 \pmod 2$$
- **U(1) Gauge Anomaly Cancellation:**
  A set of chiral fermion U(1) charges $\{q_1, \dots, q_k\} \subset \mathbb{Z}$ cancels gravitational and gauge anomalies if and only if:
  $$\sum_{i=1}^k q_i = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \sum_{i=1}^k q_i^3 = 0$$
- **Regge Simplicial Curvature Deficit:**
  At each $(d-2)$-dimensional hinge $h$ in a simplicial mesh, the deficit angle $\epsilon_h$ is computed from exact rational dihedral angles:
  $$\epsilon_h = 2\pi - \sum_{\sigma \supset h} \theta(\sigma, h)$$
- **Finite Group Commutator:**
  Verifies exact identity satisfaction for group elements:
  $$[g, h] = g^{-1} h^{-1} g h = e$$
