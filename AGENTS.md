# Agent instructions

## Terms (read this first)

A result has force only **on terms**: what must hold between the parties for it to count, as in the terms of an agreement. Terms obtain only between the parties. There is no outside viewer, and the list of terms may stay open.

- **Example:** two observers judge two sandcastles. What each can tell apart, the record each keeps, and how they hand it over must match on both sheets; those shared rows are the terms. If their rules for "better" differ, they did not disagree, they were not on the same terms.
- **In code:** the type is `Terms`. The link that supplies terms to a result is `supplies_terms`, and `holds_on` is the list of terms on each node.
- **Receipt** keeps its meaning: the record of what was checked against the terms.
- **Wording:** "terms" replaces "admission". Do not use presupposition, known condition, or any word that claims certainty, knowledge, axiom, first principles or transcendental for this idea.
- Existing code and DAG names that still say "admission" stay as they are until their own rename PR.
