**AuthorityEffect:** None. This is fictional examination staging, not a historical quotation or a source of mathematical authority.

Examiner points at the declared carrier. Adversary asks which claim its checker can establish. Gauge Breaker asks for the smallest valid near miss. Their task in this companion is **read a claim at the checker scope**.

Begin with the [study guide](study_guide.md), then attempt the [quiz](quiz.md) before reading the [separate instructor key](answer_key.md). A lower-case wire token names a role only after its interpretation is declared. Arrows sketch graph edges; they do not automatically attach higher-dimensional cells. A receipt records its stated finite check and grants no authority to extend Genesis or make a physical claim.

The examiner's standard is a concrete calculation, an explicit counterexample, or a source-bound missing obligation. The manuscript [source reading](../paper.md#41-reading-a-reported-catch-rate) governs the answer.
