## Withdrawn in this revision

The previous ledger entry P3-13 stated that a retrospective analysis of 28
adversarial probe runs isolated four recurring failure modes, with 13 detections
and 15 grounded witnesses. It is withdrawn. Those 28 rows of the harvest are 18
author-written scripted responses and 10 responses from one model, labelled by
the prompt-specific rule set; the labels were not independently checked. The
two detections on model responses fire on sentences that deny or conditionally
mention the targeted claim, and one row counted as grounded is an
author-written false claim that the rules did not flag. The previous P3-11 described the
package receipt check as cryptographic verification that rejects forged
certificates “even after digest recomputation”; it is narrowed to P3-07.

## Excluded promotions

The paper does not claim a general immunity result, a causal treatment effect,
controlled model transfer, a physical Hamiltonian bridge, arbitrary tower
extension, or correspondence between the implementation and any formal
proof-assistant model. Retaining a refusal does not authorize retaining every raw
field.

Earlier ledgers are available in Git history at the baseline. Historical passing
counts are not copied into this current ledger.
