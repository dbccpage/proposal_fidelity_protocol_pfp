# Apartment 50: Paper 002

**AuthorityEffect:** None. This is fictional examination staging, not a historical quotation or a source of mathematical authority.

Examiner asks for the calculation and both directions of the proof. Adversary asks
which claim the checker can establish. Gauge Breaker changes an edge bit, rehashes a
false receipt, and asks whether anyone notices. Their task in this companion
is **signed certificates, honest search frontiers, and the restricted
integrated-path obstruction**.

Begin with the [study guide](study_guide.md), attempt the [quiz](quiz.md), and
only then open the separate [worked instructor key](instructor_key.md).
Levels 0-7 move from a declared sign equation to the all-length weight gap
and a publication evidence audit. A symbol names a role only after its
interpretation is declared. Walk arrows do not attach higher-dimensional
cells. A receipt binds its stated check and grants no authority to extend
Genesis or enlarge the physical claim.

The examiner's standard is a concrete calculation, an explicit counterexample,
or a precisely identified missing obligation. The [manuscript](../paper.md)
governs the answers. The staging is literary; the score is an instructional
convention.
